Where UOR-R4 is today

In one paragraph. UOR-R4 is pre-alpha: 1 of 8 milestones is complete, and no model holds a useful conversation. A saved discrete geometric model passed M1's six bars on two seeds, through its integer export. That is a real language-base result. It does not establish complete replies, new kinds of answer, durable conversation, or a shippable product. This page gives the measured numbers first, then the limits and what is still open.

Correct first guesses on DEV. The saved M1 model beats both plain count tables at both seeds.

Terms are in the glossary. Claims are labelled proof, measurement or hypothesis. A passing check shows code does what it says; it is not a trained capability, and NOT_RUN never means passed.

M1 is complete

The closing artifact is antigravity-110's trunk-joint-pointer-v1-d11, a discrete 19-table uor-r4.lut-discrete-trunk/1 model exported as two seeded UORLUT01 files. It is not the separate float-trained FBS trunk. The older close grade covers bars (a)–(e); claude-211's p-f panel score adds bar (f), and M1 #2029 is closed.

The separate FBS trunk: replies in conversation (since 31 Oct)

The float-trained FBS trunk is a second model path; it has not joined the saved discrete M1 model. Since M1 closed, the work on it has been the second open question: can a model answer in a conversation? Every number below is a measurement at its named scope.

Human-written text. On a held-out panel of 1,460,697 rows of public conversation and encyclopedia text, a batch of 64 with twice the training text gets 331,698 and 342,250 next tokens right at two seeds, against T2's 256,660: ahead at both seeds (claude-301, 6 Nov). Width 256 gets about 125,000 more rows right than width 128; width 512 did not fit the GPU's memory (claude-317, notebook).

Recall in controlled conversations. Since 10 Nov the panels are conversations where a value is told and later asked for. Claude-358's three models (p-s1 to p-s3, 13 Nov) state the told value on 314, 320 and 325 of 578 recall questions. One of them, p-s2, serves on an Apple M1 at 1,567 to 1,636 tok/s decode and 4,309 to 4,499 tok/s context prefill.

What the vectors carry alone. V0, claude-376 (14 Nov) served those three models over 2,218 conversation rows twice: as exported, and with their exact turn memory dropped. Exact replies are 377, 380 and 441 as exported and 171, 173 and 211 without the memory. Almost every fact the model gets right comes from the turn memory's table, which is keyed on the turn's exact token ids and counted from TRAIN. The vector state alone does not yet carry the facts.

The native-space route (D85, 13 Nov). The owner set the default route to one persistent integer geometric state that training and serving share, with the same operators and bit-identical replay, instead of float master weights and an export gap. The first Q31 vector state has trained on CUDA in a 64-step contract test, CPU and CUDA equal after every step (codex-5); that is a contract test, not language-model training. The integer crate now restores that trained state with no numerical conversion and runs the same shared transition: after 64 trained steps, the restored state and its transition results match as raw words. No token generation is claimed (codex-8). The discrete path's CUDA training, CPU training and integer serving now agree on the same fixture bytes, on two fixtures (claude-372).

In progress (14 Nov). Carrying the state across training windows, V1 (#3979); today training carries no state from one window to the next. Vector-native reads, V2(a) (#3978). Neither has a result yet.

The point-key and nearest-readout units of late October (claude-217 to claude-232) are recorded in the float-training notebook and the open questions.

What does not work yet

  • Useful conversation. M1's next-token bars do not establish coherent, correct complete replies.
  • Facts from the vectors alone. Without the exact turn memory, the current models answer almost no fact questions (V0, claude-376); the memory's table supplies them.
  • New reply kinds. p-f has held-out prompt phrasings but 62.5% of its reference replies come from TRAIN. The FBS generation results make that limitation visible.
  • A joined model. The discrete M1 path and FBS trunk remain separate. Not every FBS part is served in one integer session (pair rows are refused by the export), and accepted-model text/chat distribution remains open.
  • M2–M8. Grounded replies, durable memory, full accepted-model serving, laptop cost, reasoning/coding, distribution and alpha release are not complete.

Historical result: K16

The 19 Oct K16-A model scored 65 and 463 of 14,287, below unigram 1,226 and T2 6,300. K16-R collapsed to 19 or 20 occupied cells. These were important negative results, but they are not the current best model and must not be used to erase the later M1 close (K16 outcome).

Four older 214M Hugging Face checkpoints predate today's rules: they used softmax and matrix multiplication and are historical evidence, not the D11 product.
