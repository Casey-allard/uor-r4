UOR-R4

Transformer parts and their geometric replacements

In one paragraph. A transformer is built from standard parts: a tokenizer, embedding table, position signal, attention, feed-forward layers, normalization, output layer, loss and optimizer. UOR-R4 replaces those parts with geometric or exact mechanisms that avoid softmax and dense matrix multiplication. M1 is complete (1 of 8): the saved discrete model passed all six bars at two seeds. Useful conversation is still open, and the separate FBS complete-reply line shows why.

The replacements at their current scope

Transformer partGeometric or exact replacement in UOR-R4Current state
TokenizerByte-level BPE, 4,096 pieces, identity d36d3e87ACTIVE; pinned to corpora, panels and artifacts
Embedding tablePrime-zeta codebook and address cells: prime/zeta addresses on a 16×16 boardACTIVE; construction and placement results exist, independent language effects remain open
PositionSpiralCore transport spin on the FBS trunk; PositionHolonomyV1 with the discrete quaternion recurrence in the M1 compositionTrained and served in their respective models; independent order effects remain open
Attention / look-backRoute, Flock and Relation reads: tree walk, neighbour consensus and copy linksACTIVE; independent effects are not all isolated
Feed-forwardHamilton rotation gate is in M1 and FBS; SpiralCore/FBS routes are on the FBS trunk onlyHamilton trained in M1 and FBS; FBS routes trained and served; dated float projection form READY
NormalizationRMS norm with integer square root on the FBS trunk; OrbitRootSnapNormV1 on the discrete M1 trunkEach served in its own model; not the same normalization mechanism
Output headAdmission: open a few address cells, then rank inside themACTIVE; M1's post-rotation OuterL1V1 readout is measured
LossRank-margin hinge: right token must beat its strongest rival by a fixed gapACTIVE; no likelihood is formed
OptimizerDiscrete learning: try every legal rotation and keep only strict improvements, including BVG-KACTIVE; produced the M1 model
Matrix-multiply runtimeD11 integer serving: integer adds, shifts, tables, multiply and divide, but no float or dense matmulACTIVE; the M1 pair and a separate FBS export serve
Exact memoryExact addressed object store and exact turn memoryOracle and familiar-DEV gains exist; new-phrasing key formation remains the blocker

A replacement being ACTIVE means it is on a current model path, not that it has proved an independent advantage. READY means built and tested but not trained in; PARKED means tested forms failed or broke a rule while the mechanism stays open. See the mechanism board.

The replacement map at 31 October 2026: M1 is complete, while FBS and durable-reply work continue.

What M1 established

The saved discrete 19-table model has exact 14,287-row DEV export/reference agreement and passes B4. It scores:

  • DEV 7,002 / 7,001 of 14,287 against unigram 1,226 and T2 6,300;
  • Fresh V2 7,215 / 7,228 of 14,479 against unigram 1,279 and T2 6,582;
  • p-f 7,850 / 7,872 of 15,953 against unigram 1,409 and T2 7,375, beyond paired band 81;
  • Apple M1 decode 10,876 / 10,566 tok/s under the bar-f protocol.

p-f has no prompt value-masked templates in TRAIN at K = 1,024, so the model reads new prompt phrasings into known reply kinds. But 62.5% of p-f's reference replies are verbatim TRAIN replies. That is not new reply kinds or useful conversation (M1 close, Fresh V2, bar-f report).

The remaining gaps

Reply opening on new phrasing. On the separate FBS path, claude-217's teacher-forced p-s openings are 17.4%, 13.7% and 18.6% at three seeds, against about 80% on later tokens. Exact turn memory keys on exact first token ids and misses reworded prompts. A cycle step ends loops but does not repair the opening.

A joined model and M4. The discrete M1 artifact and the FBS trunk do not share a table, trunk or served session. M4 needs the accepted model's full quality, CLI, session and audit path; PRLL is built and registered, but GPU held-out scoring remains open.

M5 and M7. Full-path laptop cost and accepted-model browser/library/CLI parity are unmeasured. Native M1 serving does not establish browser parity or energy.

M2 and M3. Next-token ranking is not complete correct replies or durable memory. The true-address 80/80 oracle is exact-read evidence, not learned address formation.

Historical K16

K16-A scored 65 and 463 of 14,287, below unigram 1,226 and T2 6,300. K16-R collapsed to 19 or 20 cells. These are dated configuration negatives, not the current best and not a reason to repeat the same test without new causal evidence (K16 outcome).
