Where UOR-R4 is going

In one paragraph. The goal is a fast language model that runs on your own laptop or browser, holds a conversation with durable memory, and later reasons and writes code, all through geometry and exact memory rather than a transformer. M1 is complete: 1 of 8 milestones. M2 is the next capability milestone: complete correct replies grounded in exact memory. The immediate obstacle is that the current models' facts come from an exact table rather than their learned state; since 13 Nov (D85) the route is one native integer state shared by training and serving. This page is a plan, not a promise.

M1 is complete. Current work targets replies that come from the model's own state, and one useful model end to end.

Terms are in the glossary. Labels are proof, measurement or hypothesis.

What M1 established

The saved discrete model passes six bars at both seeds: a self-contained D11 artifact; exact 14,287-row DEV export/reference agreement and a planted-fault control; tokens per second; DEV integer top-1 above unigram and T2; Fresh V2 transfer; and held-out-phrasing transfer.

  • DEV: 7,002 / 7,001 of 14,287 against unigram 1,226 and T2 6,300.
  • Fresh V2: 7,215 / 7,228 of 14,479 against unigram 1,279 and T2 6,582.
  • p-f: 7,850 / 7,872 of 15,953 against unigram 1,409 and T2 7,375.
  • Apple M1 decode: 10,876 / 10,566 tok/s, measured with a 192-token prefill and 64-token decode.

The limitation is equally important: 62.5% of p-f's reference replies are verbatim TRAIN replies. M1 reads new prompt phrasings into known reply kinds; it does not show new reply kinds or useful conversation. See CHARTER §4.8, the M1 close, Fresh V2, and the bar-f report.

The current order of work

Since 13 Nov the route is D85: one persistent native geometric state, in one declared integer or fixed-point representation, that training and serving share, with the same typed operators and bit-identical replay. Float master weights and a float-to-integer export gap become reference work. PLAN gives the four steps:

  1. Declare the state and its operators (native-space-state-contract): every carried field, each transition's shared training and serving operator, and a bit-identical replay fixture. The first Q31 vector state has trained on CUDA in a 64-step contract test, with CPU and CUDA equal after every step (codex-5).
  2. Keep gradients and optimizer updates in that state, not in a parallel float master.
  3. Native CUDA training kernels with the same transition identities the integer runtime serves.
  4. One trained end-to-end model, serialized losslessly and served with no numeric conversion, then scored on complete replies with tokens per second.

On the current FBS models the measured gap is clear. Served without their exact turn memory, the models give 171 to 211 exact replies of 2,218 against 377 to 441 with it, and almost every fact comes from the memory's table (V0, claude-376). Work in progress on 14 Nov carries the state across training windows (V1, #3979) and adds vector-native reads (V2(a), #3978). M2 and M3, then M4, follow one useful model; after that come laptop cost, reasoning and coding, distribution and the alpha release.

The late-October point-key and nearest-readout units (claude-217 to claude-232) are recorded in the open questions and the float-training notebook.

What comes after the first useful reply

  • M3: qualify durable memory: provenance, correction, abstention, reset and restart, with token-identical restored sessions.
  • M4: select the accepted model and qualify the full UORLUT01 export, CLI, quality, session and audit path. A lawful likelihood (PRLL) exists, but GPU held-out scoring remains open.
  • M5: measure real laptop cost rather than infer efficiency from arithmetic counts.
  • M6: add reasoning and coding on the same model with executable checks and causal ablation.
  • M7: prove library/CLI/browser parity on the accepted model and ship a truthful local-by-default Studio, with optional user-configured web API.
  • M8: cut one versioned, checksum-pinned release and verify it independently on consumer hardware.

Nothing ships until a trained model passes the remaining product bars. Serving an exported D11 artifact on the user's device is the product and is allowed. GPU remains the qualification authority; only the owner-authorized D84 CPU program runs development research locally (charter, sections 3.5-3.6).

The biggest unknown

Can the model's own learned state carry what a reply needs, the told values and the facts, rather than an exact table keyed on the turn's token ids? M1 answered the narrower next-token count-table question. On recall in controlled conversations, claude-358's models state a told value on 314, 320 and 325 of 578 questions at three seeds, but V0 shows the facts they get right come almost entirely from the turn memory's table. Useful conversation has not been established.
