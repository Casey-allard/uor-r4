The unit of work is one **coherent milestone** in the shared native model, not a timer, a fixed number of retries, or a fresh architectural survey. Start from the live roadmap/current state, prior best artifact and its failures. Ask Socratically: What capability is missing? What information can the model actually observe? What assumptions create the proposed mechanism's advantage? What simpler mechanism could do the same job? What observation would distinguish them? Which observation would be ambiguous because of instrumentation, data, optimization or loss denominator? Use first-principles derivation and primary literature to sharpen the question, then build the cheapest test that can change a real decision.

## Scope of a negative result

One failed empirical run does **not** retire H4, S7, Hopf, harmonic memory, a local channel, or an entire mechanism family. It may reject a particular artifact, parameterization, training recipe, panel or claim under explicit conditions. First audit source and executable identity, artifact reload, actual causal inputs, data split, labels, denominator, matched comparator, sufficient fit exposure and measurement limits. If the instrument is invalid, repair it and retain the old result as invalid/inconclusive, never as a mechanism failure. If the instrument is sound, explain whether failure is due to representation/information loss, optimization, data, integration, serving cost or test construction. A formal impossibility or invariant counterexample can rule out a defined representation more broadly, but state all hypotheses and the exact class it covers. Otherwise mark the family **conditional** or **deprioritized for this measured seam**, not disproved.

Likewise, one positive result on an authored or exposed panel does not qualify general language or unique geometric advantage. A mechanism can be retained as a useful component even when an ordinary comparator ties. Promote only the measured ability and operator at the tested scope; seek fresh contexts, loaded behavior, matched information/cost and causally relevant ablations before broad claims. A prospectively redesigned evaluation may be justified for a different question, but cannot change the historical failed verdict or promote that failed artifact. Preserve the original criterion, raw result and reason for any new comparison; do not weaken acceptance to make a candidate pass.

## Avoiding loops while preserving creativity

Before repeating a run, identify the new information it will provide and how its possible outcomes route the roadmap. If all outcomes leave the decision unchanged, do a different experiment or advance a separate dependency. Inspect individual regressions against the immediately preceding best artifact, not only aggregate performance. Keep alternative explanations alive until distinguished, but do not require every speculative geometry to be exhausted before useful local competence, memory, prose and coding work proceed. A bounded local repair can become a component while structural geometry is still investigated. When an unexpected strong result or failure appears, expand or reorder the milestone as needed; project real machine costs prospectively and preserve the evidence.

Under [D8](../docs/integration/DECISIONS.md#d8--correct-the-training-method-and-reference-ladder), complete the authorized rung through its declared decision or report an actual block. Preserve the original gate and result when a later criterion is revised. Small integrity checks are engineering evidence; meaningful natural-text learning and matched retained behavior decide the language rung.
