The third independent review of the [head contract](native_geometric_head_reassertion_973.md) verified both preceding deliveries and found one inherited selection gap on the smallest repeated-head chain and one inherited formatting gap. On `Record: nelqi in opal harbor. nelqi in opal harbor. What was the previous location of nelqi? Answer:` the correct answer under record-hop semantics is record 1, ` opal harbor.`; `fc479376` and `90ebd843` instead emitted a malformed no-read continuation (` Unknown   .   .         );            );`) in plain style and ` Unknown.` owner-first. The diagnosis localized it: the exact predecessor is present, valid and offered at depth one under chain class seven (the head-hop form of "the immediate predecessor is also the root"), the initial request scores it 7 against a deferral base of 5 and selects it, but the previous request scores it 4 (plain) or 3 (owner-first), so the selector defers and the inherited path emits no read. The artifact held learned codes for classes one to six and eight to ten and none for class seven: the construction covered longer head chains but never the two-record identical-assertion chain for the previous intent. This cycle repairs that coverage through the existing native learner, without a new class, parser or answer rule, and retains **`4568679d`**; `fc479376` stays preserved as the prior accepted comparison baseline.

## Semantics restated

Record-hop semantics are unchanged: previous is the head's immediate previous record and initial is the assertion root. For two identical assertions both name record 1 (previous through the head hop at depth one, class seven); for three, previous is record 2 (class six) and initial is record 1 two links down (class nine). When a competing owner's record sits between the repeats, the same exact records are named through the same hops. Exact selected occurrence and record are required, not merely equal text: every target is judged on the anchor's record identity, source occurrence and endpoints, or on the actual no-read decision for an abstention.

## Limitations and next implementation

- **Owner-first current-request formatting.** The eight review rows above still omit the owner on the candidate. Scoping probes on the same seam (`scope-explain-*`, three artifacts requested, the parent refused by the driver because it lacks the outer witness) show two further inherited shapes outside every trained form: `Record: {v} holds {o}. … Explain in a sentence. Name the owner first.` produces a malformed continuation on `fc479376` and the candidate alike with or without a repeated fact, and the plain `{o} in {v}.` form with both instructions and no repeat answers `Unknown.` on both. Four rows of the new family carry that malformed parent output as their inherit label; they are recorded here as failures, not credited, and belong to the emission/instruction cycle. One probe on a revised `Record:` chain returned ` selvi was in Dusk Ridge.` on `fc479376` and ` selvi is in Copper Vale.` on the candidate; a single probe is not a claim.
- **Previous-distinct-value intent** is not implemented: previous is the head's immediate previous record, so on an identical-assertion chain it returns the same value from the earlier record.
- **Intermediate-version intent** (a specific earlier version by depth) is the next implementation, with exact depth labels and abstention when the chain no longer holds it.
- **Rejected candidate.** `ec79c303` is preserved under the evidence root with its fit, evaluation, panels and comparisons; it is not retained and no consumer should load it.
- **Scope.** Bounded authored forms with fresh spellings; the three reviews' prompt sets are development evidence and only `fresh-1` is held out; no general prose, temporal-language, HTTP/browser/WASM or energy claim; the untracked scratch diagnostic `scratch_multiturn_probe.rs` is listed in `selection.json` because it is present in the worktree, is not part of the delivery and is preserved.

Next under #973: intermediate-version intent with exact depth labels, preserving initial, previous and current behavior, reassertions and heads, identical-assertion chains, competing owners, dependency abstention and causal follow-ups; the owner-first emission seam is addressed separately.
