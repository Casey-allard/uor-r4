## What the present implementation establishes

The shared core already implements causal geometric hard attention. In `crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/shared_core.rs`, `relative_score` computes `angular[query * inverse(key)]` at lines 395–399. `observe` ranks a null and up to 32 retained positions at 449–465, selects one maximum, then composes four 120-state roots at 467–493. This uses signed H4 group tables, not Hamming or softmax. It has one coarse key/query channel and a finite recent window.

The prediction interface at 498–519 consumes those four roots. It does not directly consume the selected exact bytes, occurrence/version identity, relation record or committed typed result. The four roots have at most about 27.6 bits of distinguishable direct readout state; the complete session also retains a ring, phases and position, so this is not the total session information. It is a concrete information-interface limitation. The current `shared_core/training.rs:195–235` changes individual roots, evaluates complete sequences and accepts/reverts; the recent test drivers isolate additional state/head interactions. They do not install a general joint differentiable learner.

The original Transformer learns query/key/value projections and downstream prediction together. Its attention supplies content-dependent access and value combination; the surrounding learned computation and training objective matter as much as the similarity formula. Replacing its score while retaining a restricted readout and weak credit assignment does not reproduce that learning system. We seek comparable functional roles under a different serving algebra, not numerical equivalence to its dense computation. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762).

The decoder comparison removes one explanation: adapting the decoder to varied contexts lowers recombination NLL but loses correctness; the learned varied state is worse than unchanged state even after matched adaptation. This supports reassessing representation, access-to-readout and credit assignment together. It does not prove H4 cannot learn, that all geometry has been exhausted, or that JEPA is necessary. The existing greedy branch decoder versus whole-leaf probability diagnostic is also unresolved on these current heads; do not mistake teacher-forced loss for the actual emitted distribution.

## Hamming: a real mathematical lead, with a representation requirement

For binary codes q,k of length d, Hamming distance is `popcount(q XOR k)`. Under bipolar encoding b(x)=1−2x, the exact identity is b(q)·b(k)=d−2 H(q,k). Thus Hamming ranking can reproduce bipolar inner-product ranking without executing a matrix product. This is an algebraic identity for that representation, not proof that arbitrary H4 labels or UOR digests preserve linguistic similarity. The finite metric can score structured or learned feature codes; exact object identity stays separate. A joint codebook must define how its bits relate to the primary signed geometry rather than silently replacing it.

Bricken and Pehlevan derive a conditional approximation between Transformer attention and sparse distributed memory: overlap of binary Hamming read/write neighborhoods produces distance-dependent weighting resembling an exponential over an appropriate range. This makes Hamming a substantive research candidate. It does not require the I Ching connection. Their learned projections and value aggregation still use matrices; inspecting `Implementations_Associative_Memory.py:93–104,158–160` and `utils_LearnProjections.py:61–69` confirms that. We may reuse the retrieval insight, not import their complete runtime. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05498), [author implementation at inspected commit](https://github.com/TrentBrick/attention-approximates-sdm/blob/4cb44d825408a2e5af488ef166a9aa82ad2a2b31/Implementations_Associative_Memory.py).

A weighted sum over stored vectors would recreate the prohibited contraction, even with bit values or lookup arithmetic. Our candidate route therefore retains exact addressed gathers and bounded typed nonlinear composition. Its relationship to soft attention is functional; it is not the SDM approximation theorem unchanged. Hard selection can be useful, but learning through changing discrete choices remains unresolved.

## What NEMESIS contributes, and what it does not establish

The project maps `n3mesis` to `markrnd87-cmd/NEMESIS-Theory`, reviewed at `0d106967843c2c96477cf3e57aeff213e7db1c97`. The repository's earlier audit and two additionally inspected PDFs were used; this is not a claim to have read all 205 inventoried files. No direct NEMESIS-authored runtime commit or trained attention implementation was identified by this scoped review.

The carrying-substrate report offers a useful discipline: specify the represented domain, what is faithfully retained, and which implemented transition realizes each operation. Use exact memory when a compact learned state is lossy. Quaternion conjugation also requires an explicit arithmetic domain and orientation; it cannot silently become an orientation-preserving Spin(4) transport. [Carrying report](https://github.com/markrnd87-cmd/NEMESIS-Theory/blob/0d106967843c2c96477cf3e57aeff213e7db1c97/Technical%20Report_%20Integration%20of%20Hypercomplex%20Geometries%20as%20UOR%20Structure%20Carrying%20Substrates.pdf).

The I Ching material supplies six-bit codes and hypercube adjacency, not learned linguistic relevance. A claimed eight-vertex shortest path in Q6 is inconsistent with its maximum shortest-path length of six edges. The separate eight-bit corpus contains distinct glyph/scribal identities with the same feature code (for example k_Scribe1 and k_Scribe4 both `10101010`). Similarity codes therefore cannot replace exact identity. These are specific mathematical/data objections, not a dismissal of the broader research. [I Ching/hypercube report](https://github.com/markrnd87-cmd/NEMESIS-Theory/blob/0d106967843c2c96477cf3e57aeff213e7db1c97/Franklin_s%20Magic%20Square%2C%20I%20Ching%2C%20Hypercubes.pdf), [feature corpus](https://github.com/markrnd87-cmd/NEMESIS-Theory/blob/0d106967843c2c96477cf3e57aeff213e7db1c97/Octeract%20Cypher%20Extended%20Corpus_%208-Bit%20Feature%20Vec....pdf).

The Cayley-Dickson document's proposed corruption check `neg(bnot(x)) = succ(x)` holds for every n-bit word, including a corrupted replacement: −(2ⁿ−1−x)=x+1 modulo 2ⁿ. It cannot attest corruption without independent redundancy/reference. Norm preservation likewise is not semantic preservation. Keep Hamming against aligned reference states as a descriptive diagnostic; define learned-feature Hamming separately.

## Better candidates for discrete learning

Differentiable Weightless Neural Networks learn interconnected small lookup tables and their input mapping using approximate derivatives. The author implementation's CUDA forward kernel forms a bit address with OR/shift and reads a LUT; learned mapping uses hard index choice in forward and surrogate matrix operations in backward. This is a relevant training-method donor under our offline/serving distinction. It is not a Rust laptop language model: the inspected implementation uses CUDA/float storage, and its published evidence concerns edge/tabular tasks. Any adaptation must export the actual finite geometry-compatible operators and measure hard-path behavior. [ICML paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/bacellar24a.html), [inspected kernel](https://github.com/alanbacellar/DWN/blob/9f887a0b4bd84dabf6d8c9ae35368ab2a7e0e3c0/src/torch_dwn/custom_operators/cuda/efd_cuda_kernel.cu).

Differentiable Logic Gate Networks provide a related concrete separation: soft mixtures over Boolean gates during training, hard gate selection and generated AND/XOR/OR expressions at inference. The inspected `difflogic.py:103–107` and `compiled_model.py:94–131` implement that distinction. Original wiring is fixed/random; discretization and temporal credit still need design. This paper was already cited in the project's block-learning research; the new contribution here is source-level examination and identifying the missing integrated learning route. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.08277), [inspected compiler](https://github.com/Felix-Petersen/difflogic/blob/469702c01ff0bfac9cdc6a395134252e11a56bd8/difflogic/compiled_model.py).

By contrast, *Scalable MatMul-free Language Modeling* uses ternary dense matrix-vector contractions implemented with additions/subtractions. That does not meet this project's stricter prohibition on mathematical matrix products; its name is insufficient for adoption. [Paper, especially §6.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02528v7). [JEPA](native_geometric_jepa_research_973.md) remains a plausible auxiliary prediction objective, but does not supply exact payload access, discrete credit assignment or generation by itself.

## Concrete next work and stopping boundary

Prepare one implementation-ready **learned addressed geometric attention** specification, before fitting:

1. Define exact object/occurrence/version records and a separate contextual geometric code. Preserve lexical payload, operand order, signed orientation and typed identities; name the prime, zeta and paired-H4 roles and their unresolved bridges.
2. Specify learned shared query/key/write/update circuits, the candidate domain and bounded read schedule. Compare signed-H4 compatibility and a geometrically defined learned binary-code metric on paper first. Do not relabel hash bits as semantics or add expert gating.
3. Connect an exact selected record and a bounded typed operator result to contextual state and learned byte/EOS emission. Preserve observation/commit causality. Exact copying is allowed only after a learned valid selection; no prompt interpreter or authored answer override.
4. Specify one offline Rust objective and discrete credit estimator across access, update and emission. Candidate: hard-forward small-LUT operators with finite-difference/surrogate backward, trained across unrolled causal windows. A derivation must account for downstream trajectory changes and hard export mismatch; gate-level classification results do not establish recurrent trainability. Avoid tabulating a dense contraction or a full prompt/response domain. Keep the current coordinate learner as a named comparator, not an endless search default.
5. Freeze a small causal acceptance design: changed roles/order with the same objects must change selection/output; changed payload with the same role must preserve exact content; independent turns and revisions must retain correct identity; disabling access or shuffling keys must remove the contextual advantage. Require actual continuations/EOS and a bounded executable Rust task, plus applicable old controls. Final source-separated qualification stays unopened until design selection.
6. Project complete costs from declared dimensions before implementation: G single-output Boolean k-input LUTs require G·2ᵏ table bits plus wiring (multiply by output bitwidth for multi-bit state tables); L candidates with d-bit codes require O(L·ceil(d/word_bits)) distance work; candidate retrieval itself must be bounded. Count exact payload/index storage, recurrent training activations, export, build, controls and evaluation. Do not enumerate all UOR history, silently expand tables, or claim an M1 energy advantage from Boolean primitives.
