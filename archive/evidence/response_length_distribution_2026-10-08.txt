WHAT IT SETTLES
  THE CORPUS CONTAINS ESSENTIALLY NO SHORT-FORM RESPONSES. Across 129,465 assistant replies there is
  not one of one to three tokens, and one in a thousand is ten tokens or fewer. Two thirds are longer
  than two hundred.

  That is the answer to the instruction-precision question that four arms have now failed to move.
  When a turn asks for "exactly one word", a yes/no, or a number, the model has no such reply in its
  training target distribution to imitate - the reply distribution is prose, without exception. It
  therefore does the only thing it can: it writes prose ABOUT the request, which is exactly what the
  panels show ("Imagine a response with exactly three bullet points using markdown...", "The sky blue
  is a symbol of life...").

  It also explains why the four candidate causes were each refuted in turn: capacity helped the
  things capacity can help (the refusal default, a factual answer, format awareness) but cannot
  invent a reply form absent from the data; the objective change fixed REGISTER and multi-turn
  conditioning because those ARE in the data; sampling changes style, not the support of the
  distribution; and the prompt protocol was a separate, already-fixed defect.

WHAT IT CHANGES ABOUT THE NEXT STEP
  Rounds 75-76 recorded "scale capacity rather than buy data" for the remaining gap. THAT IS NOW
  WRONG FOR THIS TARGET, and the correction is specific rather than a reversal of the earlier
  findings:
    * more tokens of the same corpus: still NO. Round 48 falsified it (all three measures flat or
      worse, 0.127 nats cost) and the 214 M mixture comparison agrees (1.0966 against 0.9607).
    * a corpus whose RESPONSE-LENGTH DISTRIBUTION includes short forms: this is the binding
      constraint for instruction precision, and it is a much smaller and more specific ask than
      "1.39 B more tokens". What is needed is on the order of thousands to tens of thousands of turns
      whose correct reply is one word, a yes/no, a number or a short list - a mixed-form instruction
      set - not a general chat corpus.
  The existing corpus remains the right base for register and conversation; what it cannot supply is
  the answer-form tail, and no amount of it will.

HOW TO GET THE MISSING PIECE, STATED WITHOUT CLAIMING IT WORKS
  The repository already contains the machinery to build such a set: prepare-chat-parquet takes
  per-source selection with skip counts, and the sources already used include task-formatted
  configurations (summarize, rewrite, constraints) whose replies are far shorter than the
  conversational ones. A first estimate of their form distribution, from the same mask method, is
  cheap and is the obvious next measurement: if those sources are also prose-dominant, the short-form
  data must come from elsewhere, and the owner would need to decide about a new licence-clean source.
  That check should precede any training run aimed at instruction precision.

SCOPE
  One corpus, one mask, exact counts from a single pass. The mask's response regions were verified
  against the marker contract in rounds 53-55; the distribution is therefore exact for this corpus
  and says nothing about corpora not measured here.
