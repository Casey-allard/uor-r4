Calibration
-----------
Population: the recovered first 4,096 consumed windows / 257,113 n-1 targets; 62,973 distinct fit
contexts. Uncentered second-moment Gram G = (1/N) sum_c n_c h(c) h(c)^T accumulated with checked
i128 arithmetic over integer features and occurrence multiplicities, mirrored to a symmetric f64
matrix, divided by N. G is never inverted, jittered, regularized, centered or rank-truncated; it is
singular. Gram build 0.84 s. Teacher mass error 3.0e-14.

Q0: the existing TernaryLinear::quantize(W_F, 4096, 128) called exactly once; its packed codes and
shifts preserved; envelope validated by PriorCore::validate. Q0 is included as an explicit candidate
in the QG search, so the fit surrogate cannot increase.

QG: for each row, candidate shifts are the sorted unique {s0-1, s0, s0+1, sE} filtered to 0..=15
(the envelope admits 15 for dv=128/norm_bits=6, derived from source). At each shift the nearest
ternary coefficients seed the search; at sE the empirical row is a second seed. Each seed gets
exactly two coordinate sweeps (j ascending then descending) with strictly-negative acceptance of
dJ = 2*delta*g_j + delta^2*G_jj, ascending tie-break, incrementally updated e and g. The full
e^T G e is then independently recomputed per candidate, and the smallest fit J wins with Q0
preferred on a tie. No convergence loop, restart, hyperparameter sweep or extra sweeps after seeing
development results. Probe: 64 rows in 0.005 s; full 4,096 rows in 0.30 s. Selected seeds: 2,098
nearest / 1,998 empirical.

Outcome
-------
Both bounded conversions FAIL the practical screen against the incumbent gradient-trained ternary
head E, so E remains the stronger qualified numerical baseline at its scoped development result.
The projection method itself PASSES its attribution test decisively: activation-aware calibration is
0.225 bits/target better than the existing quantizer and reconstructs F's scores 12.7x better than E
does. Yet lower squared-score reconstruction error does NOT imply lower cross-entropy: E is furthest
from F in fit-score error and still predicts best. This rejects these two bounded conversions; it
does not prove ternary infeasibility, feature-rank saturation or a need to widen the head, and it
does not establish geometric superiority, general language or an energy advantage.
