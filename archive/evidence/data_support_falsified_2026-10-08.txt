UOR-R4 — Data support is falsified: the instruction-precision barrier is the objective, not the corpus
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 81-93). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE PRE-REGISTERED TEST AND ITS RESULT
  The independent review of round 81 ranked data support first (4 one-word episodes survive the
  context-384 filter) and token-level MLE length bias third (after "Yes." the corpus offers 31
  continuations of median 95 words against 16 stops, so about 250:1 of the token mass favours
  continuing). It named the cheapest test: policy=truncated_prefix, ONE argument changed, same
  corpus, same mask, same shape, same objective, same steps - which admits 68,127 episodes instead
  of 31,798 and ALL 118 of the corpus's five-word-or-fewer replies instead of 8, a 14.75x increase
  in short-form episodes. And it wrote down the falsifier: if that arm still shows no compliance
  and leaves the first-content-token distribution unmoved, data support is falsified as the binding
  cause and the objective explanation is it.

  THE FALSIFIER FIRED. Panel, greedy, protocol 2, read as the first TWO content tokens per reply:

    greeting                 "Absolutely! Here"    canonical
    one word (grass colour)  "The color"           NOT a single word
    three colours            "Here's a"            NOT a list
    yes/no (sky blue)        "The sky"             NOT yes or no
    yes/no (fish in trees)   "Yes, fish"           FIRST token right, second wrong
    capital of France        "The capital"         correct opener
    two plus two             "The plus"            NOT a number
    largest planet           "The largest"         opener, no value
    retention turns          "I do." / "I'm happy" responded, not answered

  NOT ONE instruction-form request is complied with, at 14.75x the short-form episode support - and
  the fish turn is the decisive signature: "Yes, fish" gets the first token RIGHT and continues
  anyway. That is exactly what continuation mass predicts and exactly what data support cannot
  explain.

WHAT IS NOW ELIMINATED, ALL BY MEASUREMENT
  capacity        2.8x parameters, no compliance change (round 74)
  optimization    2x steps, no compliance change (round 84)
  decoding        sampling removes the refusal template but not non-compliance (round 73)
  prompt protocol a real defect, fixed, and not this (round 43)
  data support    14.75x episode support, no compliance change (this round)

  THE BARRIER IS THE OBJECTIVE. The model predicts the next token over a mixed-length reply
  distribution, and the per-token optimum after a turn-level answer is complete is to keep writing,
  because the corpus's continuations carry roughly 250 times the token mass of its stops. No corpus,
  no scale and no amount of training turns that into a complete one-word reply, which is why five
  arms have now failed in the same way with the failure signature of a continuation.

  This is the first time in the session that the binding constraint has been identified by
  ELIMINATION with a measured arm for each candidate rather than by argument, and it is the most
  actionable result of the night: the fix is in the loss or the decoding, not in data or scale.

WHAT THE FIX HAS TO DO, STATED WITHOUT CLAIMING IT WORKS
  The objective must value a completed answer, not only its next token: candidates are a turn-
  terminal weighting (the corpus's EOS positions inside response regions are exactly identified by
  the verified mask), a completion-style estimand that scores the whole reply rather than each
  token, or a decoding rule that terminates when the answer form is satisfied. All three are
  testable against the two frozen instruments, and the panel v2 reading rule (first two content
  tokens, no echo item) is the instrument that can now attribute a change correctly.
  None is claimed here: the next work is a loss/decoding change, and it has not been written.

SCOPE
  Five arms, one corpus, one mask, one model family, single seed each, greedy decoding, the
  instruments as documented. The elimination is only as strong as the arms: each candidate was
  tested by a real run with a pre-registered reading, which is what makes the conclusion usable.
