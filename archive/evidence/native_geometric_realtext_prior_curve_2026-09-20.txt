3. THE GATE — corrected sign convention
================================================================================
`paired_interval(a, b)` returns the paired document-cluster difference `a - b`. The runner passed
`(ctx, reference)`, so the printed "gain" carried the model's sign the wrong way round and the
verdict logic compared the wrong quantity. The source is corrected to pass `(reference, ctx)` so a
positive value means the reference is worse. **The retained numbers are unaffected**; the corrected
gains are the negation of the printed ones:

| Comparison | Printed (ctx − ref) | Corrected gain (ref − ctx) | Paired 95% interval | Excludes zero |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| exact fit-only unigram | −1.9174 | **+1.9174** | [+1.8073, +2.0321] | yes, improving |
| identical quantized constant | −1.9892 | **+1.9892** | [+1.8822, +2.1030] | yes, improving |
| permutation penalty | +2.8744 | +2.8744 | [+2.7273, +3.0290] | yes, positive |

Frozen gate (declared before fitting): contextual CE at least 0.10 bits/target below **both**
references with both paired intervals excluding zero, plus a positive permutation penalty whose
interval excludes zero. **All three criteria are met under the corrected direction: the step-512
gate PASSES**, at roughly nineteen times the required margin.

The permutation is a genuine negative control: 5,805 of the development context-target associations
were changed (5,805 non-PAD, 0 PAD — paragraph-initial contexts form a stratum of size one and are
non-permutable, reported rather than dropped), the context marginal and the target list are preserved,
and the penalty is 2.87 bits/target with an interval excluding zero. A marginal-only correction cannot
be invariant to it.

Position knockouts are diagnostics, not the control: zeroing the predecessor table costs 0.4347
bits/target (7.1425 → 7.5772) and zeroing the current-token table costs 1.3572 (7.1425 → 8.4997), so
the prediction depends on both inputs, more strongly on the current token.

6. SECTION-2 PRIMITIVE REPAIRS (all executed and tested)
================================================================================
* **Integer-only trace.** `Trace` now stores the ReLU mask as `u8` and the normalization shift as
  `u32`; float conversion and inverse-shift credit happen only in the offline backward. Greedy serving
  therefore carries no floating auxiliary value, and the retained V4 artifact's integer predictions
  are unchanged (its parity test still passes).
* **Checked embedding sum.** The shifted ternary sum is computed in i64 and clamped, so two shifted
  coefficients cannot overflow i32 before the clamp; `norm_bits = 0` now bounds the hidden value by
  `PRIOR_CLAMP` in validation rather than by an assumed unit bound.
* **Shared validation.** `PriorCore::validate` checks shapes, element range, bias-code range, row-shift
  range and the readout envelope, and is applied to **both** constructed and reloaded cores, so a
  constructed core cannot bypass loader checks.
* **Exact continuation.** The active Fisher–Yates permutation is persisted in checkpoint version 3 and
  restored (validated for cardinality, range and uniqueness) instead of being cleared. Non-finite or
  invalid optimizer fields, weight decay and negative second moments are rejected, and a rejected load
  leaves the live trainer unchanged.
* **Hand-computed STE fixture.** A closed-form check of `gZ`, `gWo` and `gE` for both embedding tables
  and the output, with active and clamped ReLU coordinates, a nonunit normalization shift, a nonflat
  bias and a nonzero residual all asserted to occur.
* **Mid-pass continuation.** A test at mid-pass, through the partial final batch and across a pass
  boundary compares the permutation, cursor, masters, both moments, consumed window IDs, the next
  batch, the next update and the exported cores, with nondefault betas and weight decay.

`prior_learning` 12 tests pass; the V4 gate result from #1298 is preserved and unchanged.
