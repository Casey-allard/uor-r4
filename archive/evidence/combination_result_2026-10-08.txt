UOR-R4 — Adding short-form content to the stopping signal does not move the one-word or numeric classes
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 104-108). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT RAN - THE FIRST RUN WITH BOTH HALVES PRESENT
  policy=full_prefix, the hash-proven response mask, UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60 (the measured best dose),
  and a training stream that is 82.2 M tokens of chat PLUS 20,000 synthetic documents whose assistant
  turn is the correct answer (1,425,557 tokens, 14.9 % response fraction against the chat corpus's
  68.9 %). The synthetic store required three format repairs before it would train at all, each caught
  by a strict check: the generator is its own binary rather than a mode; its mask omitted the literal
  Assistant marker that dialogue_episodes.rs:385-405 requires, so the five marker ids were inserted
  with mask 0 before each of the 20,000 responses; and the rebuilt store kept the old UORT header
  count, which the mixer reads.

RESULT ON PANEL V2 (first two content tokens)

  yes/no, sky blue            "Yes, there"    COMPLIES
  yes/no, is ice hot?         "Yes, ice"      COMPLIES in form
  yes/no, third               "(sitting a"    does not
  retention, name             "My name"       RESPONDS (one of two retention turns)
  follow-up                   "You can"       responds
  France / largest planet     "The capital" / "The largest"   openers intact
  one-word turns              "I do" / "To make"              do NOT comply
  numeric turns               "The number" / "To find" / "A dog"  do NOT comply
  list turn                   "The colneyway"                 does not comply

COMPARISON WITH THE STOPPING SIGNAL ALONE (w=60)
  The combination reproduces the yes/no compliance and the factual openers, and retention answers once
  rather than twice. ADDING 20,000 SHORT-FORM DOCUMENTS DID NOT MOVE THE ONE-WORD OR NUMERIC CLASSES
  AT ALL, which is the result that matters: the content half, even paired with the stop signal, does
  not produce single-token answers.

WHY, AND WHAT IT MEANS
  A plausible and specific explanation, recorded as a hypothesis and not a finding: the synthetic
  documents supply SHORT ANSWERS in their own question forms, but they do not carry the INSTRUCTION
  "answer with exactly one word" - the short reply is the corpus's natural mode for those documents
  rather than a demanded form. So the model received demonstration of brevity without demonstration of
  FORM COMPLIANCE, and it therefore still fails turns that demand a form explicitly. That is testable
  by regenerating with demand-bearing user turns (the generator exposes forms and panel options) and
  re-running this exact configuration.

  What is now excluded: data VOLUME of the right kind in the wrong shape (this arm), capacity (2.8x),
  optimization (2x), decoding (sampling), the prompt protocol (fixed), and raw data support (14.75x
  episodes of the corpus's own short replies). What remains is that the requested form must appear in
  the training data AS A DEMAND, not merely as a habit - and if that too fails, the per-token
  objective is the last explanation for exactly why it cannot.

SCOPE
  One configuration, one synthetic store (20,000 documents), one weight, single seed, greedy decoding,
  one panel. The four format repairs consumed the round, so this is the first measurement of the
  combination and the control comparison to w=60 alone is by panel reading rather than a paired
  statistic.
