UOR-R4 — The response-form intervention fails on all three yardsticks: the deficit is the objective, not the data
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 48). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE PRE-REGISTERED TEST (recorded in form_diversity_intervention_2026-10-08.txt before the arm ran)
  Same frozen 7.16 M chat recipe, 13,348 steps, one change: the training stream is 50/50
  chat-v0-p2 and tinydialogues (42.8 M tokens of short turn-pair dialogue). Judged by the 22-turn
  instruction panel and the max_new_tokens=1 first-token probe, both fixed in advance.
  Stated falsifier: "neither yardstick moving means the deficit is not the form distribution of the
  corpus - it would then be the objective (what the model is asked to predict) or the model's
  capacity, and the next step becomes an objective change rather than a data change."

RESULTS - ALL THREE MEASURES MOVED THE WRONG WAY OR NOT AT ALL

  1. FIRST-TOKEN PROBE: no improvement.
       turn                              chat-only     form-mixed
       "Reply with exactly one word: yes"     " "          " I"
       "is the sky blue?" (yes/no)          " The"        " The"
       "do fish live in trees?" (yes/no)      " "          " "
       "capital of France?"                 " The"        " The"
       "how many days in a week?"           " To"         " The"
       "largest planet in our system?"      " The"        " The"
     Still articles and pronouns. NOT ONE of the eight opens with the answer in either model.

  2. INSTRUCTION PANEL: worse, with new failure classes.
       greeting          "Imagine a world where the world is a symbol of the human experience..."
       "colour of grass" "The grass is a fundamental color in the grass, with a bright red and green green."
       "three colours"   "The \`calculate_list\` function is a fundamental program that uses..."   (code artifact)
       "two planets"     "The Earth is a fundamental concept in number theory..."
       "is the sky blue" "The sky blue is blue, and the sky blue is blue. It's a beautiful color, and..."
       "fish in trees"   "1. Create a detailed and detailed description of the fish..."           (echoes "detailed and detailed")
       "capital of France" "The capital of France is Paris. It is the first point in the [city] of the [city]..."
                                                                        (correct opener, then literal placeholders)
       "two plus two"    "The three plus three plus three plus"
     Backtick code artifacts and "[city]" placeholders are tinydialogues' low-register form leaking
     into the chat register, and repetition loops are more frequent than in the chat-only model.

  3. CHAT FIT: worse. Final development nll 2.017235420824099 against the chat-only control's
     1.8900316432118416 - a cost of 0.127 nats, or about 0.065 BPB, on the corpus the model is
     actually judged on.

CONCLUSION, TAKEN FROM THE PRE-REGISTERED FALSIFIER
  FORM DIVERSITY IN THE TRAINING CORPUS DOES NOT PRODUCE RESPONSE-FORM CONDITIONING. Adding 42.8 M
  tokens of short question/answer dialogue to a chat corpus neither made the first token responsive
  nor improved a single panel category, while measurably damaging the register and the chat fit.
  The deficit therefore lies in the OBJECTIVE - what the model is asked to predict - or in capacity,
  not in the form distribution of the data.

  That is a decisive and cheaply obtained negative. It removes the data-shaped half of the remaining
  option space and it is worth more than a marginal positive would have been: it says the next work
  is a change to what training optimises, and it argues against spending on more corpus of any
  register until that is done - including the 1.39 B-token chat-v1 rebuild, which the three
  independent lines of evidence (rounds 36, 44-46, and now this) all point away from.

WHAT THE OBJECTIVE CHANGE WOULD BE, STATED WITHOUT CLAIMING IT WORKS
  The model is trained to predict the next token of a flattened stream. Nothing in that objective
  distinguishes the tokens of a reply from the tokens of a prompt, so "answer the turn" is not a
  thing it is asked to learn; the panel's failures are exactly that - fluent continuation of the
  topic, with no notion of the requested form or of a response boundary. A loss that marks the
  response region, or an objective that conditions generation on the turn rather than continuing
  the transcript, is the class of change the evidence now calls for. The tools for response
  regions exist in the repository (the corpus carries a response mask, and the dialogue trainer
  takes one); this document does not claim a result for using them here.

SCOPE
  One model size, one mixture weight, one corpus pair, single seed, two mechanical probes fixed in
  advance. The arm-2 training could not separate form from topic distribution by construction - but
  the result is negative on BOTH, so the confound does not rescue it.
