UOR-R4 — Commitment-weight sweep on the copy-store mixture: 30 preserves the copying signal, 60 regresses, 100 moved both axes
Date: 2026-10-09 (overnight rounds 159-162). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE SWEEP
  Three arms, identical in every respect except UOR_COMMITMENT_WEIGHT, all on the content-copy store
  mixture (1,006 documents, 10,757 response tokens, turn-1 answers are the premise's own token span, no
  acknowledgements) with UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT held at 30, 19,929,136 parameters, policy=full_prefix,
  12,207 steps, panel v2 read as the first two content tokens.

    weight 100 (round 160)  "Yes, the" / "No, you" (correct) ; numerics "10." "3." "300." ;
                            copying visible "rain, the" ; NAME TURN "Anany."
    weight 30  (this round)  "No, the" / "Yes, you" ; numerics "2." "2." ; copying visible
                            "run, walk." (a verbatim premise span) ; NAME TURN "Ane." ;
                            one-word and list turns do not comply
    weight 60  (this round)  REGRESSES TOWARD PROSE: "My favorite" / "To find" / "The capital" /
                            "At the" / "An ecosystem"; the only in-form items are "Ag." (a flashcard
                            symbol) and a responsive "I'm here."

WHAT THE THREE ARMS TOGETHER SHOW
  1. THE COPYING BEHAVIOUR THE STORE WAS BUILT TO TEACH IS VISIBLE IN THE OUTPUT at two of the three
     weights: "run, walk." is a verbatim premise span, and "rain, the" at weight 100 is of the same
     kind. That is the first direct evidence that the content-copy store changes what the model emits
     in the way it was designed to - it restates premise content rather than a register.
  2. THE VALUE-LIKE RETENTION OUTPUT APPEARS AT TWO WEIGHTS. The name turn returns "Anany." at weight
     100 and "Ane." at weight 30 - both containing the name the user gave, in contrast with the
     acknowledgement register ("Noted.", "I see.") produced by every earlier store arm and with the
     no-store control which produced the value only in prose. Two independent weights producing a
     name-like reply is a REPRODUCIBLE SIGNAL rather than one lucky string.
  3. IT IS NOT A CLEAN DOSE-RESPONSE, AND 60 IS THE WORST OF THE THREE. Weight 60 returns to prose
     templates, the failure mode that predated the demand store entirely. With one seed per weight this
     non-monotonicity is not resolvable here; the honest statement is that 30 and 100 both preserve
     compliance on some classes and surface value-like content, while 60 does neither.
  4. NO WEIGHT SOLVES BOTH AXES. At 30 the copying is cleanest but one-word and list turns still fail;
     at 100 the yes/no class is correct but those same classes regress. The trade is between the
     commitment strength that fixes the answer's first token and the one that keeps the other form
     classes compliant.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
  * ESTABLISHES: the two merged changes (content-copy store, commitment weight) do affect the axis the
    binding probe identified - the answer's first token - and the effect is visible as (a) premise
    copying in the output and (b) name-like replies on the retention turn, at two of three weights.
  * DOES NOT ESTABLISH: binding. A reply CONTAINING the name is not a reply that ANSWERS the question,
    and "Ane."/"Anany." would both be scored misses by the exact rule. The measurement channel the
    probe showed to be necessary - log-probability mass on the value span at the answer position - has
    not yet been run on these checkpoints; it is the next measurement, and it is what would turn
    "contains the name" into "reads the value".
  * The next steps in order: (i) run the binding probe's DELTA and read-mass channel on the cw30 and
    cw100 checkpoints, which is inference-only and decisive about whether the value is now READ rather
    than echoed; (ii) if read mass is present, sweep the weight with multiple seeds at 30 and 100 to
    separate the two form classes properly; (iii) if read mass is absent, the copying is surface-level
    and the objective needs the span-selection term rather than a weight.

SCOPE
  Three weights, one mixture, one shape, single seed each, greedy decoding, one panel read as the first
  two content tokens. The retention observations are substring judgements and are labelled as such.
