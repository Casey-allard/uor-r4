UOR-R4 — With mass and answer length controlled, attribute extraction still fails: it is a capability gap, not a masking artifact
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 140-143). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE ARM THAT SEPARATES THE CONFOUND
  The two previous attribute arms changed two things at once. This one holds the mixture variables
  where the compliant configuration had them: 1,100 documents, 9,360 response tokens - 69.89 % BELOW
  the 31,084 of the store that produced ten-of-ten compliance - with turn-1 answers at a mean of 3.77
  tokens (max 5) rather than long authored sentences, and the dependency structure intact
  (transformation fraction 1.000000, i.e. copying turn 1's answer passes 0 of 1,100 documents).
  Verified: 16 clauses 0 offenders in two implementations, corruption rejected by the project's
  EpisodeIndex, 2,200/2,200 responses admitted with 0 excluded. Same chat corpus, 19.9 M shape,
  policy=full_prefix, UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60, panel v2.

RESULT

  form compliance - LARGELY RESTORED
    one word, grass      "green."      COMPLIES, and correct
    yes/no sky blue      "Yes."        COMPLIES, correct
    yes/no fish/trees    "No."         COMPLIES, correct
    yes/no ice hot       "No."         COMPLIES, correct
    numeric x3           "3." "3." "3."  numeric FORM, facts wrong
    further items        "Neon." "Nineteen."  short, in-form
    one-word bees, list, capital, planet  do not comply ("Another case", "A simple", "The capital")

  retention - STILL NOT ANSWERED, AND NOW WITH A VISIBLE CAUSE
    the two retention turns both return "Noted." - which is a TURN-1 ACKNOWLEDGEMENT from this
    store's own family, not a corpus behaviour and not an answer. The model has learned the
    acknowledgement register of the mixture and applies it where a recalled attribute was asked for.

  content - UNGROUNDED
    the numeric items all return "3.", the count family's canonical answer, regardless of the
    question. The model has learned the FAMILY PATTERN of the store rather than the grounding of a
    particular question - which is exactly the failure mode the short-premise design was built to
    avoid, now observed with mass and length controlled.

WHAT THIS SETTLES
  1. ATTRIBUTE EXTRACTION ACROSS A TURN IS A CAPABILITY GAP AT THIS SCALE, NOT A MASKING ARTIFACT.
     Rounds 134 and 136 confounded attribute difficulty with long-answer dilution; this arm removes
     that confound - the mixture teaches within the compliant envelope (9,360 response tokens, mean
     answer 3.77) and still produces no cross-turn recall. So the earlier failures were not caused by
     the store's length profile, and the remaining explanation is capability: a 19.9 M model in this
     architecture learns the SURFACE FAMILIES of the mixture without binding a follow-up to the
     attribute it asks about.
  2. FORM COMPLIANCE, BY CONTRAST, SURVIVES ALL OF IT: four configurations, two shapes, two terminal
     weights, and now a store 70 % smaller than the one that first produced it - still with correct
     yes/no facts and a correct one-word colour ("green."). What the demand store teaches is robust;
     what the attribute store was meant to add is not learned at this scale.
  3. THE DEGENERATE "Noted." IS ITSELF EVIDENCE. It is the store's own acknowledgement token
     reappearing where an attribute was requested, which localises the failure to BINDING rather than
     to the absence of the training signal: the model reproduces the mixture's surface forms and does
     not connect a question to the span its answer depends on.

WHERE THIS LEAVES THE PROJECT
  The measured recipe is unchanged and is the best configuration the session has: chat corpus + a
  small short-answer demand store (700-2,860 documents, and now demonstrated as low as 1,100 with
  9,360 response tokens) + the 19.9 M shape + UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60. It complies on form-demanded
  turns with correct content for the factual classes, converses canonically, states a correct fact
  ("Jupiter") and anchors a follow-up ("My cat,").
  Cross-turn attribute BINDING is now a named, isolated capability gap with three failed routes
  behind it (dependency-carrying documents, longer premises, shorter premises at 70 % less mass), and
  it is the honest next target - not a mixture-tuning question any more, and not solvable by making
  the store better.

SCOPE
  Six store/scale configurations across the night, one shape family, single seed each, greedy decoding,
  one panel read as the first two content tokens. Exact for this panel; the capability conclusion is
  about cross-turn binding at 19.9 M and does not claim a scaling law.
