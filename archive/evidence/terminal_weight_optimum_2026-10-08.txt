UOR-R4 — The terminal-weight optimum sits near 60x: compliance and retention together
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 99-101). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE THREE DOSES, ONE PANEL, ONE RECIPE
  Same 7.16 M shape (rrarra), same corpus, mask, policy=full_prefix, steps and seed; the only
  difference is UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT, which weights the response's terminating <|eos|>.

    weight   what the panel shows (first two content tokens)
    20x      yes/no compiles 2 of 3 ("Yes, the"); retention answers once ("My name");
             one-word and numeric turns do not comply
    60x      yes/no compiles ("Yes, the", "Yes, that's"); BOTH retention turns answer "My name";
             the follow-up responds ("You can"); France and largest-planet openers intact;
             one-word and numeric turns still do not comply
    200x     yes/no REVERTS ("The sky"); retention BREAKS ("The main"); list artifacts return

  So the ordering on the behaviours that matter is 60 > 20 > 200, with 200 a clear collapse and 60
  the first dose at which compliance and the existing behaviours hold TOGETHER.

WHY THIS IS THE MOST USEFUL RESULT OF THE NIGHT ON THE CHAT PATH
  1. IT IS THE FIRST SETTING THAT DOES NOT TRADE ONE BEHAVIOUR FOR ANOTHER. Every previous
     intervention moved either compliance (never, before this change) or register/multi-turn, and the
     first two doses here showed the trade explicitly: 20x bought yes/no at the cost of losing one
     retention turn, 200x lost both. At 60x the yes/no class complies AND both retention turns answer
     the question asked, with the factual openers intact.
  2. IT CONFIRMS THE CAUSAL STORY. The session's five eliminations left token-level MLE length bias -
     after a complete answer the corpus's continuations carry roughly 250:1 the token mass of its
     stops - and a single scalar that changes only what a completed answer is WORTH to the loss
     moves compliance up, back down, and to a better point in between. Nothing else tried tonight did
     that, and no data, scale or decoding change came close.
  3. IT LEAVES A CLEAN SEARCH. The optimum lies between 20x and 200x and appears near 60x; the next
     doses (30, 60, 100) would locate it, each one arm of the same recipe with the same instrument.

WHAT REMAINS UNMOVED
  One-word and numeric turns still do not comply at any dose ("To make", "To find", "To solve"): the
  model produces a procedure or a phrase rather than the requested single token. Either the weight
  that reaches those classes is outside [20, 200] - which the 200x collapse makes unlikely - or the
  requested form for them is not reachable by terminal weighting alone, and something further is
  needed. That is the next question, and it is now a narrow one.

SCOPE
  Three doses, single seed each, one shape, one panel read as the first two content tokens with the
  echo item removed, greedy decoding. Doses are single measurements, not a fitted curve; "optimum
  near 60x" means it is the best of the three measured, not that 60 is optimal.
