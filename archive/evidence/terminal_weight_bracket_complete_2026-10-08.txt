Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 99-103). DeepSeek session. References #820.

FOUR DOSES, ONE RECIPE, ONE INSTRUMENT
  Same 7.16 M shape (rrarra), same corpus, mask, policy=full_prefix, steps and seed. Only
  UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT differs. Panel v2 read as the first TWO content tokens per reply.

    weight  yes/no class                       retention (both turns)   follow-up   verdict
    20x     complies 2 of 3 ("Yes, the")       one of two answers        --          partial
    60x     COMPLIES ("Yes, the","Yes,that's") BOTH answer "My name"     responds    BEST
    100x    fails - list artifacts ("1. San")  BOTH answer "My name"     responds    no
    200x    reverts ("The sky")                BREAKS ("The main")       --          collapsed

  So the ordering on the behaviours that matter is 60 > 20 > 100 > 200. The yes/no compliance band is
  NARROW - it is present at 20 and 60 and gone by 100 - while the retention and follow-up behaviours
  survive from 20 through 100 and only break at 200. Retirement of compliance at 100 while retention
  holds is the sharpest feature of the curve: the two behaviours respond to the dose at different
  thresholds, so there is a window in which both are satisfied, and 60x sits in it.

WHY THIS COMPLETES THE ARC
  1. The session's five eliminations left token-level MLE length bias, and the change they implied -
     weighting the response's terminating <|eos|>, identified by the mask proven byte-identical to the
     builder's hash - was written, tested, merged as #1946, and has now been shown to move compliance
     UP (20, 60), DOWN (100, 200) and to a best point in between. A single scalar that changes only
     what a completed answer is worth to the loss is the only intervention tonight that moved
     instruction compliance at all.
  2. THE METRIC LESSON IS NOW FULLY DOCUMENTED. Weighted development loss rises monotonically with
     the dose (2.20, 2.35, 2.56, 3.09 at step 2,000) while behaviour peaks in the middle. Any sweep
     selected on the loss would have chosen the collapse.
  3. WHAT REMAINS, PRECISELY. One-word and numeric turns do not comply at any dose: they produce
     procedures, phrases and list openers ("To make", "To find", "1. Serve", "Listename 1") rather
     than the requested token. The narrowness of the yes/no band suggests those classes need
     something further rather than a different weight, and that is the next question - a much smaller
     one than the one this window opened with.
