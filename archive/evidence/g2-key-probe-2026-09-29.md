## Conclusion

**Linear per-head readouts trained on training phrasings do not transfer relation atoms to held-out phrasings at splits 2 and 4. Whether the limit is the trunk, the readout, or template diversity (about 5 training templates per family vs 1–2 held out) is unresolved.**

The separating experiment this calls for: an MLP probe, other layers or positions, and an untrained-trunk baseline.

Two earlier sentences are corrected:

- The guard sentence ("that is why the guard fails") conflated the in-distribution guard with held-out transfer. `decode-1`'s prediction that the rerun's guard "should now pass" was **falsified** by the rerun (2I in-distribution 0.884/0.906).
- "The joint key is not recoverable" is contradicted by in-distribution key accuracy of about 0.98. Softmax split-4 key-correct (1 517/3 089) equals `decode-1`'s held-out relation-correct: the failure is **held-out relation transfer**, not an unrecoverable representation.

## Design amendments and their timing

- **Original pre-registration (comment 5890046442, quoted verbatim).** "Both arms use identical data, features, seeds, update count and evaluation." … "Below 0.5 on the lexical phenomena at both splits: the trunk lacks the semantics at this scale; report to Lab 1." It fixes **no steps, learning rate or episode counts and names no arm**; those details came from later comments and code defaults (see Headline).
- **Root bound amendment.** The pre-registered pairwise `|q·r| ≤ ½` for 14 relation roots is infeasible: an exhaustive search over the 120 icosians finds at most 12 mutually separated roots (the 24-cell `2T` antipodal quotient). The 14 relation roots are the best feasible greedy min-max subset at `RELATION_ROOT_MAX_INNER = φ/2 ≈ 0.809017`, indices `[0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,15,24,25]`; an in-source test records the obstruction. The entity head is a plain 8-way softmax in both arms; the compared factor is the relation-atom head.
- **Exposure amendment (comment 5892575118, posted 14:43Z — recorded before the rerun only).** It postdates `keys-1` (sealed 13:54:22Z) and `decode-1` (sealed 14:29:31Z). The first run (1 500 steps over 24 k episode visits) was diagnosed as exposure-limited; the predeclared fix cached only the supervision-row features and ran **up to 30 000 steps with plateau early-stop** (the 2I arm did not plateau; the softmax arm early-stopped). No metric, guard or decision rule changed.
