UOR-R4 — Response mass caused the compliance collapse; the attribute store buys form without content
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 134-136). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE SEPARATING ARM
  The attribute store DILUTED 4x - 650 of 2,600 documents, 13,602 response tokens against 53,671 -
  with the dependency structure IDENTICAL (transformation fraction 1.000000 in the material kept) and
  everything else fixed: the same hash-proven chat corpus, 19,929,136 parameters, policy=full_prefix,
  UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60, 12,207 steps, panel v2.

RESULT

  compliance by FORM - most of it returns
    one word, grass    "White."    form correct (fact wrong)
    one word, bees     "When you"  not compliant
    yes/no x3          "Yes." "Yes." "Yes."   form correct (two facts wrong)
    number             "5." "23." "- 1." "5."  numeric form (facts wrong)
    list, capital, planet  "Another number" / "The capital" / "The largest"  not compliant
    retention          "I can"     not answered

  So form compliance goes from 2 of 10 at full mass to 7-8 of 10 at a quarter mass, while CONTENT
  correctness does not: "White" for grass, "5" for the days in a week, "23" for two plus two, and
  "Yes" for whether ice is hot.

WHAT THE TWO ARMS TOGETHER ESTABLISH
  1. RESPONSE MASS WAS THE CAUSE OF THE COLLAPSE. The only variable changed was the demand-side
     response token count, and form compliance recovered with it. That confirms round 122's reading
     from the opposite direction: what the mixture teaches as ANSWERS must stay short and few, and a
     store can carry perfect dependency structure while destroying compliance simply by adding
     long-answer mass.
  2. FORM AND CONTENT ARE SEPARABLE, AND THIS STORE TRADES CONTENT FOR FORM. Every earlier demand
     store produced correct facts with the compliant form ("Green.", "Honey.", "7.", "4.", "Paris.");
     the attribute store produces the form with wrong facts ("White.", "5.", "23."). Its long
     authored PREMISES teach the model to emit a short token without grounding it, which is the
     opposite of what the attribute families were meant to teach.
  3. IT DOES NOT BUY RETENTION. At full mass and at a quarter mass the multi-turn turns return prose
     fragments ("I can"), so the dependency structure - 2,600 documents requiring a transformation,
     measured at fraction 1.000000 - did not produce recall in either arm. Retention remains the open
     problem, and the attribute route is now ruled out as its solution at this scale and mixture.

WHAT THE PROJECT SHOULD CARRY FORWARD
  The best measured configuration is unchanged: the hash-proven chat corpus plus a SMALL demand store
  of short answers (the 700-2,860 document range measured tonight), at 19.9 M parameters, with
  UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60. That configuration complies on ten of ten form-demanded turns WITH correct
  content, converses canonically, states a correct fact ("Jupiter") and anchors a follow-up
  ("My cat,"). The attribute store is preserved as a negative result with a clear mechanism rather
  than deleted, and the generator remains available if a future hypothesis needs transformation-heavy
  documents with SHORT premises.

SCOPE
  Three attribute-store arms (full mass, quarter mass, and the round-134 full-mass run), one shape, one
  weight, single seed each, greedy decoding, one panel, exact for the ten form-demanded turns used
  throughout the session. Content correctness is judged on the same items as every earlier arm, so
  the comparison is like for like.
