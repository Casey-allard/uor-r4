WHAT THIS SHOWS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
  1. THE TWO CHANGES MOVE THE RIGHT THING, BUT NOT YET ENOUGH. The commitment weight and the
     content-copy store together produce (a) compliant yes/no forms with correct facts, (b) numeric
     forms, and (c) the first retention reply in any store arm that contains the value that was given.
     That is movement on exactly the axis the binding probe identified - the answer's first token -
     and it is the first arm in which a store-armed model emits the recalled value at all.
  2. COMPLIANCE IS MIXED RATHER THAN SOLVED AT THIS WEIGHT. One-word and list turns regress ("Dr. A",
     "1. Another"), so commitment=100 with terminal=30 is past a good operating point on some classes
     while being the first setting that surfaces the value on another. The review's sweep range was
     30-100; the evidence now suggests trying 30 and 60 at this store, and reading both channels.
  3. THE MEASUREMENT GAP MUST BE RESPECTED. "Anany." contains "Ana" and would be scored a miss by the
     two-token exact rule; the log-probability channel the probe showed to be necessary has to be part
     of the reading, or this movement is invisible.
  4. NOT A CONCLUSION EITHER WAY. This is one weight pair on one store at one shape, single seed. The
     honest summary is that the review's combined recommendation produced partial movement on both
     axes simultaneously for the first time, and that the next step is a sweep over the commitment
     weight at this store rather than a new mechanism.
