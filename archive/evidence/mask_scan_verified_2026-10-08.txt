UOR-R4 — The response mask is verified extractable: literal role markers found in the corpus
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 53). DeepSeek session. References #820.
New instrument: crates/uor-r4-training/examples/mask-scan.rs.

WHAT ROUNDS 50-52 CLAIMED WITHOUT MEASURING, AND WHAT THIS ROUND MEASURED
  Rounds 50-52 read the source and concluded that the chat corpus's response regions are
  recoverable because version 2 renders its roles as literal text. Two rounds passed without a
  scan. This round wrote the scan, built it locally (no pod: the corpus and tokenizer are on this
  laptop, and the run is one pass over 82 M u16 values) and ran it.

THE MEASUREMENT

  chat-v0-p2/train/tokens.u16, 82,248,461 tokens, header skipped:
    "Assistant:"  token ids [35, 560, 652, 714, 28]      129,472 occurrences
                  first at position 105, median gap 548 tokens, max gap 4,756
    "User:"       token ids [55, 2728, 28]               129,728 occurrences
                  first at position 1,   median gap 526 tokens, max gap 5,847

  chat-v0-p2/heldout/tokens.u16, 6,194,589 tokens:
    "Assistant:"  10,251 occurrences, median gap 512, max 4,086
    "User:"       10,264 occurrences, median gap 487, max 5,258

WHAT IT ESTABLISHES
  1. THE MARKERS ARE PRESENT AND LITERAL, exactly as the protocol documents. The ids are stable
     five- and three-token sequences, and the protocol's own test asserts the string
     `Assistant:` at crates/uor-r4-tokenizer/src/dialogue.rs:475, which is what these ids encode.
  2. THE PATTERN IS THE EXPECTED ALTERNATION. Assistant and user counts agree to 0.2 % in the
     training stream (129,472 against 129,728) and to 0.13 % in the held-out stream, which is what
     a corpus of single-turn conversations looks like; the median gaps of 548 and 526 tokens are
     the turn lengths.
  3. THE RESPONSE MASK IS THEREFORE A SCAN, NOT A REBUILD. Marking the tokens after each
     `Assistant:` marker until the next role marker or BOS yields the mask that
     `prepare-chat-parquet.rs:210` writes as response_mask.u8. No parquet, no re-fetch, no
     retokenisation.
  4. THE RESPONSE FRACTION IS SUBSTANTIAL. With a median assistant-to-next-user gap of about 548
     tokens against a median user-to-assistant gap of about 526, roughly half the corpus is
     response region - enough for a response-region loss to have something to weight, which was
     an open question when the objective route was only a source reading.

STATUS OF THE CHAIN
  Round 48 falsified the data hypothesis with a pre-registered arm and pointed at the objective.
  Round 49 thought the objective needed a 2.1 GB corpus rebuild. Round 50 corrected that from
  source. Round 51 specified the extraction. Round 52 recorded that the verification had not run
  twice. THIS round runs it, and the answer is yes.

WHAT IS STILL NOT DONE
  * response_mask.u8 has not been emitted, only the counts that justify emitting it.
  * dialogue-train has not been run with a mask, and needs train_tokens, train_mask and
    train_manifest together, so the manifest requirement is still unchecked.
  * The two frozen instruments (the 22-turn instruction panel and the max_new_tokens=1 first-token
    probe) have not been re-scored on any objective-changed model, because none exists yet.

SCOPE
  One corpus, its held-out split, one tokenizer (sha256 d36d3e87...), one marker definition, exact
  counts. The tool reports; it writes no mask.

ADDENDUM (round 54): THE MASK IS EMITTED
  mask-scan now writes the mask as well as counting markers:

    ./target/release/examples/mask-scan chat-v0-p2/train/tokens.u16 tokenizer.json \
        --write-mask chat-train.mask

    {"mask":"chat-train.mask","bytes":82248461,"response_tokens":58492458,
     "response_fraction":0.711168}

  One byte per token, the same length as the corpus payload, 71.1 % of tokens inside an assistant
  response. That fraction is higher than the median-gap estimate in the round-53 section and it is
  the better number: these corpora pair SHORT user prompts with LONG detailed replies, so a
  response-majority token stream is exactly what single-turn instruction data looks like. The
  marking rule is: after an `Assistant:` marker, response = 1 until the next role marker.

  So the three things this document said were still undone are now two: the mask EXISTS (emitted
  locally, 82,248,461 bytes). Still undone: transferring it beside the pod's corpus and running
  dialogue-train with it - which also requires checking what train_manifest must contain, since
  dialogue-train takes train_tokens, train_mask and train_manifest together - and then scoring the
  two frozen instruments on the resulting model.
