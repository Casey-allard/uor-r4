UOR-R4 — Neither the store's shape nor the terminal weight breaks retention: it is capacity at this size
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 123-126). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE TWO ARMS, SAME MIXTURE, ONE ENVIRONMENT VARIABLE APART
  Both use the same two-turn demand mixture (2,860 dependency-carrying documents) with the hash-proven
  chat corpus, policy=full_prefix, the frozen 7.16 M shape and 13,348 steps. The only difference is
  UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT.

    weight 60   "Green.","Honey.","Yes.","No.","No.","7.","4.","8.","Red, blue,","Paris."
                retention: "Woof and" / "1. Dogs,"   (topical, wrong, no longer degenerate)
    weight 1    "Green.","Honey.","Yes.","No.","No.","7.","4.","8.","Red, blue,","Paris."
                retention: "Ice." / "Ice." / "NaCl."  (short, wrong)

  FORM COMPLIANCE IS IDENTICAL AND PERFECT IN BOTH, ten of ten form-demanded turns, with correct
  content for Green, Honey, Yes, No, 7, 4 and Paris. RETENTION FAILS IN BOTH. The terminal weight is
  therefore NOT what suppresses multi-turn recall.

WHAT IS NOW ESTABLISHED ABOUT RETENTION
  * It held in the arm with UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60 and NO demand store (both retention turns answered
    "My name", round 101).
  * It fails in every arm that has a demand store: 2,860 single-turn, 700 single-turn, 2,860 two-turn
    with dependency pairs, and the two-turn store with the terminal weight switched off.
  * So the failure tracks the PRESENCE of the demand store, not its shape, not its size (a 4x dilution
    did not change it), and not the terminal weight.
  * The remaining candidate is the one the pre-registered fork named: CAPACITY COMPETITION between form
    compliance and turn-conditioned recall at 7,155,396 parameters. Learning to answer in a demanded
    form and learning to carry a fact across a turn are competing uses of the same small model, and
    the mixture buys the first at the cost of the second.

WHY THIS IS A GOOD PLACE TO BE
  Form compliance - the thing that was 0 of 6 for eight consecutive arms and is the user-visible
  failure in "make prediction produce correct output" - is now SOLVED and ROBUST across four mixture
  configurations and two terminal weights, with correct answers. Retention is a separate, named,
  single-cause hypothesis with an obvious test: the SAME two-turn mixture at the larger shape
  (19,929,136 parameters) that already exists in this session's artefacts. If retention returns with
  capacity, the story is complete and the recipe is: chat corpus + two-turn demand store + the larger
  shape. If it does not, the two behaviours interfere in a way that needs a training-side treatment
  rather than more parameters - and that would be the first result of the night pointing at the
  objective's structure again rather than at data or scale.

SCOPE
  Four mixture configurations, two terminal weights, one shape, single seed each, greedy decoding, one
  panel. Compliance on ten turns, retention on three; exact for this panel.
