WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
  1. THE ROUTE IS CLOSED. The gate responds to supervision roughly linearly (+0.03 at 0.1, +0.15 at 1.0),
     and at BOTH weights the response distribution degrades, monotonically with the weight. So the
     supervision term competes with the response loss rather than complementing it, and raising the gate
     is not a viable path to the answer at any weight tried. The gate was a correct diagnosis of WHERE
     the failure sits and a wrong remedy: the mixture's gate is low because the response loss prefers it
     low, and overriding that preference damages the very distribution the answer would be expressed in.
  2. THE UNGATED POINTER ARM IS THE BEST CONFIGURATION ON BOTH AXES and should be the base for further
     work: it is the only arm that combines (a) value-driven copying of values that exist in no store
     family or chat prior, measured at pointer mass 0.90-1.00 on the value span in 9 of 21 cells and
     value-paired DELTA +3.85 to +10.39 nats, with (b) partial form compliance that includes yes/no with
     correct facts. It was produced by the pointer alone, with no gate supervision, no span supervision
     and no read-binding supervision.
  3. THIS IS THE FIFTH ROUTE EXHAUSTED FOR BINDING, and the fourth objective-side intervention
     (terminal weight, commitment weight, commitment span, gate supervision) to be measured and
     rejected or bounded. What remains untried on this path, in the order the evidence now supports:
     (i) the READ-BINDING supervision the review identified (read_binding_supervision= /
     read_binding_labels=, present in the trainer and never run on this path) which supervises WHERE the
     read should look rather than how much the output uses it; (ii) the generator/pointer identity
     mixture itself - a token-identity key instead of a hidden-state key, which the review flagged as
     the pointer's honest limitation; (iii) the constrained/two-token decoder, which sidesteps the
     mixture competition entirely by drawing the answer from the span.
  4. WHAT NOT TO DO: no further gate-supervision weights, no further capacity, no further dependency
     stores, and no panel-only readings - the emitted-value test must run through the probe on
     out-of-family values, which is the standard that caught this session's own over-read.

HONEST POSITION
  A clean negative that narrows the field: the gate hypothesis is bounded from both sides and the
  ungated pointer configuration is preserved as the best measured state. The night's solved result (ten
  of ten form compliance on the demand-store configurations; 0.93277 bits/byte served) is unaffected.
