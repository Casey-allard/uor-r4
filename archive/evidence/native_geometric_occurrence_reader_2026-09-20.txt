DECISION
--------
Targeted selection works and the geometric slot feature carries accuracy the exact-identity control
cannot, but abstention is uncalibrated and the all-position loss regresses beyond the declared
tolerance on both the synthetic and the raw-text panel. No candidate is promoted. Recommended next
action (see the result record): add a declared abstention signal learned from natural-text positions
where a candidate exists but no exact successor is useful, before any further integration claim. Do
not re-fit this selector, widen the ring/candidate bounds, or change width/precision/corpus.
