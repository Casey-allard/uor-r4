UOR-R4 — The quantisation third of the served gap is 97 % the mixer maps
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 20). DeepSeek session. References #820.
Instrument: `geometric-stack rounding-attribution artifact=... model=... valid=... windows=64`.

CONTEXT
  Round 19 decomposed the served-to-fused gap (0.05522 BPB) into 64.8 % transport snap and
  35.2 % export quantisation, and retired calibration as the knob. This asks the next question:
  inside the quantisation third, what is actually losing the information?

THE ATTRIBUTION (19.9 M chat stack, export a1, chat-v0-p2/heldout, 64 windows)

  group          parameters rounded                        cost (nats)   share    top-1 vs float
  all            every embedded parameter                   0.0173756     100.0 %  0.9244
  all_maps       every layer's mixer_maps + mlp + embed/head 0.0168514     97.0 %  0.9256
  all_scalars    every layer's mixer_scalars                  0.0004643      2.7 %  0.9908
  l7.mlp         the last layer's MLP alone                   0.0006545      3.8 %  0.9815

  The mixer maps carry essentially the entire quantisation loss. Rounding the scalars alone costs
  0.00046 nats and keeps 99.1 % top-1 agreement with the float model; rounding the maps costs
  0.01685 nats and drops agreement to 92.6 %. Rounding every parameter costs almost exactly what
  the maps cost on their own (0.01738 against 0.01685), so the two groups are not additive
  problems - the maps are the problem.

WHAT IT MEANS
  1. The quantisation term is not spread evenly through the artifact and not a grid-resolution
     question in general: it is concentrated in one parameter class. A finer or differently
     structured representation for the mixer maps is the whole of the available recovery here.
  2. It also explains why the round-19 export arm failed to help. More calibration windows and
     finer damping change how the *grid* is chosen; they do not change how much of a map's
     information one grid cell can carry. The lever is the map's representation, not the fitting
     procedure.
  3. Combined with round 19, the served gap now reads: 0.03578 BPB from the transport snap plus
     0.01944 from quantisation, and of that quantisation, 0.01886 is the mixer maps and 0.0005 the
     scalars. Two thirds representation, one third one parameter class, and almost nothing
     elsewhere.

SCOPE
  One model, one export, one corpus, 64 windows, single run of a deterministic tool. Costs are
  nats per token relative to this model's own float evaluation inside the same tool, so they are
  comparable to each other and to the round-19 numbers in sign and share, but the absolute value
  differs slightly from the 24,576-target d11-evaluate figure because the scored position set
  differs.
