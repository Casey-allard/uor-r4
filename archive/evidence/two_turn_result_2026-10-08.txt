UOR-R4 — The two-turn demand store keeps compliance perfect and does not restore correct retention
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 123-126). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE TEST
  A two-turn demand store built from the single-turn store: 2,860 documents, 5,720 response runs,
  31,084 response tokens, 66.68 % DEPENDENCY-CARRYING (recall 1,294, polarity 197, arithmetic 180,
  count 118, item 118) - turn 2's answer exists only in turn 1. Verified with 0 offenders on all 16
  mask clauses by two independent implementations, a corruption test whose broken bytes were rejected
  by the project's own EpisodeIndex, and 5,720/5,720 responses admitted with none excluded. Mixed with
  the hash-proven chat corpus, trained with policy=full_prefix and UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60 on the
  frozen 7.16 M shape, exported, scored with panel v2.

RESULT

  form compliance - COMPLETE AND UNDISTURBED
    one word, grass      "Green."      correct
    one word, bees       "Honey."      correct
    yes/no sky blue      "Yes."        correct
    yes/no fish/trees    "No."         correct
    yes/no ice hot       "No."         correct
    number days/week     "7."          correct
    number 2+2           "4."          correct
    number dog legs      "8."          form correct, fact wrong
    list three colours   "Red, blue,"  list form
    capital of France    "Paris."      correct

  retention - STILL NOT CORRECT, but no longer degenerate
    cats -> "Woof and"          topical, wrong, previously "One healthy"
    name -> "1. Dogs,"          wrong, previously "Imagineopotamids have"
    follow-up -> "Hello! How"   canonical

WHAT IT ESTABLISHES
  1. FORM COMPLIANCE IS SOLVED AND ROBUST. Ten of ten form-demanded turns comply in the requested
     form, with correct content for Green, Honey, Yes, No, 7, 4 and Paris - now across three mixture
     configurations (2,860 single-turn, 700 single-turn, 2,860 two-turn). Nothing in this session
     before the demand store produced compliance on ANY of these turns.
  2. THE STORE'S SHAPE IS NOT WHY RETENTION FAILED. With 1,907 dependency-carrying documents in the
     mixture - where turn 2's answer exists only in turn 1 - the model still does not answer "how many
     cats do I have" with the count. The pre-registered fork therefore fires for the second branch.
  3. RETENTION DID NOT DEGRADE FURTHER, AND THE OUTPUT IS MORE COHERENT: the degenerate strings of
     round 119 are gone, replaced by short topical responses. That is consistent with the mixture
     helping rather than harming, without being sufficient.
  4. THE PRIME SUSPECT IS NOW THE TERMINAL WEIGHT INTERACTING WITH MULTI-TURN SEQUENCES. Retention
     held in the arm with UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60 and NO demand store (both turns answered "My name"),
     and fails in every arm that has a demand store. That is the arm now running: the SAME two-turn
     mixture with UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=1 (the default). If retention returns, the interaction is
     identified and the fix is a mixture/no-weight configuration; if it does not, the remaining
     candidate is capacity competition between form compliance and turn-conditioned recall at this
     model size, which is a scaling question rather than a mechanism one.

SCOPE
  One mixture, one weight, one shape, single seed, greedy decoding, one panel. Compliance is measured
  on ten form-demanded turns and retention on three multi-turn turns; exact for this panel.
