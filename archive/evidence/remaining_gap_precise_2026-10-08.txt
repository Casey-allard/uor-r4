UOR-R4 — The remaining gap, stated precisely: the project has demands without short answers and short answers without demands
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 109). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT THE GENERATOR'S OWN DOCUMENTATION SAYS
  synthetic-memory-corpus.rs, header: "the reply always keeps the single canonical answer form, so an
  arm that varies `forms` varies how a fact is stated and asked, NOT HOW IT IS ANSWERED." And `forms`
  is clamped to 1..=8, with 8..12 held out for the panel.

  So the synthetic generator cannot supply what the previous arm needed. It produces SHORT ANSWERS
  reliably - 20,000 documents at a 14.9 % response fraction, against the chat corpus's 68.9 % - but
  its USER TURNS are relation phrasings ("what is X?"), never a demand on the answer's form ("answer
  with exactly one word"). The instruction half of the combination is not available from that tool at
  any setting.

WHAT THE CORPUS HAS, AND WHAT IT LACKS
  Measured in round 81: the chat corpus contains 17,205 turns whose user text says "your answer must /
  should ..." and exactly 142 turns in the whole contract family (one word / yes-no / only the answer /
  no explanation / reply with only). It has DEMANDS - and their replies are prose (median 78 words) -
  but it has 26 one-word replies in 129,465 responses, of which 4 survive the context filter.
  The synthetic store has SHORT ANSWERS - and no demands at all.

  SO THE PROJECT POSSESSES EACH HALF SEPARATELY AND NEITHER TOGETHER:
    demands without short answers      the corpus's 142 contract turns
    short answers without demands      the 20,000 synthetic documents
    the pair, together                 DOES NOT EXIST IN ANY STORE THE PROJECT HAS

  That is the precise reason the combination arm of round 108 reproduced the stopping signal's
  behaviour and moved nothing on the one-word and numeric classes. It is not that the model cannot
  learn the form; it is that nothing it has been trained on ever shows a demand and its matching
  short answer in the same turn.

THE ARTEFACT TO BUILD, NAMED
  A demand-bearing short-form store: user turns that instruct a form ("Answer with exactly one word.",
  "Answer with a number only.", "Answer yes or no.") paired with replies that are exactly that form,
  in the project's own literal-role protocol 2 with the mask convention dialogue_episodes.rs:385-405
  requires (the five Assistant marker ids with mask 0, the response content marked 1, terminating in a
  genuine EOS, no EOS inside the run - the three clauses this session has now satisfied twice).
  The generator can be extended to emit demand surface forms for its existing relations, or a small
  standalone generator can be written; either way it is a bounded piece of work with a known format,
  and the two frozen instruments already measure the outcome.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT YET ESTABLISH
  Whether the pair is sufficient. If a store with demands and matching short answers still leaves the
  one-word and numeric classes non-compliant, then the per-token objective is the last explanation
  standing and the fix moves to the loss or the decoding rather than the data - which is the same
  fork the session has been narrowing toward all night, now with one artefact left to rule out.

SCOPE
  Read from the generator's documentation and the round-81 measurement. No new store was built and no
  arm was run in this round; the contribution is the precise statement of what is missing and why the
  previous arm could not have supplied it.
