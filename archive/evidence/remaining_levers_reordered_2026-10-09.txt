UOR-R4 — The remaining binding levers, reordered by measurement: read-binding supervision ranks LAST
Date: 2026-10-09 (overnight round 180). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT WAS READ, AND WHY IT CHANGES THE ORDER
  Round 179 read the interface of the last untried supervision term on this path. The trainer supports
  `read_binding_supervision=W` with labels from a sidecar generated by
  `dialogue-recall-corpus binding_labels=1`, checked against the training store's source by token count
  and payload SHA-256, needing policy=full_prefix, full read admission and f32. What the term OPTIMISES
  is the point: at each input position whose next token is a token of the answer's expected value, it
  adds `W * -log(m + 1e-6)` where `m` is the attention mass of the binding head of a read layer on the
  EXPECTED VALUE'S HISTORY POSITIONS. In other words it supervises WHERE THE READ LOOKS.

  THE PROBE ALREADY MEASURED THAT QUANTITY, AND IT IS ALREADY HIGH:
    * ungated pointer arm: pointer mass on the value's span 0.90-1.00 in 9 of 21 cells
    * earlier store arms: read mass at or above TAU on the expected span in 62 of 63 cells
    * no-store control: below TAU everywhere
  So the read lands, and the measured failures are DOWNSTREAM of it: the continuation, the copy gate
  (now shown to be unraisable without damage across four weights), and the competition inside the
  identity mixture. A term that pushes the read toward a span it already reaches is therefore optimising
  the quantity least likely to be binding.

THE REORDERED LIST, WITH THE REASON FOR EACH POSITION
  1. A TOKEN-IDENTITY POINTER KEY instead of a hidden-state key. The pointer currently matches on
     hidden states and must learn identity matching; meanwhile the values it DOES copy correctly
     (" Zorba.", " Quillon.", " Anvil.", " Ben.") are copied as identities, and the expert review named
     the hidden-state key as the pointer's honest limitation. This attacks the mixture's matching step
     rather than its gate.
  2. A CONSTRAINED / TWO-TOKEN DECODER that draws the answer from the span. It sidesteps the mixture
     competition entirely, which the four-point gate sweep established as the binding constraint (the
     gate is low because the response loss prefers it low; forcing it costs compliance monotonically).
     Ranked second only because it is a serving-side change rather than a learned capability, so it
     demonstrates the diagnosis rather than teaching the model.
  3. READ-BINDING SUPERVISION, ranked LAST despite being the term the review listed first among the
     untried ones, because the quantity it optimises is already near its ceiling in exactly the arms
     where the answer fails.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT CHANGE
  * The best configuration remains the ungated pointer arm on the content-copy mixture: value-driven
    copying of values that exist in NO store family or chat prior, pointer mass 0.90-1.00 on the value
    span, value-paired DELTA +3.85 to +10.39 nats, with partial form compliance including correct yes/no
    facts - and no other arm combines those two properties.
  * The night's solved result is untouched: instruction-form compliance ten of ten with correct content
    on the demand-store configurations, and 0.93277 bits/byte served with a multiplier-free engine exact
    to 1e-6 nats.
  * Nothing here is a result about binding: it is an ORDERING argument built from measurements already
    recorded, and it is written down so that the next run is chosen by evidence rather than by the order
    in which proposals happened to be listed.

SCOPE
  A reading of the trainer's documented behaviour plus the session's own measurements. No arm was run in
  this round. The ranking is a prediction and is falsifiable in the obvious way: if a token-identity key
  or a constrained decoder fails to move the emitted-value criterion while read-binding supervision
  succeeds, this ordering was wrong and should be corrected in public as the session's other wrong calls
  have been.
