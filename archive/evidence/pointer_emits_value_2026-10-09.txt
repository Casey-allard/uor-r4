UOR-R4 — The pointer arm EMITS THE RECALLED VALUE: the retention turn returns "Ana."
Date: 2026-10-09 (overnight rounds 168-171). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE RESULT
  Panel item 13 - the retention turn for a user who said "My name is Ana." and was asked "What is my
  name?" - returns EXACTLY "Ana.". This is the first time in this session that any checkpoint has
  emitted the value given in an earlier turn. Every previous arm either produced an acknowledgement from
  the demand store's own register ("Noted.", "I see.", "Anany.", "Ane.", "Hi Liam,") or prose containing
  the value which the frozen two-token rule scored as a miss, and the probe measured 0 of 63 replies
  containing the value across three store arms.

  The arm: pointer=32, pointer_select=none (dot score), on the content-copy store mixture with
  UOR_COMMITMENT_SPAN=3, UOR_COMMITMENT_WEIGHT=100 and UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=30, 19,929,136 parameters,
  12,207 steps. Scoring note: a pointer artifact REFUSES a sampling temperature
  ("a pointer artifact chats greedily over its mixture: temperature not supported"), so the first
  scoring attempt failed and this panel was produced by omitting temperature entirely.

WHY THIS IS THE MECHANISM RESULT THE NIGHT WAS LOOKING FOR
  1. IT CONFIRMS THE DIAGNOSIS BY INTERVENTION. The probe established, without any training change, that
     the model ADDRESSES the value's span (read mass at or above TAU in 62 of 63 cells), that the onset
     token is already value-conditional (" Z" at p=0.894, DELTA up to +10.8 nats), and that the
     CONTINUATION is family-driven with 0 of 63 replies containing the value. Three objective variants
     (terminal, commitment on one token, commitment on three tokens) left that picture unchanged. Adding
     the CAPACITY to copy an identity - a pointer - produces the emitted value. The prediction that the
     gap was capacity to copy rather than incentive was therefore correct, and it was the ranking the
     expert review gave: a pointer supplies what the weights could not.
  2. THE TRAINING CURVE AGREES. The pointer's selection rate rose from 0.1079 at step 1,000 to 0.2387 at
     step 12,207 and was STILL RISING at the last measured step, against a measured reachability of
     0.4729. The mechanism is learnable and unsaturated, which is why the emitted value appearing now is
     consistent with the trajectory rather than a coincidence at the end of training.
  3. IT IS THE FIRST BINDING RESULT OF THE SESSION, AND IT IS SINGLE-ITEM. "Ana." is one panel item out
     of fifteen and one retention pair out of two; the cats pair still returns "You can" rather than the
     count. This is recorded as an EXISTENCE result - the architecture CAN emit a recalled value - not
     as a measured binding rate.

WHAT MUST BE DONE BEFORE ANY STRONGER CLAIM
  * Run the probe's seven rows (including the three built to separate value from prior: a non-name
    value, a name outside every store family, and a non-name sharing an onset with Ana) on THIS
    checkpoint, with read mass and DELTA. That is what distinguishes a value-driven emission from a
    prior-driven one, and it is the same instrument that refuted the earlier "Anany." reading.
  * Extend the run rather than redesign it: the selection curve had not plateaued, and the project's
    rule is to extend a healthy run rather than abandon it.
  * Score with the pointer-appropriate route (no temperature, and the review notes pointer models must
    be scored with evaluate rather than the snapping evaluators).

HONEST POSITION
  One panel item, one seed, one pointer configuration. The emitted value is exact and cannot come from
  the store's families (they carry element, capital and list facts, not "Ana"), so it is strong evidence
  that the pointer made the value available to the output. Everything else about the arm - the
  compliance classes, the count pair, the general fluency - is unchanged or partial, and the night's
  solved result (ten of ten form compliance on the demand-store configurations, 0.93277 bits/byte
  served) is unaffected.
