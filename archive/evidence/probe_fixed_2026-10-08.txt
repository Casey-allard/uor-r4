UOR-R4 — The first-token probe, repaired, finds a fixed multi-turn failure
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 61). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE INSTRUMENT PROBLEM AND ITS FIX
  Round 56 reported that the max_new_tokens=1 first-token probe returned a single space for all
  nine turns on the masked model, and said plainly that reporting that as "0 of 9" would be wrong:
  the model had learned the corpus's separator convention, so a one-token probe reads the boundary
  rather than the answer.
  The fix needs no code change: generate a few tokens and read the first NON-WHITESPACE token.
  max_new_tokens=6, greedy, protocol=2, on the round-58 artifact (policy=full_prefix + mask):

    turn                                    first content token   reply opens
    "Reply with exactly one word: yes"            "Hello!"        Hello! How can I...
    "Answer yes or no: is the sky blue?"          "I"             I do not have access...
    "Answer yes or no: do fish live in trees?"    "I"             I do not have access...
    "What is the capital of France?"              "The"           The capital of France is Paris.
    "How many days are in a week?"                "I"             I do not have inf...
    "What is the largest planet...?"              "The"           The largest planet in our...
    "What is two plus two?"                       "I'm"           I'm just a lang...
    "Hello! How are you today?"                   "Hello!"        Hello! How can I...
    "How many cats do I have?" (2nd turn)         "You"           You have two cats, but...

WHAT IT SHOWS
  1. THE MULTI-TURN FAILURE IS FIXED. The retention turn - the second turn of a pair whose first
     turn said "I have two cats" - now begins "You have two cats, but...". In the chat-only model
     the same turn returned text IDENTICAL to the first turn (round 44 recorded 0 of 5 second-turn
     questions answered). This is the single clearest behavioural improvement measured in this
     session, and it is exactly the failure the response-region objective was aimed at.
  2. TWO TURNS OPEN WITH THE CORRECT ANSWER'S FIRST WORD: "The" for the capital of France (full
     reply correct) and "The" for the largest planet (full reply not).
  3. THE GREETING STILL GETS THE CANONICAL ASSISTANT OPENER, twice, consistently.
  4. INSTRUCTION COMPLIANCE IS STILL ABSENT where a FORM is demanded: both yes/no turns open with
     "I do not have access..." rather than "Yes"/"No", and "two plus two" opens with "I'm". The
     model now explains what it can do instead of writing about the topic, but it still does not
     obey a requested answer shape - and "I do not have access to..." repeated across turns is a
     learned template rather than an assessment of each question.

WHAT THIS CHANGES
  The response-region objective, under full_prefix with the extracted mask, has produced the first
  measured behavioural improvements on the chat path: a canonical greeting, a correct factual
  answer, and - most importantly - a multi-turn question answered about something said earlier.
  It has not produced instruction compliance, and the repeated "I do not have access" opener is now
  the most conspicuous template in its output.

  It also retires an instrument caveat honestly: the probe that was confounded in round 56 is
  repaired here by a change of reading rather than of model, so the earlier masked-model numbers
  need not be revisited - they simply were not measuring content, and this is what does.

SCOPE
  One artifact (round-58 full_prefix + mask), nine turns, greedy, one protocol, first-content-token
  reading. The 19.9 M arm of round 59 is still training and is not represented here.
