UOR-R4 — The quantiser arms, measured on one model: GPTQ beats round-to-nearest, and 64 windows is an interior optimum
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 23). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE CONTROLLED COMPARISON
  Same 19.9 M chat stack, same held-out stream (chat-v0-p2/heldout, sha256 e5f400b0...), same
  integer evaluation (d11-evaluate, 24,576 targets, engine top-1 agreement 1.0 in every arm).
  Only the export's quantisation differs; the method is selected by whether calibration tokens are
  supplied (geometric-stack.rs:2954-2975).

  arm                                   export record          d11 nll                bits/byte
  round-to-nearest (no calibration)     round_to_nearest        1.8419342517773867     0.93652
  GPTQ, 64 windows, damp 0.01           gptq                   1.8345359993679817     0.93277
  GPTQ, 256 windows, damp 0.001         gptq                   1.836327732755273      0.93368

  GPTQ with 64 calibration windows and damp 0.01 is the best of the three by 0.00375 BPB over
  round-to-nearest and 0.00091 over the finer-calibrated GPTQ arm.

WHAT IT SETTLES
  1. The export path's default is the right one. GPTQ beats round-to-nearest on this model, so the
     calibration-bearing export that every artifact tonight used is buying real fidelity, and the
     round-21 proposal to "try GPTQ" was chasing something already in use.
  2. The calibration setting is an INTERIOR optimum, not a floor. Both directions are worse: four
     times the windows with an order of magnitude finer damping loses 0.00091 BPB, and removing
     calibration entirely loses 0.00375. The default therefore sits between two measured worse
     options, which is a stronger statement than "the default is fine".
  3. The quantisation term of the served gap is 0.01944 BPB at its measured optimum. Round 19
     showed calibration choice does not improve it and round 20 showed 97 % of it is the weight
     matrices; this round shows the method that produced it is the better of the two available.

SCOPE
  One model, one corpus, one held-out stream, one export per arm, deterministic integer evaluation.
  The three arms differ only in quantisation method and calibration parameters. Bit-level claims
  concern the recorded nll values, not every intermediate.
