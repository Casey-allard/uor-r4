UOR-R4 — Doubling the training does not close the instruction-precision gap
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 75-84). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT RAN
  The working configuration (19,929,136 params, rrarrarr, width 512, heads 8, read L2,
  policy=full_prefix, the verified response mask) trained for 24,414 steps, i.e. TWICE the 12,207 of
  the arm it is compared against. Final development response nll 1.6801235877441423. Exported and
  scored automatically by the watcher installed for it, with no intervention.

RESULT ON THE TARGET: NO CHANGE
  Panel, greedy, protocol 2, read as the first TWO content tokens per reply (the reading the round-81
  review established as necessary, since the yes/no class fails at the second token):

    greeting turns (x3)      "Hello! How"       canonical, as before
    largest planet           "The largest"      promising opener, no value
    name retention (x2)      "My name"          RESPONSIVE - the question is answered in kind
    cats retention           "Smallly, cats"    responsive in kind
    Japan follow-up          "Planning a"       topical
    cake turn                "To cook"          topical
    two plus two             "A classic"        still no number
    one-word turns           no bare token      still non-compliant
    numeric turns            no bare number     still non-compliant

  NOT ONE instruction-form request is complied with. Doubling the training changed nothing about
  compliance, which is exactly what the round-81 review predicted from the data: of 129,465
  responses only 4 one-word replies remain after the context-384 episode filter, so there is almost
  nothing to learn the form from, and no amount of further exposure creates it.

WHAT IT DID NOT BREAK
  The behaviours the objective bought are intact and, on the retention class, clearer than before:
  greetings stay canonical, the follow-ups stay topical, and BOTH retention turns now open by
  answering the question asked ("My name", "Smallly, cats") where the chat-only model returned the
  previous turn's text verbatim. The regression guard the review asked for therefore holds: extra
  training did not buy anything on the target and did not cost anything on the behaviours already
  won.

WHAT IT RULES OUT
  The remaining explanations for the instruction-precision gap were: data support, capacity,
  optimization, the objective, and decoding. This arm rules out OPTIMIZATION (2x steps, no change)
  and, with the round-74 capacity arm, CAPACITY (2.8x params, no change on compliance) and DECODING
  (round 73: sampling removes the refusal template but not non-compliance). Two candidates are left,
  and they are the two the review ranked first and third: DATA SUPPORT, which the queued
  policy=truncated_prefix arm tests with one argument and 14.75x the short-form episodes, and the
  TOKEN-LEVEL MLE LENGTH BIAS, which predicts that even with support the model continues after "Yes"
  because 31 continuations carry about 250:1 of the token mass against 16 stops.

  If truncated_prefix fails as this arm did, the objective explanation becomes the leading one, and
  the fix moves from data to the loss or the decoding - which is a different piece of work than any
  tried tonight.

SCOPE
  One model, one objective, one mask, one training length doubling, single seed, greedy decoding, the
  earlier panel read with the corrected two-token rule. The dev nll (1.6801) is worse than the
  12,207-step arm's (1.4176) while behaviour is equivalent, which is a third data point that
  development nll does not track this objective's quality.
