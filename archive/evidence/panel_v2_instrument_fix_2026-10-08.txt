UOR-R4 — Panel v2: repairing the two instrument defects the review found before spending on the target
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 82-83). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHY THE INSTRUMENT HAD TO BE FIXED FIRST
  The independent review of round 81 found that the frozen six-item compliance panel cannot separate
  the two live explanations for the instruction-precision failure:
    * data support - the effective training population contains 4 one-word episodes; or
    * token-level MLE length bias - after "Yes." the corpus offers 31 continuations of median 95
      words against 16 stops, so ~250:1 of the token mass favours continuing even though 22 % of
      turn types stop.
  Two of the six items were weakly posed, and the first-token probe reads the protocol-2 separator,
  which makes it the wrong instrument for a class whose failure is at the SECOND token. Measuring a
  target with an instrument that cannot distinguish its explanations is the error pattern this
  session has repeatedly caught, so the instrument is repaired before more GPU is spent.

PANEL V2 (panel4.json, 14 items) - WHAT CHANGED AND WHY
  1. ECHO REMOVED. "Reply with exactly one word: yes" contains the demanded word in the prompt, so
     "Yes, that's a good example." may be compliance or echo; it cannot be scored. Replaced by
     "Reply with exactly one word. What colour is grass?" and "Answer with a single word only. What
     do bees make?" - the demanded FORM is stated, the answer is not.
  2. SECOND-TOKEN READING. Every reply is reported as its first TWO content tokens
     (/root/read2.py), because the yes/no class is right at token one and wrong at token two; a
     one-token probe cannot see the failure it is meant to measure.
  3. THREE YES/NO ITEMS, THREE NUMERIC ITEMS, one list, two factual, two retention pairs, one
     follow-up - so a class-level result is possible rather than a single anecdote per class.
  4. The retention and follow-up pairs are kept because they are the two behaviours that DID move
     (rounds 58 and 74), and they serve as the regression guard: a change that buys compliance by
     destroying multi-turn conditioning is not progress.

HOW IT WILL BE USED
  The arms now running or queued (the 2x training run model-r7, and the review's
  policy=truncated_prefix arm model-r8) were launched with the earlier panel; their results remain
  valid as measurements of that instrument. Panel v2 is the instrument for the next comparison, and
  the reading rule it introduces is the one that can attribute a result to data support or to
  continuation bias.

SCOPE
  An instrument change, recorded before the runs that will use it. No result is claimed for either
  arm; both are still training or queued.
