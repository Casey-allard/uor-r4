THE PRE-REGISTERED CRITERION, AND IT FIRES
  The independent methodology review set the stop rule in advance: "Stop if KN-5 >= ~1.25 BPB
  -- the existing 8M model already meets the gate, so the next spend must buy
  context/memory, not BPB." Measured KN-5 here is 1.280275, and the 8M model is at 1.1199.
  **The criterion fires.** Further spend on beating this gate with a better n-gram-class
  scorer is buying a result the project already has.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE POINTS AT NOW
  1. The largest measured gap in the project is not a gate, it is the SERVING path: the
     continuous trainer 1.2372 vs the shipped discrete artifact 1.8055 on TinyStories
     (0.5683 BPB), and here the trained stack 1.1199 vs the served scorer 4.2111 (3.09 BPB).
     Both are far larger than any mechanism delta measured this session.
  2. The state channel is not the place to look: an independent review measured the served
     120-state as Haar-uniform with a full-lookup BPB of 3.3027 against 3.3050 for a random
     root map and 3.2909 for a unigram, and I(S;X_{t-L}) flat at the random null for lags
     1..63. Read-side nonlinearity cannot recover information that is not in the state.
  3. The next spend should buy CONTEXT AND MEMORY, not corpus-fit BPB: the served scorer is
     a 5-gram-class model with a 6.9-bit side channel, while the trained stack that reaches
     1.1199 uses 255-token context. Long-context retention (the plan's K-tuple direction,
     K~16-64) and the continuous-to-served gap are the two levers the measurements leave.

SCOPE
  BPB is comparable only within a corpus and only with one byte basis; all numbers here use
  the tokenizer's own byte lengths on chat-v0-p2 held-out (sha e5f400b0). The 1.1199 figure is
  a conversion of a sealed nats-per-token evaluation; the file itself records bits_per_byte as
  null. A document-clustered protocol (per-document delta sd 0.2148 BPB, clustered SE 0.003489
  over the full file) is required for any claim below about 0.01 BPB, and this session's
  retracted -0.0196 was 0.09 sigma under it.
