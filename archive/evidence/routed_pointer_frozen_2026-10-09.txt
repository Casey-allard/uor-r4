UOR-R4 — The token-id route FREEZES the pointer: reachability becomes a fixed property of the admission rule, and it is lower
Date: 2026-10-09 (overnight rounds 185-186). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT RAN
  The review's recommended lever, reached as CONFIGURATION rather than new code: the trainer's pointer route
  derives keys from TOKEN IDS (`pointer_route_keys`, geometric_stack.rs:3281) instead of from hidden states.
  Flag validated with a 20-step smoke test first (and corrected once: the route's key window is 1 to 6
  tokens, not 8). Full arm: pointer=32, pointer_select=none, pointer_route=ngram:4, content-copy mixture,
  UOR_COMMITMENT_SPAN=3, UOR_COMMITMENT_WEIGHT=100, UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=30, 19.9M, 12,207 steps.

WHAT THIS MEANS
  1. THE ROUTE'S KEYS ARE DETERMINISTIC, SO THE POINTER'S REACH IS A PROPERTY OF THE ADMISSION RULE
     RATHER THAN OF LEARNING. The route admits candidate sources by shared prime factors / n-gram overlap
     over a key window; with a value that shares no n-gram with the question asking about it - which is
     exactly the recall case, and exactly the case the review warned about - the admission set is fixed in
     advance and training cannot enlarge it. That is why the quantity is frozen.
  2. IT SEPARATES TWO THINGS THE REVIEW HAD BUNDLED: "a token-identity KEY" (which the identity mixture
     wants, because identity copying is what the successful copies are) from "a prime/ngram ADMISSION
     RULE" (which novel-value recall does not want). This arm swapped BOTH at once and the result is a
     lower, frozen reachability - so the evidence does not support the token-id route as the fix, and it
     does support keeping the LEARNED keys while changing what the pointer is asked to MATCH.
  3. THE NEXT CHANGE IS THEREFORE SMALL AND SPECIFIC: keep the learned query/key pathway (reachability
     0.473, hit rate rising) and add a TOKEN-IDENTITY MATCHING TERM to it - for example a component that
     scores a candidate source by whether its tokens appear in the question's premise, which is neither
     the n-gram admission rule nor a pure hidden-state dot product. That is a bounded change to the
     pointer's score, not a new architecture, and the instrument that would judge it is already built:
     the 28-row novel-value rate with its 0.000 control, the gate stratification, and the value-versus
     -prior rows.
  4. THE RECALL RESULT IS UNTOUCHED. The unrouted pointer still copies never-seen values at 0.417 of
     cells against 0.000 for the no-pointer control at every budget, with the read landing (pointer mass
     0.90-1.00 on the value's span) and the gate near-necessary-not-sufficient. This arm adds a negative
     about ONE way of changing the matching, not about the mechanism.

HONEST POSITION
  A clean, early negative that cost one smoke test and one partial run: the routed arm's panel is not needed
  to reach the conclusion, because the pointer's own quantities never moved, which is a stronger and
  simpler signal than any panel reading. Recorded as the sixth route explored for binding and the first
  one whose failure mode is visible in the training curve itself.

SCOPE
  One route configuration (ngram:4), one mixture, one shape, single seed, 12,207 steps; the panel was
  still pending when the frozen quantities settled the question. Exact for these readings.
