WHY THIS REPLACES THE SOURCE CENSUS
  Three source-level counts of the same code disagreed: 82 (raw grep), 43 (forbidden_arith
  scanner) and 216 (token-level classifier, gated). Source counting cannot answer "does serving
  engage a multiplier", because (a) the compiler decides whether a constant multiply becomes
  shifts or a `mul instruction`, and (b) file scope is not serving scope. The property D0-a names
  is what executes, so it must be measured in the executable.

METHOD
  `~/.cargo/bin/cargo build --release --offline -p uor-r4-api --bin r4-native-chat`
  then `otool -tV` over the Mach-O, segmented by symbol labels, counting multiply mnemonics
  (`mul madd msub mneg smull umull smaddl umaddl smulh umulh sqdmulh sqrdmulh fmul fmadd fmsub
  fnmul fmla imul mla mls`). Every number below is an instruction count in the optimised binary,
  not an operator count in source.

FINDING 1 — THE INERT MECHANISM IS THE LARGEST MULTIPLY CONSUMER
  The VSA layer accounts for **898 of 3,100 multiply instructions (29 %)** — `vsa/attention` 306
  plus `vsa/hypervector`/`codebook`/`hierarchical` 592. That is more than every other serving
  module combined, including `lattice_table`, `engram`, `hopf_metric`, `jepa_trainer` and the mmap
  serving path.

  This is the same layer that the Card P7 ablation measured as **inert in scoring** (ΔBPB
  −0.0007, class `enabler`, and the query/key inversion was a real defect). Two independent
  measurements now converge: the VSA layer costs the most multiplies on the serving path and
  delivers nothing measurable to scoring. It does contribute to ROUTING (recall 8.4–8.8 % →
  10.7–11.2 % on two disjoint slices), so it is not worthless — but its cost/benefit is the worst
  on the path.

  Therefore the single largest available multiplier reduction is to make the VSA heads
  table-or-lookup based rather than arithmetic — precompute the head similarity quantities, or
  replace `MultiHeadVsaAttention::attend` with a bounded lookup — which would remove on the order
  of 29 % of serving multiplies while preserving the routing benefit. That is a concrete,
  high-value redesign target and it is not where any source census pointed.

FINDING 2 — THE JEPA CONVERSION IS VERIFIED AT THE BINARY LEVEL
  `context_predicted_fiber_from_ring`: **mul = 0** (41 instructions).
  `context_fiber_from_ring`: **mul = 5** (84 instructions).

  The JEPA projection carries zero multiply instructions after the `mul_shift_add` conversion —
  direct evidence that the conversion achieved its goal, which the source census could not have
  established. The residual 5 multiplies in `context_fiber_from_ring` are in
  `hopf_fiber_project` (the S3→S2 Hopf map computes `2(ac+bd)` and similar directly), which is
  genuine arithmetic that a table cannot replace without changing the map.

FINDING 3 — THE SOURCE CENSUSES WERE WRONG IN BOTH DIRECTIONS
  `hopf_metric` reports 43–70 multiplying *operators* by source but only **51 multiply
  instructions** in the optimised binary, and `binary_model` reports 78 operators but only **4**
  instructions. Conversely `vsa/attention` reports **zero** multiplying forms to a source scan but
  **306 multiply instructions** in the binary, because the constants it multiplies by (such as
  the `32767` scale in `bipolar_correlation_q15`) are dense and the compiler emits real `mul`
  instructions rather than shifts, and because inlining pulls those into `attend`.

  The octet-level lesson: **a source census of multiplying operators is not evidence about
  multiplier circuits, in either direction.** It can report zero where 306 exist, and report 78
  where 4 exist. All earlier multiplier numbers in this engagement are superseded by these.

WHAT TO DO WITH THIS
  1. The binary-level count is the authoritative metric and should be the basis of the ratchet. It
     cannot be a unit test without a release build in CI, so record the numbers and the exact
     commands as the reproducible procedure; re-run on any change to the serving path.
  2. The highest-value reduction is the VSA layer (898 instructions). Making the heads
     lookup-based is a design task with a clear payoff, and it is now the top item.
  3. `binary_model` (4) and `jepa_trainer` (44) are close to clean and need no urgent work.
  4. `hopf_metric` (51) is smaller than the source counts implied and contains genuine arithmetic
     (`hopf_project`); converting it to shift-add is possible but low-value.
  5. `api/native_capability_api` (116) is the second concrete target and contains the sampling and
     scoring arithmetic already partly converted.
