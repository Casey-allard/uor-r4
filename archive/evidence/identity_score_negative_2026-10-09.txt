UOR-R4 — The token-identity matching term HURTS recall, monotonically in its weight: 0.458 -> 0.135 -> 0.000
Date: 2026-10-09. DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT RAN
  The token-identity pointer score (merged as #2002, silent-config bug fixed as #2003) tested against
  the learned hidden-state match on the same 28 invented values, three conditions, 96 cells per arm,
  all scored IN THE SAME SESSION with the probe that reads the model directly (export refuses an
  identity pointer - it has no integer port - so a LUT path would have failed spuriously).

    arm                                    exact copy                contains
    baseline arm-ptr (learned keys)        44/96 = 0.458 [0.362,0.558]   65/96 = 0.677
    A: pointer_identity=0.5 (mixed)        13/96 = 0.135 [0.081,0.218]   18/96 = 0.188
    B: pointer_identity=1   (alone)         0/96 = 0.000 [0.000,0.038]    0/96 = 0.000

  THE IDENTITY TERM DOES NOT HELP. It hurts, and monotonically in its weight: half weight costs about
  32 points with NON-OVERLAPPING intervals against the baseline, and full weight destroys recall
  completely, reproducing the no-pointer control's number (0/84 in the earlier run) despite the model
  having a pointer. The baseline also reproduces its earlier measurement (0.458 here against 0.417 on a
  slightly different cell count), which is what makes the matched in-session comparison credible.

WHY, FROM THE TRAINING CURVES AND THE DEFINITION
  * REACHABILITY IS IDENTICAL ACROSS ALL THREE ARMS (0.4729) - and identical to the learned-key arm.
    So the span is ADDRESSABLE in every configuration, and what differs is purely the score's ability
    to DISCRIMINATE. Reachability is a property of the data, not of the score, and it cannot be used to
    judge a matching term.
  * THE TERM IS ANTI-CORRELATED WITH THE CORRECT SOURCE IN THIS TASK. The value's tokens appear in the
    PREMISE, so every candidate source near the premise shares those tokens; a local-window Jaccard
    therefore cannot distinguish the answer's location from its neighbours. Half weight halves the
    effective weight on the only signal that works; full weight discards it.
  * The hit-rate curve agreed in advance: arm A's selection rate rose (0.2215 -> 0.2373 over steps
    6,000-8,000, reaching the baseline's 0.2387 earlier) because the DOT component still carried the
    run; the copy rate is what exposed the damage.

A METHOD NOTE THAT MATTERED
  A first extraction printed 0/96 for ALL THREE arms INCLUDING the baseline. That was absurd on its
  face - the baseline had already measured 0.417 - and it was caught because a known number was
  compared against. Cause: the probe stores the value inside the record id as "n_vantel", and the
  parser was comparing "n_vantel" against " Vantel.". The corrected extraction is the table above. This
  is the third time in this session that the error was in the reading rather than the model, and it is
  why every number here is checked against something already known before it is believed.

WHAT THIS SETTLES
  The learned hidden-state match is doing the work, and local token overlap is not merely unhelpful but
  harmful here. Combined with the earlier route failure (token-id keys with a prime/ngram ADMISSION
  RULE froze at 15 decimals and lost 61 % of reachability), BOTH ways of injecting token identity into
  the pointer have now been measured and rejected:
    - token-id KEYS via a route: reachability collapses (0.182) and the metrics freeze;
    - token overlap as a SCORE term: recall collapses monotonically (0.458 -> 0.135 -> 0.000).
  The remaining lever on this axis is therefore to SHARPEN THE LEARNED MATCH itself rather than to
  replace or augment it with token identity - and the instrument that judges any such change already
  exists: 28 novel values, three conditions, a matched in-session baseline, Wilson intervals, and a
  per-value gain/loss check.

HONEST POSITION
  A clean negative for the eighth route explored on the binding problem, from an arm that reproduced
  its own baseline - so the negative is about the change, not about the run. The night's solved result
  is untouched: ten of ten form compliance with correct content on the demand-store configurations,
  and 0.93277 bits/byte served with a multiplier-free engine exact to 1e-6 nats.

SCOPE
  Three arms (baseline, identity 0.5, identity 1) x three conditions x 32 rows = 96 cells per arm, one
  checkpoint lineage, greedy decoding, single seed per arm, scored in one session. The 28 invented
  values were verified absent from all 19 stores plus the chat corpora (~9.2e8 token positions).
