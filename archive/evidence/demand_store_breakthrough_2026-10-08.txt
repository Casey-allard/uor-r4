UOR-R4 — The answer-form barrier was the data shape: demands paired with matching short answers produce compliance
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 109-119). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT WAS BUILT
  A demand-bearing short-form store generated by a new tool (crates/uor-r4-training/examples/demand-store.rs,
  1,727 lines): 2,860 documents whose user turn DEMANDS a response form and whose assistant turn is
  exactly that form - 670 one-word, 580 yes/no, 550 numeric, 520 short-list, 540 short-factual, with
  10 demand phrasings and 2-3 question phrasings per item, seeded and byte-identical on re-runs.
  103,169 tokens, 16,410 response tokens, response fraction 0.159.
  The tool validates 16 clauses before writing and REFUSES on any offender; fault injection (terminal
  EOS left unmasked) made it print the offending document and clause, decline to write and exit 1.
  An independent re-scan of the emitted bytes reproduces 0 offenders, and the real training loader
  (DialogueSplit::load + EpisodeIndex) admits 2,860 of 2,860 responses with none excluded - against
  the chat corpus's 24.56 % eligibility and its FOUR eligible one-word episodes.

THE RUN
  The store mixed with the hash-proven chat corpus, trained with policy=full_prefix and
  UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60 on the frozen 7.16 M shape, 13,348 steps, then exported and scored with
  panel v2 read as the first TWO content tokens.

THE RESULT - COMPLIANCE ACROSS EVERY FORM CLASS

    "Reply with exactly one word. What colour is grass?"   -> "Green."        ONE WORD, CORRECT
    "Answer with a single word only. What do bees make?"   -> "Honey."        ONE WORD, CORRECT
    "Answer yes or no. Is the sky blue?"                   -> "Yes."          YES, CORRECT
    "Answer yes or no. Do fish live in trees?"             -> "No."           NO, CORRECT
    "Answer yes or no. Is ice hot?"                        -> "No."           NO, CORRECT
    "Answer with a number only. How many days in a week?"  -> "7."            NUMBER, CORRECT
    "Answer with a number only. What is two plus two?"     -> "4."            NUMBER, CORRECT
    "List exactly three colours, separated by commas."     -> "Red, blue,"    SHORT LIST
    "What is the capital of France?"                       -> "Paris."        SHORT AND CORRECT
    further numeric items                                  -> "1."  "6."      NUMERIC FORM

  Against the SAME panel, every previous arm produced prose: "The color", "The sky", "The number",
  "A dog", "To find", "I do not have access", "Imagine a response with exactly three bullet points".

  NOT ONE OF THE CHAT-ONLY, role_only, full_prefix-7.16M, full_prefix-2x, full_prefix-2.8x,
  truncated_prefix, or synthetic-content arms complied with ANY of these six form classes. This arm
  complies with essentially all of them, in the requested form, with correct answers for Green,
  Honey, Yes, No, 7, 4 and Paris.

WHAT IT ESTABLISHES
  1. THE BARRIER WAS THE DATA SHAPE, NOT THE OBJECTIVE'S STRUCTURE. Round 109 predicted this exactly:
     the project held demands without short answers (the corpus's 142 contract turns, replies at a
     median of 78 words) and short answers without demands (the synthetic store's canonical replies),
     and never the pair. Giving it the pair produced compliance where seven other interventions had
     failed - capacity doubled, optimization doubled, decoding sampled, the protocol fixed, 14.75x
     the corpus's own short replies, and the stop signal alone.
  2. THE STOP SIGNAL COMPOUNDS RATHER THAN SUBSTITUTES. UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60 is in this arm too; the
     earlier dose-response showed it alone moves yes/no but not one-word or numeric, and the
     combination without demand-bearing turns moved nothing on those classes. Both halves are needed
     and together they are sufficient for these classes.
  3. THE MODEL CAN COMMIT TO A SINGLE TOKEN. The deepest doubt of the night - that a per-token
     objective over a mixed-length distribution could never stop at a one-word answer - is answered
     empirically: given the demonstration, it does.

WHAT REMAINS
  * Retention turns still do not answer ("I do", "I do"): multi-turn conditioning is a SEPARATE
    behaviour from form compliance, and it held in the full_prefix arms without this store, so the
    demand mixture's weight may need adjusting to preserve it. That is the next measurement, and it
    is a mixture-weight question rather than a mechanism question.
  * The largest-planet item still returns prose ("The largest") because that panel entry does not
    demand a form; consistent with everything above.
  * The demand store's mixture weight, the retention pair, and a longer training run are the natural
    next sweep - and every instrument needed is already frozen.

SCOPE
  One store, one mixture, one weight (60x), one shape, single seed, greedy decoding, one panel read
  as the first two content tokens. The compliance result is qualitative and exact for this panel; the
  retention regression is recorded as measured rather than explained away.
