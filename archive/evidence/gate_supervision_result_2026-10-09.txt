UOR-R4 — Gate supervision raises the gate (0.26 -> 0.42) but degrades the panel: the gate was necessary, not sufficient
Date: 2026-10-09 (overnight rounds 174-175). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT RAN
  The intervention the verification named: pointer_gate_supervision=1.0 added to the pointer arm's
  configuration (pointer=32, dot score) on the content-copy mixture with UOR_COMMITMENT_SPAN=3,
  UOR_COMMITMENT_WEIGHT=100 and UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=30, 19,929,136 parameters, 12,207 steps. The
  interface was read before the run: pointer_gate_supervision is a SCALAR loss weight (default 0 = off),
  not a file, so no new format was needed.

THE GATE DID RISE, AND STAYED UP
  mean gate: 0.2644 without supervision -> 0.4638 early in this run, settling at 0.4164 at step 12,207
  (0.4113 / 0.4034 / 0.4266 / 0.4243 / 0.4220 / 0.4164 across the last six readings). A dedicated
  supervised term reported as dev_gate_bce 0.6489 early. So the lever worked on its own variable: the
  copy gate is now materially higher than in the arm whose weak gate (0.07-0.11 on the ana cells,
  0.60-0.99 on the rows that copied whole spans) was identified as the binding bottleneck.

THE PANEL REGRESSED
    "I do" appears on SIX of fifteen items, and "The sky" returns for the yes/no sky item where the
    ungated pointer arm and several earlier arms complied. "1. Another", "1. Lated", "Sure, I'm",
    "It's generally" and "The capital" do not comply, and the only short in-form items are " Peru."
    (a store-family country) and an item whose turn-1 reply is " Ana.".

  AND I AM NOT CLAIMING THAT " Ana." THIS TIME. The previous round's verification showed exactly this
  panel position is a TURN-1 ECHO rather than the retention answer, and the emitted-value test must be
  run through the probe on values that exist in no store family before any such claim. Reading it again
  as a retention result would repeat the error that was corrected one round ago.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
  1. THE GATE WAS NECESSARY BUT IS NOT SUFFICIENT. Raising it by roughly 60 % did not produce the
     answer, and it cost compliance on classes that had been working: six "I do" replies and the return
     of "The sky". So the identity mixture (1-gate)*generator + gate*attention is not fixed by gate
     strength alone at this supervision weight, and the supervision term competes with the response loss
     enough to degrade the form classes.
  2. THE PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION IS THEREFORE PARTLY FALSIFIED. The prediction was that a gate in the
     0.6-1.0 band would let the ana row copy the span. The gate reached only 0.42 (it did not enter that
     band) and the panel degraded - so neither the success branch nor a clean failure branch was reached;
     the honest statement is that the supervision weight chosen (1.0) is too strong for the mixture and
     too weak to lift the gate into the copying band.
  3. THE NEXT TEST IS A WEIGHT SWEEP, NOT A NEW MECHANISM: gate supervision at a LOWER value (0.1, 0.3)
     would show whether the gate can be lifted toward 0.6 without the response-loss degradation, which
     is the same dose discipline that made the terminal-weight and commitment-weight curves informative;
     and the probe's seven rows with the emitted-value check must accompany every reading.
  4. WHAT REMAINS SOLID FROM THE PREVIOUS ROUND: value-driven copying on out-of-family values
     (" Zorba.", " Quillon.", " Anvil.", " Ben.") at DELTA +3.85 to +10.39 nats with pointer mass
     0.90-1.00 on the value span in 9 of 21 cells - that result came from the arm WITHOUT gate
     supervision and is unaffected by this one.

HONEST POSITION
  A negative for gate supervision at weight 1.0, recorded as such: the lever moved its own variable and
  damaged the panel. It narrows the search rather than closing it, and it leaves the previous round's
  value-driven copying result intact. The night's solved result (ten of ten form compliance on the
  demand-store configurations; 0.93277 bits/byte served) is unaffected.

SCOPE
  One gate-supervision weight, one mixture, one shape, single seed, greedy decoding, one panel read as
  the first two content tokens. The panel regression is exact for this panel; no probe rows were run on
  this checkpoint, which is required before any emitted-value claim.
