UOR-R4 — The objective change moves a compliance class: turn-terminal weighting at 20x
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 94-99). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT RAN
  The change merged as #1946: dialogue_episodes.rs now weights the response's TERMINATING target
  (the <|eos|> that ends the reply, identified by the mask proven byte-identical to the builder's
  hash) by UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT, defaulting to 1.0 so existing runs are unchanged. First arm:
  UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=20, the frozen 7.16 M shape (rrarra, width 288, heads 6, context 384, read L2),
  policy=full_prefix, 13,348 steps, the same corpus and mask as every comparison arm. The pod's
  checkout and binary were verified at commit e0b714e, so the run cannot be a stale-binary no-op.
  Final development response nll 1.4160077217960014.

RESULT ON PANEL V2 (14 items, echo item removed, read as the first TWO content tokens)

  yes/no, sky blue            "Yes, the"      COMPLIES at the first token
  yes/no, is ice hot?         "Yes, ice"      COMPLIES in form (the fact is wrong, which is a
                                              different defect)
  yes/no, fish in trees       "1. Smodates:"  does not comply
  retention, what is my name? "My name"       RESPONDS to the question asked
  one-word turns              "The buildings" / "I don't"          does not comply
  number turns                "The number" / "A dog" / "Sanding is" does not comply
  France / largest planet     "The capital" / "The largest"        openers only

WHAT IT MEANS
  1. TWO OF THREE YES/NO TURNS NOW OPEN WITH "Yes". That is the FIRST time any arm in this session
     has moved a compliance class. Every previous intervention - capacity (2.8x), optimization (2x),
     decoding (sampling), the data-support arm (14.75x short-form episodes) - left compliance at zero;
     weighting the terminating target moved it.
  2. THE PRE-REGISTERED FALSIFIER DID NOT FIRE. It was conditional on "every factor still producing
     no compliance". Compliance moved, so the continuation-mass explanation is SUPPORTED rather than
     dead, and the right next step is to escalate the intervention that showed an effect rather than
     to switch direction.
  3. NOTHING WAS LOST. Retention still responds ("My name"), the France opener is intact, and there
     is no evidence yet of the failure mode to watch - a model that stops immediately everywhere.
     Fluency and the retention pairs remain the guards precisely because a high weight can cause that.
  4. OTHER CLASSES ARE UNMOVED. One-word and numeric turns still produce phrases ("The number",
     "A dog") rather than the requested token. If the dose-response closes them, the explanation is
     confirmed; if they stay flat while yes/no improves, the requested form for those classes is not
     reachable by terminal weighting alone.

THE DOSE-RESPONSE NOW RUNNING
  UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=200, everything else identical. Early signal at step 2,000: development
  response nll 3.0944227027583624 against 2.196886646585287 for the 20x arm at the same step. A much
  higher loss is expected when terminal targets carry 200x weight, and it is also the risk: too high
  a weight can make the model stop immediately in prose turns too. The guards are fluency and the
  retention pairs, the instrument is panel v2, and development nll is explicitly not the metric.

SCOPE
  One weight, one shape, one corpus, one mask, single seed, greedy decoding, one panel. The 20x arm
  is a single measurement of a dose, not a dose-response; the response is what the 200x arm begins,
  and no conclusion beyond "a compliance class moved" is claimed.
