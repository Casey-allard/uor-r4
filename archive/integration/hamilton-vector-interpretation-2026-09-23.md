# Vectors and a Hamiltonian operator: scoped interpretation

The owner's hint is retained as a research lead, not as evidence that geometric attention has been solved in this repository. Two distinct constructions must not be conflated: quaternion Hamilton multiplication and Hamiltonian dynamics generated by an energy function.

## Exact implemented component
`learner/hamilton_transport.rs` applies the eight signed Q8 left actions to four-coordinate integer vectors. For the pure quaternion basis element i, the left action is `K_i(a,b,c,d)=(-b,a,-d,c)`. Analogous j/k actions are exact signed permutations. They preserve squared norm, admit exact inverses and distinguish order; overflow on a required negation is rejected. The tests exercise all actions and preserve the central sign, rather than identifying q with -q.

For a pure unit-axis action K, `K^T=-K` and `K^2=-I`. On the complexified vector space, `H=iK` is Hermitian and `exp(-i*pi*H/2)=K`. This connects an exact finite quaternion action with one particular Hamiltonian evolution. No complex exponential, matrix product or transcendental function executes in the implemented finite action.

The relation is useful but not sufficient for attention. For any common orthogonal action R, `<Rq,Rk>=<q,k>`: rotating every query and key together cannot change their inner-product ranking. A meaningful mechanism must expose relative/content-dependent operations, a different observation, or a transported value, and must demonstrate an advantage against an equally informed control. The helper `relative(query,key,value)` performs the explicit inverse-query/key left action, not a learned semantic selector.

## Connection to the current language results
The warm-start and local-readout experiments in this session do not execute Q8 as their language prediction mechanism. Their measured improvements cannot be attributed to Hamiltonian or H4 geometry. The new module is a bounded reusable operator component with arithmetic/structural tests, not an alternative transformer hidden behind terminology and not a model-quality qualification.

A next geometric experiment should learn the observations that select shared relative actions while retaining exact occurrence identity and provenance. Fixed rotations, finite capacity witnesses and norm preservation must remain distinct from learned retrieval, useful order-dependent prediction and complete-system efficiency.

## External context, not project evidence
- Arjovsky, Shah and Bengio, *Unitary Evolution Recurrent Neural Networks*, arXiv:1511.06464: structured unitary recurrence addresses gradient propagation; it is not proof that this finite operator supplies useful language attention.
- Tay et al., *Lightweight and Efficient Neural Natural Language Processing with Quaternion Networks*, ACL 2019, P19-1145: quaternion-valued NLP models use Hamilton-product parameter sharing. This is different from the repository's finite lookup/sign-permutation implementation.
- Greydanus, Dzamba and Yosinski, *Hamiltonian Neural Networks*, arXiv:1906.01563: learned energy-gradient dynamics are a separate meaning of Hamiltonian modeling and are not silently substituted here.

Independent server-side checks of the three pure-axis generators, Q8 composition/associativity and the Hermitian relation are preserved with the session evidence. These are arithmetic results, not empirical evidence about a learned attention model.

## Notation
Hamilton's quaternion algebra is conventionally H; the Coxeter H4 root system is not that algebra. Its roots admit a quaternion realization through the binary icosahedral/spinorial construction. This corrects terminology without changing the owner's vector/Hamiltonian research lead. Primary context: Dechant, *Clifford algebra is the natural framework for root systems and Coxeter groups*, arXiv:1602.06003, and the quaternionic root-system construction in arXiv:1205.1451. These papers are mathematical context, not model-quality evidence.
