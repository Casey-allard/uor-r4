# Mechanism admissibility and preservation — October 2026

**Authority and scope.** The owner's 1 October direction adopts the principal
research synthesis as the active course toward a learned geometric language
model, with grounded conversation and durable memory first. This policy applies
prospectively through protected delivery. It operationalizes
[D12](DECISIONS.md#d12--gates-promote-never-kill-reopen-geometric-candidates-port-the-native-engines-mechanisms-keep-a-geometric-toolbox),
the [progress-control policy](agent-execution-policy.md#progress-control--owner-correction-september-25)
and D17's paired decision rule. It changes no completed work card, measurement
or historical verdict. The roadmap owns scheduling; this document owns how an
experiment can support a mechanism decision.

The objective remains a learned, geometry-first, transformer-free model with
the adopted integer/table serving contract and useful complete-path laptop
behavior. A kernel audit, exact algebra or successful fixture establishes its
own scoped property. It does not establish conversation, general reasoning or
lower energy.

## Decisions have different scopes

| Decision | Evidence needed | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Component feasibility | A correct construction/proof or an actual positive witness on its declared domain | The operation is possible at that scope |
| Development admission | A current blocker, one causal change, an executable consumer, an affordable projection and outcomes that change the next action | The next implementation or bounded measurement is worth doing |
| Promotion or replacement | A valid instrument, relevant practical quality and deployment conditions, and the applicable comparison rule | This artifact or implementation enters this role now |
| Family parking | D12's written root-cause case, remaining alternatives, re-entry condition and owner authority | A programme priority decision, not impossibility |
| Impossibility | A proof with exact assumptions and quantifiers, or an exhaustive result over a genuinely complete finite scope | Only the stated mathematical or finite impossibility |

A failed fit does not retire a mechanism family. A valid negative remains a
negative for its artifact, data, operator, information, control, optimization
and budget. An invalid instrument cannot support the claim it failed to
measure. An execution failure or resource-stopped run is not automatically a
quality result. Losing a superiority comparison does not make a component
useless. An oracle-assisted success is a conditional result, not a deployed
capability.

Preserve the original evidence and the strongest surviving statement. Record
the next diagnosed step or explicit re-entry condition. D9 still rejects an
unchanged retry justified only by another seed, more dose or hope. There is no
requirement to run every preserved mechanism or restart its old engine.

## One work card, proportional checks

Use the existing owning issue. Before decision-bearing compute, record:

1. **Consumer and blocker:** the integrated deliverable, current observed
   failure, source/artifact identity and input/output boundary.
2. **Causal change:** what changes, why it addresses the failure and what stays
   fixed. Reuse valid binaries, checkpoints and relevant checks.
3. **Outcome decisions:** success, valid miss, invalid measurement and execution
   stop must have distinct consequences.
4. **Learning and information:** data/splits, labels, tokenizer, train/evaluation
   lengths, available memory, realized task load, objective/credit path,
   controls and informative checkpoints.
5. **Acceptance:** metrics with units and denominators, claim scope, matching
   conditions, seeds/draws and applicable decision rule.
6. **Complete cost:** preparation, review, build, data, training, evaluation,
   resumes, checkpoints and delivery; threads, RAM, new/temporary storage,
   reserve and shared resource ownership.

These are parts of one short card, not new dossiers. Apply only the checks
needed to resolve risks that could invalidate the current deliverable. A
serialization repair needs focused serialization evidence; it does not need a
learning-curve campaign. Existing valid evidence need not be regenerated when
its inputs and mechanism are unchanged. End validation when those risks are
resolved and advance the useful implementation.

## Validity before attributing a failure

**Instrument and data.** Verify the actual tokenizer, target format, units,
input/output allowance and population of gated cells. Report realized
association count and token distance rather than only requested settings.
Oracle/positive cases must be capable of exercising the output boundary.
Identify gold task dispatch, oracle values, reference prior answers and other
shortcuts: they may localize a defect but restrict the claim. Teacher wordings
need relation, argument, value and speech-act fidelity; exact-string overlap
screening does not validate meaning or create untouched qualification data.

**Information and representation.** Preserve the exact IDs, roles,
occurrences/versions and source spans the consumer requires. A lossy
observation declares what it discards. If two cases requiring different
answers have exactly the same complete observed representation, readout
tuning cannot separate them. Count such collisions before an expensive fit
when this is a concrete risk. Similar vectors or a weak linear probe do not
prove information loss. A frozen last-plus-mean probe measures that
representation/readout/fit combination, not an upper bound on the trunk's
mutual information or on what a trained compiler can learn.

**Learning credit.** Name the parameters that can influence candidate
admission, key formation, write act, value span and generation. Verify that
the relevant objectives reach them. Fitting a head over detached arrays is a
frozen-feature probe. A discrete store may remain nondifferentiable while
auxiliary losses train its interface; state which credit is auxiliary and
which actually comes from answer loss. Do not describe an answer objective as
training keys across a detached path. A cheap overfit check or a retained
positive learning witness can identify a broken pipeline when needed; neither
is a new universal prerequisite.

**Controls and attribution.** Match information, samples, candidate
opportunities and the useful output interface. Declare whether the question
holds total parameters, state bits, active bytes, candidate inspections,
training exposure or complete cost fixed, and report material differences in
the others. Those matching choices answer different questions. A post-hoc snap
does not test trained-in codes. A geometry-specific advantage needs an
ordinary or permuted-structure control with the relevant information and cost
matched. Product usefulness does not require a geometric superiority claim.

**Execution.** Bind results to source, configuration, tokenizer, data, seed,
learned artifact and executing path. Seals establish identity, not validity.
Deployment claims require the persisted artifact and its own decisions. An
audit of a non-pointer path does not qualify a new pointer, compiler or
sampler. A full scan followed by top-k selection is not bounded index access.

Failure of both geometric and ordinary positive controls leaves the
architecture comparison unresolved. It does not erase the practical failure
of both recipes or justify declaring either family impossible.

## Give learning a meaningful opportunity

Before a costly run, state why the dose can answer the question: relevant
class/operation exposure, reachable credit, initialization/optimization
assumptions, and measured or pilot throughput. Keep training and development
curves at the planned checkpoint opportunities, including critical categories.
A model that has not acquired training bindings has not yet tested their
transfer. A wall-time stop is not evidence of convergence.

For a fixed-budget comparison, match optimizer updates or examples and report
actual cost. For an attainable-quality or training-efficiency question, use a
declared learning-curve design instead; differing time to reach quality is
then part of the result. Size walls from measured rate under actual
concurrency, with room to checkpoint. Choose checkpoints and practical
improvement criteria from the task's variability and decision tolerance,
rather than an arbitrary timer or an unlimited fit.

Preserve an unfinished checkpoint and label a cap-stopped comparison as
execution-censored where appropriate. Continuation needs new causal evidence
about the unanswered implication—for example, an uncompleted curriculum
phase or observed acquisition that has not reached its declared exposure.
Repeatedly improving training loss while fixed transfer behavior stays flat
may instead call for a data, representation or objective change. More dose
alone is not a diagnosis.

Necessary local allowance extensions already authorized by the owner still
require the complete projection, reason, increment and updated cumulative
limit before use, including the storage stop margin. A completed immutable
card's explicit stop remains part of its record; any authorized successor is
versioned prospectively. No external/paid compute or destructive cleanup is
implied.

## Counts, uncertainty and D17 parity

Report numerators/denominators for gated cells and critical operations, plus
independent units: conversations, paraphrase families, entity/value
assignments, draws and training seeds. Repeating one wording with many values
does not create many independent paraphrase families. Use paired row outcomes
where possible. When uncertainty affects the decision, use an analysis that
respects shared templates/conversations; do not portray correlated repetitions
as independent evidence. No blanket significance-test campaign is required.

Retain **D17 parity rule v2 exactly** unless prospectively amended:

- Degradation is `loss_geometric - loss_ordinary`, or
  `accuracy_ordinary - accuracy_geometric`. Positive means geometry is worse.
- Freeze two paired seeds, or disjoint draws for a training-free comparison,
  the metric and tolerance. The default tolerance is **0.02 nats** or
  **0.03 accuracy**.
- If both differences are `<= tolerance`, retain geometry at this scope.
- If both are `> tolerance`, select ordinary at this scope and preserve
  geometry with its diagnosed result.
- If exactly one exceeds tolerance, take the one predeclared third paired
  seed/draw. Retain geometry if the mean of all three is `<= tolerance`;
  otherwise select ordinary at this scope. Equality belongs to retention.
- Nonfinite/unavailable observations are not automatic retain/replace results.
  Diagnose them under the work card.
- Claim advantage under this rule only if **every evaluated paired
  difference is strictly below `-tolerance`**. Retention is neither advantage
  nor whole-model promotion.

The prescribed second and conditional third pair belong to the declared
comparison, not a post-hoc retry campaign. This is a programme decision rule,
not a statistical proof of universal superiority.

Aggregate accuracy must not hide false writes, unsupported answers, missed
writes, wrong relations, wrong spans or incorrect abstentions. Report these
separately. Zero observed errors means zero in the tested population, not
zero population risk. Development remains available for design; obtain fresh
qualification evidence after selection. Preserve historical thresholds and
their original task definitions rather than relabelling an old miss as a pass.

## Immediate application: complete the semantic memory loop

The next integrated development artifact connects a **saved/reloaded
relation/act/value-span compiler**, an immutable input log with versioned
storage, and the learned emitter or a declared exact-span copy interface.
Begin with the current ten relations and a clearly scoped entity/history
contract. Unsupported people, relations, views, polarity and quoted statements
must not be silently coerced into supported positive facts about the user.

The [v11 result](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/issues/1552#issuecomment-5925661022)
remains a miss of its original E3 gate. R1 plus paraphrases scored
**1,737/2,098** on relations; lexical scored **1,756/2,098**. R1 queries were
correct on **364/595**, with **231/595** labelled assert. Updates were correct
on **5/313**, with **305/313** labelled assert. The frozen four-class act task
and an operational store-action task are related but different instruments.

Define a prospective operational alphabet from the actual consumer, such as
`Write(relation, span)`, `Read(relation, view)`, `NoWrite/NoAction` and explicit
unsupported/clarify. **Do not merge labels merely to improve accuracy.** Assert
and update may share an operation only if the supported store semantics make
that transformation valid. Keep correction/reassert/version distinctions
where they affect meaning. Preserve the old four-class scores as diagnostics.
Questions carrying a possible value must remain NoWrite.

**E4 development integration is allowed before E3 promotion.** A saved,
predicted-input integration can expose a store, span, emission or joint error
that an isolated probe cannot. This does not change E3's historical ≥0.90
relation and ≥0.95 act gate, declare it passed, or promote the resulting
artifact. Freeze the new integrated contract before its decision-bearing run.

The owning issue's acceptance should distinguish implementation from promotion:

- Persist/load the real heads/codes, tokenizer/input contract, label registry,
  version semantics and span convention; reject mismatched identities.
- Production execution receives transcript and its own state, without
  generator intent, gold category, oracle value or reference prior answers.
- The predicted actions/spans actually drive the store and output consumer.
  Queries/unsupported inputs do not mutate memory. Exercise question-with-value,
  supported updates and ambiguous/unsupported cases at the implemented scope.
- A fresh process reproduces the declared reads and deterministic outputs from
  the saved model/session under pinned settings. Bound address counts,
  per-address history, tombstones/eviction metadata and temporary memory.
- Reuse fixed E2/E3 rows for diagnosis; report actual integrated outputs,
  denominators and errors across phrasing/value axes. Include arbitrary
  multi-token spans and token/whitespace boundary cases.
- Use oracle actions only to localize defects. Attribute failures to admission,
  relation/act, span, version selection, emission and absence. Apply
  changed-source and NoRead/NoWrite controls to the causal claims being made.
- Compile/exercise the changed Rust path and loaded integration. Deliver a
  useful implementation or a scoped negative with its next causal repair;
  promote only under the frozen capability and serving conditions.

If oracle actions succeed and predicted actions fail, improve the compiler's
representation/data/credit. If correct actions fail, repair the actual
store/span/emitter boundary before more compiler training. If memory works but
general responses fail, improve the language path while preserving memory.
**Complete durable integration before requiring a new geometric advantage.**
The matched geometric comparison can proceed at the shared compiler/index
boundary without becoming a new prerequisite for finishing it.

## Preserve geometry's information and test its consumer

Keep exact entity/value/occurrence IDs separate from lossy learned geometry.
Retain role/order, polarity, magnitude/radius and Hopf fiber where the consumer
requires them; justify any discarded channel. Typed paired-H4/icosian witnesses
are not automatically independent learned lanes. An m-tuple of 2I roots offers
`120^m` combinations; multiplying it into one root restores only 120 states.

`q^-1 k` supplies common-left-frame invariance. Conjugacy-class scoring adds
right-action invariance and can erase direction. Directed multichannel
relative codes lowered to bounded tables are a plausible route, not an
established semantic advantage. Preserve exact IDs and trained-in hard
decisions when testing them. A shared orthogonal rotation preserves dot
rankings; a proposed improvement relying only on that transformation needs no
fit to reveal the no-effect result.

Credit geometry at a causal consumer: binding/generalization, admission,
stable state transition or selected learned-parameter access. Group closure,
an arbitrary prime assignment and a heatmap are not semantic evidence.
Ordinary/categorical or permuted controls retain the same relevant information
and cost. A useful neutral geometric primitive can remain without an advantage
claim.

Distinguish contextual state from learned parameter memory, and fixed from
growing storage. Exact recall of N arbitrary values from an M-symbol alphabet
under N known keys requires at least `N log2(M)` bits of distinguishable
state. A growing log pays this cost; it does not evade it. This bound neither
proves a small recurrent task impossible nor turns a many-lane additive state
into a 120-state automaton because its transport alphabet is 2I.

## D5 cost and energy have explicit units

Freeze the consumer, model artifact, token accounting and denominator before
claiming an access fraction. Report:

- Logical learned parameters consumed per generated token, divided by the
  declared relevant learned-parameter total, with the full-model figure beside
  a layer/bank-local fraction. Unused padding cannot improve the denominator.
- Distinct parameter/codebook/scale/index bytes touched and total traffic
  including repeat reads where measured. Packed codes and derived LUTs must
  not hide dense logical contributions that are still evaluated.
- Router, compiler, output head, sampler, table construction/amortization and
  contextual-log/value access. Parameter traffic and contextual traffic are
  separate counters; both belong to complete-path cost.
- Prefill/decode, cold/warm conditions, context/output lengths, useful-task
  quality, latency, memory and retained/temporary storage.

Rows/token, bytes/token and seconds/token are distinct. **J/token requires
measured energy** over a stated interval divided by its corresponding token
count, with device scope, power sampling and idle/baseline treatment recorded.
Distinguish absolute from incremental estimates. Joules per successfully
completed useful task is an additional measure; it is undefined when no task
succeeds. A byte/operation model is an estimate, not measured joules. Correct a
row-inspection field labelled J/token prospectively; its old value is not
energy evidence.

Measure useful selected access and include selection's own cost. A failed
post-hoc imitation of dense weights qualifies that selector/weight pair. A
jointly trained selector is a different causal premise requiring its own
consumer, card and resources, not an automatic expensive continuation.

## Re-entry and adversarial review

A historical re-entry note names the old artifact/result, exact failure,
surviving property, new consumer, changed causal premise, cheapest useful
discriminator, cost and next decisions. Valid premises include restored
magnitude/order, a consistent codebook, repaired credit, trained-in
discretization or a diagnosed data/optimization defect. Renaming a mechanism,
loosening an old threshold or unexplained extra dose is insufficient. Preserve
the original measurements and scope.

The compiler-first route also faces substantive objections:

- **Slot-filling ceiling:** ten relations establish an interface, not general
  language. Later milestones must test new compositions, broader language and
  relation acquisition on the same native path.
- **Teacher/rule dependence:** generated labels can drift, and fixture-specific
  filters can hide missing semantics. Keep provenance, check meaning and
  evaluate predicted actions on fresh phrasings.
- **Disconnected credit:** auxiliary heads may never improve actual answers.
  Integrate early, persist the consumer, intervene on sources/reads and inspect
  the actual gradient path.
- **Decorative geometry:** identify its causal contribution and matched
  comparison while allowing the durable interface to finish.
- **Unbounded exact memory:** bound all address/history/metadata growth, expose
  eviction and absence honestly, and distinguish a rejected candidate from an
  absent record.
- **Process replacing progress:** retain one short card, reuse valid checks and
  stop validation when the declared risks are resolved. The integrated artifact
  and useful behavior remain the deliverables.

Independent review should challenge the inference from evidence to decision,
not merely reproduce the author's preferred result. Review at the exact
delivery head, record substantive objections and their resolutions, and retain
protected delivery. Agreement among reviewers is not a model result.
