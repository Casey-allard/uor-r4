## Why the next step changes from copying to realization

`scoped_memory.rs::SessionAction::Emit` copies successive tokens from the owned payload. `competitive-reader.rs::local_logits` computes E(previous,current)+S(query(current)); it has no older-history dependency. These are useful components, but neither currently turns a consumed relation into flexible words.

A scalar copy boost changes one token's logit; it cannot change the odds between two uncopied tokens. A gate mixing frozen E/S with a pure pointer has the same limitation outside pointer support. Hence the next step needs an actual vocabulary interaction with owned evidence/derived state as well as learned Copy/Vocabulary/Stop selection. Exact copy progress remains separate and pinned. The same policy must serve ordinary and computed answers, with actual autoregressive output, source-separated text checks and prior lifecycle retention.

A finite context-indexed vocabulary table or repaired low-bit residual is a reasonable first implementation. DeepSeek may adapt the donor when a reduced development test shows the frozen prior prevents useful learning; retain the frozen comparator and count additional data/work. We should not recreate an endless series of scalar-confidence features or call a host answer wrapper prose generation.

[Pointer Sentinel](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07843), [CopyNet](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.06393v3) and [Pointer-Generator Networks](https://aclanthology.org/P17-1099/) support separating exact reproduction, vocabulary generation and repetition control. Their dense implementations are not adopted. The July2026 [spectral group-learning result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02993v2) is a conditional finite harmonic-feature lead, not a proof for our quantized language learner. [Sparse Delta Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07386) reinforces capacity/access separation, not a compliant replacement. Sources and concrete tests are expanded in the execution brief.

The retained E/S output map still visits524,288 coordinates at V4096,dv128, and the full120×4096 i32 S-row cache occupies1,966,080bytes. Multiplier-free arithmetic does not eliminate memory traffic. Whole-path D0-b, good sustained prose, general reasoning/coding and energy savings remain unqualified.
