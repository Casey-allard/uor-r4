## The next mechanism and its mathematical reason

The current 16-bucket controller observes a few exact-context/recency flags and one rank-margin bit. It does not observe the selected payload's local score and is fitted using a soft distribution over actions, then deployed as one deterministic action. Better source ranking has not removed its text harm. Directly fit a small **causal hard-action policy** for the frozen ungated top source, using actual finite-action costs and compact integer observations that can separate incompatible decisions.

For a selected payload with local probability `p`, action `a≥0`, and target indicator `I`, the reference one-logit loss change is

`Δ(a)=log(1+p·(exp(a)−1))−a·I`.

For each causal bucket, average these **individual** costs, with a declared fit mixture and shrinkage/fallback, then choose among NoRead and the existing finite strengths. Do not insert mean `p` into the nonlinear formula. An integer selected-payload/top-logit gap is a cheap candidate observation; it is not a calibrated probability. Offline training may use true probabilities; serving uses only declared integer comparisons/table actions. A raw token/family/domain label is not a substitute for causal utility evidence.

With an observed ranker frozen, this isolates integration from relearning source selection. A direct finite action table can have an explicit hard empirical objective without thousands of soft score updates or treating a two-sweep coordinate result as a global optimum. Match feature availability, table budget and fitting opportunity for H4 and categorical. Keep the old controller and NoRead as references. A zero-action policy that merely removes all text reads is harm containment, not transferable attention; preserve useful relational behavior and demonstrate actual beneficial reads on held-out language before that stronger claim.

The next task has a stopping interpretation: if one justified causal observation/objective repair cannot learn useful text influence, report whether the missing distinction is contextual source/query representation, training support, quantization or the local predictor. Do not launch endless controller refinements. A demonstrated contextual alias can move the next implementation into learned role/scope state; absent targets require generative operators and cannot be solved by widening a copy pool.

## Research and mathematical roadmap

[Pointer Sentinel Mixture Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07843) and [Pointer-Generator Networks](https://aclanthology.org/P17-1099/) distinguish copying available context from generating tokens. The transferable lesson is that pointer support is not the whole vocabulary, and read influence must be learned jointly with the decision to use local generation. Their dense LSTM/attention implementations are not adopted. The April 2026 revision of [Retrieval as a Decision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09803) motivates checking local confidence when deciding whether retrieval helps; its RAG thresholds and efficiency claims do not transfer to this token-level integer policy.

Recent geometry research remains relevant: [PaTH, revised February 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16381) makes sequence transforms data-dependent; [GRAPE, ICLR 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07805) formalizes relative, compositional group actions. These suggest finite learned causal transport as a later contextual-state mechanism, using our exact tables instead of importing their matrix kernels. The June 2026 [Lie-Algebra Attention preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20547) is another relation-kernel proposal, not evidence that its continuous log/norm score suits this task. Here, useful partner compatibility can favor the antipode −1, not closeness to identity. Geometry provides a relation algebra; learning determines useful compatibility.

The five-stage plan is unchanged in purpose but advances in implementation: **(1)** learned source access now has stronger retained evidence; its active task is useful text/absence influence. **(2)** Learn structural role/scope persistence and correspondence when causal aliases or eviction require it. **(3)** Reuse dependent-language scheduling and typed operators for dependent reads and derived outputs. **(4)** Integrate broader prose, conversation and executed Rust in one model. **(5)** qualify useful scale, delivery and complete energy/cost. Profile at every stage.

The owner's bridges remain available at specific seams: signed H4/Spin state and scalar utility are active; existing `source_routing.rs` and `relational_attention/runtime.rs` can inform contextual routing, `dependent_language/scheduling.rs` supplies a composition interface, and historical rule-based `contextual_role.rs` supplies distinctions but not a learned grammar claim. Retained Hopf fiber, explicit role banks, S7/paired-H4/E8 and harmonic coefficient memory are candidates for measured aliasing/retention limits. Orthogonality cannot decide whether a retrieved fact should affect the next word, and no additional sphere resolves a target absent from stored payloads. Preserve exact identity/version separately from geometric relations.
