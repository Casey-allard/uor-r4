# Geometric attention value-to-residual composition

This opt-in mechanism replaces the floating `read.out` projection in the
actual learned-value/NoRead StackModel path. It is part of geometric attention;
Claude's GroundedSession consumer and dialogue protocol work remain separate.
The final residual trunk, nonlinear layers, normalization and vocabulary head
remain floating. The unrestricted learned numerical coefficients in earlier
context/event/value/address potentials remain separate qualification work.

## Operator and its capacity

For each head, output quaternion lane and input quaternion lane, two terms
compute `q/4 * left * value * inverse(right)`. Both selectors are categorical
signed-H4 root identities admitted against the pinned exact group artifact.
The dimensionless numerical gains occupy signed four-bit codes -7 through7;
-8 is reserved and rejected. The action is a Spin(4) operator, not another
independently learned paired-H4/Galois companion or a distance over hash bits.

The operator consumes the existing two typed atoms in each of four input
lanes. It preserves radius, signed orientation and packet presence. It does not
classify a weighted mixture back into a root. Valid zero and cancellation do
not become absence. An occurrence is transformed once into32 coordinates per
head, then retains its original score, age and NoRead-inclusive denominator.
Two heads are summed only after their separate reductions.

There are128 terms:256 byte selectors and64 actually packed gain bytes, or
320 bank bytes before metadata/shared geometry. The hypothetical288-byte
seven-bit-selector packing is not implemented. The bank requires up to512 group
compositions and1024 canonical coordinate reads per occurrence before gains;
reducer width doubles. Actual running time and energy are not inferred from
these counts.

Two scaled rotations are a restricted family. For `M=aR+bS`, with proper
rotations R,S, `M^T M=(a²+b²)I+ab(U+U^T)`, `U=R^T S`. Its singular values occur
in equal pairs. Thus even continuous rotations cannot represent the generic
block `diag(1,2,3,4)`. Finite H4 selectors and q4 gains impose additional limits.
This is a native learned operator hypothesis, not a universal donor compiler.
A failed donor reconstruction cannot by itself retire geometric task learning.

Offline donor weights and operator responses remain permissible for declared
initialization/supervision/diagnosis. The16 left/right axis-quaternion actions
span real4×4 linear maps before coefficient quantization; that broader conversion
control has its own range/error and dense access costs. It is retained for
future weight compilation and is not silently substituted for this K2 bank.

## Numerical contract

The new composed payload is separately versioned signed i64 Q16. The legacy
reader remains i32 Q16 and retains its artifact contract. Each atom is moved
through exact signed group products and decoded with the existing canonical
Q16 codec. All q-weighted atom/input/term contributions are accumulated before
one nearest, ties-away quarter rescale. Normalization rounds independently for
each head; the final two-head sum is checked integer addition.

The maximum per-head coordinate is `M=28*2^30` over all admitted radius bins,
including radius2^14. At most128 occurrence weights, each at most2^31, give
`|numerator| <=28*2^68 <2^73`. The denominator, including NoRead, is positive
and below2^39. Checked i128 shift/add products and integer long division cover
these bounds. Normalization preserves `[-M,M]`; the two-head sum is below2^36
and fits i64. There is no clipping, radius change or target-selected scale.

The weights reuse the legacy reader's admitted exp/age/max arithmetic with a
zero scratch payload, followed by the new wide numerator/reduction. Equal
occurrence identities remain separate. Moving a linear map across rounded
reduction is not bit-exact algebra; source/reference versus compiled numerical
error is measured separately from donor capacity and answer quality.

## Offline learning and compilation

Forward selects the earliest maximum signed-H4 action and hard quarter-grid
gain, then uses the same native atom transformation and rounding. Backward uses
a declared conditional120-way categorical surrogate per selector, holding the
other selector at its actual hard choice. Credit uses the full transformed
value adjoint, including both atoms, radius and gain. It is a biased surrogate,
not differentiation of argmax or tangent-only credit. Gain shadows use an
identity STE with explicit bounded projection.

The fixed initialization gives the two terms distinct actions and nonzero
opposite quarter gains, independent of held-out answer scores. Saved source
binds exact parameter bytes, policy and admitted geometry. Native reload
independently recompiles the source and validates bank bytes, width/precision,
tokenizer and the actual context/event/span/value/potential/NoRead/reducer
identities. A model-source recompile does not authorize runtime donor access.
