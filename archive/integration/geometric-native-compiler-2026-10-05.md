# Native turn compilation: learning works, transfer fails

The native compiler reuses the existing `TurnCompiler`, labelled `Example`,
original byte-span and exact-store interfaces. Its learned readout uses the
retained R64 signed-H4 encoder and packed Q4 tables. This is a component and
integration experiment, not useful geometric chat or a geometry-family verdict.

## Implemented path

`NativeTurnHead` admits class/slot tables over all120 signed roots; prediction
uses checked integer additions and table reads. Offline Rust learning uses
ordinary CE, quarter-Q4 forward values, an STE and Adam shadows. Act and shared
relation heads see the final turn's eight roots. The span head sees word-local,
turn-relative and prefix-before/after-relative roots. Original UTF8 byte spans
and tokenizer content identity are retained. Four acts are assert, correction,
current query and unresolved; relation labels are job/home/none. Historical views
and general language generation are not implemented by this compiler.

The source-only adapter receives original text, not a supplied address, span or
answer record. Predicted spans drive actual writes, predicted addresses drive
actual reads, and each written record keeps its original statement as a cue.
Store history is serialized/reloaded after every write. The native consumer runs
own-prefix Copy/Period/Stop with the existing cue48, prefix64 and refined terminal
donors. Selected-record admission and all-current-record admission are reported
separately. The latter retains distractors in actual writing-event order.

The table scorer is a bounded numerical kernel, not a complete serving audit:
feature extraction and adapter allocations remain explicit, no instruction or
whole-path energy claim is made, and no shared GroundedSession seam changed.

## Curriculum and verdict corrections

The [posted a–e audit](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/issues/820#issuecomment-5989197931)
confirms the original radial64 training rows all supplied one answer-bearing
record. Later128-row bank training is64 single-source preservation and64
multi-source episodes, with equal episode weighting and balanced visits. Later
fresh panels are32/32 banks, without gold-record filtering. Those banks still
provide authored role cues and extracted value boundaries; their results retain
that structured-bank scope. Target prefixes are offline supervision; own-prefix
generation supplies no target tokens.

The current head fit is deterministic on one frozen carrier. Panel draw seeds
are not independent learning seeds. No family verdict follows from these rows;
the requested benchmark ladder is at least three genuine learning seeds, and
chat verdicts require at least two independent chat lineages. Incomplete,
mismatched or failed runs cannot count as seeds. Two audit compile defects were
repaired and charged; they are not model-quality negatives. Different fresh
panels across experiments are never pooled into one improvement rate.

## Next causal implementation

Preserve all compiler and reader parents. Replace endpoint-only act/relation
observations with actual ordered word occurrences: keep local signed-H4 roots,
actual predecessor roots/presence and exact `inverse(previous)*current` transport
separately. Retain original occurrence/byte identity and train on existing
labelled paraphrase knowledge; do not remove value words using gold spans, sum
root indices or assume geometric naming grants semantic invariance. Expose
nearby source-word frame information to span prediction too, rather than relying
on value roots and a value-sensitive whole-turn observation. Compare
against the retained endpoint head and a same-input ordered-product control;
report interaction, coverage, parameter and operation differences honestly.

Separately align reader learning and evaluation with original statement cues,
natural questions, gaps, corrections and distracting resident records. The
reference compiler is only an instrumentation arm; final qualification must use
predicted compilation. Shared `mqar-bench layout=fact` can measure the admitted
routing arm, while tiered conversation panels test language-to-memory behavior.
Bind tokenizer/casing/reply conventions to deployment and freeze a genuinely new
excluded panel after design selection. Neither another unchanged endpoint dose
nor retirement of H4/Spin/prime routing follows from this result.
