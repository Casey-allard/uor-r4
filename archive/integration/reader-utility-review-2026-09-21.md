At lines 3890–3898 it nevertheless creates both construction and text fit events from the unchanged frozen `base` selector. `policy_event` calls that selector's `policy_bucket` at line 3495. Legacy v3 artifacts initialize thresholds to `[0,0,0]` (`learner/relational.rs:1613` onward). By definition,

`gap = local_logit(payload) − max_v local_logit(v) <= 0`.

The bucket counts strict comparisons `gap > threshold`. Every fit event therefore uses gap band zero. The saved support is entirely in buckets 0–7; buckets 8–31 receive no fit events. Serving uses the new negative cutoffs and can address all four bands. Twenty-four entries are untrained because of the mismatch; the statement that 28 fallback entries prove inadequate contextual observations is invalid. The library's weighted finite-policy fitter implements its declared mean-cost and support rule. The orchestration supplies incorrectly identified events. ## Mathematical interpretation and research connections

For fixed selected payload probability p, actual finite action cost is `log(1+p*(exp(a)-1)) − a*I[payload=target]`; average individual costs with declared weights. A finite empirical weighted-CE minimizer is not a guarantee of emitted-argmax accuracy or preservation of each stratum. A small boost may improve probability without making the payload top-ranked. [Gneiting and Raftery's proper-scoring treatment](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf) concerns predictive distributions, not automatic equivalence to every decision utility.

After consistent indexing, distinguish support failure, selected-objective tradeoff and missing causal information. Save per-bucket/per-action CE and emitted-correct counts for development strata. Since sources/actions are fixed, those are additive over examples; a bounded development-only feasibility/Pareto comparison can ask whether another policy on the same observations would preserve both endpoints. Do not call mixed labels a representation collision: stochastic predictions naturally have mixed outcomes.

Even correctly indexed gap is not probability: `p_payload = exp(gap)/sum_v exp(z_v-z_max)`. Equal gaps can hide different normalization mass; a one-bit source margin also discards relation identity and absolute confidence. These are alternatives to persistent syntax state if a later diagnostic establishes missing information. Offline probability summaries do not authorize floating serving.

[TensorFlow Transform's primary documentation](https://www.tensorflow.org/tfx/transform/get_started) describes reusing a fitted transformation with fixed learned constants in serving. The applicable lesson is one versioned feature transform; no TensorFlow/Python model dependency is adopted. [When and How to Rely on Retrieval](https://aclanthology.org/2022.findings-emnlp.218/) motivates quality-dependent influence. [kNN-LM Does Not Improve Open-ended Text Generation](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.929/) demonstrates why predictive-loss improvements and generated quality need separate evidence; its result does not predict UOR's result.

The latest checked [PaTH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16381) and [GRAPE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07805) sources support future content-dependent transport and compositional relative actions. Their dense implementations and benchmark claims are not adopted. In UOR, signed H4 compatibility and scalar influence already have concrete roles. Prime/ordered-n-let identity addresses records; fixed zeta phases can label declared channels; neither supplies a semantic metric by itself. A witnessed older-context distinction can motivate a small learned H4/shared typed transition with exact occurrence versions and explicit lifetime. Hopf projection requires retained fiber to preserve lost information; S7/E8 or harmonic state require equal-resource comparisons for the missing distinction or retention problem. A lone 120-state product has only about 6.9 bits and must not replace ordered evidence without testing its collisions. Existing authored contextual-role grammars are learning-target donors, not learned BPE syntax.

No newly checked mathematics makes an incorrectly addressed training table evidence for a richer state. Repairing that experiment is currently the highest-information next action. If its corrected development result identifies a real omitted contextual distinction, DeepSeek may propose one bounded causal observation or learned relation-state comparison before final evaluation; it must preserve matched ordinary-code controls and explain what information that state adds.
