## Why isolate parameter families

Earlier readout-only fits optimized Copy/Period/Stop against unchanged geometric context. Copy consumes directed relative H4 observations; Period/Stop consume the current geometric state and categories. A changed basis can disrupt a fitted decoder. The source contains no singer/dancer/Klot-specific answer branch. The fixed action vocabulary and protocol2 selected-source view are component scaffolding, not a general language model.

Preservation is evidence about stability, not a veto against a more useful geometric mechanism. The regression could reflect representation/readout incompatibility, narrow-panel fitting, inadequate joint adaptation or unhelpful surrogate credit. A saved-artifact transplant can establish component sufficiency and interactions; it cannot by itself decide all those explanations.
