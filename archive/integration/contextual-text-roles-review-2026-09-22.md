## Mathematical bottleneck: the correct output is outside current support

The scorer is an additive sparse categorical model:

\[s_r(x,I)=\sum_{f\in\phi(x,I)}w_{f,r}.\]

Distinct first/last/left/right feature slots are richer than the old bag, but they do not encode all order. Candidate interior tokens are omitted, so equal endpoints, length and surrounding two-token neighborhoods collide even if interiors differ. This is a concrete limit, not a claim that every finite geometric encoding avoids collisions.

More fundamentally, `locate_marker` begins candidates after at least one token. `observe_clause` takes the entire prefix as subject, the entire suffix as object and declares a question when the suffix is empty. `Cedar Annex is Mara's office`, `Where does Mara work?`, or a trailing adjunct outside the answer cannot receive the required independent spans under that representation. More fitting, wider scores or another role classifier cannot recover a candidate omitted from support.

The next model should choose a bounded legal structured observation containing subject/query span, object span when present, goal and action. Candidate proposals may remain generic and bounded, but a fixed semantic parser must not supply the primary labels. Predict actual argument spans, not just the middle marker. Use supported span tuples or a suitable segment formulation; do not begin an unrestricted parser project.

## A concrete geometric bridge and a compatible learning method

For associative finite H4-compatible group elements, prepare ordered prefixes

\[P_j=g(x_1)\cdots g(x_j),\qquad P_i^{-1}P_j=g(x_{i+1})\cdots g(x_j).\]

After one ordered prefix pass, inverse/product tables recover a candidate interval product. This is an exact identity and a useful computational candidate; it is not an injective language representation or a semantic theorem. Role landmarks, request context and source-relative relations may supply additional learned potentials. Inspect `native_geometric/source_routing.rs::encode/score` and its actual two-lane/full-relative implementation before adapting it. Preserve exact bytes, occurrence identity and payload outside the compressed state, and count table/code traffic and preparation, not just one lookup.

A compatible structured decision is bounded argmax over legal span assignments. Where a segment model fits, max-plus recurrence combines span scores and role transitions with add/compare/table operations. Rust offline training may use structured perceptron, margins, gradients or other justified supervision. Serving compliance depends on actual learned weight bounds and implementation; an additive formula does not itself prove whole-path D0-b.

The current categorical model is the natural baseline, adapted to the same richer support and observations. Test geometry on the distinction or preparation/cost advantage it might supply. A quality tie can retain a useful interface; a weak control is not evidence of geometric necessity. Primary-source [semi-Markov segmentation](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2004/hash/eb06b9db06012a7a4179b8f3cb5384d3-Abstract.html) and [semantic-role span selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.02245) motivate span-level decisions, not importing their complete model implementations. ## Exact surface and lexical identity are different objects

The leading-space convention was a useful disclosed workaround. It did not solve identity across tokenization contexts. Retain raw document bytes and exact selected byte/token extents for output/provenance; separately derive a declared lexical identity excluding exterior separators while preserving case and internal bytes for this task. Do not lowercase or merge different names silently, and do not turn a deterministic key into semantic distance or learned coreference.

Use the pinned Rust tokenizer's `decode_bytes` and `token_byte_lengths` with original-byte validation. Per-token lossy Unicode decoding is insufficient for exact offsets. [Tokenizer encoding offsets](https://huggingface.co/docs/tokenizers/v0.20.3/en/api/encoding) distinguish token IDs from original surface extents. BPE boundaries must not determine whether the same exact lexical name can be joined across sentence positions. Wider alias/coreference learning remains separate.
