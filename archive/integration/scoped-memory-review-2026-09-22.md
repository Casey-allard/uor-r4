## Owner's topological-folding proposal, mathematically reconciled

During this review the owner identified `exp(i*pi) + pi^0 = 0^0` as a motivating proposal for geometry-dependent zero/one behavior. Under ordinary complex arithmetic the left side is exactly zero; `pi^0` adds no variable. Defining `0^0=1` makes the equality false, leaving it undefined does not produce a state, and defining it as zero merely makes this instance true by convention. An indeterminate limit form is not quantum superposition. Preserve the owner's motivation, not the equation as a theorem. [Standard exponential/power definitions](https://dlmf.nist.gov/4.2).

A well-defined phase readout is `P(theta)=|(1+exp(i*theta))/2|^2=(1+cos(theta))/2`, giving1 at0 and0 atpi. It has a quantum-interference interpretation only with relative phase and the corresponding physical preparation/measurement assumptions; global phase alone is unobservable. [IBM phase/interference](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/modules/quantum-mechanics/superposition-with-qiskit). In this classical finite implementation use a retained sign `s in {+1,-1}` and a two-entry readout `(1+s)/2`; no served transcendental calculation or quantum capability follows.

The map `z -> z^2` identifies opposite circle points; `S3 -> SO(3)` similarly identifies `q` and `-q`. This is a precise projection/lift connection, not a zero-exponent ambiguity. [Double-cover topology](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/lie/node8.html). A global continuous choice of lift is not supplied by attaching an informal sign to arbitrary rotations; the finite signed Q8 representation already retains the needed distinction directly. The next order-sensitive consumed-result test is the appropriate first experiment. Call **topological folding** a motivating projection-and-retained-phase hypothesis, not a modified Euler identity or measured quantum behavior.
