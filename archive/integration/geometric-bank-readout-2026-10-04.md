## Next attention seam

Do not repeat an unchanged readout dose. Review initial same-bank query binding
and own-prefix behavior to distinguish ranking from terminal competition. A
constructive successor is a stable text-derived cue carrier attached to actual
source occurrences, with a separately encoded query-only intent carrier and a
learned directed H4 interaction. Existing global replay, keys, controllers,
occurrence identity and alias normalization must remain. Zero new coefficients
must reproduce the old trace; role/entity/relation/event metadata remain opaque.

This is a conditional design, not an implemented result or diagnosed collision.
Define exact cue lifetime through caller ordering and preserve absence, radius,
polarity and repeated-occurrence behavior. A matched control must retain the same
cue information and access cost while removing the joint interaction. Retain
both useful frozen readout64 and mutable-context48 behavior; geometry learning
