## Mathematical and architectural synthesis

The signed Q8 computation is the first concrete consumed instance of the owner's projection/retained-phase intuition. With one declared left-action convention, order matters: `ij=k`, `ji=-k`, while observed i then j acts as `j*i*s`. Quotienting by the central sign identifies outputs required to select different records. This establishes a necessary distinction on the constructed task; an ordinary eight-state transition table can preserve it equally well. It is not proof of unique geometric efficiency, physical quantum computation or general semantic attention.

The proposed equation `exp(i*pi)+pi^0=0^0` remains unadopted: its left side is zero in ordinary arithmetic; the right side is convention-dependent/undefined in different contexts, not a geometry-dependent quantum switch. The useful research object is an explicitly defined projection and retained lift, with a task that requires the lost information. Hopf observation with retained fiber, signed H4 transport and typed paired-H4/icosian E8/S7 remain available on those terms. Orthogonal harmonic coefficients can supply a structured summary, but learning channel assignments and handling finite capacity/collisions are separate problems. They cannot recover omitted source roles or temporal intent automatically. Twin primes, scalar fields and supersymmetry currently supply no evaluated operator that supersedes this missing learned interface.

The exact memory path implements authoritative evidence; geometric state implements an ordered reusable transformation; learned observation/control connects language to them. The next experiment should connect those responsibilities across varying source paths and relevant corrections. It need not force irreversible observation/correction/emission into a reversible group law.

The following E/S donor is a local lexical scorer: frozen E plus S query-row terms with no older-history fold. Equal recent tails have equal local logits. Historical generations remain repetitive. E/S can become a vocabulary emission option after the combined lifecycle works, with learned contextual influence and source-separated text/complete responses. Attaching that scorer alone is not broad language. Preserve the earlier negative evidence from overly strong copy boosts.

The roadmap is therefore: corrected scoped memory + consumed computation → one learned observed source/control/lifecycle bundle → E/S contextual lexical emission and broader prose/conversation → executed Rust through the same path → complete artifact/API/WASM/Studio and quality-matched laptop cost/energy qualification. Profile costs throughout, and introduce richer geometry only against a witnessed representation or cost obstruction.

## Primary research and its limits

[Sequential Group Composition](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.03655v2) supports shared sequential algebraic operations and constructive recurrent solutions. Existence is distinct from optimization convergence or language capability. We retain the solved finite action rather than re-fit its arithmetic.

[From Reasoning Traces to Reusable Modules](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18089v2) separates local skills from routing and requires witnessed interfaces in its compositional result. This motivates training mixed source/operation/read traces. Its theoretical and SFT/RL setting does not mandate RL or dense inference here. [Neural Programmer-Interpreters](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06279) is relevant precedent for learned control and reusable domain operations; its neural architecture is not adopted.

[LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813) distinguishes updating, temporal reasoning and other long-term memory abilities. We use that responsibility distinction, without importing its scores or treating an authored panel as its benchmark. [Pointer Sentinel Mixture Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07843) motivates the later separation of contextual reproduction and vocabulary emission; no softmax/recurrent implementation is adopted.
