## Mathematical reason to change the observation model

The current marker score sums nonnegative token-role votes over candidate intervals. Adding another voting token cannot decrease that score. A familiar cue inside an entity or answer can therefore extend the selected marker into that content. The role score is a commutative sum, so permuting the same marker tokens preserves it. The edge model assigns one sign to each token globally, so the same BPE token cannot be retained as name content in one occurrence and treated as syntax in another when those decisions require context.

These are information losses, not merely a weak optimizer. If two causal observations map to the same representation but require different decisions, every deterministic downstream decoder of that representation must make the same decision on both. More training, more global vote counts or a later stronger ranker cannot recover what candidate construction or representation discarded.

The constructive alternative is to preserve proposed exact spans and encode their ordered local context, role hypotheses and current request goal before selection. The geometry can supply shared ordered composition and relative comparisons; exact identity and the complete payload stay outside the compressed descriptor. Learned role/action decisions remain typed nonlinear operations where the problem is not invertible.

Do not overinterpret group notation. A fixed test `a^-1*z=c` is equivalent to `z=a*c`, a paired categorical correspondence; the historical central-minus-identity case and corrected identity case are both of this form. Noncommutative transport becomes relevant only if the model uses a fuller ordered relation and the task actually requires that distinction. Exact world facts and redirects are not automatically bijective group actions.

## One complete ordinary-text task

Make the same entity occur in different grammatical positions, and include genuine multiword names and answers sharing words or suffixes. Include at least a small set where a token used as a cue or filler elsewhere is also meaningful entity/answer content. The task should require all of the following through the same served path:

- The same exact question/entity has a direct answer, a two-read answer and a three-read answer in different worlds. Development exposes the primitive actions; final evaluation includes a familiar longer composition and withheld combinations after design selection. Question length, relation ID or fixture family cannot supply depth.
- A terminal answer may also occur as a subject/key elsewhere. Membership in an entity list does not determine Continue versus Emit. A redirect changes the entity while preserving the goal; a changed question goal can require a different source from the same world.
- Shared vocabulary and modest changes in source/question structure require contextual roles and span boundaries. New names or new disjoint synonyms alone do not establish this. Do not require inference of a wholly unseen primitive meaning with no training signal and then call the failure a compositional defect.
- Multiple plausible records compete. Candidate support must admit the full correct entity and answer spans without gold roles, spans or answer-dependent proposals. Unknown/missing evidence and cycles/exhaustion have explicit non-success outcomes.
- The selected answer is emitted completely, with spaces and all subword tokens intact, followed by the declared EOS/terminal action. A display wrapper may format the answer, but cannot author its content or substitute for output scoring.

Declared document/newline boundaries and bounded contiguous-span proposals are acceptable scaffolding. Name them, measure their cost and do not call them learned segmentation. A fixed semantic parser or a supplied gold subject/role/answer registry is an informative comparator, not the learned primary system. Avoid a serving entity dictionary that secretly supplies the span and grammatical role being claimed. Exact identity over candidate spans is allowed; hash or prime identities are not semantic similarity metrics.

Choose modest, meaningful structural diversity. This is not a request to solve all grammar or to spend the run assembling a general benchmark. It is a request to remove the specific marker/position shortcut while retaining a complete useful memory answer path.

## Representation, supervision and output: make the three choices explicit

First establish **support**. Define the bounded source/request span candidates, their exact occurrence/segment/offset/version identity and their accessible ordered observations. Then choose the **learned representation and supervision**. Adapt feature extraction to a proposed occurrence, its neighbors/order and the current request. Train the descriptors/scorer needed for actual role and span selection, rather than wrapping a fixed parser in a geometric code. A contextual low-bit finite table, learned ordered categorical state or a factorized goal/action representation is a reasonable alternative or comparator. If a proposed H4 encoding has a concrete collision, diagnose and repair it or choose the smaller adequate representation; do not run a width/seed sweep to conceal a missing observation.

Offline gold span/role/goal/action labels are allowed training targets. They must never become serving fields, precomputed source pointers, correct-candidate flags, hidden teacher states or gold next-hop counts. Use the same causal feature extractor during fitting and serving. Stage supervised span/role learning and action learning if that gives clearer credit, then use complete-output credit where useful; no requirement to force a fragile joint optimizer. Reject malformed training labels instead of silently clamping or skipping them. Report which values actually learned, their initialization and same-unit pre/post results; show actual initial/final whole answers where an end-to-end learning claim is made.

Finally preserve **output and execution**. Exact selected span copying is sufficient for this milestone; do not add a new open-ended decoder merely to replace a working emitter. Complete phrase selection must be learned from observed evidence. Question observation, candidate ranking, exact capture, goal/entity update, Continue/Emit choice and emission all execute through the corrected loaded runtime. Bind any new feature vocabulary, learned tables, geometry conventions and format version to the artifact. Independently reload every evaluated learned arm; stale mode maps or a clone patched after load are not export parity.

For D0-b, offline Rust learning may use floating point, gradients and matrix multiplication. Final numerical serving must use the declared bounded geometric/low-bit integer, addition/subtraction/shift/table operations, including the at-most-four-bit weight restriction where additive maps are used. No floating/transcendental numerical kernel, multiplier-based learned map, dense transformer hidden behind tables or runtime provider response is adopted. Distinguish tokenization/setup, experimental orchestration and the declared numerical kernel when measuring operations; do not infer whole-path conformance from a small model table. The project remains on a coherent geometric-attention path, provided these components become one learned system rather than an expanding collection of isolated demonstrations:

1. **This milestone:** contextual occurrence roles and full spans from ordinary text, exact source selection and complete evidence-dependent answers in the corrected loaded runtime.
2. **Then:** multiple active entities/scopes, corrections and current/previous information, required evidence versus permissible replacement, and grounded computed results through the same read/compute/emit interface. 3. **Broader language:** source-separated prose/conversation and meaningful generated Rust that is executed. Integrate the retained local language prior with the learned contextual path; simply loading E/S assets or adding an answer wrapper does not do this.
4. **Representation and scale when a measured need appears:** structural role/scope banks for interference and lifetime; full relative H4/Spin and retained Hopf fiber for demonstrated order/frame distinctions; pages or summaries for measured admission/cost limits. Paired-H4/icosian E8, S7 and harmonic coefficient memory remain available to replace or improve a smaller mechanism when they preserve an additional needed distinction or offer an equal-resource advantage.
5. **Qualification:** one actual artifact and CLI/API/session path demonstrates useful novel behavior and complete M1 latency, RAM, memory traffic and eventually measured energy. API/WASM/Studio delivery and alpha acceptance follow actual capabilities, not a geometry theorem or a compatibility status.

Keep exact identity separate from geometry. A normalized E8 root lies on S7, but the finite root set is not a bank of mutually orthogonal semantic registers. A point on a sphere and a field with separately stored harmonic coefficients have different capacities and costs. Harmonic orthogonality does not learn roles, eliminate finite-precision/collision limits or make query extraction free. Structural banks are a concrete interpretation of the owner's long-range role-memory idea; test their benefit when concurrent roles or interference require them.

Hopf observation loses fiber unless retained. Signed finite spin/group actions can execute through tables while preserving orientation. The paired-H4/icosian companion is the concrete golden/Galois-coupled construction, not arbitrary independent learned state. Scalar compatibility and salience fields are legitimate mathematical tools; physical quantum, supersymmetry or twin-prime language supplies no semantic guarantee. There is no mandatory dimensional ladder and no requirement to inject every bridge into this run. Explain any proposed replacement from the information it preserves and its measured cost/benefit.
