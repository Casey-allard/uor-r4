# Mathematical foundations for geometric attention

Research date: 2026-09-13. Scope: primary mathematics, the actual UOR implementations named below, and a constructive hypothesis. This is a source review; no fit, model run, proof build, or computational experiment was performed. It is not an assertion that every mathematical source on the computer or internet has been read. The inventory records inspected sections and unavailable material. Repository source citations use the active shared-geometric-core worktree; preserved research uses the original checkout because those research trees are not present in the isolated worktree.

## Main finding

The strongest mathematical synthesis is **learned, causal relational geometry with explicit transport and exact object identity**. Hamming signatures can make geometric comparison cheap. Finite H4 group elements can retain the orientation and composition information that a scalar score discards. Fixed zeta phases can provide reproducible multiscale coordinates. Canonical object/version identity can preserve payload and provenance across reads. None of these individually defines linguistic relevance: the missing bridge is learning contextual query, key, transport/update and emission behavior together, with sufficient retained relational state and a usable learning signal.

The owner's proposal—more zeta channels, prime and semiprime objects, many relative positions, cached canonical relations and the coupled H4/E8 construction—is a coherent **architecture hypothesis** when those components receive explicit computational roles. Its strongest form is not one universal scalar distance. It is a typed relation graph whose edges retain relative transformations and whose query-dependent scores select a bounded sequence of meaningful state transformations.

## 1. Hamming, angle and exact position are different objects

For bit strings of length m, Hamming distance is popcount(a XOR b). If each bit is mapped to ±1, the algebraic identity is u·v = m − 2 d_H(u,v). This is an exact identity for the binary representation; it is not a proof that the original manifold's metric is preserved.

Charikar's random-hyperplane construction gives disagreement probability θ/π for two fixed vectors separated by angle θ, when the hyperplane normal has the prescribed rotationally invariant distribution. Plan–Vershynin give uniform additive-distortion bounds under explicit random construction and sample-size assumptions. They do not make a finite bit map injective on a continuous sphere. Their sphere metric is normalized geodesic distance; the abstract's looser Euclidean wording must not replace the theorem. [Charikar §3](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/spr04/cos598B/bib/CharikarEstim.pdf), [Plan–Vershynin §§1.1–1.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.4452).

**Actual UOR code.** [BoundGeometry::canonical](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/addressed_attention/artifact.rs#L101) sets bit a for root q when the real-coordinate rank of q a⁻¹ is strictly positive. These are 120 fixed H4 landmark predicates. Zero projections become zero bits. [Metric::root_distance](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/hamming_refinement/metric.rs#L56) XOR/popcounts signatures; [Metric::distance](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/hamming_refinement/metric.rs#L69) adds two lane distances. Zeta zeros are not arguments to either metric.

The preserved primitive census found 120 unique signatures, all of weight 45, and no single-root ranking reversals relative to the current angular-rank table. That is a finite-codebook result recorded by the parent task, not a new run here. It does not establish random-hyperplane error bounds, angle reconstruction, two-lane sum equivalence, or semantic adequacy. In particular, code weight alone has no discriminatory information here. A bit string retains the identities of separating landmarks; its popcount distance does not.

**Counterexamples derived here.** A query q=(1,0,0,0) has the same angle to k₁=(0,1,0,0) and k₂=(0,0,1,0), although they represent different directions. In two independent lanes, candidates at distance tuples (0,a) and (a,0) tie under an equal-weight sum even when the task needs lane identity. Any finite hemisphere code partitions a continuous sphere into cells: distinct interior points in the same cell have Hamming distance zero. More landmarks refine the cells but do not create exact continuous positioning.

**Constructive role.** Use Hamming as a bounded candidate-ranking operation and as a diagnostic of representation changes. Keep the selected root IDs, per-lane disagreements, full selected payload, and relative group elements for the update. Query-conditioned masks or a learned Boolean comparison circuit may define an anisotropic compatibility function without mathematical matrix products. Such a function must be named a learned score unless metric axioms have been checked; query-dependent weights do not automatically preserve symmetry or triangle inequality.

## 2. All pairwise angles, triangulation and chirality

The concern about too few relationships is mathematically well founded. For vectors arranged as columns of X, their Gram matrix G=XᵀX contains every pairwise inner product. If two spanning configurations have the same Gram matrix, they differ by an orthogonal transformation. This basic reconstruction fact means pairwise angles and lengths can recover relative geometry **up to O(d)**. It also exposes what they cannot recover: absolute frame and the distinction between a configuration and its mirror image. On unit vectors, angles supply G, but not radii for non-unit vectors. A signed determinant or oriented group/frame data distinguishes chirality. This paragraph is an elementary linear-algebra derivation, not an empirical result.

Triangulation has several meanings. Distances to a sufficiently nondegenerate reference set can localize a point; a simplicial mesh supports differential operators; all-pairs distances are neither automatically a triangulation nor a manifold. A topology, local-neighborhood rule and metric consistency are needed to claim a reconstructed manifold. Degenerate reference vectors make localization ambiguous or ill-conditioned. If four known R4 reference vectors form a basis, four exact inner products determine coordinates relative to that basis; four threshold bits do not.

For this project, derive a **relation packet** from available exact state: ordered source and target IDs; selected root pairs; relative elements q⁻¹k (or the declared opposite convention); signed orientation/fiber; phase differences; payload type/version/occurrence; and a bounded score. A full 4×4 root-relation block is a reasonable research candidate where all four lanes have distinct roles. Retain it until the update makes a decision instead of collapsing it prematurely to one number. This is a proposal, not implemented capability.

All-pairs relation storage can be useful as an offline audit or a small current-context cache. At N=256, unordered pairs number 32,640; a 16-byte relation payload alone is 522,240 bytes. At N=100,000, it is 4,999,950,000 pairs and 79,999,200,000 payload bytes, before identities, indices or versions. A bounded degree-16 directed graph at that latter size instead has 1.6 million edges and 25.6 MB of 16-byte payloads. These are explicit arithmetic projections, not measured runtime. Canonical deduplication can share repeated immutable relations; it cannot remove the combinatorics of genuinely distinct pairs or changing contextual states.

## 3. Manifolds, tangent spaces, transport, gauge and holonomy

A point, a tangent vector at that point, a local frame, and a transformation are different types. Comparing tangent-valued messages at different points requires a transport convention. Parallel transport on a curved manifold can depend on the path; a global frame change should not accidentally change a scalar prediction. Gauge equivariance expresses how coordinates and operations transform consistently when local frames change. It does not imply every representation should be invariant: an invariant representation may erase directional information needed by the task. [Cohen et al., §§2–3](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/cohen19d/cohen19d.pdf).

Crane's discrete differential geometry notes implement this discipline with oriented edges, connection angles, parallel transport and loop holonomy. They also distinguish an angle-only direction field from a full vector field with magnitude. Their discrete differential/exterior calculus is a real operator framework, but the course's Poisson solvers and matrix operators are not permitted UOR serving implementations by default. [Crane, §8.3 and chapters 4/6](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/DDG/paper.pdf).

Singer–Wu provide a particularly relevant donor: vector diffusion maps distinguish scalar affinities from the transformations aligning local frames. Their connection-Laplacian operator transports neighboring vectors and averages them. The method's actual implementation uses local PCA/SVD and block matrix operations, so importing it unchanged violates the serving target. Its transferable principle is to store **relationship plus alignment**, not affinity alone. [Singer–Wu §§2–3, equations 2.4 and 3.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1102.0075).

**Actual UOR source.** [HELM-D frame encoding](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/helm_d_r4_attention.rs#L471) and [transport_local_block](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/helm_d_r4_attention.rs#L637) implement F_queryᵀ F_source transport and matrix-vector actions. This is an existing offline/reference donor, not a legal final serving path. [BoundGeometry::product](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/addressed_attention/artifact.rs#L140) supplies the inexpensive finite-group alternative.

**Proposed lowering.** If messages and frames both inhabit a closed finite H4 domain, relative frame and transported message are finite composition/inverse lookups. The 120×120 table has 14,400 entries, 28,800 bytes at u16. That preserves an actual group law. It is not equivalent to transporting arbitrary real-valued vectors. Curved-manifold parallel transport may require retained path/edge connection data; simply assigning g_i⁻¹g_j gives a pure frame-change construction with telescoping loop products, not a measured nontrivial curvature model.

## 4. S3, Hopf observation and the coupled H4/icosian construction

Unit quaternions describe S3 and compose associatively; they double-cover SO(3). H4's 120 unit roots form the binary icosahedral group under the chosen quaternion realization. H4 as a root system, that order-120 group and the full H4 reflection group are related but not identical objects. Dechant explicitly derives the 120-spinor H4 construction and an E8 construction using a second copy and a **reduced inner product**. The change of inner product matters: E8 is not literally an isometric 8D Euclidean space squeezed losslessly into ordinary R4. [Dechant, §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.04805).

The code's [fixed_icosian_profile](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/canonical_lexical_ingestion.rs#L2578) and [icosian_coordinate_at_index](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/canonical_lexical_ingestion.rs#L2777) bind exact ordered Z[phi] coefficients, golden conjugation, phi-scaled companion and inverse witness. Preserve the project shorthand H4 ⊕ phi H4 with its basis/glue/maps. The companion is derived, not an independent second learned state. This is useful exact canonical representation and operator reuse. It does not multiply independent semantic capacity merely by describing the same root twice.

[UnitS3Q30::hopf](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/prime_route_attention.rs#L547) supplies an S2 observation; [rotate_common_fiber](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/prime_route_attention.rs#L557) applies a common phase to both complex coordinates. The Hopf base cannot distinguish points along that S1 fiber, including q and −q. [SpinTorsionState](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/prime_route_attention.rs#L633) appropriately retains full signed S3, fiber and torsion separately. A chart-dependent S2/fiber reconstruction requires chart transition rules; it is not a reversible universal S3→S2→S1 chain.

Constructive attention role: Hopf/base neighborhoods can organize candidate pages; full signed state and relative group elements can resolve within-page orientation and transport. An arbitrary S1 rotation need not preserve the 120-root finite set. Use a closed discrete action set, retain phase as a separate channel, or explicitly define and assess quantization.

## 5. Spherical harmonics and heat kernels

Spherical harmonics are eigenfunctions of the spherical Laplace–Beltrami operator. On S2 the addition theorem combines the degree-l modes into a rotationally invariant function of the angle between two points. This provides a principled multiscale angular basis. [NIST DLMF §14.30, especially equations 14.30.9 and 14.30.11_5](https://dlmf.nist.gov/14.30).

For unit S3, the corresponding scalar heat kernel is

K_t(x,y) = (1/(2 pi²)) Σ_{l≥0} (l+1) exp(−l(l+2)t) C_l^1(x·y), t>0.

This is the n=4 specialization of Zhao–Song's theorem, with normalized surface measure handled explicitly through area 2pi². The full positive-time series converges; a truncated/quantized implementation needs its own approximation and nonnegativity checks. A simple exp(−distance²/temperature) is not automatically the exact spherical heat kernel. [Zhao–Song §2.2, theorem 1 and proof](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.01373).

**Proposed operation.** Offline, evaluate a small finite family of scalar kernel values for the actual finite relative-root classes and selected scales; serve as lookup scores. The policy may select a scale or use a small nonlinear relation operator. Do not import a harmonic transform plus learned coefficient contraction into serving under another name. Scalar isotropic kernels discard orientation and are symmetric; query/key roles and transported directional messages must supply asymmetry where language needs it. Repeated unconditioned diffusion can homogenize distinctions. An attention operation needs learned selectivity, source identity and a useful message update, not merely smoothness.

Spherical harmonic frequencies l(l+2) come from the Laplacian of S3. Zeta-zero ordinates gamma_j are a different sequence. Equating them requires a specified operator and a proved or measured spectral correspondence; neither the common word “spectrum” nor a visual oscillation supplies that correspondence.

## 6. Zeta zeros, prime coordinates and RH

The Riemann hypothesis concerns the real parts of all nontrivial zeros of the analytic zeta function. Clay still lists it as unsolved on the live page checked for this report. NIST states the critical-strip symmetry and defines Hardy's real Z(t), whose sign changes locate critical-line zeros. A finite list of supplied zero ordinates can be used computationally without proving an all-height theorem. [Clay current status](https://www.claymath.org/problem/unsolved/), [DLMF §25.10](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.10).

Riemann's explicit formula links prime-counting quantities to the zeta zeros under the stated analytic conventions; Bombieri's Clay treatment supplies a generalized explicit formula with an admissible test-function class and its Mellin transform. This is a genuine prime/zero bridge, not a theorem that prime-assigned words have semantic proximity. [Bombieri, section 5, printed pp.121–123](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/MPPc.pdf), [DLMF §25.16](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.16). The original handwritten manuscript was opened but not transcribed reliably; no extra inference is based on its scan.

**Actual UOR operation.** [zeta_phase_delta](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/prime_route_attention.rs#L909) computes gamma_j(log p_to−log p_from). [native geometry compilation](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/training.rs#L108) quantizes eight channels to u16 turns and binds the source identities. The new bound geometry [byte_phases](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/addressed_attention/artifact.rs#L164) exposes four. These are **log-scale Fourier phase coordinates**, not distances from a zero to an H4 vector.

The constructive extension is to retain more fixed phase channels at separated scales and let learning determine which combinations are useful. More zeros increase resolution of this prescribed feature family, but also storage, quantization sensitivity and correlated/redundant input. With 512 u16 channels, raw phase state is 1,024 bytes per stored token versus 8 bytes for four channels. For 256 tokens this is 262,144 bytes versus 2,048 bytes, excluding metadata. This is manageable in isolation; the cross-product lookup of all channel values is not. Phase updates can remain modular add/table operations.

**Necessary distinctions and falsifiers.** A sum of token phase increments is commutative: AB and BA have identical phase sums. The ordered H4 action and exact occurrence records must retain order. Fixed finite modulo phases can collide after quantization; high frequencies can magnify coordinate error. The zero-derived family might help stable phase alignment, scale relations or displacement cues, but must outperform equal-cost frequency controls on changed-context generation before a predictive advantage is asserted. Preserve prime assignment as identity; permuting assignments is a useful control of whether a benefit is truly tied to arithmetic structure. No prime-label metric is semantic by definition.

The preserved [spinning_top_r4_bridge.py](/Users/casey.allard/uor-r4/research/riemann-lean/spinning_top_r4_bridge.py:87) constructs F(u)=Σ exp(i gamma u)/(1/2+i gamma), F′ and the R4 coordinates (Re F, Im F, Re F′, Im F′). This is a real finite phase/derivative feature construction worth retaining as an offline donor. Its [centroid_accuracy](/Users/casey.allard/uor-r4/research/riemann-lean/spinning_top_r4_bridge.py:50) builds and judges centroids on the same supplied samples; it is not a generalization result or LM result.

## 7. Hardy–Littlewood: separate the results before borrowing the method

The circle method uses Fourier analysis to count additive representations, separating structured major-arc contributions from minor-arc contributions requiring cancellation estimates. It is not a prime oracle or a semantic attention law. Tao's worked analysis explicitly explains why magnitude-only control can fail in binary prime problems: losing oscillation/cancellation can destroy the estimate. A useful architectural analogy is to retain signed phase and relational contributions instead of only norms, but this remains a design analogy, not transfer of a number-theory theorem to language. [Tao, “Heuristic limitations of the circle method,” sections 1–2](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2012/05/20/heuristic-limitations-of-the-circle-method/).

The Hardy–Littlewood prime-tuple conjectures concern asymptotic prime configurations and local residue obstructions. The approximate functional equation concerns zeta evaluation and divisor problems. Their critical-line zero work is a third subject. They must not be treated as interchangeable uses of “Hardy–Littlewood.” The 1921 zero paper's bibliographic record and the 1923 approximate-functional-equation publisher record were located; the full originals were not successfully read, so their detailed estimates are not imported as independently checked foundations. [1921 record](https://geodesic.mathdoc.fr/item/MZ_1921__10_167645/), [1923 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s2-21.1.39).

For UOR, residue admissibility may index structural compatibility of prime-labeled objects. An admissible pattern need not contain primes at the actual queried values. A semiprime pq canonically encodes an unordered factor pair, so pq=qp loses direction. Keep the ordered pair (p,q), role and occurrence beside any product address. Repeated p² is legitimate and still differs from two distinct factors. These algebraic facts support typed relation indexing, not a learned expert or language model by themselves.

## 8. Cayley–Dickson and endomorphisms: substantial mathematics, incomplete donor code

The Cayley–Dickson tower gives real, complex, quaternionic, octonionic and higher algebras. Quaternions lose commutativity; octonions lose associativity but remain alternative normed division algebras. Sedenions have zero divisors, so merely doubling dimension does not preserve quaternionic guarantees. Parenthesization of octonionic products must be explicit. [Baez, “The Octonions,” §2.2](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/octonions/node5.html).

Furey's actual cited paper defines left/right multiplication maps and their generated endomorphism algebras. Composition of maps is associative even when the underlying algebra is not, but L_a∘L_b need not equal L_ab. It gives precise octonion conventions and a particle-physics construction, not a language-learning method. A finite catalogue of typed endomorphisms is a useful UOR proposal; arbitrary real-linear endomorphisms are matrix actions mathematically and do not satisfy the serving constraint simply because they are called endomorphic. [Furey §§II.1–II.3 and VIII.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.18450v1).

**Actual source discrepancy.** [cayley_dickson.rs](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/cayley_dickson.rs#L1) cites Furey but does not implement the paper's multiplication algebra. `from_u32` uses a multiplicative hash. `lie_product` uses neighboring-coordinate antisymmetric products; `jordan_product` uses offset-two symmetric products; `endomorphism_scalar_product` performs 16 multiply/add terms. No Cayley–Dickson recursive multiplication, Fano structure constants or Clifford anticommutation/centralizer derivation appears in this module. It must be preserved and classified as a named prototype, not a faithful implemented Cayley–Dickson engine. This finding does not reject the mathematics; it identifies the missing implementation bridge.

An exact finite signed-basis multiplication table could be implemented from a declared convention. General linear combinations would again require arithmetic and state-domain decisions. The quaternionic H4 finite-group implementation already provides the most immediately usable closed action algebra; a new octonionic donor needs a task that benefits from its additional algebraic structure and explicit associator controls.

## 10. A concrete combined hypothesis and its falsifiers

**Hypothesis.** Language-relevant attention can be learned as query-conditioned traversal and transport over a canonical graph of typed occurrences. Each occurrence retains exact byte/span identity and a distributed finite geometric state. Query/key placement is learned from causal context; Hamming signatures rank candidate geometric relations; selected messages retain full state and relative transformations; shared typed endomorphisms update the query and committed working state; a learned emission operator generates byte/EOS. Zeta phase differences supply fixed multiscale coordinates, and coupled icosian representations preserve canonical algebraic identity. The sequence of reads is causally recomputed after each update.

Prime/semi-prime “experts” can first mean **addressed reusable typed operators and relation pages**. If the intended meaning becomes a bank of separately learned neural experts with a gate, that is a different model choice requiring explicit capability and laptop-cost evidence under the project's policy. The research proposal need not adopt MoE to use prime-addressed specialization.

Canonical caching stores immutable relation facts with their construction identity. A dynamic relevance score must additionally bind query state, model parameters, metric/phase basis, source version and candidate mask; otherwise a previously correct cached weight can be stale in a new context. UOR identity supports exact reuse and invalidation. It does not make a context-dependent weight universally valid.

**Recommended representation comparison, before a broad language qualification:** keep the same trained task/data budget and compare (A) scalar Hamming scores with full selected state retained, (B) per-lane/full relative-root relation packets, (C) B plus extra fixed zeta channels, and (D) a selected isotropic harmonic-scale score only if a demonstrated neighborhood-resolution defect remains. This is a sequential diagnostic programme, not authorization to launch four fits now. Freeze a small contextual-learning task first; cumulative old regression panels become replacement tests later, not the entry threshold for a new mechanism.

Falsifiers are concrete: if B changes only telemetry but cannot learn a changed source or direction relation, rich relations have not solved the bottleneck; if C matches scrambled/equal-cost frequencies, no zeta-specific predictive advantage is established; if relational descriptors are identical for examples needing different outputs, the representation remains insufficient; if a learned finite operator cannot transfer across fresh surface forms/positions, it has not learned the intended operation; if projection or transport removes chirality that affects a task, refine the representation before adding scalar features. General prose ultimately requires held-out next-byte likelihood and actual coherent generation beyond authored copy/arithmetic panels. This research supplies a falsifiable architectural hypothesis, not a proof that these ingredients already constitute a useful language model.

## 11. The owner's sqrt(2), 2i and [0,2] bridge as a typed adapter

The repository already gives this proposal a precise starting meaning. [ADR0003](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/docs/adr/0003-fixed-zeta-prime-route-attention.md#L238) defines d_chord=2 sin(d_geodesic/2), chord range [0,2], the sqrt(2) chord for orthogonal unit vectors, and complex displacement 2i between −i and i with magnitude 2. [ADR0004](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/docs/adr/0004-geometric-intelligence-route-hierarchy.md#L155) defines typed least-cost adapter markers with explicit units, chart, error, tie rule and conversion witness. `[0,2]` in that ADR is a score interval. The owner may instead mean the coordinate pair (0,2), which is exactly the real-coordinate representation of 2i; these are distinct typed readings and the ADR alone does not settle the owner's intended notation. [Adapter metadata](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/canonical_lexical_ingestion.rs#L2095) reflects that definition; [selected_adapter](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/b267cfd2d958a76099cc8792e11c777c031895a0/crates/uor-r4-core/src/canonical_lexical_ingestion.rs#L2717) is currently fixed to complex-discrete, so the source does not yet demonstrate a generally learned or cost-selected chart bridge.

An implementable formalization uses one typed latent object x and encoding maps E_R4, E_C2, E_chart. The ordinary map (a,b,c,d)↦(a+bi,c+di) is an isometry for the standard real inner product Re(z₁ conjugate(w₁)+z₂ conjugate(w₂)). A chart adapter f is faithful to a chosen metric when g_source=f* g_target (pullback metric), or to a declared similarity when an explicit positive scale is included. For unit sphere separations, chord and geodesic are related by the nonlinear scalar function above; the endpoints sqrt(2) and 2 correspond to angles pi/2 and pi, respectively. They are therefore different raw separations, but can be related as typed design landmarks or through an explicitly chosen normalization. A scalar equality between the markers is not the needed contract.

A concrete displacement example is z=1+i, whose ordinary Euclidean norm is sqrt(2). The map F(z)=(1+i)z sends it to 2i by a pi/4 rotation and sqrt(2) dilation. Assigning the target metric (1/2)I preserves the physical squared norm 2. This is a valid typed rotation/scale example, not equality under identical default metrics; it changes ordinary radius and is not itself a proposed serving matrix operation.

The commuting obligation for a shared geometric operation T is

`E_target(T_source(x)) = T_target(E_target(x))`,

or, with explicit quantization, a declared bounded difference measured after decoding. Also require preservation of exact canonical identity, inverse reconstruction on the declared domain, orientation/fiber fields, and composition order. For scoring, bind the score conversion too: `score_source(d_source(x,y)) = score_target(d_target(f(x),f(y)))` within the stated error. Otherwise changing chart may silently change attention rankings even when coordinate reconstruction is acceptable. This formalizes the owner's bridge as a usable compiler/operator contract rather than dismissing it as a scalar arithmetic assertion.

ADR0003's separate golden radial map is explicit: (a,b)↦(b,a+b) for multiplication by phi and inverse (a,b)↦(b−a,a). This genuinely reduces that exact Z[phi] action to addition/subtraction. It rescales radius, not the ordinary C²/R4 identification. Keep radius and signed unit direction separate; bound coefficient growth and checked overflow. Hopf projection remains a lossy observation and cannot satisfy an inverse obligation without retained fiber/full S3 state. An E8/icosian encoding likewise needs its chosen inner product and coefficient inverse witness; a coordinate map is not automatically a geodesic isometry.

## 12. Multireference kernels as a further mathematical donor

## 15. The owner's prime–zero bridge: explicit formula and Hardy–Littlewood correlations

There is a substantive arithmetic bridge here. For x>1 define `psi0(x)=sum_(n<x) Lambda(n) + Lambda(x)/2`, with Lambda(x)=0 at nonintegers and Lambda(p^k)=log p. The classical explicit formula is

`psi0(x)=x−sum_rho x^rho/rho−log(2pi)−(1/2)log(1−x^(−2))`,

where rho ranges over nontrivial zeros and the sum uses symmetric height truncation, not an arbitrary reordering. Its prime side includes prime powers; midpoint convention handles jumps. Finite truncation leaves an error, and smoothing requires a declared kernel rather than an unexplained damping factor. Goldston's §3 describes the explicit formulas and their contour origin. Its §6 uses a strong-error, uniform Hardy–Littlewood prime-pair conjecture; §9's equivalence between zero pair correlation and short-interval prime variance is stated assuming RH and the specified parameter ranges. [Goldston, §§3, 6 and 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0412313).

Conrey–Keating give an especially relevant modern connection: ratios of zeta functions and conjectural correlations of arithmetic coefficients imply the same averaged formula under their stated uniform assumptions. Their theorem 1.1 explicitly assumes generalized RH; theorem 1.2 assumes either a uniform ratios conjecture or a uniform coefficient-correlation conjecture. Their introduction connects this programme to Hardy–Littlewood prime pairs, and §3 displays how off-diagonal terms require the arithmetic-correlation input. Thus Hardy–Littlewood is relevant to the bridge, but the cited results do not establish an unconditional one-to-one equality between each prime and each zero. [Conrey–Keating, introduction, theorems 1.1–1.2 and §3](https://aimath.org/~kaur/publications/92.pdf).

**Constructive architectural inference.** For a supplied critical-line zero `rho=1/2+i gamma`, `x^rho=sqrt(x) exp(i gamma log x)`; at a prime power x=p^k its phase is `gamma k log p`. Every selected zero frequency can interrogate every prime coordinate. This is a many-to-many spectral relation, which directly motivates a finite phase table indexed by `(prime or ordered prime object, zero identity)`. It is stronger motivation than an arbitrary numerical analogy. It still leaves three separate design obligations: map language context to arithmetic objects; specify the metric/connection and finite quantizer; learn which bounded relations and state transitions predict language. Equating a distribution with its spectral description does not itself determine a Riemannian metric, nor an injective finite semantic code.

The richer proposal is consequently to retain ordered prime identities and full signed geometry, compile declared finite phase relations, and learn context-dependent reading/transport over them. More zeros increase spectral resolution only in conjunction with conditioning, quantization and finite-window analysis.
