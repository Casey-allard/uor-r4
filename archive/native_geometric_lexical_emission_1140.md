## Implemented mechanism

[lexical_emission.rs](../crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/lexical_emission.rs) adds an optional artifact-bound component at the existing completion boundary. A shared binary signed-H4 score compares Base with each candidate byte/EOS or exact retained-record read. Candidate identity is represented in features; there is no runtime format enum, keyword-to-response branch, stored sentence template, provider or mathematical matrix product. The token alphabet and learned roots are artifact data.

The 375 learned feature codes combine ordered previous-token context, persistent last-read role and current-query prime identities. Rust preparation excludes words constant across construction documents, leaving the two varying words `rust` and `sentence`. This is a construction-derived registry; it is not evidence of general lexical understanding. Prime IDs are exact identities, not semantic distances. Read roles distinguish the current committed result, its first and second derivation operand IDs, and other records. Signed-H4 composition and landmark ranking choose the next action. Parent fixed zeta identities and geometry are preserved; their separate contribution and the paired-H4 construction are not isolated by this experiment.

After a record is selected, deterministic integer numeral emission reads its exact payload with a checked cursor. That operation does not create a new typed value. Last-read identity survives following lexical bytes, so the learner can distinguish emission after the first operand from emission after the second. Internal markers are disjoint from the maximum token vocabulary, and packed token fields have 18 bits. The existing 32-step completion cap remains; the selected responses stop before it.

Checkpoint state binds record ID, exact numeral, cursor and observed interval. Repeated predictions cannot commit reads; mismatching observations clear them. Old artifacts reject even an explicitly null new read field. A read-disabled control also interrupts a read restored midstream. These checks authenticate consistency with retained evidence, not the original history of an externally forged checkpoint. Existing frozen-parent validation and byte serialization remain intact. The new artifact requires the updated runtime; new-artifact browser/WASM and HTTP are NOT_RUN.
