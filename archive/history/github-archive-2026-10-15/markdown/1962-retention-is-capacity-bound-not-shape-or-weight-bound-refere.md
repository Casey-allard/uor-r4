## Body

Two arms, same two-turn demand mixture, one environment variable apart. At UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT 60 and 1 form compliance is identical and perfect - ten of ten form-demanded turns with correct content for Green, Honey, Yes, No, 7, 4 and Paris - and retention fails in both. So the terminal weight is not what suppresses multi-turn recall. Retention held at weight 60 with NO demand store and fails in every arm that has one, tracking the store presence rather than its shape, size or the weight. The remaining candidate is capacity competition at 7.16M parameters, tested by the same mixture at the 19.9M shape. Form compliance is now solved and robust across four configurations.
