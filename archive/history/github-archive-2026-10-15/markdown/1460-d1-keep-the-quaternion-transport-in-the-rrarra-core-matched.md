  - an effect at full exposure, on prose or dialogue, or at 10–30M parameters;
  - which property does the work (a parameter-matched real-gate control would separate "extra gate rows").
- **Derived** (in the note): the transport composes token-dependent rotations along the prefix, `h_t = U_t Σ_s Λ U_s⁻¹ b_s`. Its frames depend on the whole prefix, so ruling 11's endpoint factoring does not apply.
