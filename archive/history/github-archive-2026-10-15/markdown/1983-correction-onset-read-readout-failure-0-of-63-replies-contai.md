## Body

Round 162 recorded the name-like replies from the copy-store arms as a reproducible signal of the recalled value. The probe refutes that: those strings share the first token An, the value Ana is in none of them as such, and what reproduced was the onset, not the value. Over three arms, seven rows including three designed to separate value from prior, and three conditions on the EOS-bearing exact path: read mass on the expected span is at or above TAU in 62 of 63 cells, and 0 of 63 replies start with or contain the expected value. Rows differing only in the value have identical first-token candidate sets; their first word agrees in 8 of 9 cells and the whole reply is identical in 4 of 9. The emitted tokens are the store own taught surfaces and generic chat priors. The cleanest observation is the non-name row: the onset takes probability 0.894 and is the argmax, so the read is value-conditional at the first token with DELTA up to +10.8 nats, and the model then completes into the store family and never emits the value. Precise label: onset read, readout failure. The remaining gap is the continuation, so the next intervention scores the full value span at the answer position. Caveat: the arm designated commitment 100 has no recorded weight, so claims resting on it rest on an unconfirmed configuration. References #820
