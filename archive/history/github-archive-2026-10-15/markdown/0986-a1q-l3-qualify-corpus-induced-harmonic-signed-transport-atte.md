## Exactly one mechanism: CorpusSignedTransportV1

From induction data only:

1. Build a deterministic directed causal co-occurrence/PPMI field, the table-native causal candidate value `b_t(c) = sum_i phi^{-(t-i)} PPMI(x_i -> c)`, and canonical rank-8 semantic placement. Fix basis and sign ambiguity by canonical route-address order; write `v(x)` for its unit direction and `rho(x) > 0` for its separately stored, construction-derived `Z[phi]` radial/frequency factor. Hash, CID, hexadecimal spelling, prime index, and route identity are not semantic coordinates.
2. Give every lexical route one frozen `HarmonicLinkState7` row: self plus up to six non-self peers. Directed conductance is `w_xy = max(PPMI(x -> y), 0)`; retain the six strongest positive peers by conductance descending and canonical route address ascending, and encode missing peers as explicit null slots. Each slot binds neighbor CID, conductance, canonical operator/phase identity, and construction provenance. Seven is the historical center-plus-six hex-patch hypothesis, not an H4/E8 invariant.
3. Bind one complete same-frame source-free lexical-route to Cl(0,6)/SpiralCore operator mapping `O(x)`. Absence of that mapping is unavailable, not permission to synthesize a hash-derived substitute.
4. Normalize each nonempty peer row as `P_xy = w_xy / sum_z w_xz`. A zero-peer artifact row is an explicit self-loop: define `N(x)=N_6(x)` when a positive non-self peer exists and `N(x)={x}, P_xx=1` otherwise. Gate 0 nevertheless requires a non-self peer for every selected history and candidate. For every admitted candidate `c`, compute exactly six half-restart Jacobi steps of a candidate-rooted screened-harmonic field:

       H_c^0(x) = 1[x=c]
       H_c^(r+1)(x) = (1/2) 1[x=c] + (1/2) sum_(y in N(x)) P_xy H_c^r(y),
                       r = 0,...,5.

   The rows and diffusion are representation/transport, not attention. OSPF is only the analogy of link-state knowledge plus bounded propagation; it is not this algorithm. All six steps use the frozen canonical-float path; quantization occurs only at the `H^1` or `H^6` scoring boundary with the frozen quantizer, never between sweeps. No convergence-error claim is made.

   The Jacobi domain `D` is every construction-supported lexical route in the frozen induction artifact, ordered by canonical route address. One candidate evaluation performs exactly six synchronous sweeps over all `|D|` rows and all six padded peer slots: `36|D|` slot updates, including null reads, followed by the same eight padded history-slot and operator ledger in every arm. Ordered reductions make parallel scheduling irrelevant to the result.
5. For observed `x_1 ... x_t`, use the fixed eight-slot causal window, `G_0 = I`, and `G_i = O(x_i) G_{i-1}`. For every already-admitted candidate `c` and occupied history slot `i`, transport that value into the candidate frame:

       u_i(c) = O(c) (G_t G_i^{-1}) [rho(x_i) v(x_i)].

6. Form the link-aware transported value and frozen radial-angular alignment:

       utilde_i(c) = H_c^6(x_i) u_i(c)
       a_i(c) = <utilde_i(c), v(c)>
              = H_c^6(x_i) ||u_i(c)||_2 cos(theta_i(c))
       z_i(c) = a_i(c) - mean_j a_j(c).

7. For `m` occupied slots set `k = floor(m/2)` before labels. Ternarize by one deterministic balanced rank rule: assign `+1` to the strongest `k` slots, `-1` to the weakest `k`, and `0` to the one unpaired middle slot when `m` is odd. A tie crossing either cutoff is zeroed together with the matched opposite-rank slot until positive and negative counts match. Thus `q_i(c) in {-1,0,+1}` and `sum_i q_i(c) = 0` exactly.
8. Form the signed transported context and residual:

       r(c) = sum_i q_i(c) utilde_i(c)
       g(c) = <r(c), v(c)>.

   The exact zero-sum coefficients are the ternary `q_i(c)` weights over the link-aware transported values `utilde_i(c)`; `H_c^6` is part of each value, not an additional attention coefficient.

9. Center `b_t(c)` and `g(c)` over each natural candidate set. Without labels, compute `M_b` and `M_g` once from the canonically pooled candidate-centered `b_t` and unablated-full `g` values in the induction artifact. Freeze the exact MAD convention and pooled order, require both scales to be finite and nonzero, then reuse them unchanged: the full arm and every geometric control use `(M_b,M_g)`, while the table comparator and its controls use `M_b`. No control re-estimates a scale. The full score is the fixed equal-scale sum `s(c) = normalized_b_t(c) + normalized_g(c)`; the table-native comparator is `normalized_b_t(c)` alone. Select only the unique maximum whose lead over second place exceeds its score-family calibration-derived frozen margin; otherwise abstain.

The signed zero-sum weights therefore act over previous, twice-previous, and other retained route slots separately for every candidate; they are not merely a centering of final candidate scores.

Compiler/certifier floating point, allocation, and the existing Cayley-Dickson/endomorphism/Lie-Jordan research modules are permitted for this qualification. This is not a serving-kernel or UOR-exact arithmetic claim. Base-256/integer/LUT lowering occurs only after causal transfer and must reproduce the same frozen decisions.
