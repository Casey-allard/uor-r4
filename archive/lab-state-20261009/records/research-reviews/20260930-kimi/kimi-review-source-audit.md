## Verdict

The synthesis correctly separates numerical feasibility, useful language, and geometric contribution in principle. Its internal diagnosis is nevertheless materially stale and contains false claims. It describes the September 24–25 single-layer learner as the current model, misses the September 27–29 geometric stack and trained 2I transport, misstates what the 4,096 probability hashes compare, and incorrectly says that even the old reader has no content-conditioned query. The proposed billion-parameter/100B-token jump does not follow from the local evidence. Preserve the useful criticism—numerical success has not established useful conversation—but rebuild the prescription around the actual current architecture, artifact integration, retrieval defects, and measured scaling.

There are three distinct objects:

1. The retained approximately 1.68M single-layer recurrent learner and its full256 integer numerical descendants. Their preservation results are real and scoped. They are not the current stack and never qualified useful chat.
2. The active geometric stack family: `rrarra`, quaternion recurrent layers, two multi-head content reads, SwiGLU blocks, with a retained 7,153,860-parameter code configuration (width 288, six heads). S4 then trains nearest-2I transport into a dialogue stack; R1 adds M-world v1. Small 1.5M retrieval experiments are planned within this same family. Source availability is not accepted-artifact qualification.
3. A useful integrated saved/reloaded conversational model. This remains unqualified. The R1 development categories are responsive 0.52, instruction 0.27, relation 0.01, with about 7/38 on the request panel and 0/10 memory. These are the recorded development results, not my rerun or a final holdout.

## Actual gradient and artifact chain

The current stack's learned path is explicit, not inferred from names:

- `examples/geometric-stack.rs:3210–3214` applies weight-QAT when requested and transport snap according to settings after init/resume; `3265–3277` computes weighted language loss, `loss.backward()`, and `StackAdamW::update`.
- `geometric_stack.rs:842–873, 933–948` embeds IDs and dispatches each geometric layer to recurrence or geometric read, then residual and MLP updates. The tied head at `813–816` produces vocabulary logits, followed by cross-entropy at `964–979` (weighted variant follows).
- Recurrence `764–808` computes learned drives and token-dependent gate/rotation logits, applying `RecurrenceCore` with `snap: self.transport`. Forward `2786–2814` normalizes raw quaternions, selects a nearest fixed 2I root when enabled and moves the previous state. Backward `2932–2963` propagates loss through the root-valued forward with the straight-through normalization gradient into gate logits. This is a surrogate gradient, not differentiation of the discontinuous argmax as an exact smooth function.
- Geometric read `713–761` projects Q/K/V from normalized current layer content, adds learned per-head ages and NoRead logits, and invokes `fused_read`. The custom backward returns query/key/value/auxiliary derivatives (`3553–3740`); float matmul in offline training is permitted. This is real content-dependent read training, not a recency-only lookup.
- `icosian_roots` at `1467–1478` casts canonical root coordinates to F32. Consequently this training path is not an exact `Z[phi]` proof engine. The exact serving implementation has a separate obligation and artifact format.
- `StackModel::load` at `1116–1140` loads and validates tensors but sets both `served` and `transport` to `None`. Some callers explicitly restore them; others are the subject of #1533 and the codec follow-up. A successful tensor reload alone does not preserve the full trained function.

The recovery receipt `model-reload-20260930/accepted-model-reload-verified.json` binds S4 arm A weights SHA256 `2b3b568141dfe213dd85e4d67e9ce0018e802f0654ee73ea10c7e552fc327b96`, config `2db5da637cc806d992aa56869ad462fdf6902e6d5515de90b457b4ac2f23ac1c`, transport record `89e19aa43c0c0485fb2e1b9e3bef98ec67652e1b8da5dc143da03a8bab9bd1a2`. Its log reports width 288, six heads, `rrarra`, Lorentz, rotation true, `memory: None`, 78 tensors. The receipt explicitly qualifies **load only**, without generation or model-quality qualification. I read this existing receipt and log, not the model or held-out answers.

S4's recorded +0.010590 snapped-vs-free loss is one seed, a 161-response development panel and 512 updates after a schedule restart. The root-selection usage and trained gradient justify saying 2I is in the learned computation. They do not establish 2I predictive superiority, useful dialogue or all-path integer parity. The current-state sentence that S1.4 is not built is stale: #1506 merged at `0dcdb6e9`. Its open safety successor must be integrated before treating the expanded path as fully qualified.

## Corrected strongest architecture roadmap

1. **Preserve the three-way distinction and complete faithful execution.** Finish #1533 and codec/export integration so source, tokenizer, stored weights, snap, codec, memory state and loader reconstruct the same declared function. Reuse old exact kernels where applicable; audit only changed/new ranges and actual complete generation. Integer fidelity, no-float/no-multiply, dense parameter access and energy remain separately reported.
2. **Restore causal retrieval before increasing scale.** Complete #1516's oracle fixes and the pending shared selector integration. Reuse the older learner's explicit pointer-copy mixture in the current stack. Train unseen names/values, relation updates, long/short distances and new wording so memorization cannot pass. Use source edits, NoRead, wrong-value interventions, exact identity/version checks, and equal-information ordinary controls to distinguish storage, indexing, selection, binding and output failures.
3. **Integrate exact durable history with learned access.** Keep an exact append-only turn log and occurrence/version identity; use AERM/prime addresses as an index into that log, with explicit eviction and reload behavior. A slot array can support independent bindings, but it needs defined write/read/version semantics and information-matched baselines. Do not pretend the current S4 artifact already contains the optional memory modules.
4. **Measure the geometric mechanisms at their real causal boundaries.** Retain trained 2I transport under the adopted scope, and test an equally capable ordinary transport when a result can change the next decision. For phase channels compare zeta against ordinary fixed phases at equal width and cost, preserving relative-position resolution. For codebooks preserve magnitude and exact occurrence identity; for H4/E8 query/value learning account for all index scans and parameter bytes. Fixed geometry need not itself be differentiable, but its learned selector and credit assignment must be explicit. Hypercomplex weight sharing is a reasonable later compression arm, not the only permitted role for geometry.
5. **Use measured scale/dose curves after the small integrated model can retrieve.** Compare the same integrated family at feasible sizes and exposure, use a sufficient open-value curriculum, report unique data versus visits, training/evaluation lengths, stored-history access, and response truncation. Check whether loss and transfer improve before projecting 30M or larger. The roughly 20 tokens/parameter heuristic is a hypothesis for planning, not a universal optimum or automatic spend. Scale is a likely contributor, not a uniquely identified root cause.
6. **Keep Track B as a separate route into the same mission serving contract.** Establish the reference parity first, preserve the teacher's useful information during transfer, then compare geometric recurrence/sparse reads/harmonic candidates at teacher-relative quality and actual local costs. Quantized transformer serving remains a comparator; D17 requires removing the transformer architecture. Do not infer frontier parity from a small reference or an arithmetic match.
7. **Qualify the actual saved model before a frontier claim.** Complete the frozen conversation categories, independent reload streams, updated/absent memory, executable coding tasks and complete resource accounting. Only then select a declared frontier-comparison scope and pinned reference budgets. The current evidence supports concrete engineering and discriminating development experiments, not a claim that frontier-quality laptop reasoning is already solved or impossible.
