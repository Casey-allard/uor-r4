**Recommendation:** retain Kimi's separation of numerical execution, learned capability, and geometric advantage. Treat its proposed architecture as several testable hypotheses, not a demonstrated necessary remedy. Prioritize a causally diagnosed retrieval/copying and saved-session integration change at the current scale. Do not adopt a billion-parameter training commitment, declare serving complete, or retire whole geometric mechanisms from this synthesis.

## What is strongest in the synthesis

- Arithmetic fidelity and language usefulness are different questions. An integer implementation may faithfully preserve a weak parent; fidelity is valuable without being capability qualification.
- Independently addressable information, useful queries, learned writes, and usable readout deserve direct diagnosis. A local operator comparison cannot establish that the complete model has enough accessible information for the requested conversation.
- Structured weight sharing and relative positional phases are legitimate mechanisms with concrete controls. They are much more actionable than claiming that geometric elegance alone creates semantic understanding.
- The scale gap is substantial, and a scoped entity-coherence milestone is more informative locally than importing an unrelated chat score as acceptance.
- Exact external memory can preserve information that bounded recurrent state cannot preserve losslessly. This is a strong reason to integrate the existing exact log and pointer/copy path. It does not establish any particular learned addressing scheme's quality.

## Primary-source checks and corrected claims

**QLSTM: the categorical “never quality gains” claim is false.** Parcollet et al., arXiv **1811.02566v1** (2018), study bidirectional speech recognition and a copy task. Table 1 reports modest quality gains as well as parameter reductions: the four-layer 1024-unit comparison has 53.7M real-model parameters and 4.5% WSJ81 test WER versus 18.7M and 4.3% for QLSTM. Thus about 2.87× fewer parameters and slightly better WER coexist in that experiment. These results do not establish a language-model, chat, or universal quality advantage. Read this as a useful structured-sharing precedent with a task-specific outcome. [QLSTM primary paper, Table 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.02566v1)

**GHRR: the language-model claim is supported, but version and architecture matter.** **2405.09689v2, May 27 2026**, adds attention and next-token experiments absent from v1. Section V-E replaces attention inside a Transformer, testing WikiText-2 and Penn Treebank over five runs. Table III gives baseline perplexities 29.16/94.78 and, for one fixed-position variant, 27.54/93.93. Section IV-E uses complex matrix binding plus softmax of a real part; position matrices can be learned. Its matrix size limits independently represented tokens. Section IV-F gives binding cost O(Dm³) for D blocks of m×m matrices. This supports a binding-based attention mechanism, not transformer-free, multiplier-free serving or a cost-free superposition store. The quasi-orthogonality result also has distributional/independence assumptions; deterministic zeta phases do not inherit it automatically. It would be incorrect to reject Kimi's LM citation after reading only the 2024 version. [GHRR v2, Sections IV-E/F and V-E](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.09689v2)

**RoPE: supports relative rotations, not privileged zeta frequencies.** RoFormer **2104.09864v5** constructs position-dependent block rotations so query/key inner products depend on relative position, and reports language experiments. That gives a precise architectural analogy for fixed phases. It does not show that zeta zeros have a semantic advantage over other frequency choices, or that every phase encoding preserves useful long-range distinctions. [RoFormer, Section 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.09864v5)

**GATr: the cited paper does not report LM/chat experiments.** Brehmer et al., **2305.18415v3**, the NeurIPS 2023 camera-ready version, exploit E(3) symmetry and projective geometric algebra in n-body, arterial-flow and robot-motion tasks. This is evidence that a correctly chosen geometric inductive bias can help tasks with known geometric structure. It is explicitly a Transformer and is not a ready-made compliant UOR-R4 serving architecture. “This paper has no LM result” is supported; “GATr can never help language” or a universal absence claim is not. [GATr v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18415v3)

**Native low-bit training is a strong precedent, not a necessity theorem.** The BitNet **2504.12285v2** report quantizes weights during the forward pass, uses a Transformer-derived architecture, and trains on four trillion tokens before supervised fine-tuning and preference optimization. Its reported quality therefore does not isolate parameter count or establish 100B tokens as a sufficient or necessary threshold. Its negative PTQ comparisons support caution at those settings, not impossibility of every conversion, distillation, progressive quantization, or trained integer bridge. [BitNet report, Sections 2–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.12285v2)

**The ternary-attention failure is a scoped negative.** Zhu et al.'s **2406.02528v5**, Section 3.3.1 and Figure 1, report failure when quantizing Q/K in the tested configuration; the paper uses recurrent token mixing instead. That is not a proof that all low-bit content queries fail. Its formulation also distinguishes elimination of matrix multiplication from elimination of elementwise products. The latest paper is v7 (2025), with additional hardware work; this review does not independently qualify the attachment's Loihi/T-MAC throughput numbers. [MatMul-free LM v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02528v5), [latest version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02528v7)

**Memory precedents do not validate every exact-addressing claim.** Memory Layers at Scale studies learned key/value parameter memory augmenting a model; this is not the same object as K ephemeral 256-dimensional entity slots. Engram's static n-gram lookup is a useful precedent for conditional memory, and its reported MMLU improvement is an actual scoped result. Neither paper establishes that prime addresses encode semantic distance or that its model can be replaced with an exact event log while retaining the result. Retrieval must distinguish learned parameter memory, episodic state, nearest-neighbor lookup, static n-gram tables, and exact occurrence/version identity. [Memory Layers at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09764), [Engram](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07372)

## First-principles analysis of the proposed remedy

**The attachment has not identified capacity as the cause.** A worse NLL for one quaternion lane intervention and a much smaller training run than an external model are compatible with many explanations: insufficient data coverage, wrong target/objective, weak or missing gradient to a selector, inaccessible historical values, train/export disagreement, decoding or copy-mixture errors, and insufficient state or parameter capacity. They do not isolate these explanations. Changing slots, heads, sharing, positional features, data volume, and parameter count at once destroys that causal distinction.

**A genuine capacity bound is narrower.** If every history is compressed into a finite B-bit state and no external history can be accessed, there are at most 2^B distinguishable states. Losslessly retaining N independently chosen values from V possibilities requires at least N log2(V) bits, before keys, roles and versions. This follows by counting, not analogy. An exact external log changes the premise: the state can act as a controller that retrieves information rather than a lossless archive. The bound does not select K×256 slots, multiple heads, or a billion parameters. Fixed slots also eventually saturate and require explicit write, identity, eviction and collision semantics.

**K×256 is a possible memory organization, not a parameter scale.** An FP32 K×256 state requires 1024K bytes per session per layer: K=256 is 256 KiB, K=1024 is 1 MiB. Neither implies 1–3B weights. With one shared transition operator, increasing K may grow state and access work without growing learned weights much; separate per-slot operators have different sharing and generalization properties. This architectural choice must be specified before costing or comparing it.

**Multiple heads are not synonymous with information access.** A single content-conditioned query can already discriminate records; several read steps can implement multiple accesses. Multiple simultaneous heads can help separation and parallel access, but splitting a fixed total width among heads is a different intervention from increasing total width. First establish whether the current query is content-dependent in actual source and whether it selects the correct record under source edits. Recency initialization does not prove that a trained selector is permanently recency-only. The attachment's “no content-conditioned query” claim requires the independent source audit.

**The tied output layer is not by itself a proof of lost token identity.** A d-dimensional hidden vector with a linear V-way readout constrains the family of distributions, but d<V does not prevent identifying or choosing every one of V tokens. One can assign V distinct directions even in a small real space; what matters is learned separability, precision and usable margins. Low-rank logits can limit fitting arbitrary distributions, yet a factual-copy task may need only selection of one known value. Test whether the source evidence reaches the decoder and whether oracle selection restores the answer before concluding that vocabulary size requires a much larger hidden state.

**Quaternion sharing has an exact algebraic benefit and an empirical tradeoff.** An arbitrary real linear map from R^(4n) to R^(4m) has 16mn scalar weights. A Hamilton-structured quaternion map has 4mn real scalar weights: a fourfold reduction for that matrix, not necessarily for the whole network. It is a restricted family of real linear maps. That restriction can improve generalization or remove useful flexibility. Count real scalar components, not quaternion objects, when comparing parameters. Weight sharing reduces stored coefficients but does not automatically reduce the number of real arithmetic operations by four; the straightforward quaternion product still uses sixteen scalar products per quaternion pair. Measure the actual compiled add/shift/table implementation, activated bytes, and total latency against ordinary structured sharing at matched capacity and cost.

**The useful phase identity has no dependence on zeta.** For block rotations R(p)=diag(R(pθ_j)),

`<R(p)q, R(t)k> = q^T R(t-p) k`

for any fixed real frequencies θ_j, because each block satisfies R(p)^T R(t)=R(t-p). This is an exact real-arithmetic identity. It does not select a frequency distribution. If instead the same rotation is applied to both vectors, the dot product is unchanged. A zeta design must specify the mapping from zeros to frequencies, scaling, retained orientation, and interaction with content. Check near aliases where Δθ_j is close to an integer multiple of 2π across retained components. In a table implementation, check finite periods, rounding error, and whether the discrete operation preserves the needed composition law. These checks are compatible with the geometric mission; none requires assuming zeta phases are useless.

**QAT and export are separate claims.** Training floating-point weights and then exporting integer codes is not logically equivalent to fake-quantized forward training with an explicit gradient surrogate. The actual graph decides which method is present. A bridge may still work without textbook QAT, but it needs measured fidelity. Conversely, quantizing learned weights during training does not guarantee that recurrent updates, accumulators, nonlinear tables and retrieval scores match serving. Preserve D8's staged ladder and inspect the actual hard/relaxed bridge instead of classifying it by its filename.

**Integer softmax does not restore information removed earlier.** Q/K weight precision, Q/K activation precision, accumulated score precision, and output-probability precision are separate design choices. A lookup exponential and integer normalization can be exact for a specified discrete algorithm while approximating the real softmax. If two relevant contexts collapse to the same quantized scores, a downstream normalization cannot distinguish them. Nor does a finite collection of matching hashes prove all reachable inputs, all compiler outputs, future architectures, session persistence, D5 parameter selection, or useful generation. Preserve the tested kernel; do not freeze it as globally complete while proposing new state/query operators it does not yet implement.

**Failure disposition must preserve scope.** A negative at one artifact, data distribution, seed, control and numerical budget can justify stopping an unchanged repeat. It cannot retire all Hamilton transport, prime indexing, zeta phases, H4, or geometry as an architectural family. Historical mechanisms can remain available with a precise reactivation condition. An experiment should earn another run by changing a causal hypothesis that affects an actual programme decision.

## Scale and laptop bounds

These are transparent conditional calculations, not predictions of local measured throughput. Decimal GB and binary GiB are distinguished. Parameters mean real scalar parameters. The baseline follows the attachment's rounded 1.68M parameters and 30M target visits; a local record also reports 29,999,104 visits. Repeated target visits are exposure, not necessarily distinct tokens or independent facts.

| Quantity | 1B proposal | 2B proposal | 3B proposal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Parameters / 1.68M | 595× | 1,190× | 1,786× |
| 100B target visits / 30M | 3,333× | 3,333× | 3,333× |
| Visits per parameter at 100B | 100 | 50 | 33.3 |
| Ideal packed 4-bit weights | 0.50 GB / 0.466 GiB | 1.00 GB / 0.931 GiB | 1.50 GB / 1.397 GiB |
| FP32 weights + gradients + Adam m,v | 16 GB / 14.90 GiB | 32 GB / 29.80 GiB | 48 GB / 44.70 GiB |
| Dense 6ND training proxy | 6×10^20 FLOPs | 1.2×10^21 FLOPs | 1.8×10^21 FLOPs |
| Proxy / baseline 6ND | 1.98 million× | 3.97 million× | 5.95 million× |

The 2–3B parameter ratio is about **3.08–3.25 orders of magnitude**, and exposure is **3.52 orders**. Neither is five orders. Their product is about 6.60–6.77 orders, under a dense-compute approximation; one should not replace the original error with an architecture-independent training-cost assertion.

The common 6ND approximation is a rough dense-model accounting model. Chinchilla's empirical compute-optimal results concern trained model families and budgets, not a universal law prescribing twenty tokens per parameter or a threshold for a new geometric architecture. [Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556)

Four-bit weight storage can be compatible with a laptop while ordinary full-model Adam training is not: 2–3B requires roughly 30–45 GiB for those four FP32 arrays alone. Mixed precision with master weights often retains a similar footprint; optimizer compression, offloading, partial training, checkpointing and sharing can change the calculation, but must be designed and costed. Add scales, activation history, temporaries, loaded duplicate weights, indexes, exact memory, clients and OS. Naive backpropagation through B=16, T=256, K=256 states of width 256 at FP32 requires 1 GiB per layer just to retain those states; K=1024 makes it 4 GiB. Sparse updates or recomputation can change this conditional example.

If every 4-bit weight is streamed once per output token, 2–3B entails at least 1–1.5 GB of weight traffic per token under that assumption. Twenty tokens/second requires 20–30 GB/s of effective weight traffic before activation traffic, scales, addressing, output normalization and I/O. This is not a throughput forecast. D5 selected access matters because avoiding arithmetic without avoiding data movement does not remove the bandwidth cost. Account for actual accessed weights and table size rather than assuming that parameter compression alone satisfies D5 or produces a fixed energy saving.

For 100B visits, a sustained end-to-end rate of 1,000 training tokens/second means about 1,157 days; 10,000 means about 116 days. Evaluation, preparation, retries, checkpointing, downtime and review add cost. Under the dense proxy, 2–3B at a hypothetical sustained 10 TFLOP/s would take 3.80–5.70 years. These rates are illustrative, not claimed M1 performance. A measured short run with the intended architecture is required for a resource projection.

A fully materialized 100B-token corpus would occupy 200 GB with 16-bit token IDs (only where the vocabulary permits) or 400 GB with 32-bit IDs, excluding text and indexes. Repeated streaming does not require that much unique storage, but also does not turn a small curriculum into 100B unique tokens. At 2–3B, one full four-array FP32 training-state checkpoint is already 32–48 GB before metadata and activation checkpoints. Multiple retained ancestors and atomic replacement headroom must be reserved. No new model campaign or external spending is authorized by these arithmetic examples.

## Cheapest causal discriminators for this project

The following are proposed successor work cards, not tests already run. Use existing open development data and valid binaries where possible; do not consult the sealed final panel during mechanism selection. Execute only after the shared runner/admission path is healthy, with a prospective complete resource projection.

1. **Locate the first lost distinction.** For existing source-separated entity/relation/update failures, trace the actual loaded session's write identity, stored value, candidate set, query, selected record, copy probability and emitted token. Change one source value while retaining wording and require the answer to change appropriately. Vary entity names, relation labels and distance independently. If the correct record is absent, repair write/index/history access; if present but unselected, diagnose query/selection; if selected but not emitted, diagnose mixture/decoder. This is more discriminating than another aggregate NLL run.
2. **Use an oracle only as a diagnostic intervention.** Override record selection with the correct development record, leaving the decoder unchanged. Also compare an exact copy-only diagnostic against the learned mixture. If oracle reads restore answers, the decoder already has relevant capacity for those cases; repair retrieval before enlarging it. If they do not, inspect read representation and decoder training. These diagnostic oracles never become serving authors or capability claims.
3. **Check that learning can affect the failing path.** Reuse the existing gradient and hard/relaxed bridge checks when their source and scope apply. On the relevant open example, verify a language-loss contribution reaches query/read/write/copy parameters, and that changing them changes the intended decision. A nonzero gradient alone is insufficient: compare direction, scale, saturation and actual next-step effect. A missing path calls for a graph/objective repair, not a larger model.
4. **Run the smallest capacity intervention only after that diagnosis.** Compare a few slot counts or read steps at fixed total state/parameter/access budgets, then separately compare an increased budget. Include an ordinary exact-key/pointer or structured-memory control with the same available information. Specify occurrence/version/eviction semantics. A benefit supports that scoped organization; an unchanged outcome weakens the capacity explanation for those failures.
5. **Test geometry one role at a time.** Quaternion sharing versus an ordinary structured-sharing control; zeta frequencies versus a matched geometric/log-spaced or distribution-matched frequency control. Match dimensions, norm, frequency range, information, tuning budget and precision. Record the actual integer path and total access cost after a learned gain exists. Small paired seeds and uncertainty are preferable to presenting one winner as a law; choose replication only when it can change the adoption decision.
6. **Scale from measured learning curves.** Once source-dependent retrieval and saved-session behavior work, estimate loss/capability versus parameter count, distinct data, repeated visits and measured wall time. Increase one dimension at a time within the local ledger, keeping a competent control. A small-domain result may be valuable even when broad chat is not yet qualified. Decide the next rung from achieved behavior and projected cost, not from a borrowed billion-parameter threshold.

The immediate scientific goal should be a saved, reloadable native geometric session that uses the correct persisted evidence under unseen names, values, phrasings and updates, with source interventions establishing why it succeeds. Slotted state, multiple reads, quaternion sharing and zeta positional structure remain candidate ways to achieve that goal. The available evidence does not establish them as the only ways, nor that their combination can be obtained by keeping the existing integer session unchanged.
