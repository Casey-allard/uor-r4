# Shared calibrated geometric byte decisions

September 12, 2026. References #973 and #964. This implements the next causal change from the [saved-state emission diagnosis](native_geometric_shared_core_diagnostic_973.md). It is an experimental version of the shared core, not a replacement for retained `15baec48`.

## Changed computation

The old emitter collapsed two ordered state components into one H4 root and classified it by a zero-threshold signed angular landmark. The diagnostic measured both conflicting target labels after that collapse and high errors even for the best legal landmark on the frozen states.

Schema 2 keeps the two ordered H4 components separate through two signed angular comparisons. For each byte-tree node, let `a = angular(root[d], landmark[0]) - threshold[0]` and `b = angular(root[d+1], landmark[1]) - threshold[1]`, with state lanes interpreted modulo four. The branch margin is a learned choice of `min(a,b)` or `max(a,b)`; a positive margin goes right. This is an intersection or union of two geometric caps. Thresholds lie in `[-5,5]` against the exact angular-rank domain `[-4,4]`, so the class can express constant decisions. No multiply, float, feature-weight contraction, full-state Cartesian table, dense vocabulary projection or provider is added to serving.

There are 512 bounded branch slots, each with two learned root landmarks, two integer thresholds and one union/intersection bit. Recurrence, causal 32-slot context access, fixed prime/zeta geometry and byte/EOS tree structure retain their existing computations. Legacy emission parameters remain in the migrated artifact for identity/lineage but are not used by the schema-2 byte branch. Exact durable memory and typed-operator/API integration remain unimplemented on this experimental path.

The synthetic separation test uses two different ordered root pairs with the same old product code and shows the new operator can distinguish them. This establishes a broader decision class, not repair of the actual 168 conflicting construction labels. Calibration alone cannot recover distinctions if inputs are still collapsed elsewhere or lack useful organization.

## Learning and acceptance fixed before execution

The driver first claims an exclusive representation attempt, writes the complete experiment design and then loads the preserved `5979f2e2`. Legacy schema dispatch accepts its pinned original implementation identity; it must round-trip byte-for-byte and reproduce both saved construction and opened-holdout metrics. New artifacts bind the new schema/source. Snapshots bind the appropriate schema and artifact.

Upgrade preserves recurrent parameters and records the legacy parent, clearing inherited fit metadata that would misdescribe the new emission. Calibration then performs exactly three deterministic coordinate passes on the original construction states. It considers each landmark, threshold and union/intersection field against the common branch logistic loss with recurrence fixed. It records its own parent, data digest and pass count. A calibrated checkpoint is saved separately from both the upgrade and later joint fit.

The predeclared development gate requires 672 root and 660 ASCII-branch positions, at most 12 root errors and fewer than 216 ASCII-branch errors. It follows focused constant/pair-separation, all-leaf, checkpoint and artifact checks. Failure at three passes is a calibration-dose failure, not proof that this class cannot represent the data; the joint fit must not run after a failed gate.

Only after that gate, one joint hard-forward fit may run with seed 443, at most 100,000 proposals and 120 seconds. All nine parameter families remain under one objective; schema-2 reports name the last emitter families `cap_landmarks` and `cap_thresholds_union`. A new authored six-document holdout and four generation prompts are frozen before calibration and never used by it or fitting. The predictive gate requires greater than 2% NLL improvement against both legacy and calibrated stages, no lower byte accuracy against either, and greater than 1% loss increases under the defined context and prior-root interventions. Legacy, upgrade, calibration and joint-candidate results and generations are separate. Useful generation and termination are still required for advancement; predictive smoke does not promote a serving model.

## Execution result

**Actual gate: FAIL_CALIBRATION_DOSE_NO_JOINT_FIT.** The legacy artifact reproduced its saved construction/opened-holdout metrics and original bytes. Upgrade 331a9ef1 and calibrated 968cf841 are preserved separately. Three fixed-state passes changed construction loss from 5.612466 to 3.473156 and correct byte/EOS predictions from 4/672 to 65/672. The legacy candidate had 25/672 correct at 5.223800. Root errors fell to 12/672, meeting that condition; the next ASCII branch made 220/660 errors, missing the required fewer than 216. The joint fit, new holdout evaluation and new continuations were not run.

The thresholds and operator were not changed after this result. A read-only exhaustive audit enumerated all hard decision patterns obtainable from 120 landmarks and 11 thresholds per operand, for both union and intersection, on the saved construction states. The exact minimum is 10/672 errors at the root and 202/660 at the ASCII branch. Thus this class can express decisions meeting the two conditions on these fixed states. The descriptive parameter witnesses were not installed in a model, and the failed calibration gate remains unchanged.

The ASCII hard-error witness has branch NLL sum 422.567973, worse than the calibrated operator's 414.318812. The root witness likewise has higher loss. Equivalent hard patterns can have different margins, and this audit minimized errors rather than likelihood. It therefore does not establish that coordinate search missed a lower-loss setting, that these are minimum-loss witnesses, or that a joint model would generalize. The learning objective and hard-decision gate have a demonstrated tradeoff; search quality remains unresolved.
