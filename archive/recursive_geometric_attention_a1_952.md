The scorer was not implemented. All three predeclared matched contrasts lost earlier causal order at every exposed hierarchy level when digest and CID identity fields were excluded. The result therefore takes the decision contract's first branch:

`REDESIGN_ORDERED_ROUTE_SUMMARY`

This is not evidence that route storage, natural candidate admission, H4, Hopf, or SpiralCore have no value. The same probe established that the real candidate and value path is reachable. It is specifically evidence that the current additive/last-child hierarchy summary cannot supply the ordered state that recursive attention requires.

## Historical interpretation

The early Angular Manifold Routing work established useful sparse fixed routing but explicitly did not test attention; its PTB and WikiText-2 language-model controls found Hopf and a permuted fixed map indistinguishable. The later *Canonical Explorations* synthesis proposed a shift from fixed sector membership toward ordered, stateful, invariant-constrained trajectory evolution, but did not rerun the decisive permutation control on that proposal.

The #952 result is consistent with that history: candidate admission may remain geometric and bounded, but selection cannot become trajectory-sensitive after the hierarchy representation has erased order. #967 therefore changes the reusable ordered summary first and holds the working candidate/value path fixed.
