[saved 145-reply model](../m2-cross-resume-2026-10-10/README.md) increase complete

supply the evidence for this continuation; they are prior results, not this
attempt's endpoint. The run uses the pre-registered scorer, width, rate, seed,
data, evaluator and sole local 256 endpoint, with no adaptive selection.

Only the existing 115,200 shared cross-state Q4 coefficients learn. Their
energy depends jointly on directed signed H4 relatives of the factual
post-bridge state and independently replayed query/prior-prefix state, and
reaches Generate and every physical Copy alias before the existing common clip
and alias pool. Source48/Generate64, Context, Potential, Generate prototypes and
coefficients, bridge, Cue, Prefix, tokenizer and exponent table remain frozen.
The native kernel, shift22 policy and 65,536-byte padded payload are unchanged.
No row-ID feature, output repair, answer-source shortcut or output union is
introduced. Existing dense vocabulary access remains; this run does not measure
full-path serving energy or establish a geometric advantage.

The fixed schedule is 256 additional AdamW updates, batch 8, seed 1001, learning
rate 0.03, betas 0.9/0.999, epsilon 1e-8, weight decay 0, global norm clip 1 and
quarter-master clamp [-1.75,1.75]. The raw-identity gradient policy and all-answer,
equal-episode objective are unchanged. Four repetitions of the same shuffled
512-row order give 2,048 episode draws and 26,656 answer/EOS position draws;
6,664 distinct positions are cached. Local 0→256 corresponds to cumulative
lineage 320→576. Checkpoints every 32 updates are for recovery; local 256 is the
sole selected endpoint. Training and evaluation both use the exposed frozen

## Registered decision and limits

Integrity passed, but the endpoint failed the prospective **complete>145**
bar. Reject 139, retain 145 and stop further dose/rate/seed continuation at this
scope, as registered. Retention of every old success was not a veto, but losses
are fully reported; the rejected endpoint is not combined with its parent to
manufacture a larger count.

**Before opening the PR: if this came out the other way, would my next step
differ? Yes.** A gain would retain the candidate and advance learning or fresh
qualification. This regression retains 145 and closes the continuation recipe.
After protected delivery and cleanup, choose and pre-register one distinct
objective or mechanism aimed at complete-reply failures. Lower mean CE paired
with fewer whole answers motivates considering an episode-bottleneck objective,
but that is a hypothesis, not an implemented or registered successor. The
inherited count remains 1/3; renaming the next piece does not reset it.

All 512 examples are exposed development data. This run alone cannot establish
fresh-draw transfer, general conversation, geometric advantage, unique causal
necessity of the factual carrier or serving-energy improvement. Passing the
frozen threshold alone would not complete M2's fresh-draw acceptance. The
standing goal continues beyond this result cycle.
