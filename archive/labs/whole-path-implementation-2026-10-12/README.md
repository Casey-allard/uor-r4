rebinds Cue/Prefix to that parent, exports C/G/bridge/U/route/status, saves

bytes, floating masters, mode/seed and consumer identity are separate evidence.
Any master change invalidates an existing live tape, packet and session.
Another revision rejects old snapshots. Restore validates index eligibility,
original encoder witness, exact content/hash/version and frame before committing

A sampled failure closes that trajectory, rather than silently continuing RNG.
