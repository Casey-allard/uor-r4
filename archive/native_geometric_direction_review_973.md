## Decision and observed bottleneck

Build a learned role-aware occurrence selector shared by response entry and
copying (#1137), then exact learned relation memory (#1138), selective geometric
access at complete cost (#1139), and typed operator composition (#1140).

The latest correction removes one unbound prefix-score contribution and improves
the documented wording/open-transfer results. Required answer words remain
retained. `word_copy_runtime::binding_mask` compares four recent query words
against three predecessor offsets per source occurrence; shared prefix scoring
pools those contributions and their multiplicity. This is a positional relation,
not a learned general role representation. Remaining wording failures are
consistent with this limitation; it is not yet a proven sole-cause diagnosis.

Sharing a source decision repairs coordination but does not alone teach wording
equivalence. Learn relative local role/context features and keep their selected
occurrence attached through causal commit and generation. Do not start by adding
context, a city-specific rule or another independent response head.

## Archived angular-routing evidence: source-derived qualifications

The canonical [router archive](../research/ai-research/ai-router/router-research/README.md)
contains angular concentration on semantically structured proxies, conditional
admission and guarded refinement. Duplicate archived copies are shared lineage,
not independent replications. Number-theory multiscale/prime-transport traces
are different populations from language; a supplied next-return-gap field is
not a free causal feature for a language model.

Inspection of [INC-0171 source](../research/ai-research/ai-router/router-research/_inc0171_analysis.py)
qualifies the [original LM report](../research/ai-research/ai-router/router-research/docs/research/increments/INC_0171_lm_integration.md):

1. `BaselineMoEFFN.forward` uses gate probabilities only through `argmax` and
   does not weight selected expert outputs by the gate. Its cross-entropy
   training loop supplies no routing auxiliary loss. There is no differentiable
   loss path to gate parameters in the shown implementation; hidden inputs can
   still learn and thereby change routing. This is a static source finding,
   not a newly executed gradient experiment.
2. The permuted Hopf arm relabels the same partitions' expert IDs. It does not
   destroy their partition structure and is not an independent random partition.
3. `eff_buckets` is exponential occupancy entropy. Each arm visits one expert
   per token. Its ratio is not executed expert work, wall time or energy.

The recorded PTB perplexities, 152.26 versus 164.54, remain measurements of the
implemented systems. They do not establish a valid learned-gate comparison or
a complete geometric speedup. The transformer attention and dense experts also
remain. Reuse candidate-shortlisting ideas; do not import the stronger prose
claims or rerun this old system as an immediate native prerequisite.

## Representation and cost requirements

Prime/UOR identity is exact addressing, not semantic distance. Eight fixed
u16 zeta phase channels do not independently preserve order. An H4 root is a
finite ordered state; its golden/Galois companion is a reversible dependent
representation, not a second independently learned factor. Hopf observation
does not replace retained fiber/orientation. Exact `Z[phi]` and typed geometry
remain required at their actual declared boundaries.

Use exact occurrences/relations for recoverable values and geometric summaries
for query-dependent access. Learn which roles, writes and operators apply.
Summaries must identify their retained fields, information loss, exact-member
references and fallback rule. A hash-derived atom has no semantic metric by
itself. Existing exact algebra and SpiralCore signed operators remain candidates
for a task that needs them; their semantic mapping is not already learned.

Count load/validation, tokenization, observation, role features, routing,
candidate scoring, comparisons/gathers, selected operators, writes and output.
Add offline fitting, artifact/index construction and checkpoint I/O to lifecycle
cost. Report artifact/state/storage, peak RAM and comparable latency alongside
operation/byte counters. Zero dense products is a constraint, not proof of low
complete cost. A selected operator should suppress work it makes unnecessary.

## External mechanisms and boundaries

- [Zoology, Arora et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927):
  input-dependent associative recall matters; synthetic success alone does not
  establish language capability.
- [Pointer-generator networks, See et al. (ACL 2017)](https://aclanthology.org/P17-1099/):
  learned copy/generate coordination; reuse the idea with native bounded
  selection/commit, not the original dense/recurrent implementation.
- [DeltaNet (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06484) and
  [Gated DeltaNet (2024/2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06464):
  targeted associative correction and forgetting have distinct roles. Their
  matrix memories do not directly meet this serving target.
- [Vector symbolic architectures, Kleyko et al. (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05268):
  role binding, order permutation and bundling are useful design vocabulary;
  superposition/interference do not eliminate the need for recoverable sources.
- [Product-key memory, Lample et al. (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.05242):
  factorized access separates stored capacity from values touched per step;
  original dense projections and search costs remain part of that system.
- [Engram, Cheng et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07372v1):
  deterministic local-pattern lookup complements contextual computation. Its
  [official demonstration](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/Engram/blob/main/engram_demo_v1.py)
  still has dense key/value projections, normalization, gating and convolution;
  the paper's full backbone is a transformer. UOR needs its own learned sparse
  contextual gate, not merely larger n-gram tables or that dense module.
- [HELM, He et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24722):
  compatible geometric learning/transport is relevant, but coordinate
  preservation does not remove the original transformer computation.

The owner-associated semantic-routing proposal also separates exact role/filler
structures from versioned geometric access. Its grounding/planner remains a
proposal. Adopt the minimum exact-binding subset; a full facet/proof/network
platform is not an immediate prerequisite. UOR Atlas, MSA, Nemesis and formal
archives remain structural research at their own scopes, not demonstrated
language learners. Associated synthesis manuscripts and image-only slides are
not independent experiment replications; their strongest claims require source
verification. No mathematical universality or quantum claim was audited here.

## What stays deferred and how the plan changes

Larger context comes after retained-content binding. Learned consolidation comes
when eviction loses useful associations. Geometry-specific efficiency requires
a proper matched comparison including fallback. Learned nonlinear geometric
transitions remain broader model work; the fixed quaternion-cube substitution
degraded text, while its budget-stopped fit is unavailable learning evidence.
Neither result qualifies a new general learner or warrants the immediate fit.

The four stage issues carry concrete acceptance and failure branches. If a
representation, operator or route fails, retain that scoped result and name the
smallest next change. Do not turn a mechanical pass into capability, a negative
into an automatic larger campaign, or this review into a permanent research gate.

## Owner clarification after #1145: recover learned geometric routing

Build a geo-transformer LLM that replaces dense transformer execution, learning
where information belongs and which context and operators to activate. Exact
binding, memory and NoWrite reuse remain useful components; they do not fulfill
the language-model objective by themselves. Move learned placement and selected
transformation into #1139 now, and include raw-text prediction and short
composition in its direct evaluation. Further NoWrite score-bound work is
secondary. The canonical plan specifies the current implementation and resource
projection requirements; the new block is unimplemented and unqualified.

The original [angular architecture note](../research/prime-analysis/research/routing-infrastructure/ai-router/router-research/geometric_routing_architecture_summary.md)
separates fixed routing structure, learned semantic placement, transported state
and retrieval keys. Keep those roles distinct. Its hyperbolic H^4 notation is
not the finite H4 root group. The archived
[hard prime-router timing source](../research/prime-analysis/research/routing-infrastructure/ai-router/router-research/tools/prime_transport/run_router_trainsoft_inferhard_v1.py)
does bypass the routing MLP but retains dense attention/readout; `make_batch`
targets the first token. Its reported speedup is scoped to that implementation
and task, not matrix-free language generation. The later prime-router server
contains both local generation and an Ollama voice path; the latter is not a
native model capability result.

Supporting mechanisms remain available for actual integration. The existing
NAF crate implements core/integer/tensor/address interchange and GNAF claim
vocabulary, with state/operator/plan domains incomplete. R4G1 supplies packed
tables, typed references and borrowed execution; its dormant route-attention
operator uses masked XOR/popcount and bounded top-M aggregation. These are
concrete building blocks, not a learned semantic model or universal compressor.
Inspect the original source and use each at its matching boundary rather than
adding a broad conformance programme before language learning.
