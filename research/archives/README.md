# Archives

Preserved research sandboxes and working material, brought in largely as-is.
Build artifacts, regenerable caches, VCS metadata, and byte-duplicates of trees
already present elsewhere in this repository were omitted.

- `gemini-dev/` — an r3/r4-era development sandbox: text corpora, generation and
  routing scripts, a `prime-router/` prototype, an early `rust/` wasm-router
  checkout with vendored UOR standards, `scratch/` notes, and `extra_reading/`
  research PDFs. Excluded: GloVe/manifold caches (~490 MB), a nested duplicate of
  AI-Research (present at `research/ai-research/`), and non-research business
  material.
