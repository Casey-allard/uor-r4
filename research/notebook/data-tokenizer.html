Earlier records, which are not results

Three records on this line fall on or after 12 October 2026. None is a result:

  • 2026-10-19, d41-offline-donor (process): the owner approved a one-time exception. A large open model (gpt-oss-120b) may generate a training corpus offline and is never used again by the student. Running it needs a separate owner release.
  • 2026-10-19, distill-d1-consumer-verifier (tooling): a host-only checker that validates the planned donor corpus against its registration sheet before admission. Its 32 self-tests pass; no GPU run and no generation took place.
  • 2026-10-19, rust-bpe-trainer (tooling): a deterministic host-only Rust command to train a byte-level BPE tokenizer for a planned 16,384-token vocabulary. It does not change the active tokenizer and produces no model result.

What a row here will need

A row needs the data and tokenizer identity (hashes), a bar fixed beforehand and a comparison on the same evaluator. A tokenizer that changes the vocabulary changes what every panel row means, so it is compared on the fixed development panel and reported with its cost.
