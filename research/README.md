# Research foundations and archives

The project's mathematical and architectural research spans quaternions and S³,
icosians and exact golden coefficients, E8 constructions, prime/zeta addressing,
SpiralCore transport, octonions and addressed memory. Start with the
[illustrated geometry](../docs/geometry.md), [mechanism briefs](../docs/mechanisms/README.md)
and [source/research map](../docs/PROJECT_MAP.md).

Large imported research collections are stored outside the source checkout.
Their absence here is not evidence of deletion or of an available local payload.
The [contributor guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md#data-artifacts-and-compute) and
[storage tool](../scripts/storage/research-data) describe recovery.
