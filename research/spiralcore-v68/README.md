## What the HTML implements

The static source review inspected the Prompt Trace handler (line 8822), core
address functions (3673), E8 addressing and operator routing (4530), Bell codec
and route metadata (3200 and 3411), route audio plans (3511), BFS (5140), orbit
tracing (6321), and the Kuramoto research solver (11629–11653). Scripts were not
executed, and fixture claims were not newly reproduced.

- Prompt Trace uses fixed transliteration/value sums and modular coordinates,
  followed by a seven-waypoint BFS route. This is deterministic input projection
  and graph routing; no learned attention or credit-assignment learner was found
  in the inspected mechanisms. Sum/modulo coordinates do not retain arbitrary
  ordered text identity.
- Typed IPv4-shaped addresses and documentation-only IPv6 mirrors retain node
  type, identity, and declared demarc context. They are application-local
  representations, not IP packet forwarding or a semantic-distance measure.
- Bell multi-frequency encoding uses fifteen pairs drawn from six frequencies
  (700–1700 Hz), with separate R1 and No. 5 profiles. Layer events, actual AXIS2
  ports, H2-root events, and E8 operator actions have distinct meanings. These
  frequencies encode declared labels, not learned token relevance; equal
  cardinality does not identify subnets, operators, or semantic classes.
- Route records retain actual actions, graph epochs, context, and selection
  policy. Orbit traces calculate geometric closure under a specified rotation;
  closure is not evidence of recurrent language memory.
- The bounded Kuramoto solver implements sine-coupled phase dynamics and
  floating-point RK4 on declared carriers. It is research simulation, not a
  derivative of the current hard H4 transition/read model, a text-learning
  objective, or an established discrete serving export. Its observable limits
  include global synchronization magnitude being blind on the pi lift.

## What the FBS reference contributes

The selected PDF passages distinguish data that a reduced representation retains
from data that it loses. Printed page numbers below differ from physical PDF page
numbers because of the front matter.

- **Printed page 11, physical page 16:** typed binder ancestry and terminal
  coordinates recover ordered support lengths and atomic tau, but do not recover
  the parameter g at fixed tau. Two ancestry matches that factor through the
  same tree isomorphism are not independent confirmations of an external model.
- **Printed page 34, physical page 39:** the reference reports nine local A5
  moves and larger reachable sets after composition, but explicitly states that
  native FBS has no canonical field selecting one move at each promotion rung.
  A lawful action space does not supply a learned selector.
- **Printed page 35, physical page 40:** the reported route audit distinguishes
  all lawful routes from the representative chosen by adjacency-order BFS. It
  reports one pair with 80 equal-shortest routes but only 34 display signatures.
  Those external computational counts were not independently reproduced here.
  The important proposed distinction is between intrinsic history and its
  possibly colliding display observation.
- **Printed pages 35–36, physical pages 40–41:** the proposed product state
  `(geometric route position, FBS frontier state)` keeps route motion and closure
  promotion separate. The document classifies this as architectural consistency,
  not proof that geometry realizes FBS or that FBS controls SpiralCore routing.
