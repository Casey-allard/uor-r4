UOR-R4

Checked against its sources at d5eb4a556 (2026-11-14); 3 later commits change its sources.

What is still open

M1 answered a narrow question: can a saved discrete geometric model beat two simple count tables on next-token prediction, through its integer export and at two seeds? Yes, at the registered panel bars. The larger open question is whether one joined model can start and complete correct replies on new phrasing, remember across sessions, and run as one product. That remains unproven.

M1 is complete (1 of 8). Since 10 Nov the work is recall in controlled conversations, and almost every fact the model gets right comes from the turn memory's table. Until then the blocker was reply opening on new phrasing, not the count-table bar.

Terms follow the glossary. Claims are proof, measurement or hypothesis.

Answered at M1's scope

The saved 19-table uor-r4.lut-discrete-trunk/1 model passes all six bars on two seeds:

  • self-contained UORLUT01 artifacts load in the clean-room D11 reader;
  • DEV 7,002 / 7,001 of 14,287 against unigram 1,226 and T2 6,300;
  • Fresh V2 7,215 / 7,228 of 14,479 against unigram 1,279 and T2 6,582;
  • p-f 7,850 / 7,872 of 15,953 against unigram 1,409 and T2 7,375, beyond paired band 81;
  • exact 14,287-row DEV export/reference agreement and B4;
  • Apple M1 decode 10,876 / 10,566 tok/s under the bar-f protocol.

p-f has no prompt value-masked templates in TRAIN at K = 1,024, so it tests new phrasings. But 62.5% of its reference replies are verbatim TRAIN replies: M1 reads new prompts into known reply kinds, not new reply kinds or useful conversation (M1 close, Fresh V2, bar-f report).

The central unknown: correct reply opening on new phrasing

Claude-217 gives the sharpest current measurement:

  • Teacher-forced p-s reply openings are 17.4%, 13.7% and 18.6% at three seeds, against 80.8%, 80.2% and 79.9% on later tokens.
  • Exact-reference replies are only 3, 1 and 4 of 256, against 38–40 on DEV.
  • Exact turn memory keys on the turn's exact first token ids. It adds 322–337 of 1,226 openings on DEV but only 14–16 of 1,400 on p-s.
  • The cycle step ends every registered loop, yet every content decision is underpowered and greedy remains the decode.

So a loop-free reply is not necessarily a correct reply. Claude-218 found trunk-state keys that improved openings offline. Claude-219 then served the point key for the first reply token in free generation on fresh p-v: templates improve by +13/+8/+12 and exact replies by +9/+8/+12 at three seeds. It reaches only 12–18% of the forced-opening lift; the sign key is inconclusive at this panel size. Claude-220 then served the point key inside one float model on fresh p-w: templates improve by +41/+40/+42 at three seeds with exact claude-219 parity, while recalling TRAIN replies rather than generalizing. Claude-222 has built the integer point table, search and /8 record, with 380 library tests and a full serving audit. Claude-224 has built the panel decode and grade. Claude-227 passed retention GPU parity within the 3% bar; claude-228 built the run's reference/read-back/BOS checks; claude-229 found an instrument fault: the integer session uses the native writer instead of the trained whole-table nearest readout, so all grade pairs are (0, 0). Claude-231 built the nearest readout and export on native and browser paths; claude-232 then missed greedy parity in every cell. Since 10 Nov the work is recall in controlled conversations: claude-376's pure-vector baseline found 377, 380 and 441 exact replies of 2,218 as exported, but 171, 173 and 211 with the exact turn memory dropped. The open question is whether a state carried across training windows (V1, PR #3979) or vector-native reads (V2(a), PR #3978) can carry those facts; both are in progress. Useful conversation remains unproven.

What remains open beyond that key

  1. Complete correct replies and durable memory (M2/M3). Can one unified model answer from exact memory on DEV and fresh prompts, preserve provenance, accept corrections, abstain, reset and restart with token-identical continuation?
  2. Independent mechanism effects. Which read, gate, route, pointer and memory choice causes the M1 result? A whole-model pass is not an ablation for each mechanism.
  3. A joined model and full serving (M4). Can the accepted model pass its full UORLUT01 export, quality, CLI, session and audit path? PRLL is built and registered, but GPU held-out scoring is open.
  4. Cost and scale (M5 and beyond). What are the full-path latency, memory traffic and energy per token? Arithmetic counts do not answer them. How does ability scale with data, width and context?
  5. Distribution (M7). Do library, CLI and browser produce bit-identical tokens and sessions on the accepted model, with a local-by-default Studio and only an explicit user-configured web API?
  6. Generalisation beyond known reply kinds. p-f holds out prompt phrasing but shares most reference replies with TRAIN. A stronger test needs novel reply kinds and correct content, not just new n-grams or loop suppression.

Older questions, now closed at their declared scopes

These should not be repeated as if untouched:

  • Can a discrete model beat unigram and T2 through D11? Yes, at M1's two-seed bars.
  • Does a table-native discrete exporter exist, and is the M1 export exact? Yes: 14,287-row DEV agreement at both seeds and B4 pass.
  • Does a lawful likelihood exist for later BPB scoring? PRLL is built and registered. Its GPU held-out scoring is the open part.
  • Does the Studio connect to the integer engine? Yes. It verifies a synthetic stand-in and also serves the publicly released M1 trunk as top-8 next-token prediction, not chat. Accepted-model parity is still open.
  • Does every training step need a typed owner release? No. D67 uses the lab's own pre-run review; D41's donor pass is the named owner-at-dispatch exception.
  • Does BVG-K only have construction tests? No. Stage T training and the M1 lineage used it.
  • Does exact storage prove learned memory? No. The true-address 80/80 result remains oracle scope, not learned address formation.

Failed configurations, not closed mechanisms

K16-A's 65 and 463, K16-R's 19 or 20 cells, A8's admission erosion, banned softmax/matmul recipes, panel fitting, and the older dense table-serving energy result remain useful negative evidence. They close configurations, not mechanism families, and they do not erase the M1 close. Read the original records before re-running any of them.
