UOR-R4

Research line: grounded replies from exact memory (M2)

This line tried to make the model answer a question from a fact it was told earlier, and to say "I don't know" when it was never told. Facts sit in an exact store, so storing and fetching are not the hard part. The hard part is getting a trained model to read the right fact and write it out. One model reached 437 of 512 correct replies on the rows the team had been tuning on, then 0 of 128 on fresh rows.

What it set out to do

Build the second capability after a language base: a reply that is correct because it came from an exact record, not from a guess. Exact storage is easy and was never the problem; an exact store scored 1.000 whenever its register was right (exact memory dossier). The line tested which learned reader or writer could use it.

What happened, in order

  1. September. Fifteen records dated 3 Sep are source audits and acceptance reviews of a text-to-clause adapter and a native bridge. A pre-registered exact-pair cache lowered the count reference by 0.238 bits per target on exposed project Markdown, not general prose (result). An always-on exact-store read failed its gate on all three seeds, with held-out accuracy of 0.03 to 0.16 against a 0.90 bar (G1). A diagnostic put 77.7 percent of held-out failures at query-entity tagging (decomposition).
  2. 1 to 5 Oct. Adapting only the readouts raised complete own-prefix source replies from 4/20 to 18/20 (coadapt), yet a prospectively frozen fresh panel gave 0/8 (frozen transfer).
  3. 9 Oct. Traces showed why stored numbers failed. The copy pointer attended the repeated sentence frame, never the second digit of the value (0 of 13 rows), and the 4,096-piece vocabulary has no multi-digit tokens (positional read). Several attempts to build legal parameter changes with an exact solver stopped on numeric errors, which are blockers, not negatives.
  4. 10 Oct. Whole-answer replies on the 512-row development panel went 8, 22, 145, 175, 177, then 437 after training each token against its strongest wrong rival (260 gains and no losses on that last step) (pooled rank). Five other recipes lowered it (8 to 2, 8 to 4, 8 to 0 and 145 to 139 replies). The saved 437 model then scored 0/128 against a bar of 52 on fresh role and value combinations (fresh).
  5. 10 to 12 Oct. Training the read to land on the asked value was flat at first (19/40 against 20/40). A powered rerun over three seeds cut wrong-value failures from 47 to 30 of 120, and at four times the length from 49 to 23 (scaled). Both met their bars, so each was a KEEP (target met without a significant loss elsewhere), but on a softmax read. A whole-path source reference was built; CUDA execution has not been run (NOT_RUN) (recovery). The softmax ban (D23, 12 Oct) and the M2 training hold stopped further runs.

What it taught

Development is not fresh (lesson 4 in what the project knows). An exact log retrieves; the learned pointer copied recency (lesson 9). Chat failures came from missing knowledge and fluency, not retrieval (lesson 10), and binding failures came from the recipe (lesson 20). Hard selection needs a training bridge (lesson 12). On 10 Oct the three-negatives rule (D21) fired, and D22 ruled that a negative closes a configuration only.

Current state

M2 is held. These mechanisms are PARKED, each with the conditions that would reopen it: exact memory, read binding, pointer key, carrier credit, legal construction and native geometric. The next step in the exact-memory dossier is to build the address itself as the read, with an oracle test first. The durable store lives in the M3 line.
