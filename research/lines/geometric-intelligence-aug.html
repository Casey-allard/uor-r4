UOR-R4

Research line: the August geometric intelligence programme

In August the project asked a basic question: can the geometry it likes, rotations in four dimensions (R4/Spin coordinates) and hyperbolic (Lorentz) space, carry the work of attention in a language model? The programme (issue #973) reproduced ordinary attention exactly inside these coordinates, which is a useful parity check, but never found a case where the geometry did better than a plain control. This line holds its nine records. Its questions moved into the September native model.

Parity was achieved; advantage was not.

What it set out to do

Replace or enrich the standard transformer's attention with geometry from the project's own toolbox, using a small donor model (SmolLM2) as the reference. A positive result would have meant a measurable gain over a matched control that uses the same parameters and no geometry.

What happened, in order

  1. 25 to 26 Aug: a decoder plan and a mixer. A one-layer mixer distilled from a donor layer beat a coordinate-permuted control by 4.44% against a 5% gate, verdict REDESIGN_REPRESENTATION (record). A layer-29 geometric spike passed its first gate with 32 cycle-free tokens and 4/5 on a control review, promoted to the next gate with no quality claim (spike). The decoder roadmap records that its next gate (G1) ended in REDESIGN_REPRESENTATION (plan).
  2. 28 Aug. Retention with a geometric gate scored 16/28 against 23/28 for plain gated delta on a synthetic fixture (record).
  3. 29 Aug. Intrinsic Lorentz attention was stopped before held-out data. Its covariance residual was 9.12e-8 against a frozen ceiling of 1e-8, and the curved arm trailed the flat one (record). A learned Lorentz manifold had cross-entropy 7.71 against 4.48 for a flat Euclidean arm and 3.67 for the donor (record).
  4. 30 Aug. A tangent value readout was 1.0644 times worse than a normalised-centroid readout, and a plain Euclidean score beat the Lorentz score as a diagnostic (record). A preflight for finite Lorentz score radii was parked and never run (record).
  5. Undated. The full decoder with ordinary causal softmax run through coherent R4/Spin frames matched the donor's logits to a maximum absolute difference of 1.05e-5 over 196,608 logits, with no R4 advantage (record).

What it taught

Exact algebra reproduces ordinary behaviour perfectly but carries no meaning on its own (lesson 8 in what the project knows). Fixed geometry usually tied or lost to a matched control (lesson 6). The Lorentz idea returned in September, where a Lorentz read beat a dot read by 0.018 nats on code in 3 of 4 seeds before it lost when adapted (stack line). Each negative closes only its exact configuration (decision D22).

Current state

Closed. The programme record survives as inactive evidence.
