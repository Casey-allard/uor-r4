Geometric recurrence gates

In plain words

A recurrent layer keeps a running memory and decides at each step how fast old content fades and how much new input gets in. The old recipe used smooth curves for those decisions (sigmoid, exponential, tanh), and the project's rules now ban them. The replacement opens the input with a clamped straight line, picks the fade rate from a short table of powers of two, and carries the memory forward by a rotation. Each step is a table read and a rotation, so nothing smooth is needed. It is a drop-in for the gates of one particular recurrent design, not a standard part of a transformer.

What it replaces in a transformer

A plain transformer has no recurrence, so this replaces nothing there directly. It belongs to the recurrent alternative to attention used in the project's older float stack. In that design it replaces four squashing functions inside the recurrence: a sigmoid opening, an exponential and softplus decay rate, and a tanh-shaped output gate.

A table of 16 fixed clocks replaces a smooth dial. Each step multiplies the old state by the chosen rate and a rotation, and adds new input scaled by the matching keep factor.

How it works

The trunk's sequence mixer is a recurrence over lanes of four numbers (quaternions). From the source (geometric_stack.rs), one step per lane is \[h_t \;=\; \lambda_t\,u_t\otimes h_{t-1}\;+\;\kappa_t\,b_t,\] where \(u_t\) is a unit quaternion built from the gate projections (the rotation), \(b_t\) is the convolved input drive, and \(\otimes\) is the quaternion product. Four squashing sites of the historical form are replaced in the mode rec_gates=geometric:

  1. Opening (Definition). \(s_t=\mathrm{clamp}(g_t+d,\,0,\,1)\), where \(g_t\) is the gate projection and \(d\) is a per-lane decay parameter read raw, with no softplus. The clamp has an exact subgradient.
  2. Rate (Definition). The index is \(i=\min(\lfloor 16\,s_t\rfloor,\,15)\). The rate and the keep factor are read from two 16-entry tables: \[\lambda=2^{-(i+1)},\qquad \kappa=\sqrt{1-\lambda^2}.\] The rates are exact powers of two, from \(1/2\) down to \(2^{-16}\), and \(\kappa\) is precomputed beside each one, so serving needs only table reads. The pair \((\lambda,\kappa)\) lies on the unit circle up to \(6\times10^{-8}\) (measurement; the last four \(\kappa\) entries round to exactly 1 in 32-bit floats).
  3. Carry (unchanged). The quaternion scan above.
  4. Output gate (Definition). The Hamilton rotation gate on the recurrence output, replacing a tanh-shaped gate.

Credit through a hard choice (Definition). Picking a table entry has zero derivative almost everywhere, so nothing would train without a bridge. The backward pass uses a declared secant surrogate: it pairs the incoming adjoints for \((\lambda,\kappa)\) with the full counterfactual change in the table between the selected entry and each neighbour, and resolves the contrast along the fixed edge of length \(1/16\), half to each side and the whole weight to the single neighbour at either end. No probability or exponential is involved. It is a rule, not the derivative of hard selection. The 256-step descent check passed, and the later 1,024-step six-recurrence fit and D11 export ran with mixed transport/export-gap outcomes.

A reading of the source (proof from the table, not a recorded finding). Every table entry satisfies \(\lambda\le\tfrac12\), so a step keeps at most half of the old state times a rotation. The historical rate \(\lambda=\exp(8\,\sigma(g)\log\sigma(d))\) can approach 1 as the opening goes to 0. Whether a table capped at one half per step keeps enough long memory is untested.

The historical mode refused non-GPU devices, export and quantisation-aware training. The pre-registered fit and D11 export have now run; lut-stack/5 carries the FBS recurrence with exact rotations and the export gap inside its bar at DEV scope. The historical mode stays the default so old artifacts reproduce.

Rule check.

  • D11 (integer serving): designed for it, since the rates are table reads, the opening is a clamp and the scan is rotations. The FBS lut-stack/5 export now carries this geometric recurrence with exact rotations and the export gap inside its bar at DEV scope; the older blocked-export note is historical.
  • D23 (no softmax or squashing): a grep on 13 October found 0 squash-family tokens in the geometric arms (the historical branches keep 8 by design).
  • D25/D30 (no dense matrix multiplication): a clamp, a table read, a scan and a per-block rotation. The no-matmul and no-softmax counters were unchanged by the delivering PR.
  • D32/D37: a float operator. D32 froze it and D37 allows it back only by a new offline sheet (decisions). The saved discrete M1 model carries a separate DiscreteQuaternionRecurrenceV1; these READY float recurrence gates are not that M1 component and belong to the FBS path.

Reading these honestly. The failures are failures of other configurations: an unbounded dense state, a 28-event fixture, a quantisation recipe on the continuous learner, one paired seed with a control that is not worse. The quaternion-against-identity result (+0.07 nats) shows transport matters; the quaternion-against-reflection tie shows rotation is not yet shown better. The 25 October rows establish (1) in #2886, that the 16-entry dyadic secant surrogate (rate_select_bwd) crosses discrete rate boundaries (>30% of decay parameters) and yields genuine descent beating both zero-credit and norm-matched Rademacher random-refit controls beyond the two-seed noise band, and (2) in #2890, that at 1,024 steps on mw-balp/train, the 16-entry dyadic recurrence gates with identity transport (Rec_Geometric_IdentityTransport_Control) and commutative \(U(1)\) transport (Rec_Geometric_U1_AbelianControl) shift rate bins on 49.2%–57.3% of lanes and beat both Rec_FrozenRates_Null and the train-split unigram count table (1,226 / 14,287) on both seeds in both float and D11 integer export (106k–113k D11 int tok/s), whereas unconstrained 4D \(S^3\) unit-quaternion carry (Rec_Geometric_S3_Active) exhibits seed-1 top-1 instability and direct post-training Q8/Q12 state rounding loses 221–1,914 top-1 rows over 192 causal steps without hard-forward discrete state snapping during training.

What has never been tested

  • Hard-forward discrete state/gate snapping (QAT) during six-recurrence training (#2890 tested unconstrained float forward training followed by post-training D11 integer table export).
  • Whether 16 table entries are fine enough beyond 1,024 steps compared with a learned continuous decay (256-step secant descent passed in #2886; 1,024-step U1 and IdentityTransport fits passed in #2890).
  • A fused geometric recurrence kernel and a half-precision margin kernel (I-Standalone-CUDA-Parity qualified rate_select_fwd/bwd and quaternion_scan_fwd/bwd in #2890).
  • The quaternion arm against its Householder control with more than one paired seed on the geometric-gates trunk (#2890 tested \(S^3\) against \(U(1)\) and identity transport across two seeds).

What would reopen it

The gates are READY, so these are the steps to make them ACTIVE.

  1. A new D37 offline-float sheet (separately scanned module, fresh test-fitness review, pre-run review) that runs the six-recurrence fit with rec_gates=geometric (#2890, now unblocked by #2886). Ran in #2890 (record): Rec_Geometric_IdentityTransport_Control and Rec_Geometric_U1_AbelianControl beat Rec_FrozenRates_Null and TrainSplit_CountTable_Ref (1,226 / 14,287) on both seeds in D11 integer export, while unconstrained Rec_Geometric_S3_Active failed seed-1 top-1 (G-Rec-Sheet-Fit = FAIL). Reopening for ACTIVE status requires a follow-on sheet with hard-forward discrete state/gate snapping during training (closing the 221–1,914-row multi-step recurrence rounding gap) and stabilized transport (IdentityTransport or \(U(1)\), or icosian-snapped \(S^3\)).
  2. A device qualification that names these kernels at full-rung scale (registered as I-Standalone-CUDA-Parity in recurrence-gates-six-recurrence-fit-2026-10-25.md). Answered in #2890 (I-Standalone-CUDA-Parity = PASS across both clean nvcc builds, record).
  3. Evidence that the secant surrogate gives descent: a falling loss, or a comparison with a random-refit control. Answered in #2886 (G-Rec-Descent = PASS, record).
  4. Fresh transfer (G-Rec-Transfer on fresh_v2), at least 2 seeds and the export gap (G-Rec-ExportGap) within the two-seed top-1 noise band on both clean builds under hard-forward discrete recurrence snapping.

Open questions: do hard-selected rates train stably under the secant credit or does the table plateau; does rotation beat reflection as the carry at scale and under a finite-precision export; is a recurrence the right sequence mixer at all, now that the active model reads context through the routed and flock reads; and what the export gap looks like. These are hypotheses.
