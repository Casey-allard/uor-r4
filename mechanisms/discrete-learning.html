Discrete geometric learning (exact-move counterfactual training)

Status: ACTIVE. It is the learning rule of the live discrete model path, crates/uor-r4-discrete. The pilot, K=8 dose, four K16 runs and Relation stops are preserved as negative or diagnostic history. Stage T and the later 19-table joint-pointer Hamilton trunk then produced the saved M1 model: DEV 7,002 / 7,001, Fresh V2 7,215 / 7,228 and p-f 7,850 / 7,872 at two seeds, with exact 14,287-row DEV export agreement. That is a whole-model M1 result, not an independent ablation for this learning rule or useful chat. On p-f, 62.5% of reference replies are verbatim TRAIN replies: reference-data overlap, not a generated-copy rate or new reply kinds. New training goes through the lab's pre-run review (D67), not a blanket owner hold (M1 close, Fresh V2, bar-f).

Labels used below: proof means derived from definitions or code, measurement means a recorded run, hypothesis means an untested idea. See docs/formal_vocabulary.md.

In plain words

The model is a big table of small whole-number vectors, one per word, plus a few routing choices. To teach it, the machine picks one table entry and tries every allowed rotation of it, 16 options counting "leave it alone". For each option it replays a batch of training examples and counts the mistakes with whole-number arithmetic. It keeps the best option only if that is strictly better than leaving the entry alone. There are no gradients, no decimals and no matrix multiplication. Every step stays inside the values the finished model can hold. Earlier forms moved counts without producing better language; the later 19-table model passed M1, but that whole-model result does not isolate this rule's independent effect.

What it replaces in a transformer

The optimiser. A transformer is trained by backpropagation: it computes a smooth slope for every weight and nudges all of them a little. Here nothing is smooth. Learning is a search over a short list of legal moves for one coordinate at a time, scored by a replay of real examples.

One learning step on one table entry: try all 16 legal states, pick the cheapest, commit only on strict improvement, then check the stored result.

How it works

State (definition). A shared integer codebook maps each of the \(V=4096\) tokens to \(B=4\) blocks of 8 integers. Each block is one of 16 roots of a single orbit of the E8 lattice (a fixed set of 16 integer vectors; not the full set of 240). The model also holds, for each routing stage, one of 64 signed-axis rotations of 8-dimensional space (the SpiralCore group), and optional gates that take the values \(-1\), \(0\) or \(+1\). Each of these is a coordinate: a vector block, a route frame or a gate.

Read and write (definition). A read turns the token window into an integer vector \(q\). A shared writer gives each token a cell from the root numbers of its first and last block (\(16\times16=256\) cells), keeps the \(K\) nearest occupied cells to \(q\) by L1 distance (admission), and picks the nearest token inside them. The same table encodes input, carries memory and ranks output.

Loss (definition). The loss is the integer rank-margin hinge. Per row, with \(T\) the token hinge and \(A\) the address hinge:

\[\ell = \frac{8\,T + w\,A}{8w},\qquad w = 8B = 32 \text{ in the lineage of record.}\]

Learning step (definition). For one coordinate \(\theta\) and a batch of rows \(\mathcal R\) whose eligibility is fixed by the baseline:

\[L(m)=\sum_{x\in\mathcal R}\ell\big(x;\ \theta\leftarrow m\big),\qquad m\in\{\mathrm{id},m_1,\dots,m_{15}\}.\]

The 15 non-identity moves are bivector rotations. The step picks \(m^*=\arg\min_m L(m)\). Ties go to the identity, then to the lowest resulting port number, then to the lowest enumeration index. It applies \(m^*\) only if \(L(m^*)<L(\mathrm{id})\), then re-validates the stored table on the GPU. Nothing is sampled, and no floating-point number appears anywhere in the step.

Consequence (proof, from the definition). Every committed move strictly lowers the loss on its own batch, and the new state is always a legal state. The pilot's receipt audit confirmed this on every accepted move (measurement, pilot record). The step says nothing about rows outside the batch. A move that helps its batch could push other targets out of the shortlist; that is a hypothesis consistent with the Q1 stops below, and causal attribution was not made.

Speed-ups. The row-parallel kernel (rows_v1) computes the baseline once per row and each candidate once per row and lane, instead of replaying every row serially per candidate. It reproduces the serial losses on every checked case (measurement: Route read 3.843 s to 0.0691 s per proposal, 55.6 times faster, 64 rows; Route-mode index coverage was widened after review). BVG-K (batched verified coordinates) scores up to 64 coordinates per launch and commits a block only if its joint loss beats the best single move.

Rule check. No softmax, exp, softplus, sigmoid or tanh (D23), and no matrix multiplication (D30): the choice is an integer minimum, distances are integer L1 and Hamming, and moves are table lookups. The saved 19-table discrete model exports through uor-r4.lut-discrete-trunk/1 with exact 14,287-row DEV agreement and B4 pass, and serves at 10,876 / 10,566 native decode tok/s on Apple M1 under the bar-f remeasurement. D37 allows offline float training; that needs a separate scanned module and a reported export gap.

What the negatives do and do not say. Under D22 a negative closes one configuration, never the mechanism (D22). The pilot diagnosis found that a fixed 8-cell access budget reached fewer target cells (5,680 to 2,180) while the address hinge rose, and that per-move causal attribution was unobserved. Separately, it found a start with only 16 distinct vectors: a coupled-index initialisation defect, not an E8 capacity result. A later census found K16 training had broken much of that aliasing (ceiling about 9,000 rows) while top-1 stayed at 65 and 463, so aliasing does not explain the K16 ranking failure (K16 outcome). In Stage 1 capacity qualification, within-cell tie mass (tokens_in_alias_groups == 0 and dev_rows_targeting_non_representative == 0 in coverage_census.rs) is enforced as an explicit gate alongside the alias ceiling (G-Cap, \(\min(\text{dev\_alias\_ceiling\_rows}, \text{full\_admitted}) > 1,941\)). On P_succ under CellEnvelopeL1V1, widening from \(B = 4\) (1,980 / 3,847) to \(B = 6\) (1,901) or \(B = 8\) (1,965) does not increase P_succ cell admission (CellEnvelopeL1V1 P_succ reach is already saturated at \(B = 4\)), whereas widening to \(B \in \{6, 8\}\) with multi-pair residual bidirectional pull (WiderBlocksV1) lifts full-DEV admission (5,709 to 6,302..6,353), lifts FullVectorCellL1V1 P_succ admission at \(B = 8\) (1,802 to 1,977), and reverses the checkpoint-0 Top-1 dip (118 at \(B=4\) to 295 at \(B=6\) and 240 at \(B=8\)) at a linear decode-speed cost (831 to 643 and 527 tokens/s). For the Q1 stops, the admission pathway is a source-linked hypothesis, not a causal attribution or a mechanism verdict: acceptance looks only at the batch's own loss, so an accepted move could eject targets from their cells (the A8 record states this as a risk before measurement, and the K16 outcome keeps ranking capacity, the read and the schedule open). The owner stopped Dose 1 on the slow path before Q1; that is not a result.

What has never been tested

  • Route-frame and gate learning in the pilot (only vector blocks 0 to 1,639 moved).
  • A collision-free start together with a wide enough shortlist (\(K\) of about 34 or more, or cells placed by address structure).
  • The next admission-preserving rerun after the full-dose run reached Q1 and stopped at batch 5,248; its outcome is NOT_RUN. The M1 whole-model pass does not isolate the placement's effect.
  • Frequent-token revisits and a full corpus pass (one 16,404-coordinate pass uses 1,049,856 of 2,425,925 training targets).
  • The independent effects of BVG-K and Flock reads at dose; both were used in the M1 composition without isolated attribution.
  • All 240 E8 roots, the icosian and quaternion adapters, and \(\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]\) pairs.
  • Useful generated conversation, complete correct replies and M2 acceptance. M1 has exact 14,287-row DEV export agreement and Apple M1 decode measured, but that is not useful chat.
  • Fresh-slice transfer for the older runs; M1's Fresh V2 and p-f transfer have now been measured.
  • A float credit sheet on the same geometry (D37 allows it; none is registered).

Related mechanisms

rank-margin is the loss each step minimises. route-read, flock-read and relation-read supply frames and gates as coordinates. admission is the shortlist whose erosion drives most stops. prime-zeta-codebook and address-cells replace the 16-vector start. spiralcore-fbs supplies the group and tree. legal-construction is the older float attempt at the same goal. carrier-credit contributed the shared token table.
