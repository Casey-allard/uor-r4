# Extended goal for any joining UOR-R4 lab

Join the UOR-R4 Geometric Language Model as a continuing autonomous research and
engineering lab. The goal is a useful laptop model for conversation, exact durable
memory, reasoning and executable coding, ultimately competitive with frontier
models. Advance that goal through investigation, source implementation, decisive
experiments, independent review and protected GitHub delivery, then take the
next ready dependency. Do not stop after one issue or require another “proceed”
for already authorized work. Do not promise continuous reasoning after your
client loses tokens: leave recoverable state and durable submitted jobs.

GitHub is authoritative. Read root AGENTS, README and STATUS; `docs/labs/README.md`,
the adopted continuation plan and shared protocol; current DECISIONS and result
pointers; live #820, the relevant epic (#1508 native chat, #1509 offline conversion,
#1510 infrastructure), and their latest comments/open PRs. Read incident #1520
while SSD recovery is active. Refresh main and your actual worktree, jobs, hashes
and resources before accepting a summary. The source/history audit and toolbox
are maps, not substitutes for inspecting your mechanism and closest historical
attempts. Recurse from mission to bottleneck, information, source callers, prior
evidence, proposed change and integration effects. Stop when further context
cannot affect the decision; do not reread the entire repo at every join.

Declare lab/session identity, tools/models, client permissions, subagent and
runner capabilities, quota limits and existing work. Record untested adapters as
UNVERIFIED and manual ones as MANUAL. Reuse/create a standing lab board. There is
no cap on labs, no permanent director and no monopoly by provider. Claim the
highest-value unblocked deliverable with dependencies, source SHA, branch/full
worktree, owned files, inputs, output and next checkpoint. Use atomic coordination
when verified, otherwise an explicit GitHub claim and peer reconciliation. Never
edit the owner checkout, overlap another writer without agreement, or reset
another lab's branch/job. Source work may proceed on verified internal storage
during SSD recovery; new heavy builds/model compute wait for admission.

Ask Socratically: what exactly fails, what state/information is present, what
ordinary alternative explains it, what earlier result bears on it, what causal
change could help, what would refute it, and what decision changes? Delegate
independent investigator, engineer and adversary roles with complete packets and
owned files. Prefer economical capable agents for simple work and expert math,
learning or systems review for difficult claims. Read primary sources and actual
code. Use exact tools such as Wolfram when available for a defined derivation;

physics analogies require an explicit computational mechanism and discriminator.

path and no integer multiply/divide instructions in served kernels. Rust offline
