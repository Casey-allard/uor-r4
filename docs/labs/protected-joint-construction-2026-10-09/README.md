including every Generate and physical Copy alias. A protected role contributes

row order, f64 unit-L2 normalization of every finite nonzero Jacobian, and final

common maximum magnitude, the original fractional masters, existing quarter-grid

actual quantized displacement must also satisfy the scaled residual bound and

one original epoch; choose minimum passing combined CE with earlier-radius tie,

Generate coordinate sweep. This is bounded feasible-direction search, not an
optimal projection or a convergence/feasibility proof. All local constraints remain surrogate screens;
strict native combined/episode descent, all15 conditional winners,17 references,
380 original winners and independent391-state reload remain qualification gates.
A positive construction immediately enters the saved-model own-feedback actual9

Distinct outcomes: native passing joint improvement supports this direction
formation; lost task descent or residual constraint violation is a bounded
solver outcome; a locally admissible vector rejected by native donor/quantized
competition identifies a mismatch at that finite proposal. None proves global
infeasibility. No post-result sweep-count, radius, tolerance or guard change.

## Limits and next decision

This is one original-parent development construction with frozen U, 15 conditional
positions, 17 reference roles and 380 winner guards. It is not held-out language
or geometric-superiority evidence. Guard gradients are native-anchored surrogate
quantities, and finite cyclic projection is neither a nearest-point solver nor
a global feasibility proof. The candidates never reached native scoring; their
actual CE, winner losses and whole-answer quality are unmeasured. The source
reviewers did not independently train or replicate the model result.

**Next:** Attribute the four saved quantized displacements using the original
masters, destination bits, direction and Jacobians. Identify which rounding,
clamping or preserved fractional coordinates reverse the protected residuals,
and whether contributions are concentrated or distributed. That saved analysis
can distinguish a quantizer correction from a discrete transaction-design issue;
it does not authorize another radius/pass sweep, weaker guards or more model
compute without a new causal decision. Merge and clean this piece first.
