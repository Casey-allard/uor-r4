## Objective and current evidence

Build one useful geometric language model that runs on a consumer laptop:
conversation, exact durable memory, executable coding and reasoning. Final
serving obeys D11: no transformer backbone, floating-point numerical computation,
or integer multiply/divide instructions in served kernels. D5 requires selected
parameter access as the end state; low-bit dense maps remain explicitly interim.
Rust offline training and declared local teachers may use floating point and
matrix multiplication. Track B is offline conversion research until its actual
runtime satisfies the same target.

At the source review at main `3bc296ca`, the main-line model could not sustain
useful conversation. R1's development scores were 0.52 responsive, 0.27
instruction and 0.01 relation, with no memory recalls on the ten-request memory
panel. S4's trained-in 2I transport was within its fidelity band (+0.010590 nats,
one seed). Exact stores worked with given keys; forming keys from language and
using in-context retrieval remained open. The stack retained dense MLPs and
causal reads. These are starting facts, not new measurements.

The whole-history review covered a chronology of 3,904 locally reachable
commits, including imported roots from March, and 1,280 commits in that main
ancestry from June. It used source/history audits, major transition records,
current code and live GitHub; it did not inspect every diff or re-execute all
historical artifacts. New labs reuse this map and follow the nearest relevant
history recursively; a full audit is not their startup task.

Key lessons are retained: historical proxy routing and geometry labels did not
establish saved compute or language; the September 8 false qualification was
withdrawn in #1198; D8 replaced disconnected selector tuning with joint language
credit; D9 protected context and stopped unchanged loops; D11 withdrew the
converted-transformer serving exception; D12 preserved geometric candidates
without automatically promoting them. Prime identity is not semantic distance,
an exact finite group state is not lossless arbitrary history, and kernel
arithmetic legality is not a measured energy improvement.

The existing first product milestone remains: 40 responsive multi-turn
conversations, 30 updated-relation conversations and 30 instructions using new
wordings of trained types, with at least 80% in each category; identical greedy
streams after fresh-process reload; declared context/eviction/output limits; and
tokens/s, RSS and whole-system J/token when measured. This bounded milestone
does not establish general reasoning or frontier equivalence. A prospective
panel change creates a new version and decision; it never changes an old verdict.

## Completion and correction

Every completed task publishes its source/result PR, exact evidence status,
cost, remaining limitations and next dependency on GitHub. Protected delivery
and model promotion are separate. A council may prospectively correct working
constraints and strategy under D14, recording reasons, counterarguments,
consequences and rollback; mission, evidence integrity, unique-data preservation
and spending authority remain owner boundaries. The programme has no arbitrary
one-ticket stop and no obligation to consume every available resource. Stop a
particular job at its declared limit, publish a recoverable result, and choose
the next justified action.
