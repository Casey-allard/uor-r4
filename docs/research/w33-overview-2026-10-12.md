## What the corpus is

- **The object:** the totally isotropic points and lines of the symplectic space F₃⁴. That gives 40 points, 40 lines and 240 incident pairs, with collinearity graph SRG(40,12,2,4), spectrum 12¹ 2²⁴ (−4)¹⁵, and Aut = PGSp(4,3) ≅ W(E6) (order 51,840).
- **The corpus:**
  - about 10,900 numbered passes, 55,000 Python test functions and 844 CI workflows;
  - GAP witnesses;
  - 57 Lean 4 files with 307 theorems and no `sorry`. These cover arithmetic lemmas, not the W33 geometry, E8 or H4.
  - SystemVerilog under `rtl/`.

  There is no Rust.
- **Authorship:** the work was built by two parallel agent tracks, Claude and Codex.
- **Evidence tiers:** E (exact), S (simulated), P (physical protocol), C (matched or postdicted) and F (frontier), alongside PROVED, CERTIFIED, CONDITIONAL, OPEN and RETRACTED.

## What holds, and what does not

**Exact finite mathematics (certified by the corpus's own witnesses):**
- clique-complex homology Z⁸¹ and a qutrit CSS code [[240,81,3]]₃;
- exact Smith forms and prime-by-prime gluing;
- a complete modular Hecke-algebra picture;
- the 120-selector 5-class association scheme and its 83-orbital algebra;
- the tetracode glue A₂⁴ + [4,2,3]₃ giving exactly the 240 E8 roots;
- the 120 local axes mapping isometrically onto the 120 E8 root lines (SRG(120,63,30,36));
- a 160-chamber Hecke pair (P, L) with Ω = LP − PL.

**Not established:**
- **Physics constants:** 12 of 14 Standard-Model constant formulas fail the corpus's own σ-test; all physical readings are CONDITIONAL.
- **"W33-RH proof"** (Pass 725, July 2026): the cited theorems do not prove RH, and the claim was not retracted.
- **Moonshine, Leech and "5 orthogonal E8 in Leech"** are mostly withdrawn.
- **A global bijection from W33 edges to E8 roots is obstructed.** Edges form one orbit and roots four; only the 40×3×2 local-axis carrier lifts.
- **HoloBox's "4.2 × 10⁹ VMs in 7 blobs"** is hash-consing of identical subtrees, not measured compression.
- **Test suite:** the tests mostly re-check the corpus's own computed values.

## Method lessons (the corpus's own audit)

- **Rediscovery dominates.** About 22.9% of files (1,308 of 5,708) re-assert an existing result without citing it. The remedy is a result-indexed lookup (`RESULTS_INDEX.md`): search by the formula or number before deriving. This note exists partly because an earlier review existed and had to be found first.
- **Verifiability and novelty anti-correlate.** True claims tended to be classical; novel claims tended to be false.
- **Certificates must be idempotent.** A rerun should reproduce the JSON byte for byte, and CI should fail closed on stale output.
- **Verify the verifier.** The Lean CI passed green while pointed at a nonexistent directory: delete the input and confirm the check goes red.
- **Write retractions into the artifact.** Keep the failure certificate next to the claim.
