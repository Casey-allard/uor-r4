## Concrete engineering continuation

The existing live bank path supplies the needed interface. The joint mode
must restore the accepted recomposition's source and Generate masters rather
than weakening the older loader's different-parent authentication. Every batch
compiles current Context/Potential, prepares current Generate, and binds U to
both. Prototype choices are excluded from accumulated optimizer gradients and
global clipping, as well as from the optimizer itself. Checkpoints rebuild
native source, cue/prefix bindings, Generate and U, then independently reload
that current checkpoint through the production generator.

The meaningful local-credit control compares enabled/disabled U-to-context
credit on the real ContextQ4 graph with nonconstant U, requiring unchanged hard
scores and changed finite transition adjoints. Zero U is the parent-replay
control and legitimately supplies zero extra context credit. Both controls
remain implementation evidence. A changed, independently reloaded checkpoint
precedes the declared construction fit; complete own-prefix behavior decides
whether the candidate advances the model.

The prospective first measurement is one full 512-case pass (64 updates,
batch8, seed1001) from the accepted parent with zero U, categorical factual
credit and RawIdentity score credit. Context, Potential, Generate unary/pair/
bias and U coadapt; prototypes and categorical map stay frozen. This bundle
tests joint adaptation, not the independent effect of each parameter family.
Retain all eight accepted parent successes and gain complete answers before
replacing the parent; report all pair regressions. Net gains with losses remain
a research tradeoff. Report correctly reached factual divergence, continuation,
teacher-prefix diagnostics and EOS separately. Existing exposed rows do not
become held out. Once selected, a candidate proceeds to a newly frozen supported
transfer panel, then shared compiler/store/session integration under #1552.

**Decision:** the recorded successor—joint factual and local-continuation credit
through the existing graph—is justified. The missing work is a connected,
freshly compiled learning path and a complete-answer improvement, rather than
another coefficient-only dose or a new model engine. The accepted
Source48/Generate64 parent remains accepted; the U32/U64 candidates remain
negative complete-answer evidence. The explanation below names what this
intervention can and cannot change.

## Geometry: exact role, information and missing links

| Mechanism | Implemented mathematical role | Boundary for this work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primes / ordered n-lets / exact occurrences | Stable identities, ordered sequences, factor/overlap indices and versioned record identity. | Commutative prime products alone erase order. A prime/hash identity supplies no semantic metric. Current bank candidates retain record, commit, token offset and token ID; U does not replace those identities. |
| Fixed zeta phases | Artifact-bound finite positional/phase basis in older native paths. | Their presence elsewhere does not establish that the current bank/U path consumes them, or a predictive advantage over another basis. No classical RH proof is needed for fixed phases. |
| R4/S3 and full signed H4/2I | Noncommutative composition, inverse and directed relative actions; 120 signed states per lane. | The full signed code preserves distinctions that a quotient can discard. Eight lanes give at most `120^8` configurations, about 55.25 bits; that upper bound says nothing about reachable states, actual entropy or language sufficiency. Exact external memory remains necessary for growing recoverable information. |
| Hopf base, fiber and chirality | Observation quotient plus retained orientation/chart information in the audited native types. | A Hopf base alone is not invertible. Local base/fiber reconstruction needs chart conventions; the bundle is not a global product. Current U scores signed group relations directly, without reducing to a Hopf base or conjugacy class. |
| Exact `Z[phi]` and paired H4 | Exact finite group construction and golden/Galois-coupled coefficient representation with inverse witness. | Project `H4 ⊕ phi H4` is a specific construction, not two freely learned H4 memories; ambient R8 is not synonymous with the E8 lattice. Compiler arithmetic and served table operations have different contracts. |
| Shared typed operators | Learned finite transition/read/action choices, followed by exact table execution. | A lawful action vocabulary does not choose the relevant action without learning. Having a composition table is not evidence of grounded selection or useful output. |

and the current [H4 field implementation](../../crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/learner/geometric_continuation_field.rs).
The historical one-root information bound cannot be applied as though the
present model had only one root: it has multiple lanes, factual bank state and
exact occurrence memory. The converse is also invalid: extra representational

## Current U capacity, cost and the joint-credit rationale

For lane states `c_l`, fixed token prototypes `p_(v,l)` and signed nibble
coefficients `u_(l,r)`, v2 computes, in nats before the common clip:

`U(c,v) = (1/4) * sum_l u_(l, inverse(c_l) * p_(v,l))`.

With eight lanes and `u in [-7,7]`, the range is exactly bounded by +/-14.
There are 960 coefficients / 480 packed bytes. The optimistic independent
per-token envelope admitting every entry target is only a necessary capacity
check: the same coefficients are shared across all rows, tokens and steps.
As a real linear factor family, there are at most 953 identifiable scalar
degrees of freedom, because redistributing constant offsets among eight lane
tables with zero net sum leaves every score unchanged. Fixed data can reduce
rank further. This is a mathematical upper bound, not a measured rank result.

Two consequences identify the next causal question:

1. Equal local-state tuples receive equal U score vectors. Tokens with equal
   prototype tuples receive equal U contributions at every local state.
   Enlarging the coefficient dose cannot undo either collision.
2. Source-swap pairs with the same query and common output prefix necessarily
   have the same local carrier and U deltas: the local branch receives query
   plus prefix, not the answer-bearing source. The factual branch must supply
   their different correct continuations. Applying the same token delta to
   every physical alias preserves same-token alias score differences before
   clipping; it does not learn occurrence selection by itself.

The [current retained measurement](../integration/current-state.md) reports
complete outputs falling 8 -> 5 -> 1 of 512 while first-token correctness rises
8 -> 455 -> 511. This supports learned local entry behavior and a failed
complete-answer intervention. It does not establish that all remaining errors
are routing errors. The joint successor allows factual geometry and local
continuation to coadapt while preserving the full bank, physical aliases,
shared vocabulary and source-swap challenge.

The [live bank graph](../../crates/uor-r4-training/src/geometric_bank_generate.rs)
already exposes `with_continuation_field(...).with_continuation_context_credit(true)`.
The [U learning module](../../crates/uor-r4-training/src/geometric_continuation_learning.rs)
constructs full120 conditional utility for actual hard local-state choices.
Its detached native anchor plus zero-valued gradient terms preserves the
integer forward while supplying a declared surrogate. This is **local
conditional credit**, not an exact derivative through global recurrent argmax
or proof of an improving exported optimizer step. The existing
[`batch` path](../../crates/uor-r4-training/examples/geometric-frozen-map-fit.rs)
recompiles Context/Potential and current Generate before constructing the
factual graph; that is the integration point.

Freeze `generate.prototype_choices` explicitly: the general Generate parameter
map includes it, but U has no prototype adjoint. Rebuild mutually bound
Source/Generate/U snapshots after updates. `FixedContinuationPosition` is
valid only for frozen upstream inputs; reusing it for joint training would
freeze factual states and scores while pretending to train a live graph.

At vocabulary 4096 and eight lanes, the native U scorer performs 32,768
prototype reads, 32,768 relative products, 32,768 coefficient reads and eight
inverses per output step. These are source-derived operation counts, excluding
Generate, factual read, alias pooling, encoding and storage. The current
[`NativeBankGenerator::step`](../../crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/learner/native_bank_generate.rs)
replays query plus the complete actual prefix for its local carrier. For query
length Q and T emitted tokens, local encoding alone visits
`Q*T + T*(T-1)/2` tokens; it is not constant work per output token.
Full120 U state credit processes `8*4096*120 = 3,932,160` conditional entries
per teacher position. One lane's 4096-by-120 F32 tensor is 1.875 MiB; this is
not a peak-memory estimate because autograd and other branches retain work.
The 480-byte field is therefore not a complete model, working-set or efficiency
claim. Native-reference CPU preparation/evaluation and CUDA learning must have
separate measured costs.

## Corrections and falsifiable limits

These findings correct interpretations here without silently rewriting old
evidence or unrelated policy:

- D20's displayed diagnostic box defines `d = loss_geometric - loss_ordinary`
  in nats but labels nonnegative d as success and negative d as a gap. That
  sign is reversed for a loss. Its explicit owner prohibition on retiring
  geometry is clear; use honest metric directions and complete-answer evidence
  rather than applying that inconsistent box mechanically.
- The [literature survey](barrier-assessment-2026-10-05/surveys/literature.md)
  identifies a generic unit-quaternion R4 action with two Householder
  reflections. For nonidentity left multiplication, `L_q-I` has rank four
  because `q-1` is an invertible quaternion; a product of two reflections fixes
  a subspace of dimension at least two. The proposed generic two-reflection
  identification is therefore false. No DeltaProduct equivalence is adopted.
- The [retired survey](barrier-assessment-2026-10-05/surveys/retired.md) calls
  additive predecessor mixing a truncation of ordered group products. That is
  an analogy, not an algebraic identity. Its predicted reuse needs an actual
  causal test. The old literature recommendations are not a new schedule.
- A mean Hamming distance near eight among eight 120-category lanes is not
  evidence that learned H4 readout is impossible: independent uniform lanes
  already have mean mismatch `8*119/120 = 7.9333`. Equality distance discards
  directed relative-action structure. Exact collisions with incompatible
  required outputs can prove a representation obstruction; an arbitrary weak
  nearest-neighbor metric cannot. Probe results must name the exact factual
  or local carrier and source artifact they observed.

The first joint rung must demonstrate nonzero, correctly routed credit and a
finite optimizer/export/reload witness through both branches, with unchanged
forward parity at admission. A positive gradient alone is insufficient.
Prospective decisions use complete own-prefix answers/EOS and rowwise
retained/gained/lost outputs, with separate entry, factual-divergence and
value-progression diagnoses. If entry improves but complete outputs do not,
the candidate remains failed and the next repair follows the observed
continuation failure. If native outputs do not follow a claimed learning
change, fix the discretization/binding boundary before interpreting model
quality. Fresh transfer, session integration, broader language and D5/energy
qualification remain separate uncompleted dependencies.

Unverified here: independently rerun historical numbers; literal rank and
collision census of the current 512-state design; whether joint credit improves
an exported candidate; its prospective transfer; useful open conversation;
full-path multiplier/float/parameter-access compliance and measured M1 energy;
rendered SpiralCore behavior; new literature results beyond the cited retained
source survey. None is inferred from compilation, a source definition or a
transport CI acknowledgement.

Main advanced during execution to
`c2a41a3047af22c77164886ab5b0d0a89c7b0c3a`

ordinal counts9/4/3 versus8/3/5 are not identical; even identical marginals do
not establish paired invariance under a question intervention. Every coordinate
changing under question and content demonstrates a lack of coordinatewise
factorization on those pairs, not an inability to decode their joint encoding.
A reversible mixing can entangle both factors in every coordinate. Near-uniform
Hamming distance is not an impossibility result for relative-H4 learning.

The useful existing-data discriminator pairs packets with the same complete
source bank, order, entity and prefix while changing only the question. Compare
selected record/occurrence identities, not merely ordinals or marginal counts,
and judge the expected relation afterward. Analyze fixed-question assignment
swaps separately. If existing rows lack those matches, retain the missing
comparison explicitly. Same-question clustering is not a universal acceptance
gate: factual answers can require different source occurrences across entities.
Empty-prefix entry and later factual continuation also require distinct
information; this diagnostic must not conflate them.

Before seeing the joint64 outcome, mathematical review fixed the following
conditional discriminator. First authenticate complete row outcomes and actual
U quarter codes. If U remains zero, investigate the measured quantization
boundary. If entry fails with nonzero U, the failure precedes any wrong emitted
feedback; a matched final Source/Generate with learned versus authenticated
zero U can isolate its contribution without retraining. If entry succeeds but
factual continuation fails, inspect paired source swaps at the common correct
prefix using full bridge occurrence traces. Correct text followed by overrun
isolates stopping only for those rows. Saved master motion or aggregate CE
cannot causally separate simultaneous Source/Generate/U changes. No additional
rate, dose, seed or selector search follows automatically from a negative.
