UOR-R4 — Criterion 2: quantisation, engine and model quality separated on identical positions
Date: 2026-10-09. DeepSeek lab. References #2029.

THE QUESTION
  M1 criterion 2 wants the exported, served artifact at <= 0.90 BPB on the chat held-out
  stream. The recorded position is "float 0.877, served 0.933, quantisation 0.00883". That is
  a 0.0552 BPB gap, and the 0.0330 "gap to target" is a subtraction of a FLOAT number from a
  SERVED number taken on a different position set. This round asks what part of that is
  quantisation, what part is engine/protocol, and what part is the float model's own quality.

WHAT WAS DONE

  checkpoint through its own forward pass is evaluation, not generation). Every number below
  comes from an existing artifact on an identical position set.

WHY THE ABSOLUTE NUMBER MOVES AND THE QUANTISATION TERM DOES NOT
  64 windows:   per-window float mean nll sd 0.9758 nats = 0.503 BPB
                se(mean) 0.1220 nats = 0.063 BPB
  512 windows:  per-window float mean nll sd 0.8709 nats = 0.444 BPB
                se(mean) 0.0385 nats = 0.0196 BPB  (re-sampling se for a 64-window draw)
  The 64-window and 512-window slices differ by 0.029 BPB on the same model, which is inside
  the 0.063 BPB re-sampling se of the smaller slice. The two window sets share only their
  first window (strides 96784 and 12074).
  The quantisation term is ~50x less position-sensitive: 0.00804 +- 0.00274 nats (64 w) and
  0.00755 +- 0.00093 nats (512 w) per window, i.e. 0.00409 +- 0.00141 BPB and
  0.00384 +- 0.00049 BPB. It is small and it is stable; the absolute score is neither.

WHAT THIS DOES TO THE RECORDED ARITHMETIC
  1. The 0.0552 BPB "served gap" is 0.0088 of quantisation measured on identical positions
     and 0.0464 of pure position-set difference. On matched positions the gap is 0.004-0.009
     BPB in two independent cells. The engine's own contribution is ~1e-6 BPB.
  2. The 0.0330 "gap to target" is float 0.877 (512-window fused float) minus served 0.933
     (64-window integer) minus 0.00883. It subtracts numbers from different position sets and
     different protocols. Its three parts are not separable as written.
  3. The part that IS separable, on identical positions, is: quantisation 0.0038-0.0088 BPB
     (26 % of 0.0330 at the high cell, 12 % at the low cell), engine < 1e-5 BPB, everything
     else window sampling. The serving path is faithful.
  4. If the 0.877 float cell is taken at face value, the residual to 0.90 is ~0.024 BPB and it
     is NOT protocol -- it is float model quality. Perfecting the quantiser buys at most 0.0088
     and cannot reach 0.90 alone, which is the arithmetic the round set out to test.

LIMITATIONS
  1. The artifact measured is NOT the artifact that produced the recorded 0.877/0.933 pair.
     The record's model (batch 32, lr 4e-4, warmup 500, chat-v0-p2 train only) is not in the
     cloud-store index and is not on this laptop. What is available, and what was measured, is
     the term-weight 19.9 M family: same architecture, 19,929,136 parameters, same 12,207
     steps, no transport snap -- but batch 16, lr 6e-4, mixed-t2 training corpus, a different
     export recipe (round-to-nearest) and a different GPTQ calibration set. Its absolute BPB
     (1.77-1.80) is far worse than the recorded model's (0.92), so this is a protocol and
     structure measurement, not a re-measurement of that model.
  2. The 0.877 float cell's own validity is unresolved. Two matched float cells of one model
     here differ by 0.029 BPB, and the one reported cell of the record's model (0.877) is 0.047
     BPB below the 64-window float cell of the recorded pair's own table (0.92394). A 0.047 BPB
     swing from window draw is larger than the 0.033 gap itself, so "0.877" is a position-set
     number, not a model property. Settling it needs the recorded artifact, not more analysis.
  3. Both absolute levels here are on a model that is 0.88 BPB worse than the record's, so the
     quantisation costs measured (0.0038, 0.0041) are specific to this artifact; the record's
     0.00883 is the comparable cell for its own artifact.
  4. criterion 2 is NOT claimed and no served-model movement is claimed. STATUS/ROADMAP/#2028
     are unchanged.
