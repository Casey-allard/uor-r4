RETRACTION OF THE PREVIOUS ROUND'S READING
  Round 162 recorded that the name-like replies from the copy-store arms ("Anany." at weight 100, "Ane."
  at weight 30) were "a reproducible signal" of the recalled value. THE PROBE REFUTES THAT. The replies
  are onset-keyed, not value-driven: "Anany.", "Ane." and "Another." all share the first token " An",
  and the value "Ana" appears in none of them as such. The word "reproducible" was wrong - what
  reproduced was the ONSET, not the value.

  The copy-store change moved the first token; it did not move the answer.

WHAT THIS CHANGES
  1. THE REMAINING GAP IS THE CONTINUATION, NOT RETRIEVAL AND NOT THE ONSET. The span is reached, the
     onset is value-conditional, and the continuation is family-driven. The single next intervention
     that follows is to score or penalise the FULL VALUE SPAN at the answer position rather than the
     first token alone - or a constrained/two-token decoder - because the first token is already doing
     its job.
  2. THE COMMITMENT WEIGHT IS A FIRST-TOKEN LEVER AND IS NOW MEASURABLY DOING THAT, which is a positive

     produces the most value-paired DELTA cells (7/7, 6/7, 6/7) while regressing on the panel's form
     classes, so the two objectives are in tension at this size.
  3. THE INSTRUMENT IS NOW TRUSTWORTHY FOR THIS QUESTION: three rows designed to separate value from
