METHOD, in one paragraph
  Per row the sequence is rebuilt exactly as the graded reply ran it: history_ids ++ the sealed last
  turn's reply ids (both from the sealed acceptance record; NOTHING is generated here). Reply step s
  is scored at position n = history_len - 1 + s, where the model sees ids[0..=n] and predicts the
  sealed next token. The value's own tokens are located in the sequence BY TEXT on the tokenizer's
  decode (an exact id-run search is kept beside it): a word value can be split differently in context
  than on its own, so the ids present in the sequence -- 'span' -- are reported beside the value's own
  encoding -- 'value'. One score_targets call per panel target overrides the target at the probe
  positions (the value's span, the distractor's span, every reply step); the forward pass is identical
  in every call, so the whole (target x position) grid costs one forward per target.

THE THREE PRE-REGISTERED OUTCOMES, against these numbers
  1. copy_mass HIGH at the value's digits -> nothing to train, the emitter is the target.
     TRUE for 3 of 13 rows -- mem-008 (0.897), mem-024 (0.717), mem-040 (0.601) -- and those are
     EXACTLY the 3 rows whose trace selects a stored digit (2, 2 and 1 steps). Two of the three
     still emit nothing, which is the emitter-side reading in its smallest measured form.
  2. copy_mass LOW at the digits against a HIGHER BASELINE ELSEWHERE -> the frame reading survives.
     TRUE for the other 10 of 13 rows: value share <= 0.20 at every reply step while the frame's
     share reaches 0.34-1.00 at the same steps. 0 of 13 rows have their value exceed the frame.
  3. copy_mass LOW EVERYWHERE -> REFUTED. The frame class carries 0.34-1.00.
  It lands BETWEEN 1 and 2, and the split is exactly the already-measured selection line: the copy
  mass follows the attention (0.60-0.90 where the argmax is on a digit; <= 0.20 where it is not).
  The mixture is faithful; the attention's target is the constraint.

ALSO MEASURED, because the plan's decision rule names positions and the value has several: the
