Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 59-74). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THREE IMPROVEMENTS OVER THE 7.16 M ARM OF THE SAME RECIPE
  1. THE REFUSAL TEMPLATE IS GONE WITHOUT SAMPLING: 0 of 22 under GREEDY decoding, where the 7.16 M
     arm produced it repeatedly and round 73 could only remove it by sampling (1 of 22 at
     temperature 0.7). Capacity removed it at temperature 0. That settles round 73's open question
     in the better direction: the template was not merely a decoding artefact, it was a small-model
     default that a larger model does not fall into.
  2. A FACTUAL ANSWER THE SMALLER MODEL GOT WRONG IS NOW CORRECT: "The largest planet in our solar
     system is Jupiter." The 7.16 M arm answered "the absence of the planet, and the small, small
     planets absorb sunlight".
  3. THE MODEL NOW REASONS ABOUT THE REQUESTED FORM: "Imagine a response with exactly three bullet
     points using markdown. Here is the first sentence:" shows it has absorbed what a three-item
     list request is, even though it does not yet emit a clean three-item list. Instruction-shaped
     openers ("Here is the revised version of the sentence:") appear where the smaller model had
     none.

WHAT IS STILL WRONG
  * Instruction compliance remains imperfect: the one-word and exact-count requests are still not
    obeyed precisely, and one reply repeats a phrase ("traditional Chinese traditional Chinese").
  * "How many days are in a week?" produced "There are approximately 6,000", i.e. the right SHAPE for
    a quantity and the wrong value.
  * Repetition and topical drift persist in several turns.
  * The retention turn is responsive in kind ("You can try keeping an...") but does not state the
    count that was given.

CONCLUSION
  CAPACITY IS THE LEVER FOR RELIABILITY, and the response-region objective is the configuration to
  scale. Each of the three regressions the 7.16 M role_only arm introduced or suffered - the refusal
  template, the factual error, the absence of format awareness - improved when the same objective
  was trained at 2.8x the parameters. The remaining gap is precision of instruction following, which
  is now the single named target rather than a set of unrelated defects.

  The session's chain, complete: the served artifact was below uniform on chat; it is now 0.93277
  bits per byte against 4.3885 (4.70x) with an exact engine; the prompt protocol was fixed; the
  deficit was located to the first token; the data hypothesis was pre-registered and falsified; the
  response mask was built against a strict validator contract; the objective produced an assistant
  register and repaired multi-turn behaviour; and on the frozen instruments, 2.8x capacity has now
  removed the refusal default and corrected a factual answer.

SCOPE
  One size comparison, one mask, one policy, single seed each, two instruments, greedy decoding. The
  19.9 M arm's development response nll is slightly worse than the 7.16 M arm's (1.4176 against
  1.3806) while its behaviour is better on every qualitative measure, which is worth remembering
  when choosing a development metric for this objective.
