UOR-R4 — The blocked data lever: the larger chat corpus is rebuildable, with a cheaper test first
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 36, independent review). References #820.

THE FINDING
  chat-v1-p2-train - 589,939 rows, 1,388,814,239 tokens - is not lost upstream; it is missing from
  this machine. It was built once, its manifest survives on the pod volume, and its tokens were
  deleted without being shipped to any store, tar or archive. The recipe is already in the
  repository: docs/compute/ladder-runbook.md section 2.3 and the tool on main at
  crates/uor-r4-training/src/bin/prepare-chat-parquet.rs (build with --features parquet-input).

  Independent arithmetic check of the recipe: chat-v0-p2's manifest gives per-source bytes/token
  ratios of 2.881 (smoltalk smol-magpie-ultra) and 3.462 (ultrachat_200k default/train_sft). Taken
  to the full upstream splits that projects 1,444.9 M tokens, less chat-v0's skipped prefixes
  (34.9 M + 20.4 M) = 1,389.6 M, against the documented 1,388,814,239 - 0.05 % agreement.

RANKED ROUTES
  1. Rebuild chat-v1-p2-train from 9 parquet exports (~2.1 GB): +1,306,565,778 net chat tokens,
     16.9x the current corpus, same two upstream sources and the same role-template protocol as
     chat-v0-p2, Apache-2.0 + MIT (no new licence class), and strict row-skip disjointness from
     chat-v0's fetched prefix.
  2. Local smol-smoltalk parquet already on disk: ~550-640 M chat tokens, zero download, but a
     different conversation shape and not disjoint in origin from chat-v0 (near-duplicate risk).
  3. tinydialogues-train, already tokenised locally: +42.8 M tokens, MIT, but low-register
     synthetic dialogue already present in the mix - more of the same, not new coverage.
  4. Remaining truncated chat-v0 sources (summarize/rewrite/constraints): ~+95 M, overlapping
     route 2, partly task-formatted rather than conversational.
  5. dolly_15k and TinyStoriesInstruct are on disk but were excluded by the fetcher for share-alike
     licences: an owner decision, not an engineering one.

DEAD ENDS CLOSED, SO THEY ARE NOT RE-WALKED
  The store holds no corpora (674 files / 10.0 GB: 653 codex results, 8 models, 6 bases, 6 data
  entries, none a chat corpus). Every local chat store found is the same 82-83 M token
  chat-v0-p2-derived set from the same six truncated sources, across eight directories. No archive
  holds chat-v1.

THE ARGUMENT AGAINST ROUTE 1, WHICH IS THE STRONGEST PART OF THE REVIEW
  chat-v1 scales the same two sources as chat-v0 - volume, not distribution coverage. The 0.1592 BPB
  data term was measured by SWITCHING distribution at a matched 82.2 M-token corpus trained for
  ~1.8 epochs, so what is measured is "matching the training distribution is worth about twice what
  size is worth", not "more tokens of the matched distribution keeps paying". The project has seen
  the opposite at larger scale: the 214 M SmolTalk-mixture stack scores 1.0966 against 0.9607 for a
  7.16 M chat-only model. And Magpie/UltraChat rows are heavily templated, so 1.39 B rows are far
  from 1.39 B unique tokens.

CHEAPER TEST FIRST
  A dose-response on the existing 82.2 M-token corpus with the frozen 7,155,396-parameter chat-only
  recipe: hold steps fixed and vary epochs, or hold epochs fixed and vary unique tokens at 41/82/164/
  329 M. If BPB is flat or rising past about one epoch, the 2.1 GB rebuild should not be bought.
  Cost is a fraction of one pod-hour, and corpus, tokenizer and evaluator all exist.

OWNER ACTION THAT WOULD UNBLOCK IT
  Approve re-fetching ~2.1 GB of already-used, licence-clean HF parquet and the compute to rebuild
  chat-v1-p2-train - or explicitly accept that the marginal return of more same-distribution tokens
  stays unmeasured. Nothing else is missing: the tool is on main, the tokenizer is local, filenames
  and skip counts are recorded, and the surviving manifest supplies the tokens_sha256 to verify
  against.
