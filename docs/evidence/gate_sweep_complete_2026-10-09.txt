WHAT THE COMPLETED SWEEP SHOWS
  1. THE GATE IS MONOTONE IN THE SUPERVISION WEIGHT: 0.2644, 0.2959, 0.3559, 0.4164. The lever does
     exactly what it is asked to do, across the whole range.
  2. THE PANEL DOES NOT FOLLOW IT. 0.1 is the worst of the four, 0.3 is intermediate with some responsive
     behaviour, and 1.0 collapses; NONE of the three supervised weights matches the ungated arm's
     combination of value-driven out-of-family copying and partial compliance. So the supervision term
     is not a tuning problem with a good setting hiding in the range - it is a term that competes with
     the response loss across the range, which is consistent with the closure already recorded.
  3. 0.3 IS THE MOST INFORMATIVE SUPERVISED POINT and is worth preserving as such: it is the only gate
     weight at which the retention turn is RESPONSIVE ("You have") and the yes/no class is correct
     simultaneously. That does not make it better than 0.0 on the emitted-value criterion, but it does
     mean the interaction is not a simple "more supervision is worse" - there is structure in the middle
     that a future mechanism could exploit, and the checkpoint is kept rather than deleted.
