UOR-R4 — The attribute store as built DIULTES the short-form mode: compliance falls from ten of ten to two of ten
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 132-134). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT RAN
  A new attribute-extraction store: 2,600 two-turn documents, 175,906 tokens, 53,671 response
  tokens, with a measured transformation fraction of 1.000000 (copying turn 1's answer passes ZERO
  documents - count 640, ordinal 600, name 480, attribute 400, number 240, polarity 200, flip_word
  40). Verified with 0 offenders on all 16 mask clauses by two implementations, a corruption test the
  project's EpisodeIndex rejected, and 5,200/5,200 responses admitted with none excluded. Mixed with
  the hash-proven chat corpus and trained at 19,929,136 parameters with UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=60.

  So compliance falls from TEN OF TEN (five previous configurations, two shapes) to TWO OF TEN, and
  the factual answer regresses as well ("Tokyo" for France, where the 19.9 M two-turn arm produced
  "Paris" and the planet turn produced "Jupiter"). Retention does not improve: the multi-turn turns
  return prose fragments ("The instruments", "Here are") rather than either an answer or the earlier
  anchored "My cat,".

WHY, FROM THE STORE'S OWN NUMBERS
  The store's turn-1 answers are LONG by construction - 2 to 31 tokens, authored sentences - and it
  contributes 53,671 response tokens, MORE than the two-turn store that worked (31,084) and more than
  half of the total demand-side response mass. The mixture therefore re-taught the long-answer mode
  that the earlier stores had specifically countered, and the model's replies drift back to prose
  ("Here are", "The wall", "The instruments"). The previous findings already contain the shape of the
  fix: a 4x DILUTION of the demand store kept compliance intact (round 122), so the demand-side
  response mass is the operative variable, and this store roughly doubled it while making each of its
  answers longer.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CHANGE
  * It does NOT weaken the breakthrough: the demand-store result stands on five configurations and
    two shapes, ten of ten compliance each, and this arm is a different store whose answers are long
    by design.
  * It DOES establish the operative variable: what matters is not only that demands and matching
    answers are present, but that the ANSWERS THE MIXTURE TEACHES STAY SHORT. A store can carry the
    right dependency structure and still harm compliance by teaching long replies.
  * RETENTION REMAINS UNEXPLAINED BY THIS ARM. The dependency structure did not produce recall, and
    the arm cannot separate "attribute extraction is hard" from "the long-answer dilution masked
    whatever the dependency structure taught".

THE NEXT ARM, NAMED AND CHEAP
  The same attribute store DILUTED - or, better, rebuilt with SHORT turn-1 answers (the count,
  ordinal and name families need only a short premise, e.g. "Metals: iron, copper, zinc." rather than
  a full authored sentence) - so the dependency structure is present while the demand-side response
  mass stays at the level that produced ten of ten. The two variables that this arm confounded are
  then separated by construction, and both instruments are already frozen.

SCOPE
  One store, one mixture, one shape, one weight, single seed, greedy decoding, one panel. The
