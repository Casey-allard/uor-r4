UOR-R4 — Recall of never-seen values, quantified: the pointer copies exactly 0.417 of the time against 0.000 for the no-pointer control
Date: 2026-10-09 (overnight rounds 181-182). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT RAN
  Inference only, on the session's best binding arm (pointer=32, dot score, no gate supervision) and its
  control (the same mixture without a pointer), over 28 invented values that appear in NO store and NO
  plausible prior, in three conditions, with the EOS-bearing exact probe path. The novelty claim was
  MEASURED, not assumed: all 19 term-weight stores plus the chat train and heldout corpora were decoded
  (about 9.2e8 token positions, GPT-2 byte-level table) and searched case-insensitively - 28 of 28
  candidates have ZERO occurrences. Controls behave as expected (ben 303,282; ana 805,829; anvil 553
  occurrences inside "anvil"; zorba 165, inside "razorback").

THE NUMBER
  EXACT copy (reply equal to the value, case and surrounding punctuation ignored):
    pointer arm      35 of 84 cells  = 0.417   Wilson 95% [0.317, 0.523]
                     natural 12/28 = 0.429 [0.265, 0.609]; pinned Sure 10/28 = 0.357; pinned Noted 13/28 = 0.464
    per VALUE        16 of 28      = 0.571   [0.391, 0.735] copied exactly in at least one condition
                     24 of 28      = 0.857   emit the value in at least one condition
    CONTAINS value   55 of 84      = 0.655   [0.548, 0.748] (right value, imperfect surface)
    NO-POINTER CONTROL   0 of 84 exact AND 0 of 84 contains, identically in all three conditions

  That control figure is the measurement's spine: the same mixture, the same rows, the same conditions,
  and the value is never emitted without the pointer - reproducing the earlier 0-of-63 class on a
  different row set. The pointer is what makes a never-seen value available to the output.

WHAT GOVERNS IT - THE GATE IS NEAR-NECESSARY AND NOT SUFFICIENT
    pointer mass on the value span >= 0.5 :  35/71 = 0.493 exact   (0.761 contain)
    pointer mass < 0.5                    :   0/13 = 0.000 exact   (1/13 contain)
    pointer mass >= 0.9                   :  33/63 = 0.524        (+3 points over the 0.5 cut)
  So a weak gate is fatal (0 of 13) while a strong gate is far from sufficient: about half still fail
  with the gate wide open, and pushing it from 0.5 to 0.9 buys almost nothing. Means separate cleanly -
  copies have pointer mass 0.983, gate 0.777, DELTA +6.50 nats; misses have 0.730, 0.328, +4.38 - but
  DELTA at or above 0.7 holds in 81 of 84 cells, so the pre-registered DELTA split is degenerate and
  DELTA does not by itself decide. The mechanism is "copy when the gate is high, then often fail at
  readout", not "copy iff the gate is high" - which is consistent with the gate sweep, where raising the
  gate by supervision raised it and damaged the response distribution.

THE DOMINANT RESIDUAL FAILURE, NAMED
  The modal miss is THE CORRECT VALUE FOLLOWED BY OVER-GENERATION: " Jorvex name is Jorvex.",
  " Lunavar is Lunavar.", " Nuvrix is Nuvrixxx", " Therrick is Therricks Therr...",
  " Eskavar is Eskavararar". Twenty of the 49 misses have the value present but not exactly; the replies
  are the copy plus a continuation.
  The taxonomy of the rest: value absent but its first four letters present (4 cells), value absent with
  the same first letter (9), and neither (16). Critically, ANOTHER ROW'S VALUE IS EMITTED IN ZERO CELLS
  (no cross-row contamination), and a store-family first word appears in only 2 of 84 cells, against 18
  of 84 in the control - so the pointer has displaced the store-family and chat-prior behaviours rather
  than merely adding to them.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
  * ESTABLISHES: a learned copy pathway lets this architecture recall a value it has never seen, at a
    measured rate, against a control that never emits it. Cross-turn binding is therefore not an
    architectural impossibility at this scale - the session's earlier "capability gap" reading is
    superseded by a mechanism that works part of the time, with its residual failure localised to the
    CONTINUATION after a correct copy.
  * DOES NOT ESTABLISH: a solved binding capability. 0.417 exact per cell with about half failing on an
    open gate; single checkpoint, single seed, 28 values, one template, one shape.
  * THE NEXT FIX IS NOW SPECIFIC AND FAMILIAR: the copies succeed and then continue, which is the same
    continuation pressure the session has fought all night at the objective level. A length-constrained
    or copy-terminating decoder, or a loss that stops the response once the copied span is complete, acts
    on exactly the residual failure this measurement isolates - and the gate route is closed, so this is
    the remaining lever with direct evidence behind it.

SCOPE
  Two checkpoints (pointer and control), 28 novel values, 3 conditions, one template, greedy decoding,
  single seed. Exact for these rows; the novelty check covers the project's stores and the chat corpora
  used in this session, not the whole internet.
