UOR-R4 — Correction: over-generation is real but the MINORITY of the recall failures; a copy-terminating decoder is worth about +10 points, not the fix
Date: 2026-10-09 (overnight rounds 183-184). DeepSeek session. References #820.

CORRECTION TO THE PREVIOUS TWO ROUNDS
  Round 182 recorded that the dominant residual failure was "the correct value followed by over-generation",
  and round 183 set out to test that a length-constrained or copy-terminating decoder would recover the
  copies. THE TEST DOES NOT SUPPORT THAT AS STATED. Over-generation is real and measurable, but it is the
  MINORITY of the failures: of the 49 misses at the full budget, only 9 start with the value and then
  continue, while 29 NEVER CONTAIN THE VALUE AT ALL. The "dominant residual failure is the continuation"
  framing was too strong and is corrected here.

WHAT RAN
  Inference only, same binary (md5 0852f7ce2b1ce8dfeea72ce9e0c249d1, not rebuilt), same 28 novel values
  and three conditions, with generation budgets 3, 5, 8 against the existing 40; plus the exact intended
  intervention - a POST-HOC CUT of the frozen 40-budget replies, which needs no decoder and no new
  generation. Determinism verified (two repeats at three budgets, 32 of 32 identical replies; the fresh
  budget-40 file is byte-identical to the original).

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PROGRAMME
  1. THE RECALL RESULT STANDS AND IS UNCHANGED: the pointer copies never-seen values at 0.417 of cells
     against 0.000 for the no-pointer control at every budget tested, and a perfect stop would raise it
     to 0.524. The control's zero is the spine of the finding.
  2. THERE ARE TWO DISTINCT FAILURES, NOT ONE, AND THE LARGER IS RETRIEVAL/SELECTION: about 36 % of the
     values are never copiable at any budget, 6 of them never even begin with the value, and 59 % of the
     misses never contain it. A decoding fix therefore cannot close the gap - it addresses at most the
     +10.7-point continuation surplus on top of a 0.417 base.
  3. SO THE NEXT LEVER IS AGAIN THE ONE THE EVIDENCE KEEPS POINTING AT: the pointer's MATCHING step,
     i.e. a token-identity key instead of a hidden-state key, which is where "the value is reachable and
     the gate is open, yet a third of values are never selected" is decided. The gate route is closed
     (four weights), read-binding supervision ranks last (the read already lands), and a copy-terminating
     decoder is now bounded at +10.7 points.
  4. RECORDED AS A CORRECTION: two consecutive rounds over-read the continuation, and the instrument
     that caught it was a pre-registered budget sweep plus a post-hoc cut, with a determinism repeat.
