Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 70-73). DeepSeek session. References #820.

RETRACTION FIRST, BECAUSE IT WAS PUBLISHED
  Round 72 reported that report directories and artifacts on the pod were "disappearing between
  commands", called it the session's most dangerous failure mode, and concluded that results could
  vanish before being read. THAT WAS WRONG. Nothing was reaped. A directory listing shows every file
  present and intact:

    sampled/chat.json          53,011 bytes   (17:07)
    export-r5b/model.lut    4,619,588 bytes   (17:07)
    panel-r5/, panel/, probe-r5/, p3/        all present

  The two failed reads were caused by MY OWN quoting bug: the Python was passed inside a quoted
  heredoc (<<PY), which does not expand shell variables, so the path argument arrived empty and
  Python looked for a RELATIVE path - the error text said exactly that ("sampled/chat.json"), and I
  read past it. The lesson is not about the pod; it is that the error message contained the answer
  and I attributed the failure to the environment instead of to my command. Retracted in full.

  The practical guidance from round 72 stands for a different reason: copy results off a pod you do
  not own as soon as they exist, and pass paths explicitly rather than through nested quoting.

THE EXPERIMENT (round 70's hypothesis, now measured)
  Round 58's panel ran greedy (temperature 0) on the round-58 full_prefix plus mask artifact and the
  refusal shape - "I do not have access to ... However, I can provide ..." - appeared repeatedly.
  The same artifact, same panel, same protocol, decoded at temperature 0.7 with top_k 40 and seed 1:

    refusal template in 1 of 22 replies      (against repeated occurrences under greedy)

  So the hypothesis is CONFIRMED for the most part: the refusal template is largely GREEDY-MODE
  COLLAPSE, not a fixed property of the model. Sampling escapes it.

  BUT THE TRADE IS VISIBLE AND MATTERS. The sampled replies are less coherent:

    "The weight of Quicken is a beautiful setting that consists of positive effects..."
    "Magnetism is a fundamental energy that involves a powerful larger that connects the heart..."
    "1. Binly summarize the main characteristics of a natural world 2. Krish in Walt on a single-..."

  Counterparts that were correct under greedy stay correct ("The capital of France is Paris."), so
  the model's distribution is broad with a noisy tail rather than simply wrong.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE NEXT LEVER
  The refusal template should NOT be attacked as a training defect: it is what greedy decoding does
  to this distribution, and it can be traded away by decoding. What sampling exposes instead is the
  underlying weakness - coherence and factual accuracy - which is a property of a 7.16 M model whose
  response nll is about 1.38 nats, and which the 19.9 M arm was launched to test.
  The next decisions therefore remain: does the larger arm improve coherence, and does any decoding
  setting keep the assistant register while reducing the noisy tail. Both are measurable with the
  instruments already frozen.

SCOPE
  One artifact, one panel, one sampling setting, one seed, greedy comparison from the earlier run of
  the same panel on the same artifact. The retraction concerns a published claim, not a measurement.
