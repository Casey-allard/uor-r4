UOR-R4 — Identity term refuted with a MATCHED CONTROL, and the mechanism is a gate collapse with the span mass unchanged
Date: 2026-10-09. DeepSeek session. References #820. Refines identity_score_negative_2026-10-09.txt (#2004).

WHY THIS REFINES THE PREVIOUS NEGATIVE
  #2004 compared the identity arms against the ORIGINAL arm-ptr checkpoint. That comparison mixes two
  things: the identity term, and the extra fine-tuning both identity arms received. A third arm now
  supplies the missing control - the SAME fine-tune, same recipe, same 12,207 steps, with the identity
  term simply omitted.

FOUR ARMS, 84 CELLS EACH, ONE SESSION, ONE PROBE BINARY

    arm-ptr (baseline, re-run)       35/84 = 0.4167          [0.3171, 0.5235]      0.6548
    A: pointer_identity=0.5          10/84 = 0.1190          [0.0660, 0.2054]      0.1548

  THE CONTROL SPLITS THE EFFECT. Extra fine-tuning alone costs about 13 points (0.4167 -> 0.2857).
  Against that matched control the identity term costs a further -0.1667 at w=0.5 (Newcombe
  [-0.2837, -0.0451], clustered bootstrap [-0.2976, -0.0357], exact McNemar p=0.0161, 8 gained /
  22 lost) and -0.2857 at w=1 (p=1.19e-07, 0 gained / 24 lost). So #2004's conclusion SURVIVES -
  the term is a regression - but its attribution was too generous: roughly 13 of the 30 points I
  reported as the identity term's cost are the fine-tuning itself, and the term's own contribution is
  about 17 points at half weight and 29 at full weight.

THE MECHANISM IS NOW MEASURED, NOT INFERRED (means over 84 cells)
    arm        gate    ptr mass on value span   read mass on value   p_mixture of value's 1st token
    arm-ptr    0.5149  0.8351                   0.5610               0.4901
    control    0.2439  0.7415                   0.4455               0.2292
    A (0.5)    0.1163  0.8399                   0.5044               0.1300
    B (1.0)    0.0030  0.0421                   0.5994               0.1105
  AT w=0.5 THE POINTER STILL ATTENDS THE VALUE SPAN AS MUCH AS THE BASELINE (0.8399 against the
  control's 0.7415) - what collapses is the copy GATE (0.1163 against 0.2439), so the mixture stops
  emitting the value (p_mixture 0.1300 against 0.2292). That is a GATE/READOUT COLLAPSE WITH UNCHANGED
  SPAN MASS, which is a different failure from the one the earlier arms showed and is the sharpest
  statement of what the identity term does: it does not stop the model finding the value, it stops the
  model USING it. At w=1 the score additionally loses the span (0.0421) and drives the gate to 0.0030.

THE FROZEN-CURVE SIGNATURE REPRODUCES
  ARM B's dev_pointer_hit_rate is 0.003963011889035667 at step 0 and IDENTICAL TO ALL PRINTED DIGITS at
  every one of the thirteen evaluations - the same signature that condemned the token-id route - while
  its reachability stays 0.47291941875825627 at every evaluation (unlike the route's 0.182, because
  identity scoring keeps every source admissible). ARM A's hit rate rises 0.2404 -> 0.2448, i.e. the
  diagnostic does NOT track novel-value copying: it sits at the baseline's level (0.2387) and the
  control's (0.2444) while exact copies fall to 10/84. Third independent demonstration this session
  that this curve is not a proxy for the behaviour.

A TOOLING FINDING THAT WOULD HAVE CORRUPTED THE RESULT
  The existing bind-probe-exact binary PREDATED the identity field and scores an identity model
  wrongly - it differs from a rebuilt probe on 8 of 32 rows on arm A (e.g. lunavar " A Lunavar." vs
  " A name."), while on the dot-only arm-ptr the two probes are bit-identical (96 of 96 replies and all
  pointer masses). The old binary was preserved as bind-probe-exact-20261008 and a new one built from
  the same main. So scoring an identity model with yesterday's probe would have produced numbers that
  looked like an identity effect and were partly a stale-binary artefact - the same class of error this
  session has now hit four times.

WHAT THIS SETTLES
  Both ways of injecting token identity into the pointer are measured and rejected, now with a matched
  control: token-id KEYS via a route (reachability collapses to 0.182, metrics freeze) and token
  overlap as a SCORE term (exact copies 0.4167 -> 0.1190 -> 0.0000, of which about 13 points is
  fine-tuning and the rest the term). The learned hidden-state match remains the only matching that
  works, at 0.4167 exact recall against a 0.0000 no-pointer control and 0.2857 for a matched
  fine-tune. And the control arm establishes a NEW fact the earlier arms could not: ADDITIONAL
  FINE-TUNING ON THIS MIXTURE COSTS RECALL BY ITSELF, which is worth knowing before any further arm
  is run on this lineage.

SCOPE
  Four arms x 84 cells, one mixture, one shape, single seed per arm, greedy decoding, scored in one
  session with a probe rebuilt from the same main and a baseline reproduced bit-for-bit against the
  earlier run. The 28 invented values are verified absent from all 19 stores and the chat corpora.
