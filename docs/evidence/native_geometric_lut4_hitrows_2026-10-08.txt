UOR-R4 — Stage 4: training on shortlist-hit rows only still does not produce a useful term
Date: 2026-10-08. DeepSeek session. References #820.
Follows: native_geometric_lut4_connectivity_2026-10-08.txt.

WHAT CHANGED
  The harness now trains ONLY on rows whose target is inside the shortlist, and measures those
  rows separately. Rationale from the previous document: for rows with the target outside the
  shortlist every candidate is negative, whose least-squares optimum is the constant term that
  produced the retracted result. 57 of 204 fit positions (28 %) and 52 of 256 held-out positions
  (20 %) are hits.

MEASUREMENT (chat-v0-p2 train to held-out, top-64 shortlist, 600 steps, 26-bit features)
  Training loss          0.252945 -> 0.223337   (the term does learn on hit rows)
  Degenerate?            YES: distinct_hard_outputs 1; chosen_gates [12 x16, 0]
                         hard_bit_tally [16384, 0]; distinct_soft_outputs_milli 3
  All held-out rows      baseline 4.211070, soft 4.201949 (-0.009121), hard 4.191505 (-0.019565)
  Hit rows only (52)     baseline 2.444325, soft 2.500545, delta +0.056219  (WORSE)

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
  * The constant-shift problem is not fixed by the data lever: the served term is still degenerate,
    so the all-rows "improvement" (-0.019565 hard) is again the uniform shortlist penalty and the
    harness again says so instead of reporting it as a result.
  * On the only rows where re-ranking can possibly help, the learned soft correction makes bits per
    byte WORSE by 0.056. Teaching the term with the right rows was necessary and is not sufficient.
  * The connectivity mechanism does learn now (loss falls, unit tests oblige non-degeneracy on a
    controlled task); what fails is the transfer of that learning to this artifact's real features.

STANDING CONCLUSION FOR STAGE 4 IN THIS SESSION
  Three forms have now been measured and none improves held-out bits per byte on real text:
    all-candidate correction, best of 1056 two-input terms   +0.0000061
    shortlist re-ranking, constant penalty (degenerate)      -0.019565  not a result
    shortlist re-ranking, trained on hit rows (degenerate)   all-rows -0.019565, hit rows +0.056219
  The Stage 4 gate is NOT passed, and the evidence no longer supports another insertion-form
  iteration at this scale. The levers that remain, in the order the measurements point to them:
    1. Data scale for the term: 204 fit positions cannot train a 26-bit interaction, and the
       honest next test needs the term trained where the features are dense, not on 200 rows.
    2. A different decision to re-rank: this term re-ranks candidates of a scorer that is already
       weak on this corpus (baseline 4.21 bits/byte), so the ceiling is the scorer, not the term.
    3. Representation and data for the base scorer, which is what this session's measurements have
       pointed at repeatedly.

SCOPE
  One artifact, one corpus pair, 204-256 positions, debug build, no serving default changed. The
  served form remains a truth-table read. The hit-row split is reported so the ceiling of any
  shortlist re-ranker (20 %) is visible rather than implied.
