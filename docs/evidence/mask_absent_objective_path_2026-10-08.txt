UOR-R4 — The objective change needs a mask the corpus does not have, and that changes the corpus decision
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 49). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT ROUND 48 CONCLUDED AND WHAT IT IMPLIES
  The pre-registered form-diversity arm failed on all three measures, so the chat model's failure to
  bind replies to turns is an objective or capacity problem rather than a data-form problem. The
  indicated change is a loss that distinguishes the RESPONSE region of a conversation from the
  prompt, or an objective that conditions generation on the turn instead of continuing a flattened
  transcript.

WHAT THE MACHINERY REQUIRES, AND WHAT IS MISSING
  `dialogue-train` accepts exactly that: `train_tokens=TRAIN.uort train_mask=TRAIN.mask
  train_manifest=TRAIN/manifest.json` (crates/uor-r4-training/examples/geometric-stack.rs:41-43).
  The mask is the response region.

  IT DOES NOT EXIST FOR THIS CORPUS. The training data on the volume is
  chat-train.u16 (164,496,986 bytes), heldout.u16 and tokenizer.json - no mask, no manifest, no
  per-turn sidecar. Searching the whole volume for a mask file returns only cargo-registry sources.
  The chat corpus was built as a flat token stream with role templates rendered INTO the text
  (protocol 2), so the reply boundaries exist only in the source conversations, not in the artefact.

CONSEQUENCE - AND IT FLIPS A RECOMMENDATION
  The objective change cannot be run on what is on disk. It requires rebuilding the corpus from the
  source parquet with a mask emitted alongside the tokens, i.e. the same ~2.1 GB licence-clean
  re-fetch and `prepare-chat-parquet` path that rounds 36 and 48 argued against.

  But the argument against it was about VOLUME, and this is not a volume reason. The rebuild would
  be bought to obtain the ROLE/MASK STRUCTURE of the conversations - the thing that makes a
  response-region loss possible at all - and that structure is not obtainable any other way: the
  flat corpus has already discarded it. Stated precisely for the owner's decision:
    * for more tokens of the same register: NO, the evidence is against it;
    * for a mask that enables the objective change: it appears to be the only route, and the
      decision should be made on that basis rather than on corpus size.
  The tool may already emit what is needed - `prepare-chat-parquet` is on main and the runbook's
  section 2.3 documents the source/skip protocol - so the first step is to read its output options
  for a mask or per-turn sidecar emission before assuming new code is required. That check is cheap
  and is the next action.

ALSO WORTH KNOWING FOR THAT STEP
  The corpus's role structure came from two licence-clean sources already used by this project
  (smoltalk smol-magpie-ultra, Apache-2.0; ultrachat_200k default/train_sft, MIT), with skip counts
  recorded in the runbook, and the tokenizer is local with a known sha256, so a rebuild is
  deterministic and verifiable. The held-out stream used by every measurement in this session
  (sha256 e5f400b0...) lives on the laptop and is unaffected by a rebuild of the training corpus.

SCOPE
  This document reports an absence, verified by listing the data directory and searching the volume,
  and records the decision it changes. No objective change has been run and no result is claimed for
  one.
