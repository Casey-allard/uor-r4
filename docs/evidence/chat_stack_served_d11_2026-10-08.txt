  CORRECTION (round 18): the served row was first published as 0.9550; the correct
  conversion of the same recorded nll on the same byte basis is 0.93277. The fused and
  snapped rows were correct. The gap from served to fused float is therefore 0.0552 BPB
  rather than 0.078, and the improvement over the 4.3885 served today is 4.70x.

  The served number is 4.7 times better than what the project serves today, and the engine
  reproduces its reference exactly - zero nll difference, every top-1 identical, not one logit
  differing anywhere. The round-5 evidence that the D11 kernel is bit-exact therefore holds on a
  second, independently trained artifact on a different distribution.

WHAT IT SETTLES
  The barrier this session spent nine rounds locating was never the mechanism, the data or the
  serving kernel. It was that the end-to-end pipeline had never been run: the project trained
  models it did not serve (geo-8m-l2 at 1.1199 idle on disk), served a model it did not train for
  the distribution in front of it (2.7 MB, 120-root, TinyStories-fit, 4.3885 on chat), and had
  already proved in a sealed record that its integer serving engine is exact. Running the three
  together gives 0.9550 bits per byte on real held-out chat.

  It also retro-explains the whole session: every mechanism delta measured on the served artifact
  was measured on a model sitting below uniform on that corpus (top-1 2.25 %), in a feature space
  of 120 roots, while a trained stack with a 1536-wide-class embedding was available and idle.

HONEST SCOPE
  * Single seed. Chat-only training, so part of the gain over the sealed 8M mix-trained model is
    in-domain data rather than architecture; the two are not separated here.
  * d11-evaluate covered 24,576 targets, snap-evaluate 196,608, and the sealed 8M 6,194,304. The
    absolute numbers will move slightly under one common full-file protocol, and a claim finer than
    ~0.01 BPB needs the document-clustered protocol from round 2 (clustered SE 0.003489).
  * The export is round-to-nearest; the GPTQ route measured 0.9379 top-1 agreement on the earlier
    artifact, so quantisation choice is a live lever, not a solved one.
  * lut-evaluate's 0.9294 agreement is against the float parent on 24,576 targets, so the served
    artifact is not bit-identical to its parent even though the engine is faithful to the artifact.
  * The D11 engine runs on CPU at 61 tokens per second: correctness is established here, throughput
    is not.

NEXT
