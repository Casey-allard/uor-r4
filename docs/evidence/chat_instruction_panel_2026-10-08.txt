WHAT THE MODEL DOES AND DOES NOT DO
  It produces fluent English in every case, and it is TOPICALLY conditioned: "Japan" produces an
  itinerary opener, "bake a cake" produces a recipe opener, "planets" produces a sentence about
  planets. It cannot yet perform the ACT the turn requests: not one-word answers, not counts, not
  yes/no, and it does not answer a question about something it was just told.

  The distinction matters and is now measured: this is not a fluency failure, not a formatting
  failure (the round-43 protocol fix removed those), and not a serving failure (the same served path
  reproduces its float model to 0.00883 BPB). It is a failure to bind the response to the request.

WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT TARGET
  Everything the session measured says the pipeline is faithful and the model is limited by what it
  learned. The panel says precisely WHAT it failed to learn: instruction-to-response binding,
  including carrying a fact across a turn boundary. The three two-turn retention pairs are the
  sharpest evidence - the model repeats the entity ("Ana") but cannot answer about it - and they
  point at training data and objective rather than at volume. More tokens of the same conversational
  register would produce more fluent topical text; binding needs examples where the reply is
  determined by the turn.

SCOPE
  One artifact, greedy decoding, 17 requests, one protocol setting, compliance judged by mechanical
  checks on the transcripts reproduced here. Small panel, single model; the counts are exact for
  this panel and are not a benchmark.
