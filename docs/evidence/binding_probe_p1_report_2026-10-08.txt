UOR-R4 — P1 binding probe: readout failure downstream of retrieval, with a method correction that matters
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight rounds 149-153). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHAT RAN
  The expert review's P1 over four rows differing ONLY in the value inside turn 1 (cats2/cats5, ana/ben),
  inference only on four existing checkpoints, no training. Conditions: natural, turn 1 PINNED to
  "Sure.", turn 1 PINNED to the store's own "Noted.", and P2's single-turn formulation. Protocol 2,
  greedy, max_new_tokens=40, TAU=0.10. DELTA(t) = ln p(first token t | own premise) - ln p(first token t
  | paired premise), from the tool's answer-position mixture distribution; read mass = read_expected_max.
  Full tables: /workspace/uor-r4/deepseek/term-weight/bindprobe/P1-REPORT.md (312 lines).

A METHOD CORRECTION THAT CHANGES THE READING OF ANY PINNED EXPERIMENT
  A raw-text single-turn emulation of a pinned turn 1 - the obvious ~30-line helper - OMITS THE EOS THAT
  CLOSES TURN 1, and that EOS is worth UP TO 1.33 NATS of DELTA (effect -0.94 to +1.33; 14 of 16
  pair-terms move more than 0.1). Re-encoding the reply text versus using the model's own generated ids
  is worth +0.0000 on all 16 terms. So the naive pinned runs are invalid and only EOS-bearing exact runs
  count. The pinned implementation was validated against the official binary bit-exactly: turn1=null
  reproduces the natural-condition marker observation with worst absolute difference 0.0e+00 over 16
  rows x 4 arms, and pinning arm-as to "Noted." reproduces its own natural window ids exactly.

RESULT - THE PRE-REGISTERED BRANCH
  BRANCH 4 for all three store arms: READ MASS ABOVE TAU ON THE EXPECTED SPAN WHILE THE ANSWER STILL
  PREFERS A PRIOR - a readout failure downstream of retrieval.
    arm-as (19.9 M, attrshort): read mass above TAU on 4/4 rows in ALL conditions (natural, pinned
      Sure., pinned Noted.; 0.16-0.36, 1-4 of 16 heads) and every row classifies c_readout at the
      marker. Replies are the store's own family strings: natural " Noted." / " I see." / " Italy." /
      " Got it."; pinned Sure. " Java." / prose / " Neon." / " Italy."; pinned Noted. " Noted." /
      " I see." / " Nineteen." / " Noted.". DELTA pinned Sure. = +1.91 / -0.11 / +2.60 / +1.67 and
      pinned Noted. = +0.78 / +0.12 / +3.33 / +1.26 - three of four at or above 0.7 in both - YET
      0/4 correct argmax and 0/4 exact match.
    arm-bind (33.8 M, same mixture): read mass above TAU on 3/4; DELTA pinned Sure. = +0.96 / -0.42 /
      +2.43 / +1.09; 0/4 exact. The one "argmax correct" row's greedy string is " Ag.", a sub-word
      prefix, not an answer.
    arm-t2 (7.16 M, two-turn store): read mass above TAU on 2-3 of 4; DELTA pinned Sure. = -0.82 /
      -0.22 / +0.57 / -0.67; 0/4 at or above 0.7; 0/4 exact.
    arm-w60 (7.16 M, NO store, the control): read mass BELOW TAU on 4/4 rows in EVERY condition
      (natural .042/.035/.069/.061; pinned Sure. .083/.086/.077/.070; pinned Noted. .077/.054/.070/.072
      - 0/12 heads at or above TAU). DELTA natural +0.23/+0.53/+0.22/+0.88; pinned Sure. -0.10/+0.50/
      +0.73/+0.84. Its ana natural reply IS THE VALUE IN PROSE ("My name is Ana, a city known for...")
      but the frozen two-token rule judges it a miss - the measurement gap the review flagged.

  WHICH BRANCHES ARE EXCLUDED
    * NOT branch 1: that required DELTA at or above 0.7 with correct argmax on all rows while form
      compliance holds. The best case (arm-as pinned Sure.) gives 3/4 at or above 0.7 with 0/4 argmax
      and 0/4 exact.
    * NOT branch 2: |DELTA| below 0.1 never holds on more than one of four rows, so binding is not
      unused.
    * Branch 3 (read mass below TAU, the queried span never addressed) holds ONLY for the no-store
      control arm-w60.
  A FURTHER FINDING THAT BLUNTS THE DELTA STATISTIC ITSELF: in arm-as the +1.91 comes from "two" (a
  high-frequency token, p 1.6e-2) while "five" (p 6.0e-4) has DELTA near zero in the same condition. The
  margin tracks TOKEN PRIOR, not a symmetric read, so DELTA at or above 0.7 alone is not evidence of
  binding and was rightly not treated as such.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE PROGRAMME
  1. THE FAILURE IS READOUT, NOT RETRIEVAL, IN THE STORE ARMS. The mixture's models DO put mass on the
     value's span (above TAU, though thin - 1-4 of 12-20 heads, per-head means 0.7-6.6 %) and then emit
     their own family strings instead of the value. The no-store control does the opposite: it never
     addresses the span and yet produces the value in prose. The two arms fail in different places,
     which is why the mixture's presence both helped form compliance and harmed binding.
  2. THE MIXTURE/REGISTER EXPLANATION STAYS LIVE and is not ruled out: form compliance is 0 in the
     pinned conditions, and the pinned "Noted." replies reproduce the store's family strings exactly.
  3. THE MEASUREMENT INSTRUMENT HAS A KNOWN GAP: the no-store arm's ana reply is the value in prose and
     the frozen two-token panel rule scores it a miss. Any future claim about binding must add a
     log-probability or prose-value channel alongside the two-token reading, as the review advised.
  4. THE NEXT CHANGES ARE THE REVIEW'S P3 AND P4, both cheap: commitment-token weighting (weight the
     FIRST supervised position of each response run, and LOWER the terminal weight to 30 rather than
     pushing both) and a store whose turn-1 answers are SHORT CONTENT-BEARING COPIES of the premise
     ("Metals: iron, copper, zinc." -> "Iron, copper, zinc.") rather than acknowledgements, so the
     turn-1 slot teaches span selection instead of a register. The probe shows the value span is
     already reached often enough to be usable, so the incentive and the readout are what to fix.

SCOPE
  Four checkpoints, four rows, four conditions, inference only, greedy, single seed; exact for these
  rows. Read mass above TAU is a max over layers x heads (only 1-4 heads exceed it), so it is a thin
  effect and is reported as such. No scaling claim. Artifacts: bindprobe/P1-REPORT.md, transcripts.md,
  runs/, exact/ on the pod; lease released.
