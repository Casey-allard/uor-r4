UOR-R4 — The pointer route learns to SELECT the value's span: hit rate 0.108 -> 0.239 and still rising at the last step
Date: 2026-10-09 (overnight rounds 168-170). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHY A POINTER, AFTER THREE WEIGHTING VARIANTS
  The binding probe established that the model ADDRESSES the value's span (read mass at or above TAU in
  62 of 63 cells) while 0 of 63 replies emit the value, and that the onset token is already
  value-conditional (" Z" at p=0.894 with a value-paired DELTA up to +10.8 nats) while the continuation
  follows the mixture's taught surfaces. Three objective variants - terminal, commitment on the first
  token, commitment on the first three tokens - did not change that picture. The expert review ranked a
  pointer BELOW those proposals because a pointer supplies the CAPACITY to copy an identity rather than
  the incentive; with the incentive route measured three ways, capacity is what remains, and EVERY ARM
  BEFORE THIS ONE WAS POINTERLESS.

WHAT RAN
  dialogue-train with pointer=32 pointer_select=none (flag-validated first with a 20-step smoke test
  before any full run was spent), on the same content-copy mixture, with UOR_COMMITMENT_SPAN=3,
  UOR_COMMITMENT_WEIGHT=100 and UOR_TERMINAL_WEIGHT=30, 19,929,136 parameters, 12,207 steps. The
  pointer reports three quantities the panel alone cannot: reachability of the span, whether the pointer
  SELECTS it, and how much the output USES the selection.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
  1. THE MECHANISM IS LEARNABLE AND NOT SATURATED. The pointer's selection rate more than DOUBLED over
     the run and was still climbing at the last measured step, at about half of the reachable rate
     (0.239 against 0.473). A quantity that rises monotonically with training and has not plateaued is a
     mechanism that is still learning, not one that has hit a representational ceiling - which is the
     first such signal on the binding problem after four exhausted data routes, three sizes and three
     objective variants.
  2. REACHABILITY IS MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED: about 47 % of the time the value's span is in the
     addressable set at all. So roughly half the remaining gap is SELECTION (0.239 of the 0.473
     reachable) and the rest is reachability itself, which is a property of the addressing scheme and
     the context rather than of the training signal.
  3. IT DOES NOT YET ANSWER THE QUESTION. A rising selection rate is not an answer: the panel read for
     this arm is not yet available (the chain's export-and-score step reported completion without the
     panel file being present when the run was read), so the emitted-value test - the criterion that
     judged the previous arms - has NOT been applied to this checkpoint. What can be said is that the
     pointer now selects the span far more often than the 0.108 it started at, and that the next
     measurement must be the panel plus the probe's DELTA and read-mass channels on THIS checkpoint
     before any claim about binding is made.

WHAT FOLLOWS, IN ORDER
  1. Score this checkpoint: panel v2 and, more importantly, the probe's seven rows with read mass and
     DELTA - explicitly including the three rows built to separate value from prior (a non-name value,
     an out-of-family name, and a non-name sharing an onset). The criterion is whether the EMITTED token
     follows the value rather than the store family.
  2. If selection keeps climbing with more steps, extend the run rather than starting a new design: the
     curve has not plateaued and the project's rule is to extend a healthy run rather than abandon it.
  3. If selection plateaus near 0.24 while reachability stays near 0.47, the binding gap moves into the
     ADDRESSING SCHEME (which is what makes the span reachable only half the time) - and that is a
     cleaner, more specific problem than "binding is hard", on the project's own geometric address/span
     machinery which remains registered but unused by the chat path.

HONEST POSITION
  This is the first POSITIVE mechanistic signal on binding: a selection rate that doubles and is still
  rising. It is NOT a binding result, and it is explicitly recorded that the emitted-value test has not
  been run on this checkpoint. The night's solved result is unaffected.

SCOPE
  One pointer configuration (dim 32, dot score, no selection), one mixture, one shape, single seed. The
  trajectory numbers are the trainer's own development readings at every 1,000 steps.
