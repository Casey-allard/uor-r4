UOR-R4 — The response-form intervention: what is running and how it will be judged
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 47). DeepSeek session. References #820.

THE DIAGNOSIS IT ANSWERS
  Three independent probes agree that the served chat model has no representation of the response
  FORM a turn requests: the 22-turn panel scores 0/6 instruction compliance (round 44), not one of
  eight decisive turns opens with the answer at max_new_tokens=1 (round 45), and answer-shaped
  prompt scaffolds change nothing (round 46). The model writes descriptive prose about the topic of
  every turn. With the serving path proven faithful to 0.00883 BPB (round 37) and the prompt format
  corrected (round 43), the remaining explanation is training data whose replies are uniformly
  prose.

THE INTERVENTION NOW RUNNING
  Same frozen 7.16 M chat-only recipe (pattern rrarra, width 288, heads 6, context 384, read L2,
  batch 32, lr 4e-4, warmup 500, 13,348 steps), one change: the training stream is a 50/50 mixture

    train=chat-v0-p2/train/tokens.u16,tinydialogues-train/tokens.u16  train_weights=0.5,0.5

  tinydialogues is 81,555,972 bytes = about 42.8 M tokens of SHORT TURN-PAIR dialogue - question and
  answer shapes, minimal prose - which is the register the chat corpus lacks. It is the only
  form-diverse tokenised corpus available on this machine; every other large stream is one of the
  eight copies of the same 82 M-token chat-v0-p2 set that round 36's data review enumerated.

  Honest limitation: tinydialogues is low-register synthetic child dialogue. If it moves the
  yardsticks, that says FORM DIVERSITY helps and a better corpus of the same kind is worth
  assembling; it does not by itself make the model a good assistant, and the mixture change also
  alters topic distribution, so the two effects are not separated by this arm alone.

HOW IT WILL BE JUDGED - the two fixed instruments
  1. The 22-turn instruction panel (docs/evidence/chat_instruction_panel_2026-10-08.txt): a rise in
     instruction compliance above 0/6, factual correctness above 1/4, or answered second turns above
     0/5 is a positive result.
  2. The max_new_tokens=1 first-token probe on the same eight decisive turns: the sharpest test,
     because it asks whether the FIRST token becomes responsive. Anything other than " The"/" To"/
     " I"/" " for the yes-no and one-word turns is evidence the form conditioning exists.
  Both are mechanical and were fixed BEFORE this arm was launched, so there is no selection.

  The dose-response volume arms are retired to make room: the 2-epoch arm finished its work and the
  queued 4-epoch arm was stopped, because rounds 44-46 all point at form rather than volume and the
  GPU is the scarce resource. That decision is recorded as a scientific one, not a budget one.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE INTERVENTION
  Neither yardstick moving after a 50/50 form-diverse mixture means the deficit is not the form
  distribution of the corpus - it would then be the objective (what the model is asked to predict)
  or the model's capacity, and the next step becomes an objective change rather than a data change.
  A fall in the panel's fluency would mean the mixture damaged the register, which is also worth
  knowing and would set a mixture-weight experiment.

SCOPE
  One model size, one mixture weight, one corpus pair, single seed. No result is claimed: the arm is
  training.
