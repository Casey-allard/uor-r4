CORRECTION 1: role_only DOES NOT MEAN WHAT I PUBLISHED
  response_region_policy_verdict_2026-10-08.txt explains the bullet collapse as role_only
  "over-weighting the corpus's bullet-heavy assistant style". THAT IS WRONG. [R] Both policies set
  weight 0 on every prefix token (dialogue_episodes.rs:572-582), so neither trains on prompt tokens.
  The actual difference is episode construction (dialogue_episodes.rs:541-554): FullPrefix keeps
  tokens[document_start..response_start]; RoleOnly keeps ONLY [BOS] plus the five assistant-marker
  ids - IT DELETES THE USER TURN.
  CONSEQUENCE, which is more useful than the claim it replaces: the bullet spam is the corpus's
  UNCONDITIONAL assistant form, and reply FORM is carried by the PREFIX. That is why full_prefix
  produces assistant-register replies and role_only cannot: with the turn deleted there is nothing
  left to condition a form on, so the model emits the marginal. The round-58 conclusion (full_prefix
  + mask is the working configuration) stands; the mechanism published for it does not.

THE ELIGIBILITY FILTER - A NEW AND DECISIVE MEASUREMENT
  [R] dialogue_episodes.rs:416-422 admits an episode only if BOS to end-of-response fits the context;
  synthetic-memory-corpus.rs:36-40 states the same exclusion independently.
  [M] At context 384 that admits 31,798 of 129,465 responses (24.56 %), carrying 3,430,907 response
  tokens = 6.07 % of all response tokens. MEDIAN eligible reply 97 tokens; median prefix 124.
  [M] AMONG THE ELIGIBLE POPULATION: 4 episodes of one word; 8 of five words or fewer; 24 of ten or
  fewer. One-word replies contribute 41 tokens = 0.0012 % of the eligible token mass. Of the eligible
  five-word replies, about 4 carry any strong short-form demand in the user turn.
  So the effective training distribution contains FOUR one-word demonstrations of the behaviour the
  panels score. No learner extracts a conditional from that, and the target is unreachable for a
  reason that is now measured rather than argued. This sharpens rounds 79-80: the raw corpus has 26
  one-word replies, and the EPISODE FILTER removes almost all of them.

THE FORM RATIO - 121:1 AGAINST
  [M] Of 129,465 responses: one word 26 (0.020 %); yes/no <=4 words 2 (0.002 %); bare number 18
  (0.014 %); <=5 words 118 (0.091 %). By word mass, <=5 words is 0.0015 %.
  [M] User turns demanding a form: "Your answer/response must|should" 17,205 (13.29 %), while the
  entire contract family (one word / yes-no / only the answer / no explanation / reply with only) is
  142 turns (0.110 %). EXPAND:CONTRACT = 121:1. "how many" turns number 2,042 and NONE has a
  five-word-or-fewer reply.
  [M] The corpus's own modal yes/no answer is a short answer plus a long justification: of 72 yes/no
  turns, 16 (22.2 %) are exactly Yes/No, but 31 (43.1 %) open with Yes/No and continue for a median
  of 95 words. Corpus-wide 6,115 replies open with Yes/No and 18 (0.3 %) are pure. So
  "Yes, fish live in trees. They are a variety of fish..." is the corpus's conditional MODE for that
  turn shape, not a contract failure - which is a sharper statement than round 44's scoring.

THE RIVAL EXPLANATION, WHICH THE DATA STORY DOES NOT COVER
  [M]+[I] TOKEN-LEVEL MLE LENGTH BIAS. At the decision point after a model emits "Yes.", 31 corpus
  continuations run a median 95 words while 16 stop: continuation carries roughly 250:1 of the TOKEN
  mass even though 22 % of TURN TYPES stop. The per-token optimum is therefore to continue, and the
  panel scores a TURN-level property against a TOKEN-level objective. This explains the one fact the
  support story does not: the model gets the FIRST token right for yes/no and loses it at the SECOND.
  Both explanations predict 0 of 6 compliance; they differ on what fixes it.

INSTRUMENT DEFECTS, FOUND IN THE FROZEN PANEL
  [M] Two of the six compliance items are weakly posed: "Reply with exactly one word: yes" contains
  the demanded word in the prompt, so "Yes, that's a good example." opens with the correct token and
  may be echo rather than compliance; and the first-token probe reads the protocol-2 separator, which
  makes it the wrong instrument for yes/no - a SECOND-token failure. The six-item instrument cannot
  separate "no form variable" from "a form variable outweighed by continuation mass". Fixing the
  instrument is now a prerequisite for spending on this target.
