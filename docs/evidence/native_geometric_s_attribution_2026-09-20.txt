DECISION
--------
No history lead. S11's measured gain is a current-token query/emission calibration: the older row
alone is indistinguishable from E, dropping it improves CE with the interval entirely below zero on
both panels, and a content-free length-conditioned fit average beats the individual state. The
conditional donor is silent because it holds the length-conditioned offset fixed, which is the only
part of the history term with a measurable effect. Corrected classification: the prompt's second
branch — preserve the local query result and target state maintenance directly; do not advertise the
120-state group product as useful memory. Not model promotion; not an equivalence proof.

BOUNDARIES
----------
One 120-state register holds at most log2(120) ~ 6.91 bits. The panel is nominal repeated open
development on correlated repository documents. No alpha, conversation, coding, frontier or energy
claim. The four-bit Continue/ResetTo monoid, exact occurrence/version access, shared composition and
full M1 cost remain unrun. Prime/zeta/R4/S3/H4/icosian roles and D0-b are unchanged.
