UOR-R4 — Run card: a synthetic short-form instruction split for the missing answer-form tail
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 81). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHY THIS, AND WHY NOW
  Round 79 measured the corpus's response-length distribution from the verified mask: median 350
  tokens, 0.0 % of 129,465 responses at three tokens or fewer, 67.8 % above two hundred. Round 80
  showed every ingredient source is prose-dominant (medians 701, 438, 225, 174, 103 and 45 tokens,
  none reaching 0.3 % at ten tokens or fewer). Instruction precision - performing "answer with one
  word", a yes/no, or a number - is therefore not learnable from this material by any mixture, and
  four arms have now failed to move it while the causes were eliminated one at a time (capacity,
  objective, decoding, prompt protocol).
  What is missing is not knowledge and not register; it is the ANSWER FORM. A synthetic split
  supplies exactly that, uses no new source, and needs no licence decision.

WHAT TO BUILD
  A Rust tool (the project's own language, and the tokenizer and dialogue protocol are already
  available as libraries) that emits, in the format the trainer already consumes:
    * tokens: the same literal-role protocol 2 stream the existing corpus uses, i.e. BOS, then
      "User:" + question + "Assistant:" + answer + EOS per turn, with the role markers encoded to
      the ids the corpus uses (User: [55, 2728, 28]; Assistant: [35, 560, 652, 714, 28]);
    * response_mask.u8: 1 for the answer tokens and the terminating EOS, 0 for BOS, both role
      markers and the question - the rule the manifest states and the mask reconstructed in round 80
      matches bit-for-bit;
    * manifest.json: the schema stack_dialogue.rs:53-80 requires, i.e.
      drops.special_token_occurrences = 0 and files[] entries carrying label, tokens and
      special_token_occurrences.
  Targets in the first version, all generated programmatically and therefore licence-free:
    one-word answers ("Answer with one word: what colour is grass?" -> "Green"),
    yes/no ("Answer yes or no: is the sky blue?" -> "Yes"),
    numbers ("What is two plus two?" -> "4"; "How many days are in a week?" -> "7"),
    short lists ("List exactly three colours, separated by commas." -> "Red, green, blue."),
    short factual names ("What is the capital of France?" -> "Paris.").
  Size: a few thousand to tens of thousands of pairs, varied in wording so the model learns the
  INSTRUCTION rather than a template - the failure mode to watch is a new template habit replacing
  the old one.

HOW TO USE IT
  Mix it with the conversational corpus rather than replacing it: the existing corpus supplies
  register, conversation and knowledge; the synthetic split supplies form. Two arms are worth
  running, a small weight (for example 5-10 % of tokens) and a larger one, because the mixture
  weight is the free parameter and the panel is the instrument.

HOW TO JUDGE IT - the instruments are already frozen
  1. The 22-turn instruction panel (docs/evidence/chat_instruction_panel_2026-10-08.txt): the target
     is compliance above 0 of 6, with the exact-count and one-word turns the sharpest.
  2. The separator-skipping first-token probe (docs/evidence/probe_fixed_2026-10-08.txt): the target
     is first content tokens of "Yes", "No", a number, or a name where those are what the turn asks
     for.
  And two cautions recorded in advance: fluency must not fall (the 22 of 22 baseline is the guard),
  and a new template must not replace the old one - the failure mode of role_only was exactly that.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE APPROACH
  If the synthetic split is trained in at a meaningful weight and the panel's compliance stays at
  0 of 6 while fluency holds, then the reply-form hypothesis is wrong too, and the remaining
  candidate is the training objective's granularity (per-token next-token loss over response regions
  with no notion of a completed answer) rather than the data. That outcome would be decisive and is
  worth the run precisely because it is cheap.

PREREQUISITES, ALL ALREADY IN PLACE
  * the mask rule, proven bit-identical against the builder's mask_sha256 (round 80);
  * dialogue-train consuming tokens, mask and manifest (rounds 55-58), with policy=full_prefix the
    measured working configuration;
  * the two frozen instruments and a working export-and-serve chain (rounds 37-58);
  * a pod with a renewed lease (23:19Z) and a watcher pattern that has already run unattended once
    (round 67's watcher produced round 74's results without intervention).

SCOPE
  This is a run card: no synthetic split has been generated, no arm trained, no result claimed. Its
  value is that the design, the exact format, the verification rule, the instruments and the
  falsifier are fixed before the work starts, which is what the pre-registered arms of round 48 and
  round 56 did well.
