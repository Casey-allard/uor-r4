WHAT IT ESTABLISHES
  1. CAPACITY IMPROVES BOTH AXES WITHOUT COST. Compliance stays perfect, a factual answer the smaller
     model could not produce appears, and the retention turn now refers to the thing it was told about.
     That is the opposite of a trade: at 2.8x parameters the model gets better at the demanded forms
     AND at using its context.
  2. BUT RETENTION IS NOT FULLY RESTORED. "My cat," refers to the right entity and does not state the
     count that was given; the name turn still fails. So the pre-registered fork lands between its
     branches: capacity is not sufficient by itself. What remains is not "does the model attend to its
     context" - it demonstrably does now - but precision in extracting the requested attribute from it.
  3. THAT REFRAMES THE REMAINING PROBLEM USEFULLY. The failure is no longer "multi-turn conditioning is
     destroyed by the demand store"; it is "the model can ground a follow-up in its context and cannot
     yet read a specific attribute out of it". Attributes of that kind are exactly what the demand
     store already teaches for single facts, so the natural next artefact is a demand store whose
     follow-ups ask for a SPECIFIC ATTRIBUTE of the previous turn (the count, the name, the colour)
     rather than a repetition of the whole answer - which is a small extension of the generator that
     produced 2,860 such documents tonight.

  answer, and a follow-up that refers to its context. It is the first configuration in this session
