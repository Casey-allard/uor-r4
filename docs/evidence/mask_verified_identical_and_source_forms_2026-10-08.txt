UOR-R4 — The reconstructed mask is byte-identical to the builder's, and no source can teach short replies
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 80). DeepSeek session. References #820.

RESULT 1: THE MASK RECONSTRUCTION IS PROVEN EXACT
  Rounds 50-55 argued from source that the response mask could be recovered by scanning the corpus for
  its literal role markers, and round 53 counted those markers. The corpus manifest carries the
  builder's own expected hash:

    manifest mask_sha256  28b7e98bb8a85368596dd7d1b8aa8aa16a277879a8368925dadbd37f20c3b3c1
    reconstructed mask    28b7e98bb8a85368596dd7d1b8aa8aa16a277879a8368925dadbd37f20c3b3c1   MATCH
    manifest mask_bytes   82,248,461          reconstructed     82,248,461                    MATCH

  AND THE MANIFEST'S mask_rule IS THE RULE THAT WAS IMPLEMENTED, verbatim in meaning: "1 = each token
  of an assistant turn's response content and its terminating <|eos|>; 0 = <|bos|>, all role markers,
  turn separators, system/user content, and an unmasked document-terminal <|eos|>".

  So the reconstruction required no rebuild, no re-fetch and no new licence decision, and it is now
  PROVEN BIT-EXACT against the builder's own record rather than argued. This also retroactively
  validates the two rejected mask rules of round 55: the validator's complaints were real and the
  third rule is the correct one.

  Per-source response-length distributions, computed by slicing the verified mask at the manifest's

  THE SHORTEST SOURCE HAS A MEDIAN OF FORTY-FIVE TOKENS, and not one source reaches 0.3 % of
  responses at ten tokens or fewer. Round 79 measured 0.0 % at three tokens for the corpus as a
  whole; this shows the same is true of every ingredient separately, so no re-weighting or
  re-selection of the existing sources can produce an answer-form tail either. The constraint is the
  data itself, not its mixture.

WHAT THIS LEAVES, STATED WITHOUT CLAIMING IT WORKS
  Instruction precision - answering "reply with exactly one word", a yes/no, or a number with that
  form - needs replies of that form, and neither this corpus nor any part of it contains them. Two
  routes remain, and they are cheap relative to the 1.39 B-token rebuild that rounds 36-48 correctly
  argued against:
    1. SYNTHETIC SHORT-FORM INSTRUCTION DATA derived from the corpus the project already has and
       already has the right to use: factual statements in it can be turned into question/answer
       pairs whose answers are a single word, a number or a yes/no, with the response mask marking
       exactly the short answer. This needs no new source and no new licence, and the tooling to
       write such a split (prepare-chat-parquet's source selection plus the mask rule now proven)
       already exists.
    2. A NEW LICENCE-CLEAN SHORT-FORM SOURCE, which is an owner decision.
  Route 1 is the one this evidence supports trying first, because it is bounded, uses material the
  project already holds, and directly supplies the missing tail rather than more of the same.

SCOPE
  One corpus, its manifest, one reconstructed mask, exact counts. The hash match is a verifiable
  identity; the per-source distributions are exact for this corpus and say nothing about sources not
  measured. No training result is claimed.
