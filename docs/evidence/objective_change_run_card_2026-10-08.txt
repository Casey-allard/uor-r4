UOR-R4 — Run card: the objective change that the elimination left
Date: 2026-10-08 (overnight round 94). DeepSeek session. References #820.

WHY THIS AND NOT ANYTHING ELSE
  Round 93 closed the elimination. Capacity (2.8x parameters), optimization (2x steps), decoding
  (sampling), the prompt protocol (a real defect, fixed) and now DATA SUPPORT (14.75x the short-form
  episode support, one argument, no compliance change) have each been ruled out by a measured arm.
  The remaining explanation, and the one that predicts the observed failure signature "Yes, fish" -
  first token right, second wrong - is TOKEN-LEVEL MLE LENGTH BIAS: after a turn-level answer is
  complete, the corpus's continuations carry roughly 250 times the token mass of its stops, so the
  per-token optimum is to continue.
  The fix therefore has to make a COMPLETED ANSWER worth something to the loss, and this card
  specifies where.

WHERE THE HOOK IS, ACCORDING TO THE ROUND-81 REVIEW
  [R] dialogue_episodes.rs:572-582 assigns the per-target weights: weight 1.0 iff
  position + 1 >= prefix_positions, i.e. every response token is weighted equally and the assistant
  marker and everything before it are weighted 0. Response regions are exactly identified by the
  verified mask (proven byte-identical to the builder's mask_sha256 in round 80), and each region
  ENDS at the terminating <|eos|> by construction of the mask rule.
  So the terminal token of every response is already addressable in the training data: it is the
  last position of each masked run.

THREE CANDIDATE CHANGES, CHEAPEST FIRST
  1. TURN-TERMINAL WEIGHTING. Multiply the weight of the response's terminating EOS position by a
     factor w > 1 (for example 5, 20, 100) and leave everything else identical. This is the smallest
     possible intervention that directly attacks the 250:1 continuation-to-stop mass ratio, it needs
     no new data, and it changes one term in the weight assignment the review located. Predicted
     effect if the diagnosis is right: the model begins to terminate after a correct short answer.
     Failure mode to watch: premature termination in the prose arms - fluency is the guard.
  2. LENGTH-NORMALISED COMPLETION TERM. Score each response by its own mean log-probability rather
     than by its summed token mass, which removes the mechanical advantage long replies have in the
     gradient. Larger change than 1 (it alters the estimand, not just a weight) and therefore a
     second experiment, not a first.
  3. TERMINATING DECODING RULE. Stop generation when the reply satisfies the requested form, without
     touching training at all. This is a serving-side patch rather than a learned capability: it can
     demonstrate the diagnosis quickly and it cannot be the final answer for a model that should
     learn when to stop - but it is nearly free and it isolates the diagnosis from the fix.
  Recommendation: run 1 with a small sweep of w, and 3 in parallel as a diagnostic, because they
  answer different questions (can the loss be made to value stopping; does stopping alone restore
  compliance).

HOW TO JUDGE, WITH THE INSTRUMENTS ALREADY FIXED
  * Panel v2 read as the first TWO content tokens per reply (docs/evidence/panel_v2_instrument_fix_2026-10-08.txt),
    with the echo item removed: the target is a first-token "Yes"/"No"/number/colour where the turn
    asks for one, and NO LONGER just a correct opener.
  * Fluency is the guard, and so are the two retention pairs: a change that buys compliance by
    destroying multi-turn conditioning is not progress.
  * Development nll is NOT the metric. Three arms have now shown it does not track this objective's
    behaviour (r5 vs r4, r6 better behaviour with worse nll, r7 worse nll with equivalent behaviour,
    and truncated_prefix's best-in-class 1.3623 with no compliance gain). It may be reported and must
    not be selected on.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE DIAGNOSIS
  If turn-terminal weighting at every factor tested still produces no compliance, then the
  continuation-mass explanation is wrong too, and the remaining candidate is that the requested form
  is not represented at all in the prefix representation the model conditions on - a REPRESENTATION
  question rather than a loss, data, or scale one - which would be the first result of the night
  pointing back at the architecture. That outcome is worth the run because it is cheap and it would
  be decisive about where the remaining work belongs.

PREREQUISITES, ALL IN PLACE
  * the mask, proven bit-identical to the builder's hash (round 80), giving the terminal positions;
  * dialogue-train consuming tokens, mask and manifest with policy=full_prefix (the measured working
    configuration);
  * panel v2 and the two-token reader installed on the pod;
  * an export-and-serve chain that produced every measurement tonight, plus a watcher pattern that
    has already run an arm to completion unattended (round 67);
  * the falsified alternatives documented on main, so this card is not re-litigating dead ends.

SCOPE
  A run card: no loss change has been written, no arm trained, no result claimed. Its value is that
  the hook, the three candidate changes, the judging instruments, the guard, and the falsifier are
  fixed before the work starts - the pattern that made rounds 48, 56 and 81 decisive.
