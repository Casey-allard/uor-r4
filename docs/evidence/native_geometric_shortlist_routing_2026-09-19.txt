WHY THIS MEASUREMENT EXISTS
  The previous experiment (`native_geometric_vsa_codebook_repair_2026-09-19.txt`) concluded
  "negative for the repair", and explicitly marked the ROUTING role NOT_MEASURED because
  `ablate-prose` scores the FULL vocabulary and therefore cannot see routing. That caveat was
  load-bearing: it turns out to have been the wrong place to look.

  The VSA context vector is also a routing input. `HierarchicalCodebook::
  route_shortlist_q30_with_memory` (runtime.rs:1200) ranks coarse H4 sectors by the predicted
  geometric state `s3` and selects FINE leaf buckets by comparing the context hypervector
  against the bucket centroids. The codebook acts on the fine level — and that path was not
  measured.

COHERENCE REQUIREMENT MADE EXPLICIT
  The artifact's stored `HierarchicalCodebook` centroids are bundles of the token vectors used
  AT EXPORT TIME, i.e. the fixed `splitmix64(vsa_seed, token_id)` codes. Comparing a
  root-derived query against those centroids is meaningless, so the `root` mode must REBUILD
  the hierarchical codebook in the matching space (`HierarchicalCodebook::new`). A codebook
  change without a matching rebuild would have measured an incoherent system.

NEW INSTRUMENT
  `crates/uor-r4-core/src/bin/shortlist-recall.rs`
    * routes each position through the Voronoi leg only (empty seed list, isolating it from the
      engram/induction seeds the served path also injects)
    * reports routed-shortlist recall of the teacher-forced target, mean routed size, and the
      mean routing rank of the target when present
    * scores EXACTLY the routed candidates and reports `shortlistBPB` — routing plus scoring,
      i.e. the served path — charging an unrouted target at `log2(vocab)` bits, because no
      score can select a candidate that was never routed
    * reports the mean Jaccard overlap of the two modes' routed sets, i.e. how much the codebook
      changes routing at all

RESULT — THE REPAIR DOES HELP, ON THE SERVED PATH
  The `root` codebook is better on every routing metric, on both disjoint slices:

  | metric                    | fixed     | root      | change            |
  |---------------------------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
  | routed recall             | 8.4-8.8 % | 10.7-11.2 % | +2.1 to +2.5 pp |
  | mean routing rank         | 27.4-28.2 | 23.7-25.2 | better by ~3-4 slots |
  | served-path shortlist BPB | 2.72-2.83 | 2.67-2.78 | -0.045 to -0.053 |
  | unrouted targets          | ~91.4 %   | ~89.1 %   | -2.3 pp           |

  The codebook genuinely acts on routing: the two modes agree at only mean Jaccard ~0.53, with
  identical shortlists on 3 % of positions.

  This REVERSES the previous conclusion, and the reversal is direct evidence for the owner's D2
  correction. Reading the fixed-hash ablation alone ("NEGLIGIBLE, mildly negative, class
  enabler") would have invited retiring a mechanism that measurably improves the served path.
  The full-vocabulary instrument could not see it by construction: it gives every position a
  100 % candidate ceiling, so it is blind to anything that acts through candidate SELECTION
  rather than through scoring.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT CLAIMED
  * CLAIMED: on the Voronoi routing leg, with the engram/induction seeds excluded, the root
    codebook improves recall and served-path BPB by the amounts above, replicated on two
    disjoint slices.
  * NOT CLAIMED: the absolute numbers are not the model's served BPB. The Voronoi leg alone
    routes only 9-11 % of targets; the real served path also injects up to 16 engram/induction
    seeds, which this instrument deliberately excludes, and engram is the largest measured
    count contributor (+0.2228 BPB when ablated). Only the DELTA between modes is meaningful.
  * NOT CLAIMED: any capability or efficiency result. This is a routing-recall and loss
    measurement on an already-open development slice.

LIMITATIONS
  * Single already-open development slice pair (tokens [0..4160) at 4,096 positions and
    [40960..45120) at 4,095 positions); not a fresh draw. Same distribution, two disjoint
    windows.
  * The unrouted penalty `log2(vocab)` is a convention; a different convention changes the
    absolute BPB but not the sign or rough size of the delta.
  * Debug build. Softmax uses the serving sampler scale at temperature 1.0.
  * `root` is NOT wired into serving. Doing so requires rebuilding the `HierarchicalCodebook`
    at load (a serving-path change with a load-time cost) and an end-to-end serve verification
    before it could become the default. Default remains `fixed`.
  * The 9,984-case regression replay was not re-executed.
