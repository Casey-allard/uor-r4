### Two-Curve Evidential Separation (Commission Requirement, #2028 6195270941 & #2029 6196365768)

The two trajectories in the 36-point table have distinct mathematical and empirical causes that must not be conflated:

1. **Across `K` at a fixed checkpoint (evaluation-time widening):** At a fixed checkpoint snapshot, token-cell assignments and the per-row `(address_distance, cell_id)` ordering of occupied cells are fixed (`crates/uor-r4-discrete/src/discrete.cu:274–319` on `main`, `:208–250` at the Stage-A build `abdf431ed`). Increasing `K` strictly nests the admitted cell set (independently verified per-row across all `4 × 14,287 × 9` records). Whenever at least one token rival is already admitted (`token_rivals > 0`), admitting additional cells can only introduce new candidate tokens and therefore can never increase the minimum rival token distance `rival_distance`; consequently, unconditional `strict_wins` (`td < rival_distance` with `token_rivals > 0`) is **strictly non-increasing across `K`** (confirmed at **`32/32` adjacent `K` steps (`4 cursors × 8`)**), while `target_admitted_rows` increases strictly at all **`32/32` adjacent `K` steps (`4 cursors × 8`)**. Newly admitted rival tokens whose full-vector `token_distance` beats the target can displace the target from global `top1` / `top8`. This is a structural property of the current writer/evaluator nesting, not a failure of attention.
2. **Across training cursors at a fixed `K` (training-time trajectory):** Across `0 → 1,640 → 4,101 → 8,202 → 12,303 → 16,404`, codebook updates diversify cell occupancy (`16 → 124 → 189` occupied cells), move query vectors `q`, and alter the `(address_distance, cell_id)` cell ordering, so no monotonicity theorem holds across time. In the 36-point quarter sweep (`4,101 → 8,202 → 12,303 → 16,404`), `target_admitted_rows` declines strictly at all **`27/27` adjacent cursor steps (`9 K × 3`)** (`2,201 → 2,131 → 1,954 → 1,920` across Q1–Q4 at `K=8`, with pre-quarter context `5,680 → 2,180` at cursors `0 → 1,640`; `3,242 → 3,206 → 2,509 → 2,400` across Q1–Q4 at `K=16`); occupancy diversification, training-induced admission erosion under the nearest-rival hinge, and parameter-family exposure across quarters (`VectorBlock 0..16,383` vs final-quarter `Route 0..19`) are the causal mechanisms under test, not already-isolated causes. This sweep does **not** prove that `K=16` training will fix the ceiling; it motivates the controlled `K=16` objective test below.

### Excluded False Universal Readings (Auditor §2.3, #2029 6197008827)

This sheet explicitly rejects two over-generalizations of the Q4 row:

- **"Admitted strict wins fall with `K`" is FALSE as a universal claim:** `admitted_strict` *rises* with `K` at 2 of the 4 cursors (`41` at `K=8` → `58` at `K=16` at Q1, peaking at `59` at `K=15`; `42` at `K=8` → `60` at `K=16` at Q2) and is non-monotone across `K` at every cursor (`Q3: 44 → 36 → 40`; `Q4: 65 → 62 → 63`). Because `admitted_strict` intersects `strict_wins` with target-cell admission (`admitted_cells - address_rivals == 1`), newly admitted target cells at wider `K` can contribute new admitted-strict wins even while unconditional `strict_wins` never rises.
- **"Widening `K` hurts `top8` (or `top1`)" is FALSE as a universal claim (Q3/Q4-only):** At Q1 and Q2, widening `K=8 → K=16` *improves* both `top8` (`1,090 → 1,160` at Q1, peaking at `1,164` at `K=15`; `967 → 1,083` at Q2) and `top1` (`96 → 136` at Q1; `94 → 138` at Q2). Widening lowers `top8` and `top1` only at Q3 (`953 → 879`, `96 → 88`) and Q4 (`747 → 586`, `149 → 145`), and only Q4's `top8` curve (`747 → 738 → 686 → 674 → 655 → 631 → 611 → 605 → 586`) decreases monotonically across `K=8..16`.

## Six Mandatory Measured Facts Governing This Redirect (#2029 6197008827)

Every design choice, gate, and control in this sheet is anchored to the six facts verified by Kimi PM's independent evidence audit of the sealed Stage-A data:

1. **Fact 1 — The address hinge is exactly `K`-invariant for `K ≥ 8` and dominates 60.7%–62.8% of the weighted loss; upweighting `A` cannot move the `K` boundary.**
   - Across all four cursors (`4,101 / 8,202 / 12,303 / 16,404`), `address_hinge_sum` at `K=8` and `K=16` (and every intermediate `K ∈ {8..16}`) is **identically equal to the integer**: `261,344 / 262,360 / 270,500 / 276,608`.
   - *Source mechanism (`discrete.cu:295–319`):* `writer` selects the first `K` occupied cells in ascending `(address_distance(q, g, s, c), c)` order and sets `rival_address` to the minimum `address_distance` among admitted non-target cells (`if (cell != target_cell && cost < rival_address) rival_address = cost`). Because `K ≥ 8` always admits at least one non-target cell within the first two occupied cells, `rival_address` is already fixed inside the first 2 (and a fortiori first 8) occupied cells. Widening `K` from `8` to `16` never alters `rival_address` or `ad = address_distance(q, g, s, target_cell)`, so `A = max(0, 8 + ad - rival_address)` is mathematically invariant to `K` for all `K ≥ 2` at fixed state.
   - Under the exact loss identity `total_loss_numerator = 8·token_hinge + 32·address_hinge` (`width = 32`), the address term `32·A` constitutes **60.7% to 62.8%** of the aggregate weighted loss numerator across Q1–Q4. Upweighting `A` in `8·T + w_A·A` only amplifies a nearest-rival margin signal that is blind to the `K`-cell admission boundary.
2. **Fact 2 — The objective is active on every eligible row (no satisfied regime anywhere in the lineage).**
   - At `16404-K8` and across all 36 cells of the sweep, the serialized `EvaluationAggregate` fields record `token_unviolated_eligible_rows = 0` (`T > 0` on all `14,287` rows) and `eligible_unviolated_rows = 0` (`8·T + 32·A > 0` on all `14,287` rows), while `address_unviolated_eligible_rows` is `0` at `16,404` and `12,303` (and `2` at `4,101`, `1` at `8,202` — active on `99.986%–100%` of eligible rows). There is no zero-hinge satisfied regime anywhere in the lineage.
3. **Fact 3 — Separate admission and ranking bars: `top1` is no longer admission-capped at `K=16`, while `top8` remains admission-capped everywhere.**
   - At `K=16`, `target_admitted_rows` at all four cursors (`3,242 / 3,206 / 2,509 / 2,400`) **exceeds Bar (i) = `1,941`**, yet `top1_correct` measures `136 / 138 / 88 / 145`. At `K=16`, Bar (i) is therefore no longer blocked by cell admission — it exhibits a **pure within-admitted ranking gap of `1,796` to `1,853` rows** (`1,941 − 145 = 1,796` at Q4).
   - Conversely, **Bar (ii) = `4,775` (`recall@8`) remains hard admission-capped at every cell** of the 36-point table (maximum admission anywhere is `3,242` at `4101-K16`, and `2,400` at `16404-K16`).
   - Consequently, this redirect **separates the admission gate (`target_admitted_rows ≥ 4,894`) from the ranking bars (`top1 ≥ 1,941`, `top8 ≥ 4,775`)**: admission-only `KEEP` is forbidden, and outcome attribution distinguishes an admission miss from a within-admitted ranking miss.
4. **Fact 4 — The strict-win guard is unreachable at every cell of the sweep (`max = 214` vs bar `≈ 525`) and widening `K` moves `strict_wins` the wrong way; re-measure baseline `u` at the new lineage's checkpoint 0.**
   - Across all 36 cells, `strict_wins` peaks at `214` (`12303-K8`) against the seed-1 `K=8` guard threshold `≈ 525` (`u = 105 / 14,287 ≈ 0.735%`, `max(1%, min(5u, u + 5pp)) ≈ 3.67%`), and widening `K=8 → K=16` lowers `strict_wins` at every checkpoint (`177 → 100`, `178 → 100`, `214 → 111`, `105 → 99`).
   - Because changing `spec.kept_cells` to `16` changes the admitted rival set at initialization, a `K=16`-trained lineage **must re-measure its guard baseline `u` at its own `K=16` checkpoint 0 before any `.apply` update** (`completion_baseline.rs:70, 279`), never reusing the `K=8` constant `u = 105 / 14,287`.
5. **Fact 5 — Capacity was trained out (`5,680 → 2,180 → 1,920`), not absent at initialization; measure the untrained `K=16` ceiling at checkpoint 0.**
   - At `K=8` checkpoint 0, `target_admitted_rows` was **`5,680` — `786` rows above the `4,894` admission gate** — before eroding to `2,180` at pilot end (`1,640`) and `1,920` at Q4 (`16,404`) as training diversified occupancy (`16 → 189` cells) under `8·T + 32·A`. The decisive phenomenon is **training-induced admission erosion**, not an inherent lack of width in the substrate.
   - Because the optional pilot checkpoint-0 (`0`) and checkpoint-1,640 (`1,640`) points were not run in `sweep-1`, the **untrained `K=16` ceiling at checkpoint 0** remains unmeasured in sealed receipts (analytically, since the xorshift low-4-bit initializer populates `≤ 16` occupied cells at checkpoint 0, `K=16` admits all occupied cells on every row at initialization). Step 3 (`D-Packet`) of the execution protocol below binds a zero-update GPU measurement (`G-Pre`) of checkpoint-0 at both `K=8` and `K=16` (and pilot checkpoint `1,640` at `K=8..16` if staged) before any training batch runs, directly separating "capacity exists untrained" from "capacity is preserved/trained in across the 16,404-batch trajectory" at near-zero cost.
6. **Fact 6 — The `K`-widening admission benefit collapses across training (`+1,041 → +1,075 → +555 → +480`); size Stage B from Q4 slopes, never Q1/Q2.**
   - The admission gain `tgt_adm(K16) − tgt_adm(K8)` falls from `+1,041` (`+130.1` rows/cell) at Q1 and `+1,075` (`+134.4` rows/cell) at Q2 to `+555` (`+69.4` rows/cell) at Q3 and **`+480` (`+60.0` rows/cell) at Q4**. Sizing any `K>8` run or cost/headroom expectation from Q1/Q2 slopes would overstate final-quarter admission gain by `> 2.2×`. All target sizing and occupancy/runtime projections in this sheet are anchored at the **Q4 (`16,404`) state**, never Q1/Q2.

## Causal Design: Minimal Discriminating Pair at `K=16` (`K16-A` Control vs `K16-R` Candidate)

At the inspected kernel (`crates/uor-r4-discrete/src/discrete.cu:274–319`), `address_distance` inspects only the first (`0`) and last (`s[1]-1`) 8D blocks, `token_distance` inspects all `s[1] = 4` blocks (`width = 32`), and `writer` selects the first `K = s[3]` occupied cells ordered by `(address_distance, cell_id)`. Because `A = max(0, 8 + ad - rival_address)` compares `target_cell` against the single **nearest admitted non-target cell** (`Fact 1`), it penalizes `target_cell` whenever it is not rank 1+margin, yet gives zero boundary signal at rank `K` vs `K+1`. Meanwhile, during training (`discrete_counterfactual`, `discrete.cu:356–369`), changing `K=8 → K=16` still alters the training trajectory under `8·T + 32·A` because `token_hinge` (`T`) and baseline row eligibility (`tr > 0`) take the minimum `token_distance` over all tokens in the `K` admitted cells.

To isolate the **admission objective** without confounding it with width `K`, address metric, read head, or schedule, this sheet pre-registers a **two-arm matched contrast at `K=16`** (`K16-A` vs `K16-R`):

| **3. Isolates the objective (`32·A` vs `32·R`) at `K=16`. |
