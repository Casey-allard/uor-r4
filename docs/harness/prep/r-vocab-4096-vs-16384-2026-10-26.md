## Why this comparison is registered

[`docs/roadmap/items/r-vocab-4096-vs-16384.md`](../../roadmap/items/r-vocab-4096-vs-16384.md) (`#2887`), [`docs/labs/repo-renormalization-2026-10-19/audit/synthesis.md:41`](../../labs/repo-renormalization-2026-10-19/audit/synthesis.md), and [`docs/research/open-questions.md:47`](../../research/open-questions.md) ask whether expanding the BPE vocabulary from `V = 4,096` to `V = 16,384` pieces is a lever on (1) writer/serving throughput and per-row primitive evaluation cost once the `O(V · B)` full-scan bottleneck is removed by `#2840` (`m1-faster-writer`), (2) BPE piece layout and numeric boundary invariants (`#2872` `number-aware-tokenizer`), and (3) discrete `B = 4` `StructuredV1` codebook/cell capacity (`crates/uor-r4-discrete`).

## Bar

1. **`B1` (Matched GPU Writer Parity & Primitive Evaluation Reduction at `V = 4,096` vs `V = 16,384`, from [`m1-faster-writer-2026-10-26.md`](m1-faster-writer-2026-10-26.md)):**
   - Exact parity (`0` mismatches on `top1_token`, `top1_distance`, `top8`, `admitted_cells`, `target_admitted`, `target_rank`, `loss_total`, `tie_count`) between `Bucketed(IdOrder)`, `Bucketed(ServingPruned)`, and `FullScan` at both `V = 4,096` and `V = 16,384` across `K ∈ {1, 8, 16}` and uniform occupancy (`16` vs `64` tokens/cell).
   - `token_cell == 0`, `cell_distance <= 257`, `token_distance <= K * max_c |B[c]| + 2`, and `>= 10x` reduction in total primitive comparisons per row at both `V = 4,096` and `V = 16,384`.
   - Quantify the exact `V = 16,384 / V = 4,096` scaling ratio for `FullScan` (`~4.00x` per-row work) vs `Bucketed(IdOrder)` (`2.00x` at `K = 8` uniform because the `256` cell distances are fixed and only `K · (V / 256)` token distances scale with `V`) vs `Bucketed(ServingPruned)`.
2. **`B2` (Matched BPE Piece & Numeric-Boundary Invariants at `V = 4,096` vs `V = 16,384`, from [`number-aware-tokenizer-DRAFT-2026-10-22.md`](number-aware-tokenizer-DRAFT-2026-10-22.md)):**
   - Byte-identical determinism across 2 training runs per `(arm, V)` (`6 / 6` pairs), lossless `decode(encode(text)) == text` round-trip, and exact digit-piece census (`pure_single_digit = 10`, `pure_multi_digit >= 100` including all `100` two-digit strings `"00".."99"`, `space_prefixed_digit == 0` on `trt` at both `V = 4,096` and `V = 16,384`; `ctrl1` `pure_multi_digit == 0` at both `V`; `ctrl2` `space_prefixed_digit > 0` at both `V`).
3. **`B3` (Discrete `B = 4` Substrate & Codebook Capacity Boundary at `V = 4,096` vs `V = 16,384`):**
   - Identify and verify the exact code-level invariants in `crates/uor-r4-discrete` and `crates/uor-r4-integer` that bound `V` in the full discrete driver (`Spec::validate` `2..=8192`, `first_n_primes` `<= 8192`, `address_cells::build` `quota = V / 256 <= 16` at line `322` under tied interior root `ROOTS[k]` at line `462`, `discrete_count_score` `<= 8192`, `d11_export` `149,386` and `IntegerDiscreteModel::from_bytes` `2..=8192`), separating what `V = 16,384` solves today from what requires `FullVectorV1`-style `(k / 16, k % 16)` interior-block indexing at `B = 4`.
