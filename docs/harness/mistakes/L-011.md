## What happened

Two deliveries in a row had to label themselves out of a closure they did not earn. The
D22 constructor transfer is headed `UNFINISHED_NOT_NEGATIVE. No current-parent model
result.` and the whole-path implementation says `KEEP the connected source reference; NOT
TRAINING READY`, with an eight-token fixture that "does not fit labels, run an optimizer,
qualify language/transfer or establish compute/energy savings". Both refuse to activate
their prepared source or satisfy their milestone. `AGENTS.md` states the rule they are
honouring: a partial result says `References #N`, not `Closes #N`, and creating a branch,
pushing commits or opening a PR is not completion.

## Symptom

A PR that closes an issue while its own record says NOT READY, UNFINISHED or NOT_RUN; or
work that is described as complete while it sits unmerged on a branch; or a merged PR whose
next step is the same as the previous one.

## Lesson

The verb in the body is a claim about state. `Closes` asserts the acceptance is met; a
fixture, a source reference or an instrument is not the acceptance. Work is complete when
the merge is verified on fresh `origin/main` with the delivered tree, and not before.

## How to apply

- Use `References #N` unless every acceptance item in the issue is genuinely met; then
  verify the merge commit and the tree on fresh `origin/main` before writing `Closes`.
- Put the honest qualifier in the record's first sentence
  (`UNFINISHED_NOT_NEGATIVE`, `NOT TRAINING READY`) and name the gates that remain.
- `uor-harness lint check` raises `prlint.org_rules` (info) when a body
  links no issue at all - an unlinked PR is invisible to the audit.
- Do not start the dependent successor while the delivery is unmerged.
