## 1. Decision and scope

Build one shared small-LUT circuit that conditions geometric query/key/transport operators and byte/EOS emission on both contextual state and selected exact content. Train its hard, discrete trajectories offline with per-invocation stochastic decisions and score-function credit. Export deterministic lookup tables. This supplies a specified credit path through changed source choices and later addresses; its variance and usefulness remain empirical questions.

Do not resume the old global two-entry categorical search in `shared_core/tied_training.rs`. That experiment already sampled shared table values once per trajectory and failed at its scope. The new distribution samples each operator invocation; repeated uses of a shared row contribute separate likelihood terms. The new object/readout interface and joint parameterization are also material changes. Merely changing the sampler without those interfaces is not this specification.

The first implementation is an experimental module under `native_geometric/addressed_attention/`, with no retained-model dispatch change. Reuse exact geometry, canonical token/phase tables and the proven identity/commit contracts; preserve the previous artifact loaders. The interface represents arbitrary observed byte spans. It does not import the old ASCII word splitter, 512-feature role inventory, prompt interpreter or answer-family heads.

## 2. Exact objects, bounded access and publication

Use an explicitly encoded 256-slot occurrence ring, two temporary 64-byte operand leases, one 64-byte active read lease, and eight committed result records. These are design capacities, not measured quality optima. The larger window permits short conversation/program examples that the old 32-byte window could not admit. The combined serialized object/session layer has a 64 KiB ceiling, including pending/provisional state; compiled model tables are separate. Allocate encoded maxima of128bytes per occurrence (32KiB),256bytes per each of three owned leases (768bytes),512bytes per result (4KiB), and the remaining27,904bytes for header/state/pending/provisional records. Explicit length-delimited canonical serialization must verify these limits; Rust struct padding is not the file format. No implicit all-history scan or chaining around the span limit.

An occurrence carries session epoch, monotonically checked sequence and host turn ID, exact byte identity, origin tag, two H4 key roots and four contextual roots. Immutable byte payload identities may use the precomputed UOR/prime byte registry; occurrence identity additionally includes epoch/sequence. Equal text does not identify the same occurrence. Never expose digest bits as distance features. Ordered spans bind start/end occurrence IDs, source kind and exact owned bytes; derived results additionally bind the ordered operand references, operator and value. Canonical encodings preserve operand order even for Add.

Candidates are at most 256 valid occurrence starts, eight committed result starts and Null. Every occurrence reference is checked against its complete epoch/sequence, not just ring index. A span cannot cross a host turn boundary, unobserved frontier or missing occurrence. Results are available only after publication. The length policy chooses from legal lengths 1..64; a selected result is a complete typed record, so its only legal extent is its full payload. Null has no payload or extent decision. No invalid address is reduced modulo the ring or vocabulary.

Copy a selected span into its lease after validation. Subsequent ring eviction cannot change the owned bytes. A result proposal is provisional until its entire canonical payload has been offered and observed in order; partial output, mismatch, clear, turn boundary or EOS cancels publication. A completed result is marked ready at acknowledgment of its final byte; the same byte's KEY phase assigns its two keys and four newly committed contextual roots, then publishes its distinct checked ID exactly once. It becomes selectable only at the next prediction. This reuses the existing KEY call, with no ninth circuit evaluation. Store reuse validates that full ID. A rejected link is not evidence of absence; a stale range is reported as invalid/evicted rather than prompting a canned abstention.

The operator vocabulary is exactly **Hold, AcquireA, AcquireB, AddAB, SubAB, Advance, Clear**. Acquire selects an owned span for the active read lease; it does not emit text. Add/Sub interpret the two learned selected spans with a complete canonical i64 codec, execute a checked scalar operation and create the provisional canonical result lease. Wrong text does not trigger a search for another number. Advance requires acknowledgment of the current lease byte and a next byte; Hold never advances; Clear cancels the active lease. Aliased operands are allowed and keep their actual identities. Overflow/conversion failure removes Add/Sub from the legal set. At most two successful arithmetic result publications per response are supported initially; the retained result store may contain earlier responses.

Parsing is only a typed selected-value codec: no request recognition, keyword dispatch or answer template. Use checked shift/add decimal accumulation and bounded integer decimal rendering (at most 20 bytes), with explicit i64::MIN coverage. Serving performs no matrix products, float, probability calculations or steady-state allocation. Scalar Add/Sub and finite group products have their declared typed meanings; this is not the frozen R4G1 runtime contract.

`predict()` is observational: create/cache one offer at the exact frontier, with selected references, proposed lease transition and byte/EOS. Repeated calls reproduce it without consuming cursors, IDs or RNG. `observe(actual, offer_id)` first validates the cached offer ID and frontier. An unknown, stale or already-consumed ID returns an error without any mutation; it cannot append a duplicate byte. A valid offer appends each actual byte once and commits only that identified offer at its matching frontier. External prompt ingestion uses a separate `observe_input(byte)` operation, which cancels pending offers/unfinished leases and ingests without claiming an offered output; the trainer must not use it to bypass mismatched response offers. A matching byte from another offer is not equivalent. An offered lease byte is acknowledged only when both the offered prediction and actual observation equal that lease byte. Mismatch cancels the lease/provisional result; it still ingests the actual observation normally. No automatic output correction occurs. Literal output is always predicted by the learned emission head, including bytes that happen to equal an exact source/result. This first design intentionally does not include a direct copy-to-output bypass.

Host `begin_turn` cancels unfinished offers/leases, advances turn ID and retains committed occurrences/results within bounds. `reset_session` changes epoch and clears them. Snapshot validation binds model/geometry, frontier, exact references, owned bytes, acknowledgment cursor, pending offer and publication state. Persistent learned assertion/revision/conflict writing is a later adapter: preserve fields for version identity, but do not claim current/initial/previous behavior from a byte ring. A future adapter must distinguish pinned-at-selection versus current-at-use reads and revalidate ordered dependencies.

## 3. One causal forward schedule

Each byte tick has at most eight evaluations of the **same** circuit with an explicit phase tag; absent fields are zero with separate validity bits.

1. **QUERY_A:** from committed roots/phase/frontier and existing acknowledged lease only, produce two query roots. Score all legal candidate keys plus Null; strict deterministic selection uses a fixed tie rule (Null first, then increasing full occurrence/result ID). No payload of an unselected candidate is an input.
2. **EXTENT_A:** after A is selected, expose its stored contextual roots and first byte; choose a legal extent and freeze A. Null skips this head.
3. **QUERY_B:** may consume the now-known A context/byte/length and committed state; select an independently addressed B, using the same query heads. This order allows a second read to depend on the first. It is not a dense weighted sum.
4. **EXTENT_B:** validate/freeze B using the same length head with a different phase tag.
5. **CONTROL:** inspect the two owned operands and current lease. Decode canonical scalar operands and the legal action set; choose one operator. Construct a pending active lease/result according to that action. Bounds and causal legality can mask actions; target correctness cannot.
6. **EMIT:** expose committed contextual roots, selected A/B bytes and the active selected/result byte, plus type/cursor/length/validity. Compute a 9-bit context address and its flat 257-way byte/EOS distribution during training, or compiled mode during serving. No target is present in these features.
7. **OBSERVE:** only after an actual byte arrives, acknowledge/cancel the identified offer, update fixed zeta phases from the observed byte's bound token identity, and use the shared circuit's four delta-root heads to compose the four new H4 state roots. Newly observed byte content is now legal input. EOS performs termination/publication validation and does not create a byte occurrence.
8. **KEY:** from this new state/observed byte, compute two roots and append the occurrence. The next prediction can read it; the preceding prediction could not.

Root update is an exact finite group action `s_l <- product(s_l, delta_l)`. The existing fixed token zeta phases are retained as typed phase-bin inputs; they are not learned or replaced by arbitrary frequencies. Four state roots are four slots, not four independently learned Galois companions. The exact paired-H4/icosian companion, basis/glue/inverse witness and fuller retained-fiber transport remain explicit integration duties; this first artifact must label them unimplemented rather than claiming them through a signature bit field. The same applies to learned durable prime/n-let pages beyond the bounded occurrence store.

Each candidate's score is the sum of two signed angular table ranks, `a(q0*k0^-1) + a(q1*k1^-1)`, with exact group composition. This is a fixed finite geometric compatibility rule using two scalar ranks, not a contraction over token value vectors. Null has two separately learned root choices. Both query decisions use those shared Null parameters. Training samples the two Null root choices afresh for each read invocation and includes each draw's score; it does not sample them once globally. A read gathers exact records; selected values are never blended with numeric attention weights.

## 4. Circuit, representation and metric decision

Use a Boolean LUT4 circuit with widths **1024 input bits → 256 → 64 → 16 → 9**, totaling 345 gates. Each gate reads four bits and indexes its 16-entry table. Layer-one wiring partitions all 1024 inputs; the next two layers partition their predecessors; the nine final gates use a seeded balanced covering of all 16 predecessors. Apply one recorded seed permutation per layer. Validate that every input reaches at least one final output, indices point only to the previous layer, and all declared live field groups can affect an output structurally. Wiring is fixed in this increment; learned mapping is not silently implied by the DWN citation. Structural reachability does not establish learned sensitivity.

The 9 output bits form an address 0..511, legal for every head. Eight root-valued heads (two query, two key, four delta), one length head, one seven-action control head and one 257-symbol emission head share this circuit. Phase tags distinguish calls; there are no experts or answer-family gates. Query and extent parameters are shared between A/B reads. Input packing, circuit topology and head tables are versioned together.

The [machine-readable contract](evidence/native_geometric_addressed_attention_contract_973.json) defines the bit layout. Geometry inputs use signed hemisphere observations against all 120 canonical landmarks, padded to 128 bits per root. Full exact roots remain in session memory for group operations; these Boolean observations are a declared lossy learned-circuit interface, not a replacement identity. Six root observation fields fit 768 bits; the remainder holds exact bytes, availability, phase, cursor/length/type and bounded age metadata. Reserved bits are zero. The phase determines which causal roots occupy the two selected-root fields, with no pre-gather content leakage.

**Choose signed angular scoring for the first implementation.** Hamming is an explicit comparator, not an extra learned weighting rule. A geometric signature can use `bit_l(r) = 1[a(r*inverse(landmark_l)) > 0]`; Hamming between these signatures measures differing geometric hemisphere predicates. It is neither Hamming on integer root labels nor on UOR hashes. This construction can have ties/distortions and is not assumed to preserve angular rank or be injective. Before any metric experiment, enumerate its 120 signatures, collisions, antipodes, symmetry and all 14,400 angular/Hamming pair relations. Freeze results without target labels. If a metric experiment is warranted, change only this score in a matched comparison; do not add an unconstrained semantic code alongside primary geometry.

The sparse-memory attention paper motivates that comparison, but its theorem does not establish this finite signature's linguistic usefulness. DWN's EFD likewise uses Hamming on local Boolean LUT addresses, a different distance/domain. [Previous source review](native_geometric_attention_reassessment_973.md) retains both distinctions.

## 5. Offline training objective and complete hard credit

All training is Rust on the local CPU. At gate/address j, sample a Bernoulli bit with `p_j = sigmoid(lambda_j)` at **every invocation**. At root, length, control and Null heads, sample a categorical legal choice using the row's logits, with temperature exactly 1. Invalid choices have zero probability because their causal legal mask is applied before normalization. Geometry, pointers, exact leases, integer operations and state transitions then execute hard. Frozen probabilities and deterministic counter-based RNG keys `(seed, batch, particle, document, event, phase, gate/head)` define reproducible draws; document/turn boundaries have explicit reset behavior.

At each scored position t, the EMIT head supplies conditional cross-entropy `ell_t = -log softmax(eta[c_t])[y_t]`. Then sample a hard offered symbol from that same distribution for the training offer. Teacher forcing observes y_t **after** prediction. This sample is necessary because acknowledgment/publication can affect later state: ignoring its score would miss downstream dependence on the emission parameters. The target never enters the pre-observation input address, source choice, operator mask or offer. Actual exported generation instead observes its own deterministic emitted bytes.

For the finite unrolled trajectory omega, minimize `J(theta) = E_omega[(1/T) sum_t ell_t]`. This is expected conditional teacher-forced loss under stochastic internal decisions, not the marginalized sequence likelihood or a promise of deterministic export quality. No JEPA, auxiliary role label, reward shaping, entropy schedule or clipping is part of the first objective.

For every stochastic event v, retain its visited row, hard outcome, pre-update probabilities and normalized downstream cost `Q_v = (1/T) sum_(t downstream of v) ell_t`, including the current emission loss if that loss follows v. The offered-symbol draw occurs **after** ell_t, so its Q begins at the next cost. A conservative implementation may use the complete mean trajectory loss L=(1/T)sum_t ell_t for every event; earlier action-independent costs have zero expected score contribution but add variance.

The gradient is

`g = direct conditional CE gradient + sum_v (Q_v - b_v) * grad log p_theta(a_v | preceding hard path)`.

A Bernoulli row contributes `(a_v - p_j)`; a categorical row contributes `1[a_v=r] - pi_j(r)` on its legal support. Add every visit into the same parameter row. Do not differentiate fictitiously through hard addresses, and do not count a repeatedly used row just once. Recompute the actual source/lease/state trajectory for every particle; no frozen-pointer replay supplies these gradients. Direct CE contributes `(softmax(eta[c_t]) - one_hot(y_t))/T` to the visited flat-head row; that head also receives offered-symbol score terms for future costs. Conditional gradients through integer input encodings are not invented.

Use four independent particles on the same fixed document batch, with **all parameters frozen until all four finish**. For initial correctness, use full-trajectory cost and the mean of the other three particles as baseline, stop-gradient through that baseline, and average all four score/direct terms. This is action-independent conditional on the frozen data/parameters. For serial particles, accumulate A=sum_b S_b, B=sum_b L_b*S_b, C=sum_b D_b and scalar U=sum_b L_b, where S_b sums every event score and D_b is the direct gradient of mean CE. Compute g=B/(P-1)-U*A/[P*(P-1)]+C/P with P=4. Replay only the completed particle's event tape into A/B after L_b is known, then release that tape. Four f64 arrays (parameters, A, B, C) suffice; overwrite B with the final gradient before the SGD update. Never finalize a particle against an incomplete baseline. One SGD update applies after accumulation. Exact arithmetic fixtures use f64, and the first trainer should also use f64; a later f32 optimization requires explicit measured comparison. No optimizer hyperparameters or training run are selected by this specification.

This construction follows stochastic-computation-graph accounting, which allows nondifferentiable hard internal choices when their sampling distributions and dependent costs are included. Its assumptions and baseline rule matter. It guarantees the estimator's expectation for the declared stochastic objective under those conditions, not low variance, improvement at finite learning rate or a useful deterministic language model. [Schulman et al., 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.05254).

DWN EFD/STE is deferred as a possible lower-variance biased surrogate. Its local Hamming-weighted input derivative does not supply a recurrent gather/address backward rule. Do not combine it with the score terms ad hoc or claim both unbiasedness and a frozen-pointer derivative. The prior global two-entry sampler remains preserved negative evidence, not a baseline to rerun merely for onboarding.

## 6. Export and artifact contract

Compile each Bernoulli table entry to `1[lambda > 0]` (zero ties choose 0). Store one root/symbol mode per row, with lowest-index ties. For dynamic legal length/control heads, store the entire logit-sorted preference order and choose the first legal value at runtime; a precompiled unmasked argmax is incorrect when its preferred span is unavailable. Null compiles to two roots. No serving logits, softmax, sampling or learned dense products remain. Flat symbol argmax is the conditional mode; it avoids the old greedy binary-tree versus global-leaf-mode mismatch. Stochastic-to-deterministic loss gap remains a separate real risk.

The artifact binds schema, all compiled gates/wiring/head orders/modes, feature layout, geometry/token/zeta identities, seed, exact training data/configuration and parameter digest, operator/codec versions and parent lineage. Snapshot binds that artifact. Validate all fields and unknown fields, nonfinite training parameters, out-of-domain roots, duplicate/missing preference entries and every size bound before constructing a runtime session. Use focused Result errors; no recoverable panic.

Before fitting, compare compiled deterministic execution with an independent deterministic interpretation of the same frozen training parameters, row-by-row and across multi-step traces. Compare selected objects, actions, intermediate roots, offer IDs, cursor/publication and outputs. This checks lowering parity, not parity between sampled and deterministic behavior. Snapshot/resume must match uninterrupted deterministic execution. A stochastic loss improvement with failed deterministic generation remains a negative result; no random serving, ensemble or dense fallback follows automatically.

## 7. Decisive implementation gate before a language run

The immediate next increment implements only the reusable finite circuit/parameter sampler and exact addressed lease/offer primitives plus a tiny causal composition test. No corpus fit, replacement-model claim or automatic parameter campaign is needed to test these contracts.

Use the existing exact H4 identity I and central order-two root -I with two exact objects A/B, whose matching keys strictly win signed attention. First action uses Bernoulli row p to preserve/toggle I. After hard attention selects A, the second action uses p again; after B, it uses a different row q. Apply the second preserve/toggle and score `L=1(final source != B)`. Enumerating the four hard paths gives

`J(p,q)=1-2p+p^2+p*q`.

At `p=0.2,q=0.7`, exact logit gradients are `dJ/dlambda_p=-0.144`, `dJ/dlambda_q=+0.042`. The four paths exercise changed subsequent addresses and repeated sharing. The second gradient must not disappear because an old source pointer was held fixed. Compare analytic derivatives, probability-weighted event-score sums and centered finite differences; enumerate the 16 ordered two-particle pairs for the leave-one-out estimator. For the direct-CE/late-offer fixture, use a two-symbol emission with p=P(offer1), current observed target1, and a subsequent fixed loss .5 after matching acknowledgment or2 otherwise. Mean two-position cost J=[-ln(p)+(1-p)*2+p*.5]/2 has direct logit gradient(p-1)/2 and future-score gradient p(1-p)*(.5-2)/2. At p=.2 these are -.4 and -.12, totaling -.52. Verify the two terms separately so omission of late-offer credit cannot pass. These numbers are analytic fixtures, **not executed results**.

Required focused checks also cover LUT gate/address ordering and reused rows; every live input's structural reach; causal no-target access; duplicate payload/different occurrence; overwrite after lease acquisition; invalid extent and legal preference fallback; negative/overflow scalars and ordered Sub; partial/mismatched publication; repeated predict and wrong offer IDs; independent turns; reset and snapshot.
