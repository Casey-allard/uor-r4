## Corrections to advisory conclusions

The mathematics review proposes a one-batch gradient-sign/moment calculation as
a decisive predictor of the next 512 updates. It is not decisive: gradients,
moment estimates, global clipping and recurrent states change across batches.
Its constant-direction travel approximation is not a capacity bound. Likewise,
an outward gradient at one boundary does not prove that fixed ranges are the
limiting factor. These calculations do not replace the authorized comparison.

Projection restores the *parameter* surrogate mask at subsequent forward
boundaries; it does not guarantee a nonzero useful loss gradient or repair the
separate interface quantizers. An endpoint derivative is verified against this
implementation. Broad statements about bitwise training-forward identity are
not used in place of actual hard-forward equality checks.

The review's assertion that projection has no plausible causal path to a
generation cycle is unsupported. Changed weights can alter every subsequent
autoregressive token. Generation retention remains one of the original gates.
A pass would not isolate gradient unmasking from the full projected optimization
policy, and a failure would not reject all geometric mechanisms.

Arm-specific parent and scale hashes must differ when their contents differ;
the paired comparison requires the same policy and projected exposure, not
identical complete transition objects. No first-step positive clipping count or
new statistical quality threshold is imposed.

The reviews are advisory evidence, not authority to change the five gates,
exposure, source population, serving contract or preserved negative results.

## Primary literature checked during the fixed run

These checks inform interpretation and a later decision; they do not change the
running treatment or its gates.

- [Scalable MatMul-free Language Modeling, v7](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02528v7)
  combines ternary dense layers with elementwise recurrence. Its fixed-point
  hardware section retains wider activation precision than its matrix weights.
  This supports examining recurrence precision separately from weight bits.
  Dense parameter access, elementwise products and non-M1 hardware remain
  material differences from our terminal contract; its energy results do not
  transfer to this Mac.
- [Quamba2, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.22879v2) separately quantizes cached
  recurrent states and uses channel/state grouping with floating scales and GPU
  kernels. It supports measuring the state path and distinguishing precision
  from distribution range. It is not a ready multiplier-free serving design.
- [Ternary Mamba, June 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18114v1)
  reports scale-related instability during grouped QAT and uses an offline
  distillation objective. However, it leaves dynamics, embeddings, output head
  and other components in FP16. Its examples of failed output amplification
  do not prove that every post-hoc recurrent correction must fail. Treat these
  as scoped observations, not proof that learnable scales always collapse,
  distillation is necessary here, or this project's serving goal is solved.

Our deduction is to keep parameter projection, scale choice, recurrent-interface
precision and the learning objective as distinct hypotheses. Loaded row-aligned
likelihood, source edits, generations and state diagnostics must determine which
one deserves the next comparison. Do not combine all four into an uninterpretable
replacement recipe, and do not introduce another treatment during this run.

## Completed-result reviews and principal decision

The independent native arithmetic reviewer verified 31 sealed roots, hashed all
files in 34 input-binding records, and recomputed 54 partition means across
18 evaluation populations. They matched the reports exactly; the 12 new packed
Read/NoRead means also matched the Rust comparator. The reviewer checked actual
target identity, all source-edit losses/gains against each prior control, final
floating/packed lineage and the state diagnostic's input/aggregate/position
arithmetic. No material discrepancy was found. No additional model, build or
test process was launched by this review.

The RDC architecture reviewer uses the configured **`moonshot-ai/kimi-k3`**
route; neither the profile name nor its self-description verifies a Max tier.
Its complete log and completion receipt are bound in the result evidence.
The reviewer independently supports **FAIL/PASS/PASS/PASS/PASS** for both arms
and the principal's rejection. It inspected the actual ten packed generations,
including the ordinary nine-token EOS response that replaces the former cycle.
This passes the frozen noncollapse gate without qualifying prose quality.

The architecture review supports the finite **2 × 2 parameter/interface
precision comparison** as the next milestone, with fixed parents and no training.
The principal adopts that recommendation and its endpoint-reproduction condition.
An earlier broader per-family ablation suggestion is not added to the scope.
The present hard-versus-shadow comparison disables both quantizer families and
cannot separate their forward effects. The next experiment must report their
interaction rather than treating separate marginal costs as additive causes.

Several stronger advisory interpretations are **not adopted**:

- Quantizer-equivalent points are fibers/equivalence classes of a nonlinear
  quantizer, not a linear null space. Entry equality does not make later updates
  invariant. Projection introduces no serving-time projection operation; total
  serving cost and energy are not zero or newly measured.
- Repeated post-update projections establish that updates leave the box. They
  do not isolate momentum from current gradients, decay or global clipping.
  This complete 512-update treatment fails; it does not prove range control or
  optimizer dynamics can never limit retention at another start or recipe.
- Flat quarter averages and smaller state RMS do not identify a dominant
  recurrent error term or rule out recurrent sensitivity. The state and NLL
  populations differ. Neither transport family is exonerated or rejected.
- Smoke-observed extrema are not grid limits: the implemented unit grid is
  signed16 at exponent −14 (approximately ±2), and the affine grid is signed16
  at exponent −8 (approximately ±128). Unit-vector/tanh ranges and observed
  logits are separate facts. An all-interface FQ effect would not by itself
  locate a geometric transport defect; it also changes ordinary read/output
  interfaces in both arms.
- The identity `hard-continuous = (hard-shadow) + (shadow-continuous)` is useful
  accounting. Holding one summand fixed while changing the learned model is an
  untested counterfactual. It supplies no bound on retraining, does not require
  both terms to shrink, and does not make additional optimization impossible.
- The floating shadows were trained for the quantized forward. Their separate
  score is not a direct measurement of optimizer error or a qualified floating
  replacement. The diagnosis uses exposed development and will influence later
  design; it has zero optimizer steps, not zero selection pressure.
- Quaternion's recovered `cap.` is a gain against the unprojected/continuous
  controls, not against rung1. Its rung1 first-noun gains are zero. The retained
  row tables govern this distinction.

The first arithmetic script's raw-parent equality check was corrected to validate
the packed binding's exact source-defined transformation and its independently
bound floating parent. Both reviews distinguish this analysis expectation error
from a model or artifact-pairing defect. No model was retrained to repair it.
The Rust comparator's original count/cache likelihood component also remains
distinct from the newer matched-continuous retention requirement.
