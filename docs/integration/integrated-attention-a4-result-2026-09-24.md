## What failed

Immediately after their own local update, all 1,336 eligible provenance examples
per arm choose the positive source. The final loaded models retain only 28/167
and 24/167 of the admitted fit sources. This is an inability to retain and
reconcile the training decisions, before generalization is even considered.
The selector also receives 22,080 later-answer utility and 3,208 natural utility
examples. Interference is plausible, but these counts do not isolate its cause.

**Scope correction:** unchanged dedicated context-token coefficients do not
mean unchanged fine codes. State training updates shared encoder rows, and
changing evidence/state changes their inputs. Relative to A3, query codes change
on 21/24 new first decisions in both arms; source codes change on 18/24 C120 and
21/24 2I decisions. Direct legacy fine/energy/gate fitting is disabled for A4,
but its representations are not frozen. The original source-bound reports are
preserved; their phrase “retaining the previous fine codes” is corrected here
and in the driver for future reports. Representation drift, objective
interference and trajectory changes are not separately identified by this run.

Actual output includes `: Yes,` followed by unrelated fragments, punctuation and
code tokens. Changed negative evidence can still produce `Yes`. Forced-source
likelihood is only a diagnostic; A4 has no new exhaustive vocabulary-MAP result,
so failure cannot be assigned to the greedy decoder alone. The development
positives also have unique candidate tokens; this panel cannot establish robust
entity binding or a geometric identity advantage.
