## Frozen output decision

Use the same five existing story prompts, 128-token cap, seeds2014–2018, and all32 existing source-edit variants. The existing exposed evaluator remains unchanged; no new holdout or additional prompt sweep. Record every parent output before training.

Judge each continuation on four binary criteria, with quoted evidence:

1. Entity identity and roles remain resolvable and uncontradicted.
2. Events form an understandable progression connected to the prompt.
3. Malformed, contradictory or repeated language does not prevent ordinary literal understanding.
4. The final text ends a complete clause at a stable or resolved narrative point.

Personification and explicitly introduced characters are allowed. Do not require a prescribed plot, repetition of every prompt fact, exact reference wording, or a particular EOS versus length-cap stop. An unfinished final clause fails completion. A fully acceptable continuation passes all four.
