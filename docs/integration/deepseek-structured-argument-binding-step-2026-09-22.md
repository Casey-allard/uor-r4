### 2. Learn argument spans instead of deriving them from marker position

Gold subject/object/role/action labels are allowed offline targets; they cannot become serving spans, source pointers, expected goals, next-hop counts, fixture IDs or candidate-correctness features.

A useful formulation is

`y_hat = argmax_{y in Y(x)} S_theta(x, y)`,

where `y` contains exact argument spans and typed goal/action choices. Candidate legality may enforce declared bounds and compatible spans. Do not encode a semantic grammar that selects the correct roles before learning. A bounded tuple search, independent span proposals with a learned compatibility term, or a segment model is adequate. Choose the simplest formulation that represents the task. It need not solve arbitrary nested or overlapping predicates in this run.

For a segment model, a max-plus recurrence such as

`D[t,r] = max_{s<t,r'} (D[s,r'] + A[r',r] + psi_theta(x,[s,t),r))`

uses additions, comparisons and lookups during inference. A small legal-role state can constrain duplicate assignments without fixing subject-before-object order. This is an example, not a mandatory CRF implementation. If a clause's relation cues are separated by an argument, a clause-level goal/action score or several contextual observations may be simpler than forcing one contiguous marker to cover them.
