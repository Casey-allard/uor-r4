## Why the free-cell claim does not follow

On source-correct fixture cells, the binding relation is fixed by the declared query/source role pair. Absorb it into `A[o]=T_bind[r(o)]*U[o]`, giving `P[o,v]=A[o]*B[v]`. Wrong-source rows may change the binding relation and are outside this two-factor statement. Three rectangle corners then constrain the fourth:

`P[o,v] = P[o,v0] * inverse(P[o0,v0]) * P[o0,v]`.

This holds in a noncommutative group in exactly that order. The fixture's observed operation/value graph is connected: values 0,3,6,7 occur with every operation. Therefore separate latent products on missing cells are not unconstrained independent parameters. If all observed latent products were known exactly, connected observations determine the factors up to a global gauge, which leaves all products unchanged.

The actual learner observes labels through a learned, potentially many-to-one decoder, not exact latent products. Label-level identifiability is therefore weaker; multiple fitting solutions may generalize differently. This distinction leaves optimization, decoder ambiguity, finite-state truncation and useful inductive structure open. The defective fit does not prove any of those is fundamental. A one-parameter action family can be a useful inductive bias, but setting `U[op]=h^op` using the fixture's hidden numeric operation index would inject the answer rule.

The old source relation collapse was already explicitly scoped to T[r] in the prior review. Separating observed-query operation from source compatibility implements the recommended response to that information need; there is no disagreement about two relation IDs being unable to carry eight distinct operation labels through T[r] alone.

## The next constructive mechanism

Use shared learned primitive actions on persistent computed state. An illustrative recurrent core is

`s0 = E[selected exact payload]`

`s[j+1] = A[observed primitive action j] * s[j]`

`output = D(s[j], phase)`.

E, action grounding A, decoder D and any continuation policy are learned from declared development observations. Every occurrence of the same primitive reuses the same action; no code is assigned to a whole compound request merely because it has its own fixture label. Offline transition/outcome supervision can encourage `E(next outcome)=A[action]*E(current outcome)` and lexical grounding, with explicitly declared supervision strength and an end-to-end comparison. This is a proposed learning mechanism, not a result or proof that a particular optimizer discovers it.

Keep **exact evidence identity**, **computed geometric value** and **response/control phase** distinct. Read obtains an owned reference or lease; compute transforms the retained result; Emit lexicalizes it; Stop ends the response; a later Read can use a result-derived query. Do not reparse the last generated tokens as a fresh query at every output step. The existing three-token loop does exactly that after the first emission, so it is not a learned response trajectory.

The current observed query-role token feeds both source selection and the operation map. A fixed-evidence operation change may therefore alter the read itself; the new control separates end-to-end behavior from computation on a fixed selected operand. Persistent source ownership and separate observed instruction/control are needed to remove that coupling. The current decoder also forces its token above the local argmax whenever a stored state emits; this is a component interface, not calibrated natural-text integration.

Reversible H4 action is suitable for transport and order-sensitive computation. Not every language transition is invertible: replacement, copy/commit, branch, emission and stopping are typed operations with their own contracts. A finite group is not required to implement the entire machine. This separation connects the new decoder to the older owned-object and Read/Emit/Stop work without importing authored byte grammar or incompatible artifacts.

Useful source donors are `addressed_attention/objects.rs`, `dependent_language/scheduling.rs`, `shared_operator_refinement.rs`, the existing exact read-conditioned action tables and the bounded nonlinear decoders catalogued in the [mechanism synthesis](geometric-attention-mechanism-synthesis-2026-09-20.md). Reuse a particular transition/lease/control interface, its arithmetic and its causal tests. Do not present their historical authored-language acceptance as qualification of the new BPE model.

## Research consulted and its limits

[Sequential Group Composition, v2, May 2026 / ICML 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.03655v2) constructs recurrent and hierarchical compositions that reuse associativity. Its recurrence results are existence constructions, not a guarantee that our discrete learner discovers them; its learning analysis assumes particular encodings, polynomial networks and training dynamics. Its distinction between group actions and more general state-machine transitions supports keeping reversible transport separate from noninvertible control.

[Understanding Robust Generalization in Learning Regular Languages, ICML 2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/dan22a.html) studies intermediate-state auxiliary supervision for compositional generalization. It supports testing explicit transition/outcome learning as a hypothesis; it does not validate our source selector or language competence. [Compositional Interfaces, 2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v274/luketina25a.html) motivates separating observation, shared controller and action interfaces; its modular agents are not adopted runtimes.

[Structural Identification, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26465) separates what a dataset licenses under declared identifications from what a learner realizes. It analyzes COGS structures with specified syntactic identifications and trains no predictive model; it supplies a scope lesson, not a served grammar. [SCAN](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/lake18a.html) remains a reminder to separate familiar-element recombination from easy distribution shifts. These references guide falsifiable design; none proves the GLM or a unique geometric advantage.
