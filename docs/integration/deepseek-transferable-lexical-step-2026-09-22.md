## Research and the larger roadmap

[CopyNet](https://aclanthology.org/P16-1154/) motivates learned vocabulary/copy continuation without requiring its dense implementation. [Recurrent language models as finite-state automata](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.502/) makes state and string-distribution expressivity precise, not sufficient for frontier language. [Scalable MatMul-free Language Modeling, revised July 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02528v7) supports researching low-bit recurrent language computation; its hardware results do not qualify our M1 costs.

The July 2026 [exact-memory complement to linear attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02303) is an optional lead for separating compressive state from exact associations, not a replacement for our source/version ownership. [Spectral representations for group composition, revised July 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02993) is a finite-group learning lead under stated projected-flow/population assumptions, not a convergence theorem for this learner. Consult further primary work when it resolves a concrete uncertainty.

Geometry remains preferred. Prime/UOR identity, signed Q8/H4 computation, retained Hopf fiber, paired-H4/icosian E8, S7 and finite harmonics have distinct roles. Do not attach every mechanism to every token or treat orthogonality as unlimited memory. The next productive connection is exact selected meaning to learned ordered lexical state. The roadmap remains this lexical milestone, then broader conversation/prose and executed Rust through the same candidate, then full artifact/API/WASM/Studio and laptop quality/cost/energy qualification. Executed Rust is conditional on useful lexical progress, not a reason to bolt on another authored response family now.
