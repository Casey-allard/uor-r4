## Proposed mechanism and the missing grounding adapter

Start from one loaded scoped runtime. Let learned observations select the request entity/relation, temporal intent and the operation-bearing span or spans. The same session should choose a supported copy/follow/apply behavior from observed request and retrieved content. Existing exact action legality and bounded execution can remain deterministic infrastructure.

The intended causal path is:

```text
observed request
  -> learned source/operation interpretation
  -> exact eligible source at one pinned view
  -> owned operand
  -> shared geometric action(s)
  -> owned grounded result
  -> result-conditioned exact query at the same view
  -> owned answer / further learned continuation / complete emission
```

The main interface mismatch must be solved explicitly. The retained operator has finite single-token payload, primitive and outcome domains. Scoped memory has exact lexical byte keys and possibly multiword payloads. Add a declared artifact-bound grounding interface between them. It may be learned from development examples or reuse already learned correspondences if their domains genuinely match. It must not derive meaning from a token ID modulo the group order, fixture index, hidden semantic label, host keyword dictionary, or tokenization accident.

It is acceptable for the operation vocabulary and eight-state alphabet to be familiar in this milestone. The target is composition and useful consumption, not zero-shot discovery of an operation never explained in training. Different BPE segmentations of the same supported lexical label must not silently become different operands. Unknown values, unknown operations, absent sources, ungrounded results and computed identity remain distinct outcomes.

One possible owned result holds the source record/key/version and scope, computation artifact identity, operation-span ownership, current geometric state, progress and grounded lexical result. Keep it distinct from a stored user assertion. A computed value may remain ephemeral inside the answer frame; do not silently write derived assertions back into durable memory unless the observed requested action requires that write. Save/reload must retain enough information to continue, without querying a hidden model or regenerating the earlier operation from a gold fixture.

Reuse `initial_state`, `apply` and `outcome_for_state` in the actual session step boundary rather than wrapping a detached `serve()` call with a predetermined host schedule. Generation, interventions, timing and resumed execution must call that same boundary. If explicit span exhaustion decides that a supplied sequence has ended, name that exact protocol; do not call it learned halting. A learned applicability/continuation choice can still decide whether computation is needed, what result to consume, and whether to follow or emit. ## Mathematical test that makes the geometry relevant

The existing signed quaternion subgroup already supplies an order-sensitive witness:

\[
ij=k,\qquad ji=-k,\qquad Q_8/\{\pm1\}\cong V_4.
\]

The quotient loses the central sign and is commutative. A representation or observation that identifies `q` and `-q` cannot distinguish these particular ordered results. Standard conjugation-based orientation observation also identifies the pair; retaining the sign/fiber information is therefore useful when the grounded outputs differ. Keep the multiplication convention explicit: with left action `s_next = A[p] * s`, the observed sequence `i` then `j` gives `j * i * s`, not the reversed convention.

This is a concrete role for signed H4/Q8 and retained orientation, without a physical quantum claim. The operator can be associative while noncommutative. Derive expected states and grounded answers independently, including the identity and inverse cases.

A correct finite shared-transition table can also implement this action. Compare against that competent control, not only a whole-program dictionary that cannot reuse familiar transitions. A tie demonstrates geometric competence and reuse; any compactness or efficiency claim requires comparable encodings and complete costs. Count common product/inverse tables, per-artifact codes, grounding maps and per-session state separately. No E8/S7 expansion, harmonic bank, new group family or geometry-wide refit is needed unless a measured missing distinction makes it useful.

World facts and corrections need not be invertible. Do not force noninjective relations, overwrites, NoWrite, history eligibility or emission into a group permutation. Exact records and typed control supply those operations. Harmonic/scalar fields remain candidate compatibility or compression representations; orthogonality alone does not learn role routing or create unlimited exact memory.

The owner calls the guiding intuition “topological folding,” originally expressed as `exp(i*pi)+pi^0=0^0`. The principal review reconciles it: the equation is not a new arithmetic identity or quantum mechanism. Preserve the useful projection/lift hypothesis. A phase readout `|(1+exp(i*theta))/2|^2` gives0/1 atpi/0; for the finite classical implementation, a sign bit and two-entry lookup suffice. Test whether a projection identifying opposite signed states loses an answer-relevant distinction and whether retaining it repairs the consumed result. Relative quantum phase and classical signed state are different claims; do not introduce floating/transcendental serving or change the definition of `0^0`.

## Relevant research, without importing unsupported conclusions

[Sequential Group Composition, ICML 2026, May 29 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03655v2) studies cumulative finite-group products and shows how architectures exploiting associativity avoid the scaling of its shallow comparison family. Its recurrent construction motivates reusing one action across steps instead of memorizing whole sequences. Its bounds have stated model/encoding assumptions and are not a proof that our language system generalizes or is more efficient than transformers.

[From Reasoning Traces to Reusable Modules, July 5 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18089v2) distinguishes reusable local operations from routing in a hierarchical latent selection account of composition. Its compound-trace results motivate fitting and testing the integrated operation-and-consumption path. They do not mandate GRPO, dense serving or a particular optimizer here.

[LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813) and [EverMemBench, March 2026 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01313v3) support keeping updates, temporal eligibility, attribution and abstention visible during integration. Their benchmark results do not qualify our small authored corpus. [SpaceE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.10245) provides relevant discussion of noninjective relations; its matrix solution is not adopted. The first-principles distinction between reversible transport and exact noninvertible memory/control is sufficient for this design.

Consult additional primary sources or project research when they answer a concrete uncertainty. New papers and elegant analogies are hypotheses or scoped evidence, not authority to silently replace the architecture. If a better mechanism resolves the observed interface with less state or clearer learning, propose and test it on the same complete behavior.
