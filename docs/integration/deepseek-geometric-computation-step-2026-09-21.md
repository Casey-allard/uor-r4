## Principal architectural direction

The objective is a transformerless local language model with learned geometric routing, exact evidence and shared typed computation, useful conversation/memory and Rust reasoning/coding, eventually at competitive quality and lower energy. Offline Rust training may use floating point and matrix products. D0-b serving permits bounded <=4-bit additive/shift/lookup linear maps with no numerical-kernel multiplier or floating computation. Geometric routing remains preferred; no hidden transformer/provider or dense backbone disguised as lookup.

Separate four jobs while learning their interfaces together:

- **Identity and retention:** exact occurrence/version/payload ownership, causal scope and eviction.
- **Addressing and relation:** query-dependent candidate access, signed finite descriptors, directed relations and useful NoRead.
- **Computation:** shared content-conditioned state/operator updates, then dependent reads and learned stopping.
- **Lexicalization:** learned conversion of a computed result to output tokens; copy and arbitrary finite lexical association are legitimate distinct operators.

One 120-state descriptor is not a lossless prefix or token vocabulary. A lexical table can be a useful part of a geometric model. Geometry should earn its contribution through structural sharing, ordered transformations, contextual generalization or measured cost, not by beating an exact learned dictionary on eight arbitrary labels. Do not solve arbitrary output labels by hand or use supervised records in inference.
