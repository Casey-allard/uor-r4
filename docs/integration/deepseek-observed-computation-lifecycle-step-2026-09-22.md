## Mathematical bridges and primary research

Retain Q8/H4 signed state when a task needs order-sensitive outputs: `ij=k`, `ji=-k`. Under left actions the observed sequence i then j produces `j*i*s`; use one declared convention in the oracle and runtime. The sign quotient is an information-loss test. It does not create a need for S7 or the full icosian representation. Exact noninvertible world updates, correction and emission stay typed operations rather than being forced into reversible group transport.

The owner's topological-folding intuition is a retained-phase/projection hypothesis, not a changed Euler identity. The current Q8 result supplies a concrete finite instance. Hopf observation/fiber retention, paired-H4/E8/S7 and harmonic/scalar fields remain tools for a witnessed missing distinction, representation sharing or measured cost problem. Eight coordinates are not eight unlimited orthogonal semantic memories; a harmonic coefficient bank and an exact occurrence store have different capacity and update semantics.

Primary sources checked for this review:

- [Sequential Group Composition, May 29 2026 revision](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.03655v2): shared sequential composition exploits associativity. The recurrent/deep results are constructive existence results; the paper explicitly separates them from an analysis of optimization convergence. This is support for retaining the solved finite action, not proof of language or transformer impossibility.
- [From Reasoning Traces to Reusable Modules, July 5 2026 revision](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18089v2): distinguishes reusable skills from routing and requires locally witnessed interfaces for its composition result. This motivates the mixed routing/operation traces above. Its theoretical model and SFT/RL experiments do not require this project to adopt RL or dense inference.
- [Neural Programmer-Interpreters](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06279): useful precedent for separating learned control, program memory and domain interfaces and for rich execution supervision. We borrow that responsibility split, not its neural architecture or measured results.
- [Pointer Sentinel Mixture Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07843): motivates separating reproduction of context from vocabulary emission in the following language milestone; its softmax/recurrent implementation is not adopted.

Consult further primary research only where it resolves a concrete uncertainty or offers a discriminating alternative. The current missing observed/control interfaces are visible in the source; an unrelated elegant paper does not override them.
