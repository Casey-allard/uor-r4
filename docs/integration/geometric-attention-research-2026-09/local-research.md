## NEMESIS: useful architectural questions with specific limitations

The live repository head is `0d106967843c2c96477cf3e57aeff213e7db1c97`, unchanged from the cached review. The complete file inventory contains 205 entries, including 165 PDFs and 36 DOCX files. That is a discovery inventory, not 205 documents read. The earlier carrying-substrate, canonical-geometry, Cayley–Dickson and blueprint reports were reused and consulted to their recorded depth. Four additional PDFs were fetched by immutable Git blob, hashed, and extracted; the local `nemesis-selected/inventory.json` records their exact provenance. No NEMESIS original is vendored into the public project by this research delivery.

**Structure-carrying substrate report.** Its useful engineering obligations are a reachable-state encoding/decoding correspondence, faithful transition representation and executable primitive interpretation. These are good questions for every proposed chart bridge. Its general compression/query-cost assertions do not provide a trained language model, a compiler for arbitrary state, or end-to-end laptop measurements. The report remains a proposal with conditions to establish.

**Positional Metric Analysis, seven pages.** This is the closest newly located Hamming-specific lead. It proposes optimizing a six-bit codon codebook against a domain cost, then measuring ordered trajectory costs. That is a constructive pattern: learn the geometry against the relationship that matters, rather than expecting arbitrary codes to be meaningful. Its reported simulated annealing and biological correlations were not reproduced. The source's own first-page table has elementary inconsistencies: `110000` has Hamming distance 2 from `000000`, not the printed 4; `110001` has distance 3, not 4; `101011` has distance 4, not 3. The page was visually inspected at full resolution, so these are not text-extraction mistakes. The prose gives only a partial codebook and no executable run receipt in this PDF. Consequently its numerical success claims cannot qualify our codebook or learner. Source: [Positional Metric report](https://github.com/markrnd87-cmd/NEMESIS-Theory/blob/0d106967843c2c96477cf3e57aeff213e7db1c97/Comprehensive%20Expert-Level%20Report_%20Positional%20Metr....pdf).

**Inverse Locus Resolution, three pages.** Separating immutable identity from a searched context is useful. The proposed certificate-first algorithm, however, must keep a target as a goal until independently verified. Its primality example changes the predicate to being a unit in a modular ring. This is not equivalent to integer primality: composite 9 is a unit modulo 256 because `9*57 = 513 = 1 mod 256`. Changing a coordinate base cannot change integer primality. No instruction to assert success or rewrite history from this source was executed. For attention, borrow typed context search and immutable identity; do not borrow goal-conditioned truth or output selection. [ILR source](https://github.com/markrnd87-cmd/NEMESIS-Theory/blob/0d106967843c2c96477cf3e57aeff213e7db1c97/UOR%20Core%20Extension_%20Inverse%20Locus%20Resolution%20%28ILR%29%20package._.pdf).

**PRISM shift blueprint, two pages.** A cyclic bit-index permutation does define a rotation, and dropping a declared limb can implement a particular width-aligned projection. Neither fact implements every logical right shift. For example, a one-bit right shift crosses limb boundaries; selecting one 16-bit component of a 32-bit word cannot reproduce it. The proposal to multiply by unspecified orthogonal basis elements requires an actual domain and operation-preservation proof. Keep shift, width reduction and rotation as distinct typed operations. [Blueprint](https://github.com/markrnd87-cmd/NEMESIS-Theory/blob/0d106967843c2c96477cf3e57aeff213e7db1c97/PRISM%20Blueprint_%20Algebraic%20Resolution%20of%20the%20shr%20Bottleneck_.pdf).

**Ifa/Sigmatics comparative report, selected sections.** The 256-state combinatorial representation and contextual interpretation discussion are conceptual donors. Equal cardinalities of symbolic systems do not establish an operation-preserving map between them, Clifford modules, E8 or a semantic attention rule. Cultural/historical analogies are recorded as analogies; the report's proposed governance instructions do not govern this task. The I-Ching/Hamming suggestion remains a representational research lead, without a demonstrated historical or modern language-learning equivalence in the inspected source. [Comparative report](https://github.com/markrnd87-cmd/NEMESIS-Theory/blob/0d106967843c2c96477cf3e57aeff213e7db1c97/Ifa%2C%20Sigmatics%2C%20and%20UOR%20Foundation.pdf).

The mathematical companion additionally audits the canonical-geometry report's Fano group naming. These specific defects do not reject the entire repository. They make the extraction rule concrete: retain a defined object, map or experiment idea; verify its arithmetic and source; supply the missing learning and operation contract before using its claims.

## Hyperdimensional computing: an additional constructive donor

Kanerva's original tutorial supplies item memory, noisy-code cleanup, ordered binding and the ambiguity of pointer chains with shared subsequences. Its sequence example shows why earlier history must distinguish two paths that converge on the same symbol. Its random-indexing example derives contextual similarity from observed language use; it does not assign meaning to random addresses by definition. For UOR, the transferable lesson is learned/context-built codes plus explicit order and exact memory. Binary superposition is finite-capacity and can lose bindings; it is not a substitute for retained occurrence records. The paper includes mathematical matrix and permutation formulations, so each proposed operator still needs the project's actual serving audit. Read scope: §§6.1, 6.4.2–6.8, 7.1 and relevant introductory limitations. [Kanerva 2009](https://redwood.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/kanerva2009hyperdimensional.pdf).

## Consequence for the research decision

These sources strengthen a relational, causally recurrent architecture with exact identity, retained orientation and a learned selector/update. They do not justify a fixed semantic metric, an untrained expert bank, a blanket RH foundation or a new broad qualification campaign. The synthesis must make the first actual learning experiment small and complete, while preserving a path to natural prose and later replacement acceptance.
