## Preserve geometry without invalid retirement arguments

The [two-seed short controls](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/issues/820#issuecomment-5964524685)
report quaternion overU(1) by.0234nats and flatL2 overLorentz by.0345nats. These are
scoped text-loss findings, not whole-architecture or energy qualification.
Softmax removes a common additive offset, not general scaling or monotone
transformations. Equal rankings can change mixtures, gradients and NoRead mass.
[Hyperbolic Attention Networks](https://arxiv.org/html/1805.09786v1) explicitly
uses geometric matching separately from aggregation. Keep the empirical L2 win
without a false Lorentz-impossibility claim.

A288-coordinate state can hold one equally precise288-coordinate value by
identity. A capacity bound must specify payload/key entropy, simultaneous
bindings, precision, errors and external storage. Curriculum or optimization
failure is not that bound. Complete-state collisions with incompatible required
future answers would be stronger evidence. Exact prime/hash identity is not a
semantic metric; geometric roots need learned conditional meaning.

The [event×age crossover](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/issues/1512#issuecomment-5965031926)
scores244/83/244/83 for parent/parent, final-event/parent-age, parent-event/final-age
and final/final on256 seed2 rows. This isolates endpoint event damage, not a
general gradient defect. Retain the finite-H4/q4 direction audit as local diagnosis,
without a repeated dose or automatic estimator replacement. Tangent finite-choice
credit can have an antipodal blind spot; ambient credit also has no general
descent guarantee. [STE analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/1903.05662v4) and
[Cold Analysis](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/shekhovtsov23a.html) have specific
assumptions, not a guarantee for our deterministic recurrent selector.

Retain exact versioned memory, ordered quaternion/group transport, geometric
value/radius/presence and accepted parents. Old finite2I replacements jointly
changed snap/magnitude/scoring; delta wrapper defects and short decay negatives
do not retire their whole families. Use the existing
[roadmap reconciliation](roadmap-reconciliation-2026-10-01.md) for re-entry rather
than restarting A1–A4/local selector/narrowed-context/unchanged fit campaigns.

## External mechanisms and runtime limits

[MatMul-free language modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02528) supports alternate
recurrent language research, not our complete no-float/no-multiply geometric
runtime or frontier-M1 claim. [Delta fast weights](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/schlag21a.html)
provide correction-to-existing-binding ideas; their floating outer products are
not an admitted native implementation. Exact addressed memory remains useful.

Preserve offline donor compilation through token/actions, contextual transitions,
read/write/value targets and output distributions. Static embeddings alone do
not specify contextual reasoning. [LoLCATs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10254)
separates operator transfer from downstream adaptation; its retained dense
floating computation is not our runtime template. Arbitrary lossless cheap
conversion into a finite alphabet is not promised.
