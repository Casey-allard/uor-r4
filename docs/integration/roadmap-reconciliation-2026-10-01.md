## State labels and ownership

- **Active:** an authorized, concrete task is being worked. Preserve its immutable inputs and ongoing execution; update only prospectively.
- **Ready:** the next integrated deliverable has adequate causal justification and its prerequisites are available. A complete resource projection and work card still precede model compute.
- **Dependency:** an unmet capability or implementation obligation remains open, but is not an instruction to start it now.
- **Parked:** retained research or operations scope has an explicit reentry condition. It is not a blocker merely because it is unfinished.
- **Historical:** an attempt, scoped result, retired directive board, or transferred tracking unit. Its evidence and limitations remain immutable.
