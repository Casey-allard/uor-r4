## First-principles architectural direction

The current data do not train a semantic dependency that asks the learner to choose different uncopied words for distinct copied payloads under the same typed facts. Widening a representation or adding epochs cannot establish that absent dependency. The next experiment needs a real text objective and one controlled grounded contrast in which the correct post-copy decision depends on source content while request, copy length and typed facts are held fixed. Exact addresses/version provenance remain external ownership. A learned state should receive enough actual token information to choose vocabulary and Stop; an owned span should be copied exactly and its emitted tokens fed back. Compare against a competent same-information low-bit recurrent or finite baseline before attributing a quality gain to geometric transport.

The architectural mechanism choice is open. Signed ordered H4 transport and the shared contextual residual are promising candidates when role/order or context collision becomes a measured limit. A finite harmonic bank or S7/Hopf/E8 representation needs a witnessed interference/capacity/cost advantage; orthogonality alone does not preserve an unbounded number of live language relations. D0-b allows low-bit additive maps at serving, so the current ternary RNN is an admissible baseline while dense parameter traffic remains a cost to measure. No quantum or geometric performance claim follows from this panel.

Primary research checked for this direction: [CopyNet](https://aclanthology.org/P16-1154/) motivates joint vocabulary and exact copying; [recurrent language models as finite-state automata](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.502/) sharpens state/string-distribution questions; [Scalable MatMul-free Language Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02528v7) supports low-bit recurrent feasibility without transferring its energy figures; and [A Hippocampus for Linear Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02303) studies exact associations beside compressed state, without validating UOR-R4.
