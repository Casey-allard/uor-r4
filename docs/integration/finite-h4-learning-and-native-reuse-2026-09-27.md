## What the learner differentiates—and what it can represent

The actual graph (`geometric_read.rs:396`) normalizes Q/K, selects signed roots, uses `u + stop(hard-u)`, composes through a smooth Hamilton product and anchors its value to the discrete group relation before scoring. Cached key codes remain detached while live continuous keys retain the surrogate gradient path. Backward therefore combines the normalization Jacobian, Hamilton derivatives at hard operands and MLP derivatives at the hard relation; it is neither the derivative of nearest-root selection nor that of a fully continuous quaternion reader.

For `s = sqrt(||x||² + epsilon²)`, the non-clamped normalizer (`geometric_read.rs:565`) has Jacobian `I/s − xxᵀ/s³`. Thus identical root codes do not imply identical raw Q/K gradients. Below epsilon, its constant identity branch contributes zero read-score gradient for that observation; other paths can still train shared parameters. Nonzero gradients establish connectivity of the declared estimator, not useful direction or descent. Hard-quantizer finite differences should not be expected to equal its intentionally different backward rule.

The scorer (`geometric_read.rs:474`) sums sixteen independent 49-parameter `4→8→1` tanh functions over 120 relation roots. Each is narrower than an arbitrary 120-entry learned function. In the **ideal/declared hard-forward mapping**, equal relation-code tuples and age give equal compatibility, erasing radius and within-cell distinctions; additive lane scoring lacks arbitrary cross-lane interactions at this stage. STE cancellation and relation anchoring can retain F32 roundoff, so this is not a bitwise equality claim about executed tensors. That numerical discrepancy is not evidence of restored intended capacity. Upstream Q/K and recurrent/value state remain richer, so these local limits do not make the whole model a 120-state machine or establish an impossibility result.

Three primary sources inform interpretation:

- [Yin et al., *Understanding Straight-Through Estimator in Training Activation Quantized Neural Nets*, v4, sections 2 and 3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1903.05662v4) distinguish coarse gradients from true gradients and exhibit identity-STE instability under particular binary-activation/Gaussian assumptions. The transferable warning is that connectivity does not establish good optimization. Their theorem and clipped-ReLU recommendation do not establish failure or prescribe a repair for this recurrent fixed-S3 reader.
- [Fifty et al., *Restructuring Vector Quantization with the Rotation Trick*, v1, Algorithm 1 and sections 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.06424v1) replace identity backward transport with a detached rotation/rescaling. It remains a surrogate. Image/video results motivate a candidate, not language evidence; Table 1 includes lower quantization error alongside worse reconstruction loss. Its within-cell-gradient explanation applies at the quantizer input, not literally to our full normalized graph. Learned-codebook norm collapse also does not transfer to fixed H4 roots, although assignments can concentrate.
- The [author implementation at `99104924`, lines 104–145](https://github.com/cfifty/rotation_trick/blob/99104924a2a61d393be30567ee8a47b350050622/src/models/vq_vae.py#L104) selects rotation in a model wrapper using detached directions, reflection terms and a norm ratio. Epsilon handling prevents assuming exact F32 forward identity from algebra alone. This is an algorithm donor, not a Python/PyTorch or serving dependency.

## Reuse the exact group; make the numerical bridge explicit

The existing [`ExactGroupTable::build()`](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/d15360527f7c69ac8b83eef0bbd5839b87c26f02/crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/learner/prefix_artifact.rs#L204) computes all 14,400 products in exact `Z[phi]`, checks the independent sorted canonical closure, and emits historical-order product/inverse bytes and the order bijection. It also invokes the old floating table as an **offline** comparison; require its reported mismatch count to be zero when claiming compatibility. Neither floating/lazy table construction nor the generic closure API belongs in the integer hot path.

Reuse the existing stride-128 layout: 15,360 product bytes, 120 inverse bytes, 120 mapping bytes and one identity byte—15,601 before metadata. The classifier coefficients add 960 bytes. These are payload counts, not measured RSS or speed. Classification and lookup must preserve signed historical IDs, identity **1**, and `inverse(query) * key`, addressed as `product[(inverse[q] << 7) + k]`. Validate codes below120; padded columns are not group elements. Sorted canonical IDs cannot be substituted directly.

Bind canonical root/product identities, historical mapping and coefficient identity, raw payload hashes, order/operand convention, scale and source in the reader artifact. [`PrefixHard::from_bytes()`](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/blob/d15360527f7c69ac8b83eef0bbd5839b87c26f02/crates/uor-r4-core/src/native_geometric/learner/prefix_artifact.rs#L544) checks row permutations, identity, inverse entries and mapping bijectivity; that alone does not identify the loaded law as canonical H4 or validate canonical padding. Reuse its field meanings with a narrow trusted-identity loader, rather than importing the old prefix model or repeating an algebra proof at serving. The locked UOR-ADDR donor `165b51e3e2113ee5d032730cde709335d4fe9b60` already supplies content identity; it supplies neither geometry nor semantic distance. The audited SpiralCore 64-state and W33 incidence operators have different domains and do not replace this table.

Three interfaces remain: quantized Q/K classification versus F32 epsilon/ties; portable exact table loading; and legal low-bit score compilation. The classifier special-cases exact integer zero, whereas the reader also maps sufficiently small nonzero float lanes to identity. Exact algebra does not establish their transfer. Compiling sixteen lane functions into 120 scores each can remove the online MLP only after a lawful low-bit learned-operator and activation/output-scale contract is defined; arbitrary F32 score tables do not establish D0-b compliance. Age, NoRead, full causal admission, values and copy/emission semantics must remain explicit. This partial attention work does not remove dense vocabulary access or establish D5.

## Outcome-driven choice

A useful retained reader result supports the existing native bridge: offline exact-table export, typed integer lookup, actual fitted score conversion and complete generated-output observation. It does not require another group implementation.

A negative result is scoped to the finite representation, score family, identity surrogate, initialization, ordinary control and exposure together. Only if the actual outcome implicates poor encoder-assignment learning in failed replies is a **backward-only rotation transport** at Q/K quantization a causally narrow candidate. Preserve the same roots, hard relation, MLP, parameter count, data and objective; preserve the zero branch and explicitly anchor hard values. Executed F32 preservation needs observation against a declared numerical tolerance, not an algebraic assertion. If erased distinctions or additive score capacity explain the failure, that estimator change cannot restore the information.

No new fit, quantization recipe, auxiliary loss, decoder variant or architecture adoption is selected here. Exact group reuse, mathematical classification, gradient connectivity, useful learned behavior and native numerical retention remain distinct claims.
