## Body

The two previous attribute arms changed two things at once. This one holds mass and answer length inside the compliant envelope: 1,100 documents, 9,360 response tokens - 69.89 percent below the store that produced ten-of-ten compliance - with turn-1 answers at a mean of 3.77 tokens and the dependency structure intact at transformation fraction 1.000000. Form compliance is largely restored: green. correct, yes/no with correct facts, numeric forms. Retention still fails, and the retention turns return Noted. - this store own acknowledgement token - while every numeric item returns 3., the count family answer. The model learned the mixture surface families without binding a follow-up to its attribute. That settles the confound: cross-turn attribute extraction is a capability gap at this scale, not a masking artifact. Form compliance survives four configurations, two shapes, two terminal weights and a 70 percent smaller store. References #820
