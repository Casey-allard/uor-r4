## Body

Three points, only pointer_gate_supervision changed. At 0.0 the ungated pointer arm shows value-driven copying on out-of-family values, yes/no with correct facts, numerics in form and a verbatim premise span. At 0.1 compliance is much worse (Fect is, Collaboration make, Youmagular for, one I do, only two numerals in form). At 1.0 compliance collapsed: I do on six of fifteen items and The sky returning for the yes/no item that had been complying. A third weight (0.3) was still training and is excluded. The gate responds roughly linearly to supervision and at both weights the response distribution degrades monotonically, so the term competes with the response loss rather than complementing it: the gate is a correct diagnosis of where the failure sits and a wrong remedy, because the mixture gate is low precisely because the response loss prefers it low. The ungated pointer arm is therefore the best configuration on both axes and the base for further work. That is the fifth route exhausted for binding. Next: read-binding supervision (present, never run on this path), a token-identity pointer key, or a constrained decoder. References #820
