## Scope kept explicit (README)

- The flat-Dot equal-start control was not run at context 256, and Dot seed 2 is read-dependent.
- The Euclidean control ran only in the 600-step pilot, with one seed and a sharp start.
- Integer parity: NLL agrees within 0.0001 nats, but the largest single deviation exceeds the 0.01 limit over 131,072 positions, for both geometries.
- The Lorentz read's two learned signed16 scalars and derived Q32 scale sit beside the ≤4-bit weight maps. Their D10 status awaits the owner.
- The corpus split hashes absolute paths, so rebuilding is not bit-exact elsewhere; the hashes pin its identity. The scratch tokenizer is independent of `DialogueProtocol`.
