Consolidate the formal analysis into a versioned monograph/design document that can serve as the long-lived research and engineering reference.

## Proposed structure

1. Problem statement and non-goals.
2. Semantic state spaces and holographic projections.
3. Graph induction from teacher behavior.
4. Predictive entropy, semantic compression, and information bottleneck.
5. Semantic operating system architecture.
6. Future-state optimization and bounded planning.
7. Compiler stages and reference intermediate representation.
8. Boolean/integer lowering and R4G1 artifact format.
9. Runtime execution model and resource contract.
10. Structural proofs and proof-status matrix.
11. Empirical certification, statistical protocol, and negative results.
12. Rust module map, migration plan, and open research questions.

## Acceptance criteria

- The document links every normative definition to its implementation issue/module and every guarantee to its evidence artifact.
- Structural guarantees, empirical measurements, and research hypotheses are visually and terminologically distinct.
- It records known negative results and non-goals, including no exact-equivalence or human-level reasoning claim.
- It includes a dependency graph for the implementation issues and a migration path that preserves the legacy transformerless artifacts.
- A review checklist verifies consistency with the PDF, implementation plan, R4G1 RFC, threat model, and proof-status matrix.
