## What changed

- `m6_exact_arithmetic.rs`: a strict, original-byte-length-bounded parser for one unsigned decimal `+` or `-`, with a 160-byte limit, 10-digit operand bounds and explicit malformed, unsupported-shape, prompt-limit, overflow and subtraction-underflow errors. Original length is measured before trimming, so leading/trailing whitespace cannot bypass the bound. Full-input candidate/operator detection covers signed, leading-dot, operator-only plus/minus, nested/alternate-grouped and date-prefixed forms, `%`, `^`, `*`, `/`, `×`, `÷`, `&`, `|`, `<<`, `=`, `~`, Unicode minus, `≤`, `≥`, `≠`, `·`, numeric grouping, trailing/incomplete subtraction, and operators at or after byte 160. ISO date/time tails are trimmed before operator checks, so extra whitespace and comma-separated arithmetic cannot bypass the explicit error or length bound. The early leading-minus helper trims sentence punctuation and rejects `-`, `- 25`, `- 2.5` and `-?` with either leading or trailing padding; bare signed numbers, signed numeric statements including spaced, hyphenated and parenthesized telephone-like fields, numbered bullets, number-leading dash prose, punctuated numeric ranges followed by unit text, clock-time ranges including spaced meridiems and seconds, and ordinary dash prose fall through. Valid ISO date/time prefixes including optional seconds/fraction, `Z`, timezone offsets, sentence punctuation, comma tails, ordinary trailing prose and trailing time ranges fall through; date-prefixed arithmetic remains an error. Continued arithmetic after a range or telephone field still errors rather than hiding behind prose.
