## Certificate mathematics

The tangent uses natural log with the explicit 1/ln 2 factor to reach bits. At every simplex vertex q = x + 1 dominates max(1,floor(x)), including zero mass and sub-unit blends. Maximizing bucketwise is valid because each bucket has its own linear weights. Aggregate over every row and every present lane. An absent lane must be distinguished from a present lane with zero mass on one row. Each directed ceiling increases the numerator, 3/2 safely bounds 1/ln 2, and the final ceiling makes the deduction conservative. The 4N allowance covers two Q12 units at each end. In the actual squaring scorer, truncation of a normalized Q63 value loses less than 2^-63 relative per round. Telescoping with the successive powers of 1/2 leaves less than 2^-62 log2 loss, plus the final 2^-12 remainder. All scoring masses are integers in [1,2^48]. Require GPU parity with lawful_likelihood::bits_q12 at its integer powers and adjacent values, not only random masses.

Each ceil numerator B(p+1) needs up to 81 bits, C less than 2^105 for N below 2^24, then 6144 C less than 2^118. Use checked split-word arithmetic on device, deterministic reductions and exact host receipt verification. A certificate anchored at or near maximal mass can still be loose. Subtracting 4N costs about 0.034% of R here. Cap the negative mathematical lower bound at zero, but reject coverage problems or impossible arithmetic. If using the per-row max companion, test or prove monotonicity of the actual scorer, or conservatively subtract a rounding allowance from both ends. Pure KN and the KN+U restriction are feasible; the two-fold parity model is outside the single weight vector per bucket family. Compare cutoff cross-products in exact Q12. A valid interval resolves only the declared 13-expert D87 DEV-tune control and confers no claim about rules with Pick, other buckets, nonlinear state or held-out text.
