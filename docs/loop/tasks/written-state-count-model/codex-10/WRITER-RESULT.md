**Where the first wrong token moved.** On the focal panel, role alone moved 53 first errors at token 0 and 52 at token 1 to exact, and moved four exact answers to errors (two at 0 and two at 1). With both interventions, 71 first errors at 0 and 52 at 1 became exact, with the same four regressions. Boundary alone moved 15 at token 0 to exact and preserved every other focal reply. The final joint distribution was 96 first wrong at 0, four at 1, and 1,628 exact. On the unseen family, role and joint each moved 156 at 0 and 126 at 1 to exact, and lost 27 and 39 exacts to errors at 0 and 1 respectively. That gives 160 first wrong at 0, 162 at 1, and 1,406 exact. Boundary alone moved six at 0 to exact and five at 0 to 1. The shared-opening value cases account for why a one-token improvement can still be a wrong answer. **Both questions about the same facts.** On the previously unopened family, role and joint increased both-correct pairs from 332 to 580 of 864 (261 gained, 13 lost). Boundary alone retained the same 332. On earlier fresh, joint increased both-correct pairs from 365 to 416 even as individual exact replies fell by 51. These are paired, deterministic interventions on a finite factorial panel. Two seeds do not add independent language examples. The role-only treatment cleared both registered decision panels. This supports trying it in the client; novel prose, unsupported queries and durable facts require separate evidence.

**An adverse example.** For “Dan kept blue. Ben kept gold. Regarding Ben, which possession was theirs? Disregard Dan. Reply with the possession alone.” the baseline emitted gold and EOS. The role arm copied blue at position 6 and lost the first output token. When Ben's fact instead says “Ben once again kept gold”, the same intervention corrects blue to gold. Both rows have identical requested person, answer, query, exclusion and tail. The role cost can depend on local fact context.
