# [codex] Preregistration — conditional geometric copy or normal generation

Question: can the native state withhold a copy on missing, ambiguous or unrelated requests and preserve grounded answers? The crossed study observed 48/48 active reads on its older 48 unsupported prompts. Use those as historical diagnostics only. Reuse the sealed role-only selection, old value/continuation, original ID/cursor writer, actual feedback, EOS rule, 16-token cap and both released checkpoints. A gate is either CopyAllowed (forward that identical source) or Generate (no read and run ordinary decode). This is one bounded action experiment. Keep the original model's default. No second tuning cycle.

**TRAIN and evaluation.** Use a newly generated balanced labeled TRAIN family containing positive, missing, ambiguous and noncopy-prose siblings, crossing role order, values and target choice. Fit only on TRAIN. Freeze a distinct evaluation family and all hashes before examining its outcomes. Evaluate the five previously graded positive panels, the prior 48 unsupported prompts, and the new mixed-family holdout. The panel supplement fixes the particular words and generator; exclude label-bearing metadata from inference. TRAIN positives are useful copy targets only if the role-only source equals the annotated source. A wrong candidate, an unsupported/ambiguous request, or prose is a negative target. Full-front-end evidence still includes every requested positive in evaluation. Fit no thresholds to held outcomes.

**Features and update.** Represent each full tokenizer word by its before/after three packed geometric roots as in the frozen role operator. Nominate the lowest-energy alphabetic query word independently of whether it repeats (requested-nearest plus the existing margin against excluded prototypes, earliest tie). Compute two descriptors: the six-root context around the chosen bound query word and around this unrestricted nominee. Append the count of exact earlier matches of the nominee's full word (0, 1 or 2+), and a bit for whether the two word starts agree. Empty source or missing word evidence generates. TRAIN writes one positive/negative prototype per eligible row. Sort/deduplicate within each class without changing nearest distance. Query contexts use the existing integer orbit-distance sum; a difference of one count or agreement unit costs 64, the existing missing-root cost. Copy iff the nearest positive prototype distance is strictly lower than the nearest negative. Ties generate. Fixed parameters, no lexical dispatch, no annotation or external grader in inference. Sensor accepts raw token IDs, seed-bound frozen role/word classes, handoff and native prototype words only. Record TRAIN fit words, held distances/tie, action, forwarded source, actual outputs. Compare shared CUDA/host integer operators and independent replay, both seeds and repeat. Report conflicting TRAIN descriptor labels and a labeled hindsight ceiling separately from blind action.

**Decision.** On the reserved mixed holdout, require at least 75% of all noncopy rows and at least 50% within each of absent, ambiguous and prose to run ordinary generation. Relative to ungated role-only, exact-through-EOS must fall by at most five percentage points on positive rows of the new mixed holdout, original focal and original unseen at each seed. Require nonzero actual copies, movement in both decision directions vs always-copy/always-generate controls, bit parity and strict leakage/provenance checks. Report all historical positive regressions, inappropriate reads on the 48, abstention on answerable rows, harm to prior exacts, both-role exact if the panel supplies pairs, counts of exacts and first-error transitions vs both controls. Demonstrate selected-vs-consumed actions and actual full negative replies, including a free prose example with no exact target. Absence of unsolicited copying alone says nothing about fluency. All-source oracles use gold only for grading; class-dependent decisions at inference invalidate the run. Wick RTX 4090 only under the shared diagnostic lock with ≥4 GiB free. Budget 1 device-hour, 4 host threads, 4 GiB RAM and 5 GiB temporary/retained files, no rental. Finite proxy fixtures may run first. Independent FIT and staged finite/identity release precede fit or scoring. A missed bar keeps this configuration diagnostic.
