## Measurements

A new regrade of the pinned #4094 device evidence found 116 focal misses with the wrong entity and 103 with the right entity and wrong value. Of the latter, 88 started too early and 15 too late. The earlier fresh panel had 197 and 552 misses in the same two categories. The counts reproduced at both seeds over four panels. [Reproduction, source archive and tables](RESIDUALS.md)
