While the fresh study waited for Wick, we inspected the already measured candidate traces and original grades from codex-17. The new study's registration, panel and device authorization preceded this inspection. No new model scores were calculated. The 24 matched cases each had one public assertion and one scored candidate at the true original source. Both artifacts gave the same row classifications.

The numerator of the geometric margin is the nearest negative exemplar distance minus the nearest positive exemplar distance. A strictly positive numerator is necessary to emit a source in all four policies. Both denominators in the fresh trial are strictly positive. Changing the denominator can reorder candidates but cannot change their signs. The four historical gold sources with positive margins therefore give a ceiling of **4/24** for these fixed-bank changes on that particular matched panel. Ranking alone, with the old veto intact, could have recovered at most two of the 18 wrong selections.

For the six vetoes, the existing trace supplies the margins even though the original policy abstained. On ordinals 1, 2, 22 and 23, the best supported candidate was a unique positive rival. On 21 and 24, gold and a rival tied at 4/2 = 12/6. Removing only the veto would release four wrong suffixes and leave two abstentions. This is a consequence of the old recorded decisions. The fresh trials supply the prospective result.
