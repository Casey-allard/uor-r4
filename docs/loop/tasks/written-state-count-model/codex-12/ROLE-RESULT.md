## First failed transition

- All 192 held “Regarding” targets went to the **excluded** slot, including cases where no exclusion was specified. Of those rows, 140 returned a requested score below threshold and 52 requested a different word. In “Regarding Zura, name the object …”, the exact target interval `[23,26)` reached the excluded slot with score 40, and the best requested score was −2. The historical “Regarding” exhibited the same inverted role on 96/96, including “Regarding Roy …” where the requested slot instead picked “Regarding” at `[13,18)`.
- Held “What did [requested] keep, ignoring [other]?” recovered 96/96 title-case and 48/96 lowercase requested spans, with zero correct excluded spans. The loss of 48 lowercase requests accounts for the case gap on held. In one lowercase row, requested “zura” was `[21,24)` and the decision instead nominated “held” at `[11,12)` with a score of 38. This candidate came from an earlier assertion.
- Fresh “Report what [requested] was carrying for Cedar” selected a different word in 192/192 rows. For “Report what vela was carrying for Cedar” its score 36 elected “Cedar” at `[34,37)` and the gold “vela” was `[28,30)`. Fresh “Set aside [other]” identified all 192 excluded spans, but its requested slot withheld 112 below threshold and 16 on tied maxima, and chose a different word on 64. Ordinary fresh requests elicited a false nomination on all 96 rows. “Can Cedar and Summit be used as names for colors?” nominated “colors” at `[27,28)` with score 56.
- There were zero start-correct/end-wrong requested predictions on every evaluated panel. The observed first error is role rank or abstention. These examples expose a candidate field that can respond to local wording in the wrong semantic clause. A directional query-region signal and an explicit no-referent transition would test that hypothesis with an untouched construction family. [Literal traces](role/EXAMPLES.json)

## Native replay and reproduction

The model uses the packed four-coordinate D11 root lattice for six offsets around each whole-word candidate. CUDA counted unspecified, requested and excluded sites from all 1,824 TRAIN rows. The complete integer counter state was compared with the Rust reference. A loaded sparse state contains 387 occupied cells and is 39,096 bytes on each seed. The two banks share the same fitted words and bind each seed's own artifact and tokenizer SHA-256. They bind the TRAIN bare tape and labels separately. The blind executable accepts only original prefixes, uses the reloaded fitted state and compares all eight CUDA and Rust transition words per row. The finite controls pass exact multipart endpoints, tied maxima, empty candidate set, truncated bank and graded input rejection. The cold run also rejects cross-seed use.

Recreate the fixture with `cargo run -p uor-r4-tokenizer --example span-connector-panel -- TOKENIZER --paired-role NEW_OUT`. Both released tokenizer files yielded every registered byte. The [Wick runner](role/run-wick.sh) checks the release and panel hashes and uses the shared device lock on the RTX 4090. Run the [grade-only join](role/grade-wick.sh) on a sealed decisions directory. Its old comparison receipts are checked by bare-tape and seed digests. The Wick records retain complete blind rows, fit and replay logs, raw bank bytes, label-only grades, the generator identities and resource records in `~/uor-codex/20261118-paired-role-fence/role`. ## Next role experiment and LexLean correspondence

First replay the frozen role bank with and without a separately labelled **query region** restricted to the same complete candidate words. Include requests split across two clauses and a distractor immediately after the query. Measure restored requested endpoints, losses on previously exact pairs and candidates that still route to the excluded class. Keep exclusion, no-referent abstention and prior mixed/unseen transfer as independent gates. A region restriction can remove “held” from an assertion, while the 192 inverted “Regarding” roles and 192 in-query “Cedar” selections show why a second, separately fitted clause-orientation and no-referent operator would need its own test. Advance a learned region only if the oracle isolates a material class of repairs with the writer and value fence fixed. Register disjoint wording families before the next deciding run. At LexLean main `0a54277a2587485a72e981241d8f31cad52ef33b`, `R4.Route.select` consumes a supplied geometry query and `bindDeBruijnSlot` consumes a supplied slot and entity payload. The generated Rust follows those interfaces. Its trunk also resolves positive score ties by unigram then token ID, while the UOR role rank abstains on tied maxima. The two root packers have different nibble order. LexLean encodes valid roots `(1,2,3,4)` as `0x1234` and rejects roots outside `[0,16)`. UOR encodes that tuple as `0x4321` in its role-bank root keys. A future proof should specify the encoding permutation and establish that removing an out-of-region candidate preserves a previously unique surviving winner. The role-swap and no-referent outcomes require the separate held-text test.
