# [codex] Where the first wrong ID comes from on new role reversals

## What did not work, first

The downstream prerequisite failed. Even with the correct requested entity supplied, the frozen reader and writer gave 232/256 exact replies on each seed, sixteen below the registered 248 floor. Each of the 24 errors started at emitted token 0. The root-distance continuation step skipped the first two IDs of the multiword value. The proposed position-dependent and identity role banks were built and exercised on finite fixtures. Project TRAIN, scored learned outputs, their paired significance tests and a policy promotion are all **NOT_RUN**. Both Wick cold stages preserve the failed oracle.

## What changed

The independently authored panel has 128 two-direction fact pairs across four families, 128 controls and 32 role-preserving reorders. Both seeds use their own bound tokenizer. Eighty-four pairs match both names' clipped multisets and identity coordinates while differing in positional coordinates. The pre-fit authorship attempts produced 58, 64, 64, 76 and finally 84 matched pairs. All four initial constructions and unmatched rows are retained. Complete TRAIN/held names and literals were disjoint. Two incidental names also occur in older connector examples. The four-family panel deliberately concentrates boundary stress and the two tokenizers emitted identical tapes here. Each seed is one repeat on the same material. ## Still open

The strongest local explanation joins the role decision, the assertion-local value start and the unchanged exact writer. The 24 misses all sharing one adjective prevents estimating lexical generalization. An earlier continuation study recovered genuine long connectors and also lost starts for “old lamp” and “old ring.” A blanket rollback would discard measured gains. Test opposing boundary roles on new material before fitting the next geometric discriminator. The whole-model roadmap also requires a deployable count baseline, equal-byte geometric predictive gain and self-fed prose and reasoning on one native artifact. ## Plain summary

The current client answered one family of questions reliably and missed the other three. Telling it whom the question meant fixed most of the replies. On the remaining cases the model found the right fact and copied the last word of the item, having discarded its color before it began writing. We can now test whether the model can learn when to keep that leading word while retaining its earlier successes with longer connectors.
