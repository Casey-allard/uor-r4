## Plain summary

- **What this task tested**: In our earlier Stage T training run (`#2836`), the geometric read placed the right next word's cell onto its 8-cell shortlist (`admission`) on `5,009` out of `14,287` development examples (`DEV`), and an exact lookup table built only from the training set's read codes (`T_restr(A_W)`) picked the right word inside those `5,009` cells `2,140` times. Yet the model's own whole-number distance scorer (`OuterL1V1` writer) picked the right word first (`top-1`) only `455` times. We needed to find out where the `2,140 - 455 = 1,685` lost answers go, and test whether a new versioned read law (`ReadLawV2`, `#3128`) and a two-pass read that conditions interior blocks on first-pass endpoint predictions (`TwoPassEndpointOutputV1`, `#3129`) can fix within-cell word selection at zero training updates (`B0`), or whether an exact training-built within-cell reranker (`R_cell`) captures the gap directly.
- **What the Stage T error decomposition showed**: On `99.96%` (`5,007 / 5,009`) of admitted DEV rows, the model's read code was already seen in the training set, and on `90.48%` (`1,597 / 1,765`) of the rows where `T_restr(A_W)` beat `OuterL1V1`, the true word lost to another word *inside its own 16-word cell* under `OuterL1V1`'s interior-block L1 distance (`3,525` admitted targets were pushed outside `OuterL1V1`'s `top-8` altogether). Replacing interior L1 distance with a training-only within-cell read-code reranker jumps `top-1` from `455` to `1,706` even inside the single rank-1 cell alone, and to `2,140` across the 8 admitted cells (vs `753` when read codes are randomly permuted).
- **What the GPU qualification and `B0` experiment showed**: On an RTX 5090 GPU (`4,034–4,198 tokens/s`), all 6 negative refusal gates (proven to fail when bypassed and refuse when active) and all 6 finite controls (`C1`–`C6`, including `256 / 256` root recovery under `ReadLawV2`'s 2:1 weighted snap vs `129 / 256` under `ReadLawV1`, and `0 / 16` old-lineage GPU parity mismatches) passed across two independent builds. However, on the real `14,287`-row DEV set at epoch 0 (`3,334` commonly admitted rows), two-pass endpoint-output framing (`TwoPassEndpointOutputV1`) scored `266` vs `260` ($d = +6 \le 24$, `ReadLawV1`) and `268` vs `264` ($d = +4 \le 24$, `ReadLawV2`) on 8-bit interior-key accuracy against single-pass nearest-endpoint framing, missing the pre-registered $d > 24 \land d^2 > 4(b+c)$ bar. Meanwhile, the training-only within-cell read-code reranker on the same epoch-0 table lifted `top-1` from `119` to `1,107` ($d = +988 > 68$, `9.30x`). Following the roadmap item's stop rule, we close the epoch-0 two-pass interior-key framing configuration and carry forward the exact-memory within-cell lookup/reranker mechanism.
