## Whole-Project Context

1. **Why `r-vocab-4096-vs-16384` (`#2887`) was created and why it depended on `m1-faster-writer` (`#2840`):**
   - During the 19 October 2026 repository renormalization ([`docs/labs/repo-renormalization-2026-10-19/audit/synthesis.md:41`](../../../../labs/repo-renormalization-2026-10-19/audit/synthesis.md) and [`docs/research/open-questions.md:47`](../../../../research/open-questions.md)), `r-vocab-4096-vs-16384` was cataloged under *"Never fairly tested, cheap (zero training, host-only, or a small check): 4,096 versus 16,384 tokenizer pieces. The trainer already exists (`tokenizer.json`)."*
   - Its dependency `depends_on: [m1-faster-writer]` (`#2840`) existed because the legacy `OuterL1V1` writer scanned all $V$ tokens in `token_cell` (`(V \cdot B \cdot 8) + V` evals/row = `36,865` at $V = 4,096$ and `147,457` at $V = 16,384$), making $V = 16,384$ `3.90x` slower per row until the CSR cell-bucketed writer (`m1-faster-writer`, merged in `5a104c6f8`, PR `#3272`) replaced the full-vocabulary scan.

2. **What matched `V = 4,096` vs `V = 16,384` runs have already executed and sealed:**
   - **Bar `B1` — Matched GPU Writer & Serving Cost (`m1-faster-writer`, `#2840`, `5a104c6f8`):**
     Under the registered sheet [`docs/harness/prep/m1-faster-writer-2026-10-26.md`](../../../../harness/prep/m1-faster-writer-2026-10-26.md) (`TEST FITNESS: FIT`, `D68`, `D67`), `writer-bucket-qualify` ran matched GPU evaluations at `V = 4,096` and `V = 16,384` (`B = 4`, `K in {1, 8, 16}`, `3` seeds x `32` rows = `96` seeded rows per `(V, K)` / `288` seeded rows per `V`, plus `32` uniform-occupancy rows per `V` at `K = 8`) across two independent `sm_120` builds (`Build A` [`qualification-build-a.json`](../../../../labs/m1-faster-writer/qualification-build-a.json) `37419bdc303b79ac4beac0fab0ea292d74dc34ac9623754457e9107d89a40fd0` and `Build B` [`qualification-build-b.json`](../../../../labs/m1-faster-writer/qualification-build-b.json) `df583e45e1ca857cb698faf23c5a266fdc6abd3073be5f897662e432c82262bb`) on `1x NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090` (`vast-57796936`):
     - At `K = 8` seeded:
       - `V = 4,096`: `FullScan` **`855` / `853 tok/s`** (`39,268` evals/row) $\to$ `IdOrder` **`47,904` / `47,151 tok/s`** (`56.0x` speedup, **`382`** evals/row, **`102.74x`** reduction, `0` mismatches) $\to$ `ServingPruned` **`51,282` / `51,336 tok/s`** (`60.0x` speedup, **`365`** evals/row, **`107.53x`** reduction, `0` mismatches).
       - `V = 16,384`: `FullScan` **`219` / `218 tok/s`** (`150,243` evals/row, `3.90x` slower than `V = 4,096`) $\to$ `IdOrder` **`22,977` / `22,884 tok/s`** (**`104.9x` speedup**, **`765`** evals/row, **`196.15x`** reduction, `0` mismatches) $\to$ `ServingPruned` **`26,936` / `26,845 tok/s`** (**`123.0x` speedup**, **`592`** evals/row, **`253.50x`** reduction, `0` mismatches).
     - At `K = 8` uniform occupancy (`16` vs `64` tokens/cell):
       - `V = 4,096`: `39,270` $\to$ **`384`** evals/row (`0 + 256 + 128`, **`102.26x`** reduction, `0` mismatches).
       - `V = 16,384`: `150,246` $\to$ **`768`** evals/row (`0 + 256 + 512`, **`195.63x`** reduction, `0` mismatches) — proving that CSR cell bucketing cuts the `4x` vocabulary cost scaling (`150,246 / 39,270 = 3.83x`) down to **`2.00x`** (`768 / 384`) at `K = 8` (`1.62x` under `ServingPruned` at `592 / 365`, and **`1.17x`** at `K = 1` where `318 / 271` evals/row is dominated by the fixed `256` cell distances).
   - **Bar `B2` — Matched BPE Tokenizer & Digit-Piece Comparison (`number-aware-tokenizer`, `#2872`):**
     Under [`docs/harness/prep/number-aware-tokenizer-DRAFT-2026-10-22.md`](../../../../harness/prep/number-aware-tokenizer-DRAFT-2026-10-22.md) and [`docs/labs/number-aware-tokenizer/README.md`](../../../../labs/number-aware-tokenizer/README.md), matched `V = 4,096` and `V = 16,384` BPE tokenizers were trained twice per arm across `ctrl1` (`Digits(true)+ByteLevel`), `ctrl2` (plain `ByteLevel`), and `trt` (`Digits(false)+ByteLevel`) on the `170,176`-byte benchmark corpus (`05f21705…`), proving that:
     - Expanding `V` from `4,096` (`blake3:ed3f625d…`) to `16,384` (`blake3:311a1e7c…`) under `ctrl1` (`Digits(true)`) yields **`0` multi-digit pieces** at both sizes (`pure_multi_digit = 0` at `4,096` and `0` at `16,384` — `100%` of digit merges blocked by individual digit splitting).
     - Expanding `V` from `4,096` (`blake3:81522e79…`) to `16,384` (`blake3:ec4c2c66…`) under `ctrl2` (plain `ByteLevel`) leaves `pure_multi_digit = 5` (`5 / 0 / 0`) and `space_prefixed_digit = 110` / `109` (`" 2026"` absorbed as `[Ġ20, 26]` while bare `"2026"` splits differently).
     - Switching pre-tokenization to `trt` (`Digits(false)+ByteLevel`) achieves `pure_multi_digit = 109` (all `100` two-digit strings `"00".."99"`, `6` three-digit, `3` four+-digit) and `space_prefixed_digit = 0` identically at **both** `V = 4,096` (`blake3:c38dabf5…`) and `V = 16,384` (`blake3:411c366d…`). Thus **pre-tokenization (`Digits(false)`), not vocabulary size (`4,096` vs `16,384`), is the lever for number tokenization**.
   - **Bar `B3` — Discrete `B = 4` Carrier & Codebook Capacity Audit (`crates/uor-r4-discrete` & `crates/uor-r4-integer`):**
     Full discrete-driver training/evaluation at `V = 16,384` is blocked (`classification: instrument` / architectural boundary) by five explicit hard caps in the current `B = 4` discrete stack:
     1. `Spec::validate()` (`crates/uor-r4-discrete/src/lib.rs:83`): `!(2..=8192).contains(&self.vocabulary)`.
     2. `first_n_primes` (`crates/uor-r4-discrete/src/prime_zeta.rs:108`): `count > 8192`.
     3. `address_cells::build` (`crates/uor-r4-discrete/src/address_cells.rs:322, 462`): line `322` enforces `quota = v / CELLS > 16` (`V > 4,096`), and `StructuredV1` (`line 462`) ties all interior blocks `1..B-1` to the same single E8 orbit root `ROOTS[k]` (`k in 0..16`), giving at most `256 x 16 = 4,096` distinct `B = 4` vectors (`#2844` also showed that at `V = 4,096`, `B = 4` already has `256 x 16` capacity saturation when interior blocks are tied). Supporting `V = 16,384` (`64` tokens/cell across `256` cells) at `B = 4` requires using `FullVectorV1`-style independent interior-block indexing `(k / 16, k % 16)` in `0..256` (as in `address_cells.rs:383-460`) and raising the `v / CELLS <= 16` (`line 322`) and `8,192` validator/prime-table caps.
     4. `discrete_count_score` (`crates/uor-r4-discrete/src/discrete.cu:633`) and `Device::count_reference` (`crates/uor-r4-discrete/src/device.rs:1890`): `vocabulary > 8192`.
     5. `d11_export::validate_and_extract_cells` (`crates/uor-r4-discrete/src/d11_export.rs:149`), `import_d11` (`d11_export.rs:386`), and `IntegerDiscreteModel::from_bytes` (`crates/uor-r4-integer/src/discrete_d11.rs:667`): `!(2..=8192).contains(&spec.vocabulary)`.

## Decision

1. **Approve the registered bars `B1`–`B3` in [`docs/harness/prep/r-vocab-4096-vs-16384-2026-10-26.md`](../../../../harness/prep/r-vocab-4096-vs-16384-2026-10-26.md) (`D68`).**
2. **Keep `V = 4,096` (`trt` `Digits(false)+ByteLevel` / `d36d3e87…`) for M1–M3 and close `r-vocab-4096-vs-16384` (`#2887`) on the matched `V = 4,096` vs `V = 16,384` GPU writer (`#2840`), BPE tokenizer (`#2872`), and discrete-carrier capacity (`B3`) results without spending redundant GPU pod hours (`CHARTER.md` §3.5).**
   - *Rejected alternative 1:* Rent a GPU pod to re-run `writer-bucket-qualify` at `V = 4,096` and `V = 16,384`. Rejected because `qualification-build-a.json` (`37419bdc…`) and `qualification-build-b.json` (`df583e45…`) already ran on `1x RTX 5090` (`vast-57796936`) at the exact current `main` tree (`5a104c6f8`) with `0` metric diffs between builds (`CHARTER.md` §3.5: *"Spend GPU only where a run can change a decision"*).
   - *Rejected alternative 2:* Switch M1 from `V = 4,096` to `V = 16,384` now. Rejected because (a) `V = 16,384` would invalidate the frozen M1 DEV panel (`14,287` rows, `U = 1,226`, `T2 = 6,300` in `CHARTER.md` §4.8), (b) `B2` proves that `Digits(false)+ByteLevel` already forms all `109` multi-digit pieces (`100/100` two-digit strings) at `V = 4,096`, (c) `B1` proves that even with the CSR bucketed writer, `V = 16,384` increases within-shortlist token distance work by `2.00x` at `K = 8` (`768` vs `384` evals/row, `26,936` vs `51,282 tok/s`), and (d) `B3` proves that `StructuredV1` (`B = 4` tied interior roots) caps injective cell capacity at `256 x 16 = 4,096` tokens until independent interior-block indexing `(k / 16, k % 16)` is used.
   - *Cost to reverse:* Zero — if a later milestone (`M6` coding/reasoning) widens `V` to `16,384`, the CSR bucketed writer (`bucket.cu`) and BPE trainer (`train-bpe` with `Digits(false)+ByteLevel`, `blake3:411c366d…`) are already qualified at `V = 16,384`, and `B3` lists the exact 5-file checklist to widen `uor-r4-discrete` and `uor-r4-integer` from `4,096`/`8,192` to `16,384`.
