## What did not work, first

- **In `antigravity-39` (`2d6c597e5539daed4d57282e57e401e759258217`), `SavedCompiler` remain gated behind `#[cfg(any())]`, hardcoded `MemoryTurnGate::default()`, and passed `None` for the `StatementMargin` in `SavedCompiler::table_action` (`crates/uor-r4-training/src/relation_compiler.rs:845,986`):** As identified in Claude's exact-head review of PR `#3079`, the margin gate in `MemoryTurnGate::check_margin` could not fire on `SavedCompiler::table_action`, and a caller could not construct an ungated `SavedCompiler` baseline arm for the two-arm GPU A/B sheet. Furthermore, when un-gating `SavedCompiler` for `#[cfg(test)]` in `antigravity-40`, our initial 7-row synthetic training slice in `saved_compiler_action_wires_configurable_gate_and_real_margin` had only two `assert` rows for `"My name is {v}."` / `"Call me {v}."` versus one `update` and one `query` row sharing `"my"` and `"name"`, which caused the bag-of-words `act_head` to rank `query` above `assert` on `"My name is Zorvak."`. Switching the test fixture to `small_world()` (`42` balanced rows across `6` names and `4` towns) gave a clear positive `act_write_gap_milli` and `relation_gap_milli` on `"My name is Zorvak."`.
- **In `antigravity-39`, `has_third_person_possessor` contained four panel-specific nouns (`"architect"`, `"cafeteria"`, `"conference"`, `"team's"`) and `check_turn_structure` lacked general negation, conditional/hypothetical, reported-speech, and present-tense `code_word` checks:** Removing those panel-specific nouns and testing unseen third-person possessors (`"The baker's specialty isourdough."`, `"Our landlord's cat is named Cleo."`, `"My brother's favorite color is green."`), negated statements (`"My name is not Alice."`), conditional statements (`"If my favorite color is blue, I will buy that shirt."`), reported speech (`"He said my favorite color is teal."`), past-tense code-word prose (`"Yesterday the code word was lantern."`), and lowercase `"I'm tired."` exposed where general syntactic rules were required. Replacing the noun list with general third-person pronoun checks, `<noun>'s` detection outside the two closed-relation slot possessives (`pet's`/`dog's`/`cat's` and `manager's`/`lead's`), kinship/person nouns after `"my"`/`"our"`, negation (`not`/`never`/`n't`), conditional/reported-speech clauses, and present-tense `"is"` grounding for `code_word` eliminated all panel-specific vocabulary while preserving `0 / 50` false writes on `nonrelation-prose-v1.txt`.
- **In `antigravity-39`, `memory_turn_gate_preserves_all_world_v2_relation_statement_templates` tested `22` hand-copied strings instead of the `79` generator templates in `crates/uor-r4-training/src/milestone_world_v2.rs`:** Importing `milestone_world_v2::relations()` directly in `#[cfg(test)]` exposed two real `milestone_world_v2.rs` templates that the `antigravity-39` frame rules missed: `"Update: {v} is the project lead."` (`manager`, which lacked `"my"`/`"our"`) and `"Actually, I'm a {v} now."` (`role`, which lacked a role cue word). Updating the `manager` and `role` frame rules in `matches_closed_relation_frame` brought direct generator coverage to `79 / 79` across both `Split::Train` and `Split::Development` (`158 / 158` template-split pairs) plus `17 / 17` held-out paraphrase templates.
- **What this PR does not close:** GPU evaluation of the gated `world=v2` session recipe (`compiler-save-op-v25-rawtable` + `op-model-v25`, `op_policy = unless_query`) on [`data/panels/nonrelation-prose-v1.txt`](../../../../../data/panels/nonrelation-prose-v1.txt) and across the 3 held-out seeds (`21, 22, 23`) of 500 `world=v2` dialogues (`1,500` dialogues / `7,605` turns) is a GPU qualification run (CHARTER §4.5) that awaits a non-author `TEST FITNESS: FIT` review of [`docs/labs/m3-memory-turn-discriminator-gate/preregistration-DRAFT-2026-10-23.md`](../../../../labs/m3-memory-turn-discriminator-gate/preregistration-DRAFT-2026-10-23.md) and owner/PM registration on `#2855`.
